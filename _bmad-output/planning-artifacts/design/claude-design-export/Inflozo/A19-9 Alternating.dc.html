Alternating

Each featured post is a band across the whole content width — picture 672, gutter 64, text 560 — and the sides swap on every band. Where 8 Lead and Two ranks its posts by size, 9 ranks nothing: two or three pieces at identical weight, read one after another down the page.

The alternation is the whole device, so it is the first thing to go when the layout narrows — below 1080 every band puts its picture above its text and the sides stop swapping. Title is fixed at 34 ⚑ and there is no Title size control: three bands at 40 is a page of headlines with photographs attached, which is a different section.

The gutter is 64 rather than the category's 24 because these two blocks are not columns of one thing, they are a picture and a piece of writing that happen to be level — at 24 the text reads as a caption. The picture keeps the larger half at every ratio; the text column is 560 and does not move, which is the floor's measure beside a picture.
Bands are centred against each other, not top-aligned. A 448 px picture beside four lines of text top-aligned leaves 250 px of white under the text and the band stops looking like one object; centring is the only alignment that holds at all three ratios.
Band three is post 3, which has no picture, and it reflows in place — text to 720 at the band's leading edge, no plate, no gap where the picture would be. ⚑ A reflowed band always starts at the leading edge whichever side its turn was: a 720 column pushed to the right of an empty 672 is a stranded paragraph, and the alternation is a property of pictures, not of text.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES ⚑ No Title size, and the space it would have taken went to Starts. Eight designs in A19 carry Title size; this one fixes the band title at 34 because the control that matters here is which way the first picture faces, and a section with three 40 px titles stacked down it has stopped being a spotlight section. Disclosed on the frame and in the spec. Divider is two values, not three. A19-8's third value — Column rule — has no meaning in a stack; there is no gutter to run a rule down. Where a design cannot offer a value its neighbour offers, it offers fewer values rather than a value that does nothing. Padding is the section's and the 96 between bands is not exposed. ⚑ Mine. Spacious raises the section's top and bottom to 132 and leaves the bands at 96, because the gap between two bands is a reading rhythm and the gap around the section is a page rhythm — one control moving both would make Spacious mean two things.
