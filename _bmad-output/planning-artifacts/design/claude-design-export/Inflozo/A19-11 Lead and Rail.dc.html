Lead and Rail

One spotlight on 848 with its picture above its text, and everything else the query returned as a rail of titles in the 424 beside it. Where 8 Lead and Two is composed for a fixed three, this one is composed for however many there are — the rail is two entries at Count 3 and four at Count 5, and it is the same rail.

The rail carries no pictures and no excerpts at any setting. It is a table of contents, not a set of small cards, and the only thing it can be told to add is a date and a tag. A lead with no picture is plated here rather than reflowed ⚑, because the rail is beside it and a reflowed lead leaves four titles hanging in the page.
