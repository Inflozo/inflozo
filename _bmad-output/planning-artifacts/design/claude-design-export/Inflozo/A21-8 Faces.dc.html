The sentence is assembled, not authored ⚑ — "Written by", up to three linked names, then "and N others" only when N is more than zero. At one writer it reads as a byline and at twelve as a masthead, with no control changing between them. The remainder counts every writer the query returned, not the site total ⚑, so "and 6 others" at How many: Six is a lie the design avoids by counting from {​{pagination.total}​}.
