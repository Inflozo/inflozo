PART A · THE RESPONSIVE SYSTEM

Responsive behaviour is a property of the archetype, not the design.

485 designs cannot each be drawn at three widths. Every design in the library reduces to one of fifteen structural archetypes, and the collapse rule belongs to the archetype. Design fifteen ladders and the whole library is covered. The table beside each strip is the deliverable a developer implements; the frames are the proof it holds with real content.
