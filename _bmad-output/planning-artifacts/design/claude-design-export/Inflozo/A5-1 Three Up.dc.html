Three Up

A centred head over three bare items on the page's own ground. The category's floor: it sets the item, the grid, the head and the section action, and the fifteen designs after it are departures from this one. Nothing is on a plane, nothing has an edge, and the only thing holding the row together is the 32 px gutter and the icons' shared baseline.

Six items in two rows of three, 32 px between columns and 40 px between rows, on a 1296 px content width — each item is 410 px, which is what the 21 px title and the 16 px body were sized for. The head is 780 px for the title and 620 px for the sub, centred; the set is 64 px below it and the action 56 px below the set, both larger than the row gap so the three bands read as three things. The section action is A1·1's button at A1·1's size — 14 px/600, padding 9×17 — and not A4's 46 px hero scale: a button in the middle of a page is not the page's first ask. Flagged as mine: the 64 and 56 px separations, the 410 px item, and the action's return to A1·1's metrics.

1 · The section title is an <h2> and names the region: <section aria-labelledby>. With no title authored the region is unlabelled and the item titles step to h2 rather than leaving a gap in the outline. 2 · The eyebrow is a <p> above the h2, as in A4. It is not a heading and not aria-describedby on the title. 3 · The items are a list. A <ul> of six <li>, each with an <h3> and a paragraph, so a screen reader announces six items before reading one. A grid of divs announces nothing. 4 · The icon is aria-hidden at every treatment. It repeats the title's meaning at best and it is chosen from a fixed set at worst, so it never carries a label — including at Icon in accent, where it is the most conspicuous thing in the section. 5 · The section action is one <a> with A1·1's focus ring. At Item link Text link each item's link is a second, and the reading order is title, body, link, item after item. No landmark, no tablist, no region per item. Contrast · title 12.1:1 light / 12.6:1 dark · item title 12.1:1 / 12.6:1 · body 5.4:1 / 6.4:1 · eyebrow 5.4:1 / 6.4:1 · glyph on tint 11.2:1 / 11.9:1 · glyph on accent 4.9:1 · action label 4.7:1. The tint container is 1.1:1 against the page and carries no meaning of its own. Zoom. At 200% text the three columns become one at the same content width and the icons stay 40 px — the container is a box, not text, and growing it with the type would push the first item's body below the fold on a laptop.

Responsive · empty · a11y · flagged Responsive: three across down to 1081; two across at 1080 and below, gaps 24/32, padding 80, title 34, item title 20; one column at ≤ 767 with 28 px between items, 24 px with Media None, the head left-aligned whatever the control says, and the action full width at 48 px.
Empty: no items → the section does not render and the editor names the field. No body on an item → the item ends; where no item has body the row gap tightens 40 → 32. No icon on an item → the fall-back mark. No eyebrow, sub, action or note → each simply absent.
