6 · Contrast Band

1 Chips on an inverted full-bleed band. The same wrapping pill row, the same head, and a ground that stops the page — the design a site uses once, between two light sections, to say the topics are a place rather than a footnote.

The band is the whole difference, and it is the whole design. ⚑ The all-topics link is drawn in the band's own text colour with a 2 px underline rather than in accent, because a pack's accent is checked for AA against background and not against contrast. A6 made the same call.
