9 · Carousel

One film at a time on a scroll-snap track, its neighbours showing at both edges, with dots, arrows and a counter beneath. The set is walked rather than scanned.

It is the section for a short series where each film deserves the whole width — an interview series, a set of five talks — and the reader is expected to move through them in order.

REDUCED MOTION AND NO SCRIPT
Under reduced motion the track jumps rather than glides ⚑ — scroll-behavior:auto instead of smooth — and nothing else changes. A14·7’s answer, carried verbatim.With JavaScript off the track is still a scroll-snap strip and still works ⚑; the dots, the arrows and the counter are hidden. That is the carousel module’s own degradation and this design needs no help from it.There is no autoadvance value ⚑ — a track that moves on a timer is autoplay applied to the arrangement instead of to the film.

Advancing the track destroys a loaded player ⚑ — nothing plays off-screen, and a film left running in a slide the reader has scrolled past is the clearest case of a section taking a decision that is not its to take. The arrows are drawn but disabled while a film is loaded ⚑ is the alternative, and it was rejected: the reader should not have to close a player to move on. The box is reserved before anything loads ⚑ — every frame is an aspect-ratio box and the poster, the notice and the player all sit inside it. Nothing moves when a film is played. Settlement 2.

Upload only ⚑ — an embed is never asked to autoplay; the facade exists to prevent the request. Ambient loop is muted, looped and playsinline ⚑ — muted is what makes autoplay browser-legal, which is why the value cannot be unmuted. The poster stands in under reduced motion and with no JavaScript ⚑.
ARCHITECT: REGISTRY ADDITION (ambient loop)Gating an autoplaying muted loop on prefers-reduced-motion is behaviour no registry module covers ⚑. The no-JS and no-module state is designed anyway — the poster with the native controls, which is Click to play — and no module name is coined.
Editor-side seeding, new in this pass ⚑: pasting a recognised URL fetches the oEmbed title and duration once, in the editor, and seeds both fields; “Use provider thumbnail” downloads the image and re-hosts it as the poster ⚑.
