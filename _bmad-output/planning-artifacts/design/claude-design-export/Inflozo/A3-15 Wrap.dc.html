Wrap

Every link in one wrapping block at reading size, with no headings and no columns. The design for a site with twenty links and no hierarchy worth drawing: instead of inventing four group names, it sets the whole list as a single field of text across the measure. The block is the composition.

Twenty-two links at 17 px with a 32 px horizontal gap and a 14 px vertical one — wide enough that the eye reads a field of words rather than a sentence, tight enough that the block has an edge. Order is the authored order, groups flattened, and no group name is shown: this design's argument is that the names were the weakest part of the list. The lockup is the standard 26 px one, above the block, and the legal row carries the social glyphs at 30 px as A3·8 does.

Six links with middots is where this design meets A3·1 Minimal Line, and the difference is one step of type size. That is deliberate: a site that prunes its footer from twenty-two links to six should not have to switch design to keep the result composed. 214 px.

1 · One <nav aria-label="Footer"> holding a single <ul> of every link. Where the authored content has groups, each group is a nested <ul> with a visually-hidden heading — the grouping survives even though the design does not draw it, so switching to A3·2 loses nothing and a screen-reader user still hears “About, 4 items”. 2 · Links are ordinary list items laid out with flex-wrap and gap — not inline text separated by spaces, and not display: contents. At 17 px with 14 px of row gap each link's box is 37 px at desktop and 44 px at ≤ 767. 3 · Separators are ::after content on all but the last item, so nothing reads “middot” twenty-one times and the marks cannot be selected or copied into a pasted link list. A field of links and screen readers · a list of 22 links with no headings is announced as “list, 22 items”, which is honest — this design is for the case where that is the truth. Above 24 links the editor recommends A3·8 Sitemap, where the headings do work. Flagged as mine: the 24-link threshold. Contrast · 17 px muted on background 5.4:1 light, 6.4:1 dark; separators 1.6:1, decorative and CSS-generated.
