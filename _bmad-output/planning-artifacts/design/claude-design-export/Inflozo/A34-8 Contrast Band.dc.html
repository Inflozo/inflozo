Contrast Band

3 Bar inverted: the same page links, on a full-width band of the pack's contrast colour, 120 px tall and set a step larger. It ends a page rather than punctuating it — A17·7 and A18·7 drew the same move for a feed, and this is the pager that belongs under them.

Every colour on the band is derived, not chosen. A17·7's rule — muted at 60% toward the band's own text, hairlines at 10% — is reused unchanged, which is what lets an inverted section survive twelve packs without a second palette. The one thing that does not survive is accent, and this design is where the library finally hits that wall: in dark, Paper's accent on Paper's dark contrast measures 2.31:1, so the value is disabled with its ratio shown.

Three derived colours and one token, and the derivations are A17·7's exactly. Muted is #A5A29D — the band's text mixed 60% toward its ground — and the ellipsis is the 10% step, #39362F, which is the same value the band would use for a hairline. Nothing here is a new colour and nothing is picked by eye; a pack supplies two values and the band computes the rest.
The numerals go 15 → 17 on the band and nowhere else in A34. ⚑ Light text on a dark ground reads optically smaller at the same size — the reason A17·7 lifted its meta a step — and 17 on the band matches the weight of 15 on the page. The 40 px box and the 44 px target are unchanged, so the row is 10 px wider than 1 Numbers' and nothing else moves.
Attachment defaults to Separate here alone. Joined puts a 1,440 px black band 48 px under the last row of a grid, and at that distance it reads as a fourth row rather than as the end of the section. 96 above and 96 below is the composition, and the band's own height is the third measurement — the same three-part sizing 3 Bar uses, one step larger throughout.

This is the first control value in the library to be disabled by a measurement rather than by a judgement. ⚑ Rule 4 has been in the project since A1 — “a value that would fail contrast is disabled with its ratio shown, never silently allowed” — and until now no design has hit it. Paper's dark contrast is a light band, so its accent is a light orange on a light cream, and no amount of care in the design fixes that. It is a pack finding, not a design fault, and the panel says which. The line reads “Accent underline is unavailable in dark on this pack — Paper's accent measures 2.31:1 on its dark contrast band. Weight only is drawn instead.” A pack whose dark contrast is a dark band would pass and the value would return, which is the correct behaviour for a token-driven library. The check has to run per pack and per mode, twenty-four times. Named for the architect: the accent-on-contrast ratio is a pack property the project does not yet record, and three categories have now derived dark accents by hand.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A disabled value states its measurement, in the panel, permanently — not on hover and not in a warning colour. ⚑ The segmented control still shows both values; Accent underline is drawn at the muted step and cannot be selected in dark, and the helper line beneath carries the ratio and the substitute. In light both values are live. The same control is therefore differently available by mode, which is a first in the library and the honest consequence of a token-driven palette. Alignment Split to the edges requires Contents Direction and position, and choosing it with Contents Numbers switches Contents rather than doing nothing — the one place in A34 where one control moves another. It is allowed because there is no sensible reading of “split five centred numerals to the edges”, and the panel names the change as it happens. Padding Compact raises to 80 rather than 64. A17·7's rule for a contrast band, inherited without argument: an inverted band 64 px from a footer looks like a mistake in the stack. Four controls. Cut: an Edge control — a contrast band is full bleed or it is a black card, and a black card under a feed is a different design nobody asked for — and a Numbers Windowed / All pages pair, because eleven numerals in contrast at 17 px is a keyboard, not a pager.

Paper's dark contrast is a light band, so the section gets lighter as the page gets darker — which is what contrast means and why the role exists rather than a literal colour. The three derived values invert with it: muted becomes #6C6961 at 60% toward #171511, the ellipsis #D7D2C6 at 10%, and A17·7's rule computes both without a second table.
The accent underline is not drawn here and this frame is the reason it is disabled. ⚑ #E0805A on #EDE7DA is 2.31:1 — a warm orange on a warm cream, a marker only a reader with good colour vision and good light would find. The band's own text at 14.6:1 does the job instead, and the current page is still unmistakable at 600 weight against muted numerals. Nothing was moved to compensate, which is rule 1 holding under pressure: the design lost a colour and kept its structure.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A full-bleed band of the pack's contrast colour, 120 px tall, carrying the windowed page links at 17 px and the position line beneath them, both in colours derived from the band rather than from the palette. The category's only inverted ground. 2 · Structural descriptor. bar · none · contrast · variable · none · inverted band
Identical to 3 Bar on four slots and separated by ground alone, which is exactly the case the brief names: the same bar on surface and on contrast are two designs, and the ground slot is what says so. 3 · Archetype. bar. Two departures. The band's height is fixed at every width except at Contents Direction and position below 767, where it takes 20 px of padding instead of a height; and Alignment Split to the edges changes another control rather than failing. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 band full bleed, 120 tall, contents on the 72 px page margin, numerals 17 in 40 px boxes on a 10 gap, position line 14 below, 96 above and 96 below. 1080 band 112. 834 band 104, page margin 40, padding 80. ≤ 767 band 88, page links dropped and the page count moves into the row at 17/600, chevrons 44 px boxes bordered in the derived 10% step, padding 64. Contents Direction and position at ≤ 767 stacks into two 44 px rows with a derived hairline between them and the band sizes to its contents. 5 · Content fields. navLabel, newerLabel, olderLabel, all optional text as elsewhere. Read-only: the six pagination values. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. The band reads no content of its own — it is 3 Bar's field list exactly, which is what makes switching between the two lossless. 6 · Controls. Band height Compact 96 · Comfortable 120 · Spacious 160. Contents Numbers · Numbers and position · Direction and position. Current page Accent underline · Weight only — Accent underline disabled in dark with its ratio shown ⚑. Alignment Centre · Split to the edges. Four, then the block's four fields and its two doors — Attachment and At the ends both defaulting differently from the category. Cut: an Edge control and a Numbers pair.
7 · Data. The route's pagination object; destinations /page/N/. 0 → no section. 1 page → nothing renders, band included — and this is the design where that matters most, because an empty inverted band is a black stripe across a page for no reason. 2 pages → two numerals in a 120 px band, weaker than 3 Bar's two in 72 and the panel names 2 Prev and Next. Many → unchanged; the window bounds the row and the band's height is constant across the archive. At Band height Comfortable or taller the position line carries both its values — “Page 11 of 11 · Posts 121–132 of 132” — and at Compact it carries one ⚑. 8 · Empty state. One page → nothing, band and all. In the editor: the band drawn at 30% opacity with the cause in the pack's page colours beneath it, not inside it — an editor notice on a contrast band would need a fourth derived colour ⚑. At the ends Dimmed uses the derived 60% muted for the spent direction link, and at Contents Numbers and position there is no direction link to dim at all, so the archive's ends change only the numerals. Position line values behave as the block says; there is no state in which the band renders empty. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. mode-toggle is the one module that touches this design, from a distance: its prefers-color-scheme branch is what decides whether the accent underline is available, and since that branch is pure CSS (FR-E4) the disabled value resolves without JavaScript. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> with an <ol>; no heading. aria-current="page", rel="prev"/"next", ellipsis aria-hidden. Measured on the band: muted numerals 5.9:1 light and 5.4:1 dark, the current page 15.9:1 and 14.6:1, the accent underline 4.79:1 and 2.31:1 — the last of which is why it is disabled in dark. The current page is never marked by colour alone: weight and contrast carry it in both modes and the underline is an addition. Focus is A6's ring in the band's own text colour, not accent ⚑ — an accent ring inherits the same 2.31:1 problem — at a 4 px offset. Targets 44. Print: prints as 1 Numbers on white, A17·7's rule, so no reader spends a cartridge on a black band. Flagged ⚑ — numerals at 17 rather than 15 on an inverted ground; Attachment defaulting to Separate; the position line carrying both values at 120 px and above; Alignment changing Contents; the band sizing to its contents when stacked; chevron borders in the derived 10% step; the focus ring in the band's text colour rather than accent; the accent underline disabled in dark at a measured 2.31:1, and the accent-on-contrast ratio named as a pack property the project does not record.
