Rows

One person per full-width row: the circle photograph at the left, the name, role and bio in a column beside it, an optional hairline between. The design for a team whose bios are worth reading — it is the only one in A12 that takes 400 characters, because it is the only one with a measure wide enough to set them.

It is also where the role stops being a caption. At Meta at the right of the row the role sits at the far end of the name's line, on the name's own baseline, which turns a masthead into something closer to a ledger — and it is the only arrangement in A12 where the role is not directly under the name. The reading order is unchanged: name, then role, then bio, in the DOM, at both values.

ACCESSIBILITY · THE ROLE MOVES, THE READING ORDER DOES NOT At Meta at the right of the row the role is still the second <p> in the DOM — the arrangement is justify-content: space-between on the line that holds both, with no order property, no absolute positioning and no duplicated text. Name, role, bio, in that order, at both values and every width. ⚑ A12-0's component rule. The rule is a border-top on the <li>, never an <hr> — twelve horizontal rules in a list is twelve separators a screen reader may announce, and they separate nothing that the list does not already separate. The first row takes no rule, so the list never opens with one. A row is not a target, a landmark or an <article>. One <ul>, twelve <li>, three <p>s each, and the name is the only focusable thing in a row. Twelve rows is twelve list items and no headings, so a reader arrives at “list, 12 items” and can leave it in one keystroke — which is the argument for the list and against twelve <h3>s. Contrast: title and name 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, role and bio 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, rule 1.3:1, underline and ring 3.3:1 / 6.3:1, initials on their fill 5.1:1 / 5.4:1. The bio is muted rather than text, which is the one contrast decision worth arguing with here: at 15 px on a 824 measure it is reading matter, and muted is 5.6:1. It stays muted because the name is the row's one piece of text-coloured type and a row of two black blocks has no hierarchy. ⚑ Mine, and stated as a judgement rather than a rule. Reduced motion changes nothing. At 400% zoom the photograph goes above the text, the bio reflows on the column, and the meta returns under the name.
