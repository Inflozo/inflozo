A10 STATS AND NUMBERS · DESIGN 5 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · THE ONLY DESIGN WITH TWO RANKS OF VALUE

Lead Stat

The first stat at Display 68, the rest at Row 40 beside it or beneath it. The only design in A10 where two values are set at different sizes, and therefore the only one that makes a claim about which number matters — a claim the site makes by putting that stat first in the repeater.

Everywhere else in the category, rank is refused: the accent is never spent on a value, no stat gets a plane or a border of its own, and 1 Row sets three numbers at one size on one baseline precisely so that none of them wins. Here rank is the whole design, and it is carried by size alone — no colour, no fill, no rule, no icon, no word like “headline”.

ACCESSIBILITY · A RANK THAT IS ONLY VISUAL One <ul> of three or four <li>, the lead as item one — not a lead element plus a nested list, and not two lists. A reader hears four stats in authored order and nothing tells them one is bigger. That is the honest report: the rank is a typographic emphasis the site made by ordering the repeater, and order is what a screen reader already conveys. ⚑ Flagged: no <strong>, no “most importantly”, no aria-label announcing rank. At Lead side Right the visual order and the reading order disagree — the lead is read first and drawn last — and the panel says so. A9·5 established this cost for a head column; it is the same cost for a stat. The alternative, reordering the DOM, would make the control change what a screen reader hears, which is worse. The hairlines in the stack are border-top on the <li>, never <hr>, and they are not drawn between the lead and the rest at any value. STATS · the item list · outside the design’s listStats is the P0·3 item list: Add stat arrives with content — value 100, label “What this number counts” — Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. Per item: value ≤ 12 · prefix ≤ 3 · suffix ≤ 6 · label ≤ 60. note, prev and share held, and nothing about being the lead is editable inside the item — the first row is the lead and the size pair is the design. Value source: Authored · Member count · Paid member count. At Member count the value renders Ghost’s {​{total_members}} string verbatim — “1,200+” in, “1,200+” out, no maths and no formatter, which is the category’s own no-formatter rule — prefix and suffix are disabled and the label stays authored. {​{total_paid_members}} is offered only when the site has paid members enabled. A bound member count can be the lead, which is the ordinary case for a newsletter. Eight is the ceiling of stats[], raised from six in this pass: “Eight numbers is as many as a reader holds in one look. A ninth is a table, and a table is a different section.” 3 Grid is the only design that draws eight; everywhere else seven or eight authored names it. Seven or eight authored: this design draws its own count and 3 Grid is advised, and the lead’s Display size is not drawn there.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesValue, prefix, suffix and label edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The value raises no marks toolbar — it is plain text, so bold, italic and a link inside a figure are withheld rather than offered and refused. Eyebrow, title, sub, source and the link label take the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. A value bound to a member count is not inline-editable: clicking it says “Edit in Ghost”, because the figure is the site’s member list and not a word anyone typed here. sourceUrl and linkUrl open the Link Picker; the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon. Reorder is the emphasis control — there is no featured flag to edit.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registrycount-up, from the fixed registry, and only at Count up On. Its own degradation is the argument: “The final value renders as static text — it is already in the HTML before JS ever runs.” JS off, reduced motion and assistive technology all get the figure rather than the animation, and the module does not re-run on the way back up the page. Edit-safe: it does not run while the section is edited, so every frame here is still a resting state. At Count up Off — the default — this design declares no module at all: Lead position, Lead side and Rules are values on the section, Beside becoming Above at 1080 is a media query, and the separators are borders.
DATA · Ghost’s two member helpers · outside the design’s listTwo figures in A10 can come from Ghost, and both are public theme helpers: {​{total_members}} and {​{total_paid_members}}, each rendering a pre-rounded string. They are reached from the item’s Value source and not from a “Populate from…” panel — there is no list to populate. Everything else is authored in this section. Refused on record: a post count from a tag (a number nobody wrote and nobody checks), any admin-API figure — opens, revenue, growth — a live figure from a third party, and the four ornaments: sparkline, dial, donut, pie. 9 Bars’ single proportional bar is as far as A10 goes.
