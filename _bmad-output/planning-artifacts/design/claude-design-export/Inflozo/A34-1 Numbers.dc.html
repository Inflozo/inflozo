A34 PAGINATION STYLES · DESIGN 1 OF 10 · PAPER PACK · FIVE CONTROLS · THE DEFAULT, AND THE ONLY ONE THAT TELLS A READER WHERE THEY ARE AND LETS THEM COME BACK TO IT

Numbers

A centred row of page links with the current page marked, an ellipsis where the window skips, and a directional link at each end. Every destination is a real /page/N/ URL, so the reader can bookmark page 6, send it to someone, and land back on it a week later.

This is A34's default and the category's answer to settlement 1. 6 Load More and 7 Endless are more comfortable to use and neither can tell you where you are; this one can, and it costs a page load to do it. It declares no behaviour module — nothing here needs JavaScript, so nothing here breaks without it.

THE TWO-PAGE FEED · TRANSPORT TAG · 19 POSTS · NO WINDOW, NO ELLIPSIS, NOTHING SPECIAL — THE WINDOW ONLY APPEARS ABOVE SEVEN PAGES
The window is first · current−1 · current · current+1 · last, with an ellipsis wherever that skips a page. Seven slots maximum, so the row's width is bounded by the design rather than by the archive — an eighty-page archive draws the same seven boxes as an eleven-page one. At one page the section does not render at all. A pager over a single page tells the reader there is more when there is not. In the editor it draws its outline and one line naming the cause, as A19's zero state does. Hover is A17's 2 px accent underline at 160 ms; focus is A6's ring at a 4 px offset, on the 38 px box rather than the numeral, so a 1 and an 11 take the same ring. Accent is spent twice in this design and nowhere else: the current page's underline and that ring.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A34 is not a section anyone places. ⚑ The ten designs are the values of one Named Select — Pagination style — on the designated main feed's own sidebar. The pager renders attached below that feed; the Pager block and the chosen design's controls appear under the select. Nothing about A34 is dragged, shuffled or given a slot on the canvas, and switching design switches the value of one field rather than swapping an object. Its ground, its vertical spacing and its top divider are the feed's — the pager carries no universal controls of its own, and the per-design Padding row that used to sit here was the feed's Vertical spacing under a second name. No visitor-facing string is authored in this panel. The five labels — navLabel, newerLabel, olderLabel, moreLabel, endLabel — are theme translation-catalog strings, edited once per language rather than five text fields per pager, and the position lines, the dimmed-end strings, the failure line, “Loading” and 7 Endless's “Back to top” join them. So there is nothing inline-editable in an A34 pager at all: no icon slot, no repeater, no authored text. Clicking a label in the editor opens the catalog. There is no Posts block, and A34 is the only category in the library without one. ⚑ A pager does not own a query — it reads the route's pagination object and draws what it finds. So there is no Source, no Show, no Order and no posts-per-page control, which is the field a user will come here looking for: it is Ghost's posts_per_page, set once for the whole site, and the block states its current value as a read-only line so the search ends here. The Pager block replaces it — four fields, identical in all ten, below each design's own controls and counted toward none of their totals. Attachment Joined · Separate. Labels Newer and older · Previous and next · Glyphs only. At the ends Hidden · Dimmed. Position line Off · Page count · Post range. Every one of the four is a property of pagination itself rather than of an arrangement, which is why it is a block and not a control. Attachment spends no new number. Joined puts the pager inside the feed's own container at 48 below the last row — A17's row gap; Separate makes it its own section on the page ground with the feed's bottom padding above it, 96 at Comfortable. Both values already exist in A17's floor, so the seam A17 and A18 promised is closed without inventing a spacing step ⚑. Five controls, and two rows left. A “Jump to page” field was drawn and dropped, and Padding left as a duplicate of the feed’s Vertical spacing: it is a form in a nav, it needs validation, and on an eleven-page archive the window already reaches every page in two clicks.

The ellipsis is the only element that needed a decision. In light it is #C4BEB3, one step below muted, so it reads as furniture rather than a page. Inverting that step lands on #4E4740 ⚑ — between border and muted, derived rather than a token, and the third value in the project to be derived this way. It measures 2.4:1 against the ground, which is below AA and correct: it is aria-hidden decoration and any reader who needs it has the numerals either side.
The hairline is border in both modes, unchanged, and A17's symmetric-step finding holds: it sits far enough off each ground to separate the pager from the feed without drawing a line anyone looks at. Accent lifts to #E0805A for the current page's underline — 5.9:1 on the dark ground, and it is the only saturated pixel in the frame.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A centred cluster of page links on the page ground, windowed to seven slots with an ellipsis where it skips, the current page marked by weight and a 2 px accent underline, a directional link at each end and a position line beneath. The only A34 design that names every destination. 2 · Structural descriptor. nav · none · page · variable · none · windowed numbered links
⚑ variable is read here as “the count follows the archive” rather than “the author decides”, stated once on the proof frame for the whole category: a pager's repeating unit is a page link, and nobody authors those. 4 Pill is the same nav in a pill; 8 Contrast Band is the same row on the inverted ground. 3 · Archetype. nav. One departure, and it is the category's largest: below 767 the page links are dropped rather than wrapped or scrolled, and the position line moves into the row to replace them. Numbers All pages is exempt. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 cluster centred on 1,296, page margin 72, boxes 38 on an 8 gap, chevrons with labels 24 from the numbers, position line 16 below, hairline above at the content width, 96 beneath. 1080 unchanged. 834 page margin 40, chevron labels drop to glyphs, padding 80. ≤ 767 page links dropped, chevrons become 44 px outlined icon buttons at the margins, the position line moves into the row at 15/600 and Position line Off is overridden ⚑, padding 64, page margin 20. All pages at ≤ 767 keeps its numerals, boxes grow 38 → 44, gap fixed at 4, wrapping to as many rows as it needs. 5 · Content fields. Almost nothing is authored. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”, 40 chars, never visible — it is the <nav>'s accessible name); newerLabel and olderLabel (text, optional, 24 chars, defaulting from the block's Labels value). Read-only from the route: pagination.page, .pages, .total, .limit, .next, .prev. In the union and unread here: moreLabel, endLabel — 6 Load More and 7 Endless spend those. 6 · Controls. Numbers Windowed · All pages. Chevrons Off · Glyph only · Glyph and label. Alignment Left · Centre. Separator None · Hairline above. Density Compact · Comfortable. Five, then the Pager block's four. Cut: Jump to page — a form inside a nav.
7 · Data. Ghost's pagination object on any paginated route — index, tag, author, and a paginated custom route. Destinations are /page/N/, with page 1 at the route root and never /page/1/ ⚑. Post range is derived: (page−1)·limit+1 to min(page·limit, total). 0 items → the route is empty and has no pagination; the section does not render. 1 page → does not render, whatever the item count: a pager over one page is a lie about there being more. Many → as drawn, and the window bounds the row at seven slots however long the archive is. The two-page case draws two numerals and no ellipsis. 8 · Empty state. One page → nothing renders on the site. In the editor the design draws its outline greyed with one line: “This archive has one page, so the pager will not appear on the site.” At the first page the newer slot is Hidden by default and keeps its width, so the numerals do not slide; at Dimmed it draws as a <span> at the muted-border step, never an <a> and never a disabled <button> — A18·15's rule against inert controls, kept by making the thing not a control. Last page mirrors it. No newerLabel or olderLabel → the block's Labels value supplies both; there is no state in which a link renders unlabelled. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical — every element is an <a> or a <span> and the page links are what Ghost's own /page/N/ routes serve. Edit-safe, with nothing to be safe about. load-more and infinite-scroll both exist in the registry and both belong to other designs in this category; adding either here would make this design one of them. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> holding an <ol>, one page per item, in page order. No heading — a pager is not a section of the document, so it takes no level and does not disturb the feed's h2/h3 ⚑. Current page is a <span aria-current="page">, not a link. Directional links carry rel="prev" and rel="next"; at Glyphs only each keeps visually-hidden text, so no link is named by an arrow. Ellipsis aria-hidden and not focusable. Targets 44 at every width, boxes 38 above 767. Focus is A6's ring at a 4 px offset on the box; no live region and no announcement — a page navigation announces itself. Muted numerals measure 5.4:1, the current page 13.1:1. Print: the links do not print and the position line does, all ten designs. Flagged ⚑ — tabular numerals as a pack requirement A34 depends on and cannot state; the ellipsis refused as a control; the ellipsis's derived dark value #4E4740; the numerals dropped below 767 with the position line as their destination; Position line Off overridden at that width; no /page/1/; no heading level; the whole category having no Posts block and no posts-per-page control.
