Lead and List

One related post at card size, and the rest as rows beside it — a 636 lead with its picture and excerpt, a 588 column of titles at 20, a 72 px gutter between them.

It is the design for a set that is not equal: the newest match gets the picture and the other two get a line each. Nothing marks the lead as special beyond its size — no badge, no label, no "Editor's pick", because the theme cannot know that and neither can the writer.
