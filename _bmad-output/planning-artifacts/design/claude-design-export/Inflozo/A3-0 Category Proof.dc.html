A3 FOOTERS · CATEGORY ARTEFACTS · ALL 16 DRAWN · READ FIRST

What the category owes, before any of the sixteen.

The four settlements A3 was asked to make, the category-wide rules the sixteen designs inherit, the tokenisation proof in three packs, the stress frame, the shared field list and the roster. Every rule here is stated once and repeated verbatim in each design's spec card and in A3 Footers — Spec.md.

1 · THE FOUR SETTLEMENTS · CONFIRMED BEFORE DRAWING

Where a value below is mine rather than agreed, it says so.

1 · Link columns

Auto-fit by the authored count. Two keep the 1fr width they would have had at four and sit against the brand block on a half measure — the right quarter stays empty and the editor does not warn about it. Three or four fill the measure as equal columns. Five or more move the brand block above the grid and wrap four across — 4 + 3 at seven, last cell empty, never justified. Seven is the cap: the editor disables Add column and says “Seven is the most a footer can hold. Consider a second column heading instead.” Eight links per column is the second cap.
At 1080 columns go two-across, except A3·8 Sitemap which goes three-across. At ≤ 767: two to four columns stack open as a two-across grid with links padded to 44 px; five to seven collapse to accordions, one per row, 48 px rows on hairlines, count at the right end, first group open.
Mine: the caps and their wording, the 1080 and five-column thresholds, the empty quarter, and the counts in the accordion rows.

2 · The newsletter form

One row only: a visually-hidden label, an email field, a button, and one 13 px reassurance line under the pair. No heading, no body copy, no image — A22 Newsletter owns the version that persuades; the footer catches someone who has already decided. The note is capped at 90 characters and the sidebar says where to go for more.
Four states, all at the same band height: empty · invalid (error replaces the note at 13 px / 500 in text, field border 1.5 px text, no red anywhere) · submitting (“Subscribing…”, field disabled, button width reserved) · done (confirmation and a way back, session-lived). Focus is a 1.5 px accent border with the library ring suppressed — A2·5 Capture's departure, repeated so the two forms behave identically. Members off in Ghost → the field is replaced by a button linking to the subscribe page.
Mine: the 90-character cap, the reserved button width, the session-lived done state.

3 · The social row

The row renders only what exists, and what exists is set in Ghost, not here — owner's ruling, 29 August 2026. Accounts are configured in Ghost Admin under Settings → General → Social accounts, and the footer iterates them with #social_accounts @site: nine platforms — x, facebook, linkedin, bluesky, threads, mastodon, tiktok, youtube, instagram — a platform with nothing set skipped by Ghost before the theme sees it, and Ghost's order the drawn order. The helper's else branch is the empty state: nothing connected and the row and its gap are removed. Never empty circles, never outlined icons pointing at a contact page, never a “follow us” heading over nothing. The authored extension list is deleted — with it the platform picker, Add, Remove, reorder and the six-entry cap, and the category's fourth invented field group. Ghost 5.x, which has no such helper, keeps Facebook and X through their own link helpers.
New this pass — the address comes from Ghost's own link helper, never from the stored handle. Ghost saves Facebook and X as usernames rather than as web addresses, so a handle written into an href is a broken link, and a handle shown as link text is an address that is not one. Ghost-sourced rows render through Ghost's helper for that platform; authored extension entries carry a full https:// address, validated on entry. Short labels (Fb, X, Li, In, Yt) are labels, never addresses. Nothing drawn changes: geometry, glyphs, accessible names and the empty state are exactly as above.
Glyph geometry, unchanged by the ruling: a 34 px box on the pack's radius, bare, hover filling with the pack's hover surface; 30 px where the row sits inside a legal line (A3·8, A3·15); a real 44 px box at ≤ 767, wrapping onto a second centred row past six rather than shrinking. The glyph is the theme's own partial for the helper's type, invoked in block form with a neutral fallback inside it, so a platform Ghost adds before the icon set catches up still renders. Accessible names are the helper's own platform names, never the glyphs; the list is a <ul> labelled “Orbit Weekly elsewhere”, each link carrying rel="me noopener" — the markup inside the helper's block is ours, which is where those attributes are added.
Mine: the box sizes, the wrap past six, and the placeholder letterforms in every frame — the real icon set is a build-time asset.

4 · The legal line and the attribution

One order everywhere: copyright · Privacy · Terms · Built with Inflozo, 13 px muted, middots in the border token, generated as CSS so nothing reads “dot” four times. The copyright's year is generated, never typed. Privacy and Terms are the two legal links the category carries; the field accepts up to six for a site that needs more.
“Built with Inflozo” is the credit, last in the line, linking to inflozo.com. On the Free plan the control is disabled-on with the reason shown in the sidebar; only Pro may hide it. The settlement's previous last item — “Published with Ghost” with a free Show/Hide toggle — is corrected by the reconciliation pass, one change across all sixteen designs. legalLinks[] gains the standard P0·3 repeater and its cap rises four → six — Privacy · Terms · Disclaimer · Imprint already makes four.
Mine: attribution last in the line, on by default, and the original four-link cap on the legal field — the six it now stands at is the reconciliation pass's, not mine.

2 · CATEGORY-WIDE RULES · TRUE OF ALL SIXTEEN, NOT REPEATED PER DESIGN
Placement and structure One <footer role="contentinfo"> per page, the last landmark, a sibling of <main>. One A3 per site, on every template. A3·16's pinned bar and its released footer are the same element, never two landmarks. Top edge. A footer whose ground matches the page's draws one hairline above it; a footer whose ground differs draws none — the ground change is the edge. A3·12's card draws neither: the gap is the separator. The footer may assume what precedes it. It is the only category in the library that can: something is always above it. Padding is Compact / Comfortable / Spacious everywhere, but the values differ by design — a one-line footer's Comfortable is 28 px a side, a column footer's is 56 px top. Each design's numbers are in its own spec.
Type, colour, depth Type floors. Links 15 px, 14 px only in A3·8 Sitemap. Column headings 13 px / 600 uppercase, tracked 0.08em, in text at full strength — never muted, they are what holds a grid. Meta and legal 13 px muted. One exception in the category: A3·10's 12 px field labels, each naming a 15 px value. Link states. Muted at rest; hover goes to text with a 35% underline, 160 ms, no movement; focus takes the library ring, 2 px accent at 2 px offset, tight to the text box. On a contrast ground hover and focus both switch to the pack's lightest surface — never accent, which measures 4.85:1 on Paper's contrast and fails in darker packs. Accent budget. Once per footer. The permitted uses are the logo mark, the newsletter button (A3·4), the current-tag chip (A3·13, via contrast) and the focus ring. A footer of twenty links has nothing to gain from a coloured one. Depth. Pack radius, hairlines, and the warm sm/md shadows. In dark, shadows are dropped and the hairline separates — A3·12 and A3·16.
Motion, data, empties Motion. 160 ms ease-out, one transition per state change. The category's whole motion budget: link and glyph hovers, the accordion's height at ≤ 767, A3·9's 1.02 image hover, A3·16's release. All suppressed under reduced-motion, thresholds unchanged. Nothing animates while editing. Ghost data. Site title and logo come from settings, and so do the social accounts — read through #social_accounts @site, which hands the theme a finished address per platform, so no handle is ever printed. Posts, tags and their counts come from content. Everything else is section content, including the contact fields and the credits — three invented groups where there were four; the extra social platforms are gone: Ghost has no field for the rest, so a site with several footers keeps them in step by hand. Empty-data floor. Every element that leaves takes its gap with it, and nothing is substituted for what is missing. No site title → no footer, and the editor says the site needs a name. Where a design's own subject is absent — no posts, no tags, no colophon, no columns — the design hides what does not apply and keeps its own arrangement (Part A·A8), and the sidebar states what is not drawn. Fields a design does not draw are kept, not cleared, and return on switching. Site-wide group. Attribution (disabled-on for Free — only Pro hides the credit), Back to top and Shows on sit below each design's own controls, beside the universal Background role, Vertical spacing and Top divider and the category's Legal layout; none count against the design's own list.

6 · THE ROSTER · SIXTEEN DESIGNS, WHAT EACH IS FOR, AND WHAT IT SETTLES · TWO MARKED [FREE]
01Minimal Line [Free] — one row: lockup, social, legal. The baseline — the least a footer can be. Settles the legal line, the attribution control and the social row's disappearance.
02Columns — brand block, link columns, legal row. Settles the column rule for the category: counts, caps, thresholds and both mobile collapses.
03Two-Tier — links on one ground, a legal bar on another. Settles the ground pairings and reuses A3·1 as its bar.
04Newsletter Band — one form row above the links. Settles the inline form and its four states, and the boundary with A22.
05Contrast Band — the whole footer inverted. Settles carried text, hairlines and focus on a contrast ground. Ground is not a control here.
06Centred Stack — one centred column on a fixed measure. The design with no collapse to specify: one order at every width.
07Big Type — the site's name as the last thing on the page. The category's one display moment; drops the mark by construction.
08Sitemap — five to seven columns, every route. The only Density control and the only 14 px links. A3·5 and A3·12 advise it past their caps; neither hands off (Part A·A8).
09Latest Posts — three recent posts beside one link column. The only design whose content changes on its own; settles the missing-image case.
10Contact Block — address, email, phone, hours. The only design with the contact fields, and the category's one 12 px type exception.
11Colophon — a paragraph about the publication with credits beside it. The only footer whose main content is reading text.
12Card — a panel inset from the page's edges. The only footer that does not touch the window; A3·2's content in a different container.
13Tags — Ghost's tags as chips. Establishes the chip for the library; the only design that marks where the reader is.
14Image Band — a photograph closing the page. The only footer with imagery as its subject; settles the scrim.
15Wrap — every link in one wrapping block, no headings. For a list with no hierarchy worth drawing.
16Mini Bar [Free] — pinned to the window, released at the page end into any other A3 design. The category's only scroll behaviour.
CAPS AND ADVICE · NO DESIGN BECOMES ANOTHER DESIGN (PART A · A8) · EVERY CAP STATES ITS REASON

Every hand-off on this card is withdrawn, and this pass clears the last of the wording. A placed design is the design that renders. Where a design's subject runs out it hides what does not apply and keeps its own arrangement: 2, 8 and 10 draw the brand block alone above the legal row; 3's empty tier leaves the bar as its own composition; 15 draws its row with fewer items; 7 shrinks a long name toward a 44 px floor and adds a line rather than cutting it ⛑; 13 below three tags drops the chip block and keeps the columns.
Caps still exist and each states its reason: seven columns, eight links a column, six social entries, four columns on 5 and 12, sixteen chips on 13, 24 links on 15. A panel may advise — 5 and 12 advise 8 past four columns, 8 advises 2 below five — and advice is a sentence in the sidebar, never a switch. 16's pinned bar and its released footer are two states of one section; footerBelow names which A3 design is placed beneath it, which is a placement, not a hand-off.

7 · THE PATCH PASSES · WHAT CHANGED ACROSS ALL SIXTEEN · FOOTER LINKS PASS, 29 AUGUST 2026, MARKED NEW
A8 · no design becomes another design Six designs used to resolve to A3·1 or A3·2 when their content ran out. None do now. Each hides what does not apply and keeps its own arrangement: 2, 8 and 10 draw the brand block alone above the legal row; 15 draws its row with fewer items; 3's empty tier leaves the bar as its own composition; 7 shrinks a long name toward a 44 px floor, one floor at every width, and adds a line rather than cutting it or becoming 6 ⚑. Caps stay, and a panel may advise — 5 and 12 advise 8 past four columns — but advice never switches a design. 16's pinned bar and its released footer are two states of one section, not two designs.
A10 · the no-JavaScript form notice
Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.
One letter each Thursday. Unsubscribe in a click.
A3·4 is the category's only design with a field, so it is the only one that draws this. Ghost's signup endpoint refuses a plain form submission, so the earlier claim that the form posts natively and works without JavaScript is withdrawn. The notice replaces the form at the band's own height, note kept. The four form states are untouched — they are Ghost's script doing its job.
The social row comes from Ghost Admin, through Ghost's own helper Owner's ruling, 29 August 2026 — nobody adds a social account in INFLOZO. Accounts are set in Ghost Admin (Settings → General → Social accounts) and the footer renders only those, through #social_accounts @site: the helper's href is a finished address, so no design in A3 can print a stored handle; its name is the accessible name; its type picks the icon partial in block form, with a neutral fallback inside so a new platform cannot error the page; its else branch is the empty state. Nine platforms, Ghost's order. Deleted with the ruling: the authored extension list, the platform picker, Add, Remove, reorder, the six-entry cap, and the fourth invented field group. The sidebar's rows are read-only, headed “Set in Ghost → Settings → Social accounts”. Nothing drawn changes — same 34 px box, same glyphs, same accessible names, same disappearance when nothing is set.
