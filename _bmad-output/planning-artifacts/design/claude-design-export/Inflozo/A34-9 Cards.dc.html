Cards

The two destinations as two cards, 636 each on A17's own gutter, each carrying a direction eyebrow and the page it goes to. 2 Prev and Next puts the same two links on the page as text; this makes them things you press.

It costs the most vertical space of any A34 design and buys the largest targets: two 636 × 112 surfaces are the easiest pager in the category to hit, on a phone or with a trackpad. It also spends no new geometry — the cell width, the gutter, the radius and the card's own hairline are A17's, and the eyebrow is A1's.

Two 636 px cells on a 24 px gutter is A17's Two division, unchanged, so the cards align with the grid above them whatever it is set to — three columns, four or six, the two cards always start and end where the feed's content does. That alignment is the design's whole argument and the reason it is worth spending 112 px of height on.
Each card mirrors the other rather than repeating it. The newer card reads glyph → text left to right, the older card text → glyph, and its text is right-aligned; the two arrows are therefore at the outer edges of the pair, as they are in 2 Prev and Next at Chevrons At the outer edge. It is the same composition at a different scale, which is what makes them two designs rather than one.
The eyebrow is A1's, at 13 px uppercase tracked .08em in muted — the design system's eyebrow, spent here on a direction rather than a section name ⚑, which is the only liberty this design takes with an inherited component. “Page 3” beneath it is the heading font at 20, matching 2 Prev and Next's label size exactly so a user switching between the two designs sees the same type at a different weight of container.

Single card Full width is the default and it breaks a rule the rest of A34 keeps. ⚑ Eight designs hold their geometry at the archive's ends — reserved slots, dimmed glyphs, nothing moving. This one re-lays itself out, because a 636 px card beside 636 px of nothing is the worst frame in the category and “reserve the space” is not an answer when the space is a visible object's worth of ground. Half width is offered for anyone who disagrees, and the panel states the trade in one line. The count line is deleted, and 2 Prev and Next's sublabel is not. The sublabel is a page number the route hands over; the count was limit, or the remainder on the last page — a multiplication, and a template cannot multiply. So Content loses its third value and the card loses its third line, which was always the line that claimed to know something about the next page. The finding stands: a real date range would need a lookahead query Ghost does not give a pager. Card style Hairline uses the page ground inside the card, so the card is a bordered region rather than a raised one; Filled uses surface. Neither takes a shadow at rest — a shadow here would make two pagers look like two posts.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A26 Post Footers reuses this card rather than drawing a second one. Adopted, not merely noted: a previous-post / next-post pair is this card with the destination's title where the page number sits — same 636 × 112 geometry, same eyebrow, same three Card styles, same whole-card <a>. Carried to the A26 patch as a requirement, with the two differences named there: the eyebrow reads a post relation rather than a page direction, and the title is Ghost-owned, so it is never inline-editable. A design control may depend on a block field, and this is the one place it does. ⚑ Single card only means anything when At the ends is Hidden; at Dimmed it is drawn greyed with the line “Applies when At the ends is Hidden”. The dependency runs design-control-depends-on-block-field and never the reverse — a design may read the block, never write it — which keeps the block's four fields meaning the same thing in all ten designs. This is the only A34 design whose layout changes with the reader's position, and the control exists so a publisher can refuse that. The rest of the category treats a moving layout as a defect; here the alternative is 636 px of empty ground next to a card, which is worse. Naming the exception is the point of having a control for it. Content Direction only is the value to use on a two-page feed and the panel says so. “Newer posts” and “Older posts” alone, at the eyebrow's size promoted to the heading font — the card's type collapses to one line rather than leaving the second empty, which is the only case where the card's internal composition changes rather than its content thinning. Four controls. Cut: a shadow option — two shadowed cards under a feed read as two more posts — and an image slot, which is where the next-post card of a post footer will start and is not a pager's business.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. Two mirrored surface cards at A17's 636 px cell width, 112 px tall, each carrying a 13 px direction eyebrow over the destination's page number at 20 px in the heading font, with the arrows at the pair's outer edges. The category's largest targets and its only contained items. 2 · Structural descriptor. split · none · page · few · none · two half-width cards
Containment is none and that is the slot most likely to be misread here — the cards are the items' geometry, not the section's, and the section itself sits on nothing. The category's only split; it shares ground, count and media with 2 Prev and Next and separates on archetype. 3 · Archetype. split. Two departures. Below 767 it stacks and abandons the mirror — both cards read left to right. And at an archive end, at Single card Full width, the surviving card takes the whole measure: the one A34 design whose layout depends on the reader's position. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 two 636 cards on a 24 gutter, 112 tall, inner padding 28, arrow 22 at the outer edges, eyebrow 13, page 20. 1080 two 456 cards, inner padding 24. 834 two 365 cards — A17's Two division at that width — inner padding 20, height and type unchanged, padding 80. ≤ 767 stacked, full measure, 88 tall, 12 px gap rather than 24, both arrows left, page 19, Single card has no effect, padding 64. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”); newerLabel and olderLabel (text, optional, 24 chars) — drawn as the eyebrow, which is the one place in A34 the two labels are set in uppercase, so a label longer than about 20 characters wraps and the panel says so. Read-only: the six pagination values; The count line that read .limit and .total is gone, so neither is read here any more. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. 6 · Controls. Card style Hairline · Filled · Filled with a hairline. Content Direction only · Direction and page — the third value, Direction, page and count, is deleted ⚑, its count needing a multiplication no template can perform. Single card Full width · Half width — greyed with its reason when At the ends is Dimmed ⚑. Height Compact 88 · Comfortable 112 · Spacious 144. Four, then the block's four fields and its two doors — Position line defaulting to Off. Cut: a shadow option, an image slot.
7 · Data. pagination.prev and .next for the two destinations, .page for their numbers, and nothing else — the optional count line is deleted in this pass. 0 posts → the feed draws its own empty state and neither card renders. 1 page → nothing renders. 2 pages → one card on every page of the archive, which is the design at its simplest and, at Content Direction only, its best. Many → unchanged; two cards on every interior page. The count line was arithmetic — limit, or total − (pages−1)·limit on the last page — and is deleted for that reason, a template being unable to multiply; it was never a lookahead: no titles, no images, no dates from the next page ⚑. 8 · Empty state. An empty feed → the feed draws its own designed “nothing here yet” state and the pager renders nothing at all ⚑ — neither card and no eyebrow — the category's largest targets have nothing to point at. A pager under a feed with nothing in it offers a route through nothing, which is the one-page rule arriving by the same reasoning. The copy belongs to the feed, not to A34, so the category gains no thirteenth catalog string; in the editor the pager's outline is greyed with its own cause — “This feed has no posts, so there is nothing to page through.” — a different sentence from the one-page notice because it has a different fix. One page → nothing renders. First and last page, At the ends Hidden: the absent card is removed and the survivor takes the full measure at Full width or holds its column at Half width — the one A34 design that re-lays out at an archive end, stated as the deliberate exception it is. At Dimmed both cards stay, the spent one at one step down in fill, border and text, its page number replaced by “No older posts” / “No newer posts”, and it is a <div>, not an <a>. Content Direction only → the card's composition collapses to one centred line rather than leaving the second row empty. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. Two anchors and their contents; the whole card is inside the <a>, as A17's post card is, so there is no nested-link problem and no JavaScript making a card clickable. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination">, no heading, two links, DOM order newer then older at every width. Each card's accessible name is its eyebrow plus its page — “Newer posts, page 1” — with the count, when drawn, inside the same name. rel="prev"/"next"; arrows aria-hidden. The target is the whole card, 636 × 112 or 350 × 88 — the largest in A34 by an order of magnitude. Focus is A6's ring on the card at a 4 px offset; hover spends two signals, the hover-surface fill and the page label's accent underline ⚑, which is one more than any other design in the category and is earned by the card being a surface rather than a line of text. Eyebrow 5.4:1, page label 13.1:1. Print: cards absent, and with Position line Off by default this design can print nothing at all — the panel's line for that is “Set Position line to Page count if this archive is printed.” Flagged ⚑ — A1's eyebrow spent on a direction; the survivor card taking the full measure, the category's only position-dependent layout; Single card depending on a block field; the mirror abandoned below 767 and the 12 px stacked gap; the count line deleted as arithmetic, with the lookahead it stood in for still named as a finding; two hover signals; uppercase labels wrapping past 20 characters; Card style Filled without a hairline named as the value to avoid in dark; the derived #2C2721 hover step, now depended on by three A34 designs; A26 Post Footers named as the place this card should be reused.
