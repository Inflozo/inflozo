The number of comments, set at 76 px in the heading font, standing in a 416 px column beside the block. The section’s one display moment is a figure the publication did not write and cannot fake — which is the argument for setting it that large.

NUMBER POSITION ABOVE · THE SPLIT IS ABANDONED BY CHOICE RATHER THAN BY WIDTH

Zero is drawn at full size. A section that shrinks its own headline when the number is small is a section that editorialises about its readers; 0 at 76 px is honest, and the reader who makes it 1 is looking at the reason to.

No control anywhere in A28 claims to style inside Ghost’s component ⚑ — mode and saturation pass through, and everything else in this panel is the chrome around the block. Type, spacing, avatars, buttons, threading, the editor and every focus ring inside the dashed outline are Ghost’s. A <noscript> line now sits under the head ⚑ — “Comments need JavaScript.”, a theme translation-catalog string (comments.noscript), not an authored field — so the chrome stops promising a thread that never loads. Drawn once, on the category proof frame.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: headingText (the label), rulesText, closedNotice. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the comment count, and every name, avatar and word inside the block, say “Edit in Ghost” or show P0·1’s lock pill. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the count’s three words are authored fields with the old literals as their defaults, and aria-label="Comments" plus the <noscript> line are translation-catalog strings. There is no Preview control and there never was one to remove.Member Visibility lands nowhere in this design ⚑ — nothing the theme draws here is a CTA. P0·4 and P0·2 never open. No count-up ⚑: the registry has it and A10 uses it, but animating the size of a discussion as the reader arrives is a claim about momentum the section cannot support.
