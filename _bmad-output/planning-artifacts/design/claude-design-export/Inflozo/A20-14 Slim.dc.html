DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Item counts are a number pickerThe Tags block’s Show is a number picker, 1–20, where it drew Four · Six · Eight · Twelve · All. Ghost returns at most 100 tags: “All” tripped Ghost’s own warning and silently returned 100 anyway, so the cap and the truncation are stated in the panel instead of promised away. This design’s own maximum is 20 — beyond twenty names the one line becomes four — drawn greyed with the reason visible.
The Remove button never greys outThe hand-picked list’s ✕ stays visible and clickable at one reference and explains why it cannot go lower — “a hand-picked list needs at least one topic”. Never dimmed, never hidden.
No design ever turns into another designThe lock reason is rewritten: it read “on a surface it is 15 Filter Bar, and on contrast it is 6”. The two rules are the design; the panel now names 15 Filter Bar as the design to place if a bar on a surface is wanted — advice, never a switch.
Member buttons · the no-JavaScript noticeNo subject. This design carries no subscribe form, no sign-in form and no paid-tier button — the all-topics link is navigation, not a call to action — so there is nothing to make conditional and nothing for a notice to replace.
NumberingUnchanged. This category is 1–15 and this design is still 14 Slim. No gap was created or closed, no number reused.
