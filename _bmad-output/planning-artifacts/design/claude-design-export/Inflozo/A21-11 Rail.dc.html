11 · Rail

A 240 px margin rail carrying the label, the writer count and one action; the names themselves in two ruled columns across the 1,008 beside it. No portraits anywhere.

It is the answer to a masthead with no photographs — a real case on most Ghost sites, where the profile picture is the field nobody fills in. Twelve names at 24 px fit in six ruled rows and the section is 480 px tall.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the rail line, the action label and the empty-state notice. actionUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the action takes an optional icon from P0·2 with its size and colour-role popover ⚑. Author names, bios, counts, locations, websites and handles are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no portrait control, because there are no portraits. That is the design: a masthead for a publication with no photographs on file. A user who wants faces has fourteen other designs, and the panel says so rather than adding a control that would make this design into 3 Rows.The rail does not become sticky ⚑ — the registry's only scroll module is header-scroll and it belongs to A1. A29·14 reached the same wall. If a sticky rail is wanted, that is a registry change, not a control.Cut: portraits, a bio, a rail-side value ⚑ (A25 fixed the rail at the left and nothing in A21 argues with it), a second action, and a per-column ordering.
