WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no "choose the lead" control and the source group says why. Ghost has no editorial ordering of authors, so a picker would either write a hard-coded author ID into the section — breaking when that person leaves — or re-implement the order control. The order decides, visibly.Every control here names its half: four for the lead, two for the list. That division is the design, and it is why a seventh control was not needed — the list has two properties and both are already exposed.Cut: a lead-side value ⚑ (the portrait is left at every width; a right-hand lead reads as 6 Founder), a bio in the list, a divider style, and a second link on the lead.
