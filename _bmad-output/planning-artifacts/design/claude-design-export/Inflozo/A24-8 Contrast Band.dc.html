Contrast Band

2 Flush Left's composition on a full-bleed band of the pack's contrast colour, 400 px tall, sitting flush under the nav. It is the loudest opening A24 offers and it opens hard on purpose: a reader arriving from a newsletter or a search result meets a block of colour with a title in it and knows immediately that the page is one piece of writing rather than a feed.

Its ground is what separates it from 2 Flush Left on the tuple — the same header on page and on contrast are two designs, which is exactly the case the brief calls legitimate. Everything it draws is derived from the band's own text colour, following A17·7, and it sets the Post block's Feature image to Off, because a photograph inside an inverted band is two grounds fighting inside one rectangle.
