WHAT THE DARK FRAME RECORDS Pale type on a dark ground reads heavier than dark type on a pale one at the same weight, and above about 72 px the difference stops being subtle: Georgia at 700 in dark closes its counters and the title looks a half-weight bolder than the light frame it is meant to match. The dark frame above is drawn at 600 for that reason ⚑, and it is the only place in A24 where light and dark do not carry the same value. This is a pack property and not a section one, which is why it is a finding rather than a control: the pack must supply a dark-mode weight step for its heading font above 72 px, alongside the tracking and leading the tokenisation proof already asks it for.
