WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Three geometry controls is the most any A24 design carries, and this design is the reason the ceiling is six. Picture height, Card width and Overlap are three numbers that have to agree, and none of them can be derived from the other two: the picture's height is editorial, the card's width is a measure, and the overlap is the relationship between them. Cut, to stay at six: a card-style control — 7 Card has Hairline · Filled · Filled with a hairline, and here the card is always filled with a hairline because it sits on a photograph and needs its edge.
