A2 ANNOUNCEMENT BARS · DESIGN 10 OF 15 · PAPER PACK

Pill

A detached bar with air around it, sitting clear of the top edge on the page's own ground. It reads as an object placed on the site rather than a band cut through it, which makes it the one design that can carry a promotional message without changing the character of the page underneath. Not a capsule: the corners are the pack's radius token, like everything else.

88 px of reserved space: a 48 px pill with 20 px of page ground above and below. The pill is surface with a hairline and the md shadow, and because it sits on background it needs both — the shadow alone is too soft in this pack to read as lifted, and the hairline alone reads as a card. The header keeps its own top hairline here, which is the boundary the pill floats above. Content is the A2·3 kit reused: accent pill label, sentence, arrow link, 32 px close.

…for fifteen years the answer to “where do you write?” was the name of a company. The personal page never disappeared; it went quiet, and quiet is not the same as gone.

Sticky is the one control on this design that changes what the pill is. In flow it reserves 88 px and scrolls away with the page. Sticky pins it below a sticky header with the same 20 px of air, so it floats over the article — and because it is 900 px of content behind a 48 px object with a shadow, it is the only A2 design where the shadow does real work rather than decorating. The reserved space is released when it pins, so nothing jumps: the spacer stays, the pill lifts out of it.
