Single

One quotation at Feature 27 on a 780 px measure, the attribution beneath it, and no card. This is the design A8-0's fourth settlement names when a site has typed one quote and picked a design that wants three — six of the fifteen hand off here at a count of one — and it is the design a site picks deliberately when it has one endorsement it trusts more than a wall of them.

It settles two things for the category. A quotation with nothing beside it does not need a plane: the page ground is the card. And the head is a problem here that it is nowhere else — a Large 40 title over a 27 px quotation makes the site's own words the largest thing in a section about somebody else's, so this design's title is capped at Medium 34 and defaults to no title at all.

The quotation is the only thing at full size and it sits on the page's own ground — no card, no border, no shadow, and no decorative quote glyph, which A8-0 refuses for all fifteen. The measure is 780 px, the same measure the head ladder uses for a title, so a quotation and a title occupy the same column when both are drawn. The avatar is 44 px here rather than the card's 36, A4·16's feature size, because at 27 px a 36 px circle reads as a thumbnail beside the type.
Separations: eyebrow to quote 20, quote to attribution 20 — the card's fixed gap, kept even without a card — attribution to note 32, note to link 10. The note and the link are drawn only where authored and neither has a control: a site that does not want the permission line deletes it. ⚑ The 780 measure, the 44 px avatar at this step and the 32 px foot gap are mine.

1 · <section aria-labelledby> pointing at the <h2> where a title is drawn. The eyebrow is not a heading and is not marked up as one; at head None there is no <h2> and the section takes no accessible name rather than inventing one. Inside: <figure> → <blockquote><p> + <figcaption>, so the attribution is programmatically the quotation's source. No <ul> here — one quotation is not a list — which is the only place in A8 the list is absent. 2 · The avatar is aria-hidden with no alt text, A8-0's rule: the name is text 12 px to its right. At Attribution Above the figcaption is still after the blockquote in the DOM and is placed above it in flow order, so the reading order is quote then source at both values and no design in A8 reorders the two. 3 · The link is the section's only focusable thing and its only accent. 2 px ring, 2 px out, the gap taking the ground — A6's ring verbatim, 3.3:1 against background in light and 6.3:1 in dark. It is permanently underlined and never accent-coloured text, which is why the underline is the accent and the label is text: accent on background is 3.3:1 and fails AA as text. On hover the underline thickens to 2 px; nothing moves. No other state exists on this design. The quotation is not hoverable, not focusable and not a link; there is no card to lift and no behaviour to run. Both frames a user can ever see are drawn above. Contrast · quote and name 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark on surface; role and note 5.6:1 / 6.4:1; the link's label carries the body colour at full strength in both. Forced colours: the striped placeholder becomes a bordered box, the underline survives as the system's link decoration.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 30 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
No design ever turns into another designNothing this design draws changed — it never had a hand-off out. What changed is how the category describes it: nine other panels now name it as advice at a count of one, where five of them used to hand their own frame over to it. A design placed on a page is the design that renders.
Item counts are a number pickerNo subject: this design draws exactly one quotation and has no count row. The maximum lives on the item list — twelve, the category ceiling — where Add is disabled with its number and its reason.
The Remove button never greys outRemove was never dimmed here and still is not, at any count. Removing the last row leaves the list empty, the section then does not render, and the editor keeps the empty repeater and its Add quote control rather than drawing a placeholder person.
Member buttons are conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-up needs JavaScriptThe section link may target Ghost's Portal, so two notes now sit on the Link Picker: where the target is a member action it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost's own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens and the reader stays where they are. Separate from Member visibility, which hides the whole section server-side. A8 draws no labelled button anywhere, so the section link is the only surface this rule touches here.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject, and nothing to withdraw: this design holds no subscribe and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for the notice to replace, and no sentence here ever promised a subscription without JavaScript. With JavaScript off this design is unchanged — every quotation, name, role and organisation is server-rendered.
Ghost’s templates cannot count, add, or rememberThe step-down and the character counters are editor values: the editor counts the authored string as it is typed and writes one value onto the item, and the stylesheet reads it. “N of M drawn” is written by the editor too — a template cannot count its own loop, and no arithmetic happens as the page is served.
Avatars with no photographWith no photograph the attribution closes up — no circle, no initials, no indent held for it — and Avatars Hidden draws the same thing. The two-initial form lands on 11 Faces only, the one design in A8 with a circle to fill; nothing here is pulled from Ghost, so the one-letter rule never applies in this category.
