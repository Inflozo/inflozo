WHAT 1 PLAYER SETTLES FOR THE CATEGORY
Nothing autoplays anywhere in A15 ⚑ — there is no Autoplay value on any of the fifteen designs, and no muted-loop value either. A film that starts itself takes the decision away from the reader, costs them data they did not agree to spend, and would have to be switched off under reduced-motion anyway.What plays on a phone ⚑ — settlement 1. The player loads in the frame, playsinline, at the same reserved box; A15 never asks for full screen and never leaves it to a native player of its own. If the reader’s browser takes the film full screen, that is the platform’s decision and the section does not fight it.The duration is typed by the author ⚑ — A4·10’s field, carried verbatim: videoDuration ≤ 8 characters. Nothing reads it from the provider, because reading it means calling the provider.
CAPTION, CREDIT AND TRANSCRIPT · SETTLEMENT 4
A transcript is a link, never a panel ⚑ — settlement 4. A15 does not host transcript text: transcriptUrl points at a Ghost page, a post or a file, and A25 Post Content Layouts owns the page it points at. The link sits in the meta row under the frame in every design that draws a meta row.Three different things, three different fields ⚑: description is the film’s own line under the frame; credit is the section’s, under the set, and belongs to the films rather than to the page; transcriptUrl is per film. No design draws a second section-level caption — the blurb is already that.Where a design has no room for a meta row — 6 Cover, 14 Slim Bar — the transcript link moves into the theatre and the panel says so ⚑, rather than being quietly dropped.

The poster is the author’s uploaded image, and if there is none it is the plate ⚑ — never the provider’s own thumbnail, because fetching that thumbnail is exactly the request the facade exists to prevent. The plate names the provider and the film, and the box is the same size either way. No third-party request is made until the reader acts ⚑ — settlement 3. There is no “load with the page” value anywhere in A15 ⚑: click-to-load is the category’s behaviour and not a control, because a control with a value that leaks the reader’s address on load is a control that will be set wrongly. The notice is drawn on the poster, not under it ⚑ — it is a thing the reader needs at the moment they act, and a permanent line under every frame in a grid of twelve is six lines of small print.
