Author Bio

The four footer blocks stacked on the article's own measure in the category's fixed order — tags, author, share, next and prev — each opening with a hairline, and the author block leading: a 56 px avatar, the name in the heading font, two lines of bio and a link to the rest of their work.

This is the category default and the one every other A26 design is a departure from. It draws every block the category has except the subscribe prompt, which one design owns and the rest refuse — settlement 3 on the proof frame.

The footer is on the measure, not the content width — 720 centred in A17's 1,296 box ⚑, so the first tag sits under the first character of the article and nothing in the footer is wider than the thing it closes.
Order is fixed and is not a control: tags, author, share, next and prev ⚑. A design may drop a block; no design may reorder one. The reason is that a footer read twice on two posts of the same site must be the same shape, and an order control makes that the user's problem.
One accent, and it is not here — this design has no subscribe button, so the accent appears only in A6's focus ring ⚑. The "More from" link is text at 600, not accent: a link at the end of every post is not an action.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESInline on canvas with P0·1 (bold · italic · underline · link; the link popover carries Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored): shareLabel, copiedLabel, authorLinkLabel. Ghost-owned and never inline-editable: post titles, tag names, author names, bios and social handles — clicking one selects the section and the sidebar says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. The author-archive link and every destination URL are Ghost’s own routes — the Link Picker opens on no field here, because this design authors no URL. The socials row’s glyphs come from P0·2’s Icon Picker with its size and colour-role popover, and the platform is fixed per handle. P0·3’s item controls land nowhere: A26 authors no array — the tags, the authors and the two neighbours are Ghost’s, and a Ghost-sourced list never shows an Add ⚑. P0·4 and Member Visibility land on 12 Subscribe alone in this category: a tag is navigation, a byline is attribution and a share link is not an offer. P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere — the footer reads the post’s own relations, not a query ⚑. No Preview control was removed because A26 never had one: previewing belongs to the editor, and P0·6’s state switcher is how the absent blocks, the copied confirmation and the member states are seen while editing. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: every authored label carries a default, and the strings nobody authors — the destination names, “and others”, the ledger’s long dates — are theme translation-catalog strings.
DATA · THE POST’S OWN RELATIONS, AND NOTHING QUERIEDtags[], authors[] (name, bio, profile_image, url, and the nine social handles plus website on Ghost ≥ 6.36), prev_post/next_post, post.url, post.title, and — at Updated line On — post.updated_at. {{#prev_post}}/{{#next_post}} are post-context only ⚑ — on a page the pair is simply absent, which is the category’s absent-block rule and not a new one. The share destinations are the site setting’s, and Share links draws the first N.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The four footer blocks stacked on the article's measure in the category's fixed order, each opening with a hairline, the author block carrying the weight — 56 px avatar, name in the heading font, two clamped lines of bio, a link to the author's archive. The category default, and the design the picker offers first.2 · Structural descriptor. stack · none · page · one · left · author block leading the footer
Count one: the author is the repeating unit the design lays out and there is normally one — the category's stated interpretation, inherited from A24. Media left is the avatar. Containment none: the blocks are bare on the page.3 · Archetype. stack. No departures. A stack narrows to a stack; the only reflow is the previous/next pair, which the archetype already gives at ≤ 767.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 measure 720 centred in the 1,296 box on the 72 margin, blocks at a 32 gap, hairline above each, avatar 56, bio 15/1.6 clamped at two lines, share row split across the measure, pair side by side at 340 each. 834 measure 754 on the 40 margin, pair at 357 each. ≤ 767 measure 350 on the 20 margin, avatar 48, pair stacked previous-above-next ⚑, share label above its buttons, every target 44.5 · Content fields. Read: tags[], primary_author and authors[] with name, profile_image, bio, url; prev_post and next_post with title and url; post.url, post.title. Authored: shareLabel (24 ch, "Share this piece"), copiedLabel (24 ch, "Link copied"), authorLinkLabel (24 ch, "More from {first name}") ⚑. Unread: subscribeHeading, subscribeLine, tagsLabel, archiveLabel.6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Blocks Author · Author and tags · Tags, author and share · Everything but subscribe · Everything. Width On the measure 720 · Content width 1,296. Avatar Compact 48 · Comfortable 56 · Spacious 72. Bio lines One · Two · Full. Rules None · Above each block · Full. Share links a number picker, 2 to 4, default 4 ⚑. Six. Cut: block order, block gap, a tag limit, a next/prev picture.
7 · Data. Tags: 0 → no row, no hairline, no gap; 1 → one pill; many → every tag Ghost returns, wrapping ⚑, because this design has no limit control. Authors: 1 → as drawn; 2 → two rows and a hairline ⚑; 3+ → overlapped avatars and a names line, no bios ⚑; 4+ → "Rosa Menendez and others". Prev/next: 2 → the pair; 1 → one destination at the full measure keeping its own label ⚑; 0 → the block is absent. Order is Ghost's and is not selectable ⚑.8 · Empty state. No tags, no bio, no next post → the block or the line is absent and the stack closes ⚑ — no placeholder, no reserved height, no "Untagged". With every block empty the section renders nothing at all; the editor draws one dashed row reading "This post has no tags, and its author has no bio yet." An empty shareLabel falls back rather than rendering an unlabelled row.9 · Behaviour module. share. No-JS, quoted: "Share links are real <a href="https://…"> URLs and work normally; only the copy-link button is hidden." Edit-safe. Everything else in the design is markup — the bio clamp is -webkit-line-clamp, the pair is a grid ⚑.10 · Accessibility. <footer> inside the post <article>, no heading of its own ⚑ — A24 owns the page's h1 and a footer block label is not a heading. Tags are a list of links at 44 px targets; the author name and the archive link are two links to the same URL and the second carries aria-hidden ⚑. The pair is <nav aria-label="Post navigation"> with rel="prev" and rel="next". Copy confirmation is A24·14's, announced aria-live="polite". Measured light: name 13.1:1, bio 5.4:1, kicker 5.4:1, hairline 1.3:1 decorative. Print: tags and the byline print; share and the pair do not ⚑.Repeating items. Three, all Ghost's: tags, authors and the two destinations. No Add, no Remove, no reorder, no per-item control anywhere — Share links picks a count from a site-wide list and never which destinations (A24·14's stated cost, inherited). Selecting the section gives three text fields.Reconciled. POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Bylines carry no number“Rosa Menendez and three others” is now “Rosa Menendez and others”. A template cannot subtract the drawn names from the author count, so the number could only ever have been hard-coded or wrong. The row, the avatars and the type are unchanged.
“More from” uses the full nameThe archive link reads “More from Rosa Menendez”, not “More from Rosa”. Ghost gives a theme one display name and no separate first name on the versions we support, so a first name was never available to interpolate. The default of authorLinkLabel changes with it.
Avatars with no photographThe avatar fallback draws one letter — “Rosa Menendez” shows R. Every person here comes from Ghost, which cannot produce two initials from a display name, so two initials were never available. The circle, its sizes and its colours are unchanged.
Item counts are a number pickerShare links is a stepper, 2 to 4, default 4 — new in this pass, where the row drew Two · Three · Four — drawing the first N of the site’s enabled destinations in the site’s own order. The + is greyed at four with the reason visible: a fifth link takes the row onto a second line.
The two free designs are the owner’s choice — ask himShortlisted the five plainest designs in the category — 1 Author Bio, 6 Slim, 2 Rows, 9 Share Row and 8 Tag Row — none of which needs the customer to have good photography. Recommended 1 Author Bio · 6 Slim, and the question is asked in plain words at the end of the written spec. Nothing is renumbered either way.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject in this design — it contains no subscribe and no sign-in form, so there is nothing for a designed notice to replace. The share links are real <a href> URLs and work unscripted; only the copy-link button is hidden.
Member buttons are conditionalNo subject at rest — this design draws no member action. At Blocks Everything the inherited subscribe bar carries the note, and the note is 12 Subscribe’s.
