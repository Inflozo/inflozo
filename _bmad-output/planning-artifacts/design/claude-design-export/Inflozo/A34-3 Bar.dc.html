A34 PAGINATION STYLES · DESIGN 3 OF 10 · PAPER PACK · FIVE CONTROLS · THE CATEGORY'S SURFACE GROUND, AND THE ONLY DESIGN WITH THREE ZONES

Bar

A full-width strip of surface between two hairlines, holding three things at once: where you are on the left, the page links in the middle, the next page on the right. It is the only design that can show all three without crowding, because the band gives it a horizontal to hang them on.

The band is not a card. No radius, no shadow, no inset by default — the same distinction A19·13 drew: a card would make the pager an object sitting on the page, and this is meant to read as the floor the feed ends on. 4 Pill is the design where the pager is an object, and it is a pill rather than a bar for exactly that reason.

96 · PADDING COMFORTABLE · OUTSIDE THE BAND

The band leaves the page margin and the contents do not. surface runs to the viewport's edges; the three zones sit on the same 72 px margin the feed above uses, so the numerals line up with the first and last columns of the grid. A18·7 and A17·7 established that a band's contents keep the page margin and this is that rule with a hairline instead of an inversion.
The outer zones are a fixed 260 px, not 1fr ⚑ — with flexible zones the numeral cluster drifts off centre as “Posts 13–24 of 132” becomes “Posts 121–132 of 132”. Fixed outer zones keep the middle at the true centre of the band on every page of the archive, and the cluster is the thing a reader aims at.
72 px of band on a 96 px padding is the proportion to hold. The strip has to read as thinner than the space beneath it or it becomes a footer; at Height Spacious 96 with Padding Compact 64 it does, which the panel flags as the one combination to avoid.

⚑ EDGE INSET TO THE CONTENT WIDTH · THE BAND STOPS AT 1,296 AND STILL TAKES NO RADIUS AND NO SHADOW · THE ZONES' OWN PADDING BECOMES 24, NOT 72
Edge Inset is the value that has to justify itself hardest. A 1,296 px surface strip with square corners between two hairlines is a legitimate thing on a page with margins either side, and it is one radius token away from being 4 Pill's container. It stays square deliberately ⚑: the pack's radius belongs to cards, buttons and images, and a pager that borrows it stops being furniture. Layout Direction at the ends loses the position line and A34 does not put it back. This is the design's own version of the honesty question: three zones can hold everything, two zones cannot, and the panel names what is missing rather than shrinking the numerals to fit a fourth thing in. Rules Top only is for a band that ends the page — with a footer directly beneath it, the lower hairline and the footer's own upper edge make a double line at 2 px, which is the one place A34 draws a heavier line than the token allows and the reason the value exists.

The band is the same height on every page of the archive, which is the point of a band and the reason this design does not blink between pages. Only the numerals inside it change, and tabular figures keep even those from moving. Two pages in a full-bleed band is 7% ink and 93% surface. It is not broken, it is just wasteful, and the panel says so with a named alternative rather than disabling anything.

Dimmed is this design's default ⚑, against Hidden everywhere else: a zone in a band cannot be reserved invisibly, it is just an empty third of a strip.
WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A block field's default can differ per design, and this is the first place it does. ⚑ At the ends is Hidden in eight designs and Dimmed in this one and in 8 Contrast Band, because both have a ground: an empty slot on the page ground is invisible, an empty slot in a band is a gap in the band. The rule: a design may set a block field's default and must say why in the panel; it may not remove the other value. Height and Padding are two different measurements and the panel keeps them apart. Height is the band; Padding is the page under it. This is the only A34 design with both, and the reason 8 Contrast Band has both too — a design with a ground needs to size the ground and the space around it separately. Numbers All pages behaves differently inside a band than on the page. On the page ground 1 Numbers wraps to a second row; inside a fixed-height band there is no second row, so the middle zone scrolls horizontally with overflow-x: auto and no scrollbar chrome ⚑. That is a real difference in behaviour between two designs sharing a control name, and it is stated in both spec entries. Five controls; two were cut. An Alignment control — the zones are the alignment. A “Show the total posts” toggle — that is the block's Position line with a different name.

Three zones do not survive 754 px and the band grows rather than shrinking its contents. Below 767 this is 1 Numbers' mobile row inside a band, and A34 accepts the convergence rather than inventing a difference: at 350 px there is one good arrangement for a pager and both designs use it. What still separates them at this width is the ground — the band, its two hairlines, and its full-bleed edges. All pages inside a band scrolls instead of wrapping, at every width ⚑ — the band's height is fixed, so there is nowhere for a second row to go. The eighth numeral is cut by the band's edge, which is the affordance: a clipped item is the only honest signal that a strip scrolls. It is a native overflow-x scroll with no JavaScript, the same mechanism marquee's no-JS branch falls back to, and this design declares no module.

The hairlines stay, and in dark they are the thing doing the work. #211D17 on #171511 is one step, and A19·13 found one step sufficient for a 600 px band; a 72 px band is the opposite case — a strip that thin needs its edges declared, and without the hairlines it reads as a rendering artefact rather than a surface. Dropping them was tried and is why Rules has no “None” value ⚑.
Nothing else is re-tuned. The numerals, the current-page underline, the ellipsis's derived #4E4740 and the direction link are 1 Numbers' values unchanged, because they sit on surface here and on background there — one step apart, and one step does not change a contrast decision. Muted on surface measures 5.1:1 against 5.4:1 on the ground.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A full-bleed surface strip between two hairlines, 72 px tall, holding the position line, the windowed page links and the older-posts link in three zones on the feed's own page margin. The only A34 design that shows all three at once. 2 · Structural descriptor. bar · none · surface · variable · none · full-width strip
Containment is none and the ground is surface: the band is the section's ground, not a box the section sits in. 4 Pill is the same contents in a pill on the page; 8 Contrast Band is the same strip on contrast; 10 Slim is a bar with no ground at all. 3 · Archetype. bar. Two departures. A bar's ladder sheds its outer zones as it narrows; this one grows instead — 72 → 88 at 834 to stack the position line under the numerals. And All pages scrolls the middle zone rather than wrapping, at every width. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 full-bleed band 72 tall, contents on the 72 px page margin, outer zones fixed 260, numerals centred on the band, 96 beneath. 1080 unchanged, zones 220. 834 band 88, two zones — numerals with the position line beneath them on the left, direction link right; page margin 40; padding 80. ≤ 767 band 64, page links dropped, 44 px outlined chevrons at the margins with the page count between them, Post range resolves to Page count ⚑, band stays full bleed, padding 64. Edge Inset at ≤ 767 becomes full bleed too — a 350 px band with margins either side is a card without a radius. 5 · Content fields. navLabel, newerLabel, olderLabel — all optional text as elsewhere. Read-only: the six pagination values. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. This design authors nothing the others do not — the band is a control's value, not content. 6 · Controls. Layout Three zones · Numbers centred · Direction at the ends. Height Compact 56 · Comfortable 72 · Spacious 96. Edge Full bleed · Inset. Numbers Windowed · All pages. Rules Both · Top only. Five, then the block's four fields and its two doors, with At the ends defaulting to Dimmed here ⚑. Cut: Alignment, and a totals toggle that duplicated the block.
7 · Data. The route's pagination object; destinations /page/N/. 0 → no section. 1 page → does not render, and the band goes with it, as A19·13's band does at zero. 2 pages → the design's weakest case — two numerals and a link in a 1,296 px strip; the panel names 10 Slim. Many → as drawn; the band's height never changes with the archive and the outer zones are sized for the longest position line the archive can produce, “Posts 121–132 of 132”. 8 · Empty state. One page → nothing renders; greyed outline and cause in the editor. At the first and last page the absent direction link dims rather than hiding — this design's default, because an empty zone in a band is visible whether or not anything is in it. At Layout Three zones on page 1 there is nothing to dim, since the left zone holds the position line and the newer link only exists at Direction at the ends; that asymmetry is the design's, stated rather than smoothed ⚑. Position line Off leaves the left zone empty and the numerals stay centred on the band, not on the remaining space. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical, including the scrolling middle zone, which is native overflow-x. Edit-safe. marquee was considered for All pages and refused: a pager that moves on its own is unusable, and the module's own no-JS branch is exactly the static scrollable strip this design already has. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> with the page links in an <ol>; no heading. DOM order is position line → page links → direction link, matching the visual order left to right at 1440 and top to bottom at 834, so the reading order never contradicts the layout ⚑. aria-current="page", rel="prev"/"next", ellipsis aria-hidden. Focus rings on the first and last numerals in a scrolling zone are inset to −4 px, A17·18's rule, because a ring at +4 is clipped by the band's overflow. Targets 44; the band's 56 px Compact height still holds a 44 px target with 6 px either side. Muted on surface 5.1:1, current page 12.6:1. Print: the band prints as a hairline rule with the position line, links absent. Flagged ⚑ — fixed 260 px outer zones rather than 1fr; Edge Inset staying square against the pack's radius token; the band growing to 88 at 834 instead of shedding a zone; All pages scrolling rather than wrapping; Post range resolving to Page count below 767; At the ends defaulting to Dimmed, and the rule that a design may set a block default and must say why; the missing newer link at Three zones on page 1; Rules having no None value.
