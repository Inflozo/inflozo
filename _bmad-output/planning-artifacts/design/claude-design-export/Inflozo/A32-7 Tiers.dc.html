WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE THREE STATES EVERY A32 DESIGN OWES
In the editor the gate draws at its resting state ⚑ — the paid gate, the fade at its control value, no hover and no ring. Nothing in A32 has a behaviour to preview, so the edit-time frame and the published frame are the same picture. With JavaScript off the section is identical ⚑ — pixel for pixel in ten of the twelve. The gate is server-rendered HTML, the fade is a gradient, the suppression rules are :last-child selectors. Only 11 Sticky Bar’s close button and 12 Meter’s label depend on a module, and both have a quoted degradation. A reader who has access never sees this section ⚑ — Ghost sends the whole post and A32 does not render. There is no “unlocked” state to design, and no design may draw one.

The card is not the target — its button is ⚑. Hovering a card raises nothing and changes nothing; the whole card is not a link, because two links to two places inside one rectangle is a card nobody can operate with a keyboard cleanly.
