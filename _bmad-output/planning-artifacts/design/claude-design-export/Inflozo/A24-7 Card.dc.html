WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Two spacing controls in one panel is the maximum the library should allow, and this is the design that earns it. They govern different things — the card's inside and the page's outside — and both are things a user will want to change for real reasons: a dense documentation page wants Card padding Compact and Padding Compact; a quarterly essay wants both Spacious. They are named with the same three words so the relationship is obvious. The plate is used here and nowhere else in A24. A19 established three answers to a missing picture and this is the only design in this category where the picture's box has to stay: a card with one column is a different object, and reflowing to it would make the design change shape on a post-by-post basis. A19's amendment applies — the plate takes the picture's box exactly and is not capped — because the two columns must end level. Cut: a radius control. The card's radius is the pack's token; a user who can set it can break the one thing that makes twelve packs look like twelve designs rather than one design in twelve colours. Cut: a shadow strength control — the two warm shadows are the system's, and Card style already offers the only choice that matters.

POST HEADERS PATCH · 28 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
No design ever turns into another designNothing to delete: this design answers a missing picture with its own Plate, unchanged by this pass, and it is no longer 9 Overlap’s hand-off target — 9 keeps its card and hides its picture, and may name this design as advice.
Bylines carry no number“Jane and 2 others” is now “Jane and others”, here and everywhere in the category. No number appears in a byline anywhere — a template cannot subtract one from the author count, so the number could only ever have been wrong or hard-coded.
Avatars with no photographThe avatar’s fallback draws one letter — “Marguerite Okonjo” shows M. Every person in A24 comes from Ghost, which gives a theme a display name and no separate first and last name, so two initials were never available. The circle, its sizes and its colours are unchanged.
The no-JavaScript noticeRestated on this frame: pixel-identical with JavaScript off. No form, no subscribe field and no member button exists in this design, so there is nothing for the no-JavaScript notice to replace and no promise to withdraw.
