THE SHEET’S THREE DECISIONS
Numbers, not captions ⚑. The number is the handle a reader uses to say which frame they mean, which is what a contact sheet is for; the caption is one keystroke away in the overlay.Square, because a sheet is a matrix ⚑ — mixed ratios at 209 px is visual noise at the density this design runs at. Crop offers Square and Landscape and no more ⚑.It is the design that makes the 48-image ceiling visible ⚑ — 48 frames at six columns is eight rows and 1,736 px of section, which is where A14 stops. The ceiling is stated in the images block and repeated here.

Below about eight frames this design stops being itself ⚑ — the panel says so and names 1 Grid, and nothing is disabled, because a nine-frame sheet is a legitimate thing to want. At one image the band still runs the full width ⚑ with a single 209 px square in it, which is the honest result of a full-width ground and a fixed column count.
