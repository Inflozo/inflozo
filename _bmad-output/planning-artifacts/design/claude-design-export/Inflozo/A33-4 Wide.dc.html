WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE FOUR STATES EVERY A33 DESIGN OWES
In the editor every card draws at its resting state ⚑ — the toggle closed, no hover, no ring. The treatment is CSS, so the edit-time picture and the published picture are the same. With JavaScript off fourteen of the twenty cards are identical ⚑. Six depend on scripts that are Ghost’s or the provider’s, not A33’s ⚑: the toggle’s open and close, the gallery’s row ratios, the audio and video players, the signup card’s post, and the embed’s provider markup. The registry has no module for any of them — a finding, not a design, and A33 declares core rather than inventing one. The treatment never restyles what is inside an iframe ⚑. The embed card’s frame, radius and caption are A33’s; the provider’s markup is the provider’s. No control in A33 styles one card of one post ⚑ — a value is written once, site-wide, and every card of that type reads it.

Nothing about hover or focus changes with width ⚑. The bookmark, the button and the toggle are the same components at the same sizes as 1 Plain; the only state this treatment adds is the caption’s alignment, which is not a state at all.

ACCESSIBILITY · THE MARKUP IS GHOST’S AND THE TREATMENT MUST NOT BREAK IT
THE MARKUP A33 STYLES · GHOST’S, ANNOTATED
IMAGE, GALLERY, EMBED<figure> + <figcaption> — the caption is inside the figure, so it is read with the image, not after it
BOOKMARKone <a> around the whole card · one focus stop · the thumbnail is aria-hidden and the title is the accessible name
CALLOUTa <div> with no role · the emoji carries aria-hidden ⚑ so it is not announced as “pushpin”
TOGGLEa plain <div> holding an <h4> and a <button> — not native disclosure markup ⚑. The heading is a real h4 and a stylesheet cannot change its level; the button is the only target and the only focus stop, and no theme can add the aria-expanded it lacks. Recorded as a Ghost limitation
BUTTONan <a> styled as a button · 48 px tall, 44 px minimum target at every width
PRODUCTGhost renders the rating as stars with no text equivalent ⚑ and a theme cannot add the words · recorded as a Ghost limitation, not something this category fixes
FILEthe link names the file and its size: “Download catalogue-2026-08.csv, 1.4 MB” ⚑
HEADERthe heading is an h2 ⚑ — Ghost emits h2/h3 and A33 never promotes one to h1; the post title is the page’s only h1
THIS DESIGN’S OWN ACCESSIBILITY NOTES
Width is not an accessibility problem; caption width is ⚑. At To the media the caption line is 1,040 wide, which is 130 characters at 13.5 px — past the 80-character guidance and stated here as a known trade, mitigated by the caption being at most two lines.To the measure is the accessible default for caption-heavy publications, and the panel says so in words rather than hiding the choice ⚑.The figure/caption pair is unchanged: a wider figure is still one figure, and reading order is still image → caption.At 400 % zoom the step-up disappears — the viewport is under 1024, there is no wide column, and every media card is the content box.

The step-up is a width, and widths are not announced ⚑. The one real cost is a 1,040 caption line, which is named in the panel and in field 10 rather than discovered.

ImagesImages and video
Two values, and the reason is Ghost’s ⚑: a gallery and an embed keep the width Ghost fixes for them and a theme may not override it, so neither can step up. Images and video is the default — it is everything that can move. Never the bookmark’s thumbnail, which is furniture rather than media, and never the product card’s photograph ⚑, which belongs to a copy card and moves only with it.
Caption alignment
To the measureTo the media
To the media is this treatment’s default and the reason it is a separate design ⚑ — the caption belongs to the picture here, not to the column.
Where these controls live This panel is the Editor cards screen (P0·7 · S14): the Treatment picker and its controls at the top, each card’s own 4–7-control panel below it, with treatment-owned values read-only in those panels. The section sidebar’s Cards module reduces to the Treatment picker and this link ⚚ — one surface, one vocabulary, and never twelve controls for one callout. Read-only help line on this panel: “Sections may resolve card widths differently inside their own ground.” The section wins inside its own ground, the treatment wins everywhere else ⚚. Inline editing does not apply anywhere in A33 — card content is authored in Ghost’s editor, so clicking a card’s text on the canvas says “Edit in Ghost”. The shared floating toolbar never appears on a card. Ghost’s signup and call-to-action cards are conditional — neither renders when the connected site cannot accept sign-ups (self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected), and with JavaScript off the signup form does nothing ⚑ — Ghost’s form carries no action; data-members-form tells Ghost’s own script to handle the submit, and the loading, success and error classes are written by that script. The CSS in Ghost’s theme docs styles those states; it does not create them. A33 cannot replace the form with a notice ⚑ — inside a post body the theme owns the stylesheet and not the markup — so the notice belongs to Ghost’s own card. Open Editor cards →

Which cards appear
Whatever the author inserted🔒
Read-only. The author writes the post ⚑ — a treatment styles every card Ghost renders and cannot add, remove or reorder one. A33 has no item list.
Card widths
The author’s, per card🔒
Read-only. Regular, wide and full are authored in Ghost, one card at a time ⚑ — the treatment decides what each of the three resolves to, which is the whole of designs 4 and 5.
Bookmark metadata
Scraped by Ghost🔒
Read-only. Title, description, icon, author, publisher and thumbnail are all scraped from the URL ⚑ — C.1’s matrix. A treatment chooses which rows show, never what they say.
Gallery rows
Ghost’s own script🔒
Read-only. Ghost’s card script computes each row’s ratios ⚑ and no registry module covers it. With JavaScript off the images render at equal widths — a finding for the architect, not a degradation A33 can quote.
Corners
From the pack’s radius token🔒
Read-only in every treatment ⚑. C.1’s matrix lists corners as a per-card setting; A33 answers that the pack’s radius token is that value and offers no other — a card that mixed radii would fail §3·1 in eleven of twelve packs.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. An image, a GIF and a video render one rung wider than the width their author gave them — regular at the wide column’s 1,040, wide and full at the content box’s 1,296 — with the text at the measure and the caption aligned to the media. A gallery and an embed keep the width Ghost fixes for them ⚑.2 · Structural descriptor. media frame · none · page · variable · edge · media one rung wider
Archetype media frame rather than article body because the media, not the column, sets this design’s geometry ⚑. Media placement edge: the card reaches the content box’s edge but never the viewport’s — that is 5 Full Bleed, and it is the only slot between them.3 · Archetype. media frame. Its ladder: the frame narrows to the container and the caption follows it. One departure — below 1024 the step-up has nowhere to go and every media card is the content box ⚑.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 text 720; media regular → 1,040, wide → 1,296, full → 1,296; caption at the media’s width; space 56. 834 text 754; every media card 754 ⚑; caption 754. ≤ 767 everything 350, space 32, caption alignment has no meaning.5 · Content fields. The whole category union, every time ⚑ — a treatment cannot decline a card the author inserted. Authored: image.src (file, required), image.alt (text ≤ 125), image.caption (rich text, optional), image.width (regular · wide · full), gallery.images[] (1–9 files, ordered), gallery.caption, bookmark.url, callout.text (required) + callout.emoji + callout.colour (nine values), toggle.heading + toggle.content (both required), button.label (≤ 40) + button.url + button.align, embed.url, product.title + product.description + product.image + product.rating (1–5) + product.buttonLabel/buttonUrl, file.file + file.title + file.description, header.heading + header.subheading + header.buttonLabel/buttonUrl + header.size + header.style + header.backgroundImage, markdown.md (rich text, no wrapper class), html.html + html.visibility (public · free · paid), email.greeting + email.fallback + email.text (newsletter only), cta.text + cta.image + cta.sponsorLabel + cta.buttonLabel/buttonUrl + cta.background + cta.visibility + cta.showOn (web · newsletter · both), audio.file + audio.title + audio.thumbnail, video.file + video.poster + video.loop + video.width, signup.heading + signup.subheading + signup.disclaimer + signup.buttonText + signup.layout + signup.background + signup.label. Space around cards Compact 32 · Comfortable 56 · Spacious 72. Captions Under, left · Under, centred · Hidden. Credit line With the caption · Its own line · Hidden. 7 · Data. Everything comes from {​{content}​} as Ghost renders it — A33 adds no markup of its own ⚑.
