The domains are the tell. Fifteen years on the same address, renewed every October by someone who never expected an audience and got one anyway. I have been keeping a list since 2019, and it has never once grown.

What they share is not taste. Some are beautiful and most are not. It is a refusal to move — off the address, off the software, off the habit — which turns out to be the rarest thing on the web.

💡If you are looking for the list itself, it is at the end of this issue — and every link on it was checked the week this went out.

Who is actually paying

Three of the four hundred are paid for by estates. Two are paid for by universities that forgot they were paying. The rest are individuals, and their renewal dates cluster in October for a reason nobody has explained to my satisfaction.

A site that outlives three platform migrations is not nostalgic. It is just paid for.Kicks Condor, 2019

I asked eleven of them why October. The answers divided cleanly:

·Four said the date meant nothing at all, and were mildly annoyed to be asked.
·Three said it was simply when they had money, which is the honest version of the same answer.
·Two said October was when their first host used to bill them. That host closed in 2013.

The renewal arithmetic

A domain costs about twelve dollars a year. Hosting a static site costs nothing at three of the four common hosts. The whole obligation, then, is one dollar a month and one afternoon a decade.

A design that adds nothing needs no controls for what it added, so the room goes to how the words sit.There is no Table of contents control and no Share rail control, and neither is greyed out — they are absent. ⚑ A24's convention is that a value which cannot work is disabled with its reason shown; this is the other case, where the control does not belong to the design at all. A user who wants either switches to 1 Measured with their content intact, and the Design line says so.Cut: a justified value. THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The article on a centred measure and nothing else — no index, no rail, no ground of its own, no box — with a paragraph rhythm control the rest of the category does not have. The category's floor, drawn as a design. 2 · Structural descriptor. article body · none · page · none · inline · the measure alone
Count none: there is no repeating unit, which is exactly what separates it from 1 Measured's variable — the index is the only thing 1 repeats, and this design has none. Same archetype, containment, ground and media as 1; the count slot is the whole of the difference. 3 · Archetype. article body. No departure, and nothing to collapse. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 margin 72, measure 720 centred in 1,296, body 19/1.7, h2 32, h3 24. 834 margin 40, measure 754, body 18, h2 28. ≤ 767 margin 20, measure 350, body 17, h2 25, images break the margin to the screen edges and captions return to the measure. Drop cap and Paragraph rhythm both hold at every width ⚑. 5 · Content fields. Read: {{content}} whole, post.access for the paywall cut. Authored: none ⚑ — the only design in A25 with no authored field, because its optional labels all belong to furniture it does not draw. Unread in the union: indexLabel, shareLabel, indexNote. 6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Measure Narrow 620 · Comfortable 720 · Wide 840. Type scale Compact 17 · Comfortable 19 · Spacious 21. Drop cap Off · Drop — three lines · Raised. Paragraph rhythm Spaced · Indented. Paragraph rhythm Spaced · Indented. Anchor links Off · On hover · Always. Five — still the shortest panel in A25, and four of the five are now the shared quartet ⚑. Cut: a justified value, a small-caps opening, a paragraph spacing ladder.
7 · Data. {{content}} as Ghost renders it, and nothing else. Headings: any number, including none — this is the design that is indifferent to them ⚑, and the only one in the category that is. Images, galleries and embeds take the width the Cards module gives them. At the paywall cut the article simply stops and A32's section follows; no element of this design spans the cut, which makes it the safest of the twelve on a members post ⚑. 8 · Empty state. An empty {{content}} renders nothing at all — no padding, no rule, no measure. In the editor, one greyed line: "This post has no body yet. Write it in Ghost." There is no second empty state, because there is nothing else that can be absent. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. The only design in A25 whose no-JS page and whose scripted page are the same document ⚑ — every other one derives an index, copies a link or opens an image. 10 · Accessibility. <article> with no h1 — A24's header owns the page's only one — and Ghost's h2/h3 keep their levels. scroll-margin-top: 96px on every heading so a sticky header never covers a jumped-to target ⚑.
