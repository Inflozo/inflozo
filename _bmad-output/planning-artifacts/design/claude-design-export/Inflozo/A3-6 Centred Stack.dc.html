Centred Stack

One centred column on a fixed measure: lockup, tagline, a single row of links, the social row, then the legal line. No columns and no grid — the links are one wrapping row, which is the arrangement that suits a site with eight links and no hierarchy between them. It is the design that collapses least, because it is already stacked.

Everything sits on a 640 px measure centred in the page, and the hairline above the legal line is that measure wide rather than full-bleed — a full-width rule under a centred stack cuts the composition in half. The lockup is one step up from A3·1's, 30 px mark and 22 px wordmark, because at the top of a centred stack it is the only anchor. Links are a wrapping row with 28 px gaps and no separators: separators in a centred row read as an equation.

With more than eight links the row splits by content, not by wrapping: the first authored group becomes a primary row at 15 px / 500 in text, everything after it a secondary row at 14 px muted.
