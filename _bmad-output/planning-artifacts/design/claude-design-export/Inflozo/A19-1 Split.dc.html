Split

One featured post: eyebrow, title, excerpt, meta and a call to action on a 560 px measure at the left, its picture at the right on 636. This is what a site gets when it adds a Featured section, and the other fourteen designs are departures from it.

The proportion is the design. The picture takes 49% of the content width and the text 43%, with 100 px of air between them — enough that the two halves read as one composition rather than as a card cut in two. A 34 px title on 560 px runs to about 42 characters a line, which is why A19's spotlight measure is 560 and not A18's 820.

It is also the design the rest of the category hands off to. 2 Full Bleed, 4 Poster and 7 Overlap name it when their post has no picture; 8 Lead and Two becomes it at one post; 5 Contrast Band prints as it. So its missing-image behaviour is the category's, and it is drawn here rather than described — A19-0 settlement 3 calls it Reflow.

A17's tag plate exists to keep a grid's cells the same size, and there are no other cells here to match — a 636 × 424 striped rectangle beside a title would be the largest thing in the section and would be carrying no information. So the image column is removed, the text goes to 720, and the composition becomes 6 Big Type's at this design's title size. Nothing is centred, nothing is enlarged to compensate. 720 is the reflow measure and it is a category rule, used by the six other Reflow designs: wide enough that the section does not look like a mistake, narrow enough that a 34 px title still holds its line. The section is 268 px tall reflowed against 424 with a picture, and a page with two Reflow sections in a row will look thinner than the editor expects — the panel says so. WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES FOR THE WHOLE CATEGORY The Count stepper is drawn disabled rather than hidden, and this is the pattern for the other eight one-post designs. A user who cannot find the count assumes the section is broken; a user who sees it greyed with “this design draws one post” knows the answer is the Design picker. The count line still reports the truth beneath it — “5 posts are featured. 1 drawn.” — because knowing four were passed over is the information that sends someone to 11 Lead and Rail. ⚑ “When nothing matches” is the last of the Data group's six fields, and it is A19's addition to a group A17 shipped with four. Hide the section · Show the latest post, defaulting to Hide. It belongs to the query rather than to the design, so it is the same in all fifteen and counts toward none of their totals. Hide is the default because a section captioned Featured over an unchosen post is worse than no section; Show the latest post exists because a home page with a hole in it is a real problem and a site that accepts the trade should be able to say so. It is offered by three designs only — this one, 4 Poster and 13 Quote — because a face is a second identifying object and a row or a follower cannot carry one without competing with the picture it sits beside. This is the first time profile_image has been drawn since A17. Cut: an alignment control. A centred split is two centred columns and reads as a slide. Cut: a “featured badge” toggle — the eyebrow is the badge, and a coloured chip beside a 34 px title is a second focal point. Cut: a background control. Ground is a structural claim in A19 and the design that answers it is 5 Contrast Band, one click away with the query preserved.
