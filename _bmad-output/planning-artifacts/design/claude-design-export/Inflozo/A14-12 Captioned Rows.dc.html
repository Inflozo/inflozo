WHY THIS IS TWO TO FOUR AND NOT TWELVE
Each row is 636 px tall ⚑. Four rows plus the head and the gaps is 3,000 px of page, which is the honest ceiling for a section that is not the whole page. Above four the panel names 1 Grid and 2 Masonry rather than disabling anything ⚑ — a five-row set is legible, it is simply long.Sides alternate by default ⚑ and the control offers Left and Right as fixed alternatives. Alternating is the default because three identical rows read as a table and three alternating rows read as a sequence.The hairline between rows is a control value, not structure ⚑ — at Rules: Off the rows are held apart by 56 px of space alone.

One image is a legitimate section here ⚑, unlike in the grids — a single photograph with a paragraph beside it is exactly what this design is for. A caption left empty keeps its column ⚑ and draws the number alone, so the row does not become full-width — the alternative would make one row a different design from its neighbours.
