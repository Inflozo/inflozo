Fifteen ways a post can be a row

A18's content is posts and the user does not author them. There is no repeater, no Add, no Remove and no drag handle in the category — the section is handed a query, Ghost answers it, and the fifteen designs draw whatever comes back. The post card, the tag plate, the Posts block, the focus ring and the section head are A17's and are used here unchanged.

What A18 adds is the row. A grid divides a page; a list has to decide what one post does with a whole page width — and every settlement below follows from that one question.

Controls-reconciliation patch — 25 August 2026. The fifteen designs were audited against the PRD's control vocabulary and Ghost's verified data surface. The shared editor primitives are P0’s and are reused by name, never redrawn — the P0·1 inline text toolbar and its link popover, the P0·2 icon slot and Icon Picker, the P0·3 item controls, the P0·4 member-aware action editor, the P0·5 “Populate from…” panel and the P0·6 state switcher. What changed here: the universal trio now sits outside every design's control list, the Posts block is the Data group, Count is a stepper, Source gains Hand-picked, eleven designs gain Tag Show · Hide, three gain Thumbnail shape, and the amendments below are marked ⚑ in place.

1 · Row density and separators, and the smallest row that reads. Row density is a second control beside the universal Vertical spacing — ⚑ amended, because a list has two spacings: the space around it and the space inside it. The per-design “Padding” row was the first of those under another name and is gone from all fifteen panels; ⚑ amended 3 September 2026 under one control name means one set of values: the three named values are the set and each design states what they resolve to in its own geometry, so a different ladder is a resolution rather than a second value set and nothing is renamed — 8 Row Cards' card padding keeps the name, and 7 Contrast Band's band scale stays Vertical spacing's own resolution at 80 · 96 · 132. The resolutions: 16 · 24 · 36 on an ordinary row, 44 · 56 · 72 on 3 Slim, 20 · 28 · 40 on 9 Big Type and 13 Timeline, 12 · 16 · 24 on 14 Index, and card padding on 8 Row Cards. Separators are one vocabulary of three: Off · Between rows · Boxed, defaulting to Between rows in eleven designs; Boxed is never a default, because a list that closes itself top and bottom is a table and A17·10 Ledger is where a site should go for one. Three designs have no Rule control at all — 8 Row Cards has a plane, 13 Timeline has a rail, 12 Panel has a container — and the rule is one separator per design. The smallest readable row is 44 px, drawn by 3 Slim at Compact and by 14 Index without a date: A1's touch-target minimum, because a row that is a link should not be smaller than a button. It breaks the 8 px grid and the touch floor wins. 2 · Thumbnail on, off, or on the leading edge. Nine of the fifteen draw no image at all and are, by construction, immune to a missing one. One design groups: 5 Grouped, by Month · Year · Tag · Nothing, derived from the data and never authored. ⚑ Amended in this pass: the grouping needs a small script. Ghost’s templates cannot tell that the month changed between two posts — no arithmetic, and no access to the previous item inside a loop — so the design declares group-headings and renders flat and ungrouped without JavaScript: every post present, in order, each row with its own date as drawn, no group heading and no group date, and no extra space. Responsive floor. Nine designs hang something at the right edge at 1440 and all nine drop it under the title at 834, into A17's meta row unchanged. Reading time leaves the meta below 767 in every design; the date never does. Multi-column designs step down one rung at each breakpoint and reach one column at 767.
The card. A17's post card, verbatim: DOM order image → tag → title → excerpt → meta; the whole row is the link, taking its name from the title; no card fill, border, shadow or radius; hover is a 2 px accent underline on the title at 160 ms; focus is A6's ring on the whole row. Three departures, and ⚑ 3 September 2026 they are A17’s to declare: 4 Dated and 13 Timeline put the date first, 9 Big Type moves the tag into the meta after A17·9 — three of the four entries in the card’s own DOM-order register, which lives with the card in A17 under the card’s exceptions belong in the card. The register is closed: a design not named in it may not depart, and a new departure is added there or nowhere. 3 Slim’s single-line clip is written into the card’s title promise the same way — the only truncation in the library. Exceptions to “no plane”. Two: 8 Row Cards gives each post a plane and takes A17·4's hover lift; 12 Panel gives the section one. Both lose their shadow in dark, as A17 found, and both are measurably weaker there. Dark. Ground deepens, surfaces lift one step, the plate lifts to #2A251E, hairlines take #332E27 — A17's measured symmetric step, 9.1 lightness points below the light ground and 9.8 above the dark one, and A18 is the harder test of it: 14 Index draws eighteen hairlines on one screen and 3 Slim draws ten at 44 px apart. Photographs untouched. Shadows dropped. Accent is spent twice a section — a title's hover underline and A6's ring. Two designs spend a third and both are controls: 7 Contrast Band's lifted accent and 15 Load More's Solid button. A tag is never accent, a numeral is never accent, a timeline marker is never accent. Behaviour. Two designs declare a module — 5 Grouped, group-headings ⚑ new in this pass, and 15 Load More, load-more — and thirteen declare none. core is assumed, not declared. No A18 design loses a post without JavaScript, thirteen are pixel-identical with it off, and the two that are not say so on their frames: 5 Grouped renders flat and ungrouped, and 15 Load More renders Ghost’s numbered pagination links in place of its button. No module name was invented and infinite-scroll stays refused category-wide. Structure. <section aria-labelledby> named by its title, an <h2>; posts a <ul> of <li>, each title an <h3>; dates <time datetime>; plate aria-hidden. Two departures: 11 Numbered is an <ol>, the only one in the library; 5 Grouped makes each heading an <h3> and steps its titles to <h4>.
