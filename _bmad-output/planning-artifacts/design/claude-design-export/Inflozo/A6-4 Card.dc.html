Two paddings, and only one of them is a control. The section's own vertical padding is fixed at 96 — A5·16's decision, carried, because a card that could be given 132 px of section padding and 88 px of its own turns into a card floating in an empty screen. The card's padding is the control: Compact 48 · Comfortable 64 · Spacious 88, one step tighter than the section scale it replaces, since a plane's edge does part of the work the space was doing. The inset is A4·8's values unchanged — Snug 16 · Comfortable 40 · Wide 72 inside the page margin — so a site running A4·8's hero and this banner reads one shape at two scales.
Above the footer this is the design that needs no help. The card's own bottom edge is the boundary, so the footer's hairline is doing nothing the card was not already doing, and the section's 96 px sits between two visible edges rather than between two grounds.

A5·4's three treatments, carried verbatim rather than re-decided: a card in a feature grid and a card holding a banner are the same object at two sizes, and a site that changes section should not meet a fourth kind of card. The dark substitution comes with them — Raised becomes a hairline and no shadow in dark, since a warm shadow on a near-black ground is invisible, which makes Raised and Hairline the same object there. The editor says so rather than hiding the value. Tinted is the one that behaves differently at banner scale: a 1,216 px block of hover surface with no border is the largest tinted area in the library, and on a page whose other sections are on background it reads as a band with rounded corners rather than a card — which is a legitimate thing to want, and 5 Contrast Band is the louder version of it.

At Left the measure is 620 px inside the card and the right of the card stays empty — 2 Flush Left's arrangement on a plane, with the plane's edge doing what 2's hairline does. It is the only alignment value in A6 that changes what a design is rather than where its text sits, and the reason the two designs both exist is the ground: 2 works on a page with no planes anywhere, 4 on a page made of them. The card never takes a whole-card link — A5·4's Whole item has no equivalent here, because a card whose whole surface is a link and which also holds two actions is a link inside a link. The panel does not offer it.

1 · The card is a <div> and nothing more — not a region, a group, an <article> or a landmark. The section around it is already named by the title; a plane inside a named region does not need a second name, and A5·16 settled the same question for its panel. 2 · The focus ring's 2 px inner gap is the card's colour, not the page's — surface at Hairline and Raised, the hover surface at Tinted — since the ring has to read against whatever the button is actually sitting on. On the card the accent ring is 2.8:1 against surface, which passes 1.4.11's 3:1 only against the accent fill it surrounds; the ring is therefore drawn 2 px thick rather than 1, and that thickness is what carries it. No whole-card link at any value. A card holding two actions cannot itself be one, and A5·4's Whole item has no equivalent here. Two tab stops, primary first, DOM order as all fifteen. Contrast · title 12.1:1 light / 12.6:1 dark · sub and note 5.4:1 / 6.1:1 on surface and 5.1:1 / 5.9:1 on the Tinted card, which is the one treatment where the muted text is measured against the hover surface · button 4.6:1 / 8.1:1. Forced colours drop the tint and the shadow and keep the Hairline border, so Tinted and Raised both become Hairline — three treatments, one forced-colours result, which is the honest consequence of the plane being decorative.
