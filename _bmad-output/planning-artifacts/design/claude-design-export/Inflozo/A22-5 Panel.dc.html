FIVE CONTROLS, NOT SIX — AND WHY
Internal padding is fixed at 56 × 64 ⚑. A plane that fills the content box has no outside gap to trade against, so an inside-padding control would be the only thing setting its height, which makes the plane a shape rather than a ground.A22’s norm is six; five is what is left when a control does not earn its place. The brief’s ceiling is seven and its floor is four.2 Card is the same ask as an object, and the panel names it ⚑ so a user comparing them knows what they are choosing between.

Identical to 2 Card’s behaviour, on a plane instead of an object: the plane’s height does not change through a submit ⚑. The difference the two designs make is not to the states but to the edge — a plane has one, the page has none, and a card has two.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label and the note. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. The native POST to /members/api/send-magic-link/ stays as the no-JS floor.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESFive controls of its own. Cut, beyond the four 2 Card cuts: an internal-padding value, for the reason above. Nothing was added to reach six — the brief’s floor is four and a control invented to hit a number is the thing rule 5 exists to stop.Nothing here is per-state. Field width, alignment and the note apply to all seven states at once — a control writes one value onto the section and the stylesheet reads it. The seven form states are states, not items: nothing is added to them and nothing is reordered ⚑, and they are viewed through P0·6’s State pill rather than anything in this panel.Where the address goes is the shared group below this design’s own: which newsletter, whether a name is asked for, what a signed-in subscriber sees, who sees the section at all ⚑ new in this pass, and what happens when members are switched off. The form always posts to Ghost ⚑ — the endpoint is read-only, because a section that could repoint it could silently send addresses somewhere the publication did not choose.
