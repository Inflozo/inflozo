This design draws no form ⚑, so it declares no behaviour module and there is nothing in it to degrade: every element is server-rendered text, a link, or a static image. The delivery group is absent from the sidebar and the form fields are kept on the section ⚑, returning the moment the user switches to one of the ten designs that has a form. The grid is drawn for two and three ⚑. At one the card takes 640 and sits at the left rather than stretching to 1,296 — and the panel names 7 Map Split as the design for a single place, since a card that wide beside 656 px of nothing is a worse answer than a map. At four the grid wraps to two rows of two rather than shrinking the cards to 312 ⚑, because a 16:9 thumb at 312 is 175 px tall and the address under it wraps to four lines.

THE DELIVERY GROUP · ABSENT HEREThis design draws no form ⚑, so the delivery rows are not shown, and the mailto: honesty fix does not reach it: every address, number and directions link here is already a real link. The rows are kept on the section and return the moment you switch to a design that has one.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesEvery visible text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, each card’s name, address and hours, the new phone and email lines, and the directions label. The field labels and the placeholders are edited by clicking them on the canvas ⚑ — this panel holds no label boxes. Nothing drawn here is Ghost-owned ⚑, so no line carries the “Edit in Ghost” lock — A16 reads only @site.title, and only as an aria-label fallback. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker. Buttons accept an optional icon before or after the label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size / colour-role popover, off by default ⚑. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: directionsLabel (default “Directions”) and every item string are authored fields with defaults, and “Sending…”, “Send another message”, the failed banner’s first line, “Email us at {address}” and the honeypot’s hidden label are theme translation-catalog strings.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registryNone ⚑ — text and real links: mailto:, tel: and the social URLs. There is nothing to degrade, which is why the form panels offer this design as the fallback A16 always has. No module name is coined, and the contact-form registry addition the form designs ask for does not touch this one.
DATA · Ghost’s surface, and what A16 cannot readNothing is read from Ghost’s content API ⚑ — no Show ladder, no filter, and the P0·5 “Populate from…” panel is not offered anywhere in A16 ⚑, because no design reads a Ghost list. This design draws no action, so Member visibility is not offered ⚑ — recorded rather than invented; a members-only page is A32 Paywall’s ground.Ghost has no location object ⚑ — the finding stands: nothing in Ghost’s settings or content API carries a postal address, a set of opening hours or a phone number, so every card is authored. The map thumbs are uploads, so no tile provider is called at render and none is named in v1.
