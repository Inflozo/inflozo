Alternating Media

Two to four steps as full-width halves, the picture changing side at every step. The design for a process where each step is worth showing rather than describing, and the one where A13-0's numeral plate is most visible — a step with no picture gets a plate the size of the picture it does not have.

Alternation is a rule rather than a control. The user sets which side the first step's picture is on, and every step after it alternates from there — a per-step side would be a per-item design control, which A13-0 refuses in writing. It is also the design that shows most plainly why the tuple's media slot reads left: the tuple records the default's first step, not the pattern.

Alternation is a rule, not a control, and the control is where it starts. First step's media: Left · Right. Every step after it alternates. A per-step side is refused in writing — it is a per-item design control, which A13-0 rules out by construction, and a site that wants two pictures on the same side wants 12 Media Top. Two to four steps, and the ceiling is low for a stated reason. Four steps at Even and 3:2 is 1,940 px of section before the head; five is 2,425, which is two full screens of one section. Five or more → 3 Rows, named in the panel before the add, which keeps the bodies and drops the pictures. ⚑ Mine, and it is the tightest count ceiling in A13 after 2 Track's. Crop: Landscape 3:2 · Square 1:1, one named ratio for the whole section, object-fit: cover, centred, at the pack radius. Portrait is not offered: a 632 × 790 portrait beside three lines of type is a column of nothing. No focal point, no per-step crop, no filter, no scrim, no caption and no frame — the answer to a badly cropped picture is a different upload. The plate follows the crop, so it is 632 × 421 at Landscape and 632 × 632 at Square, and its figure is 0.4 × the shorter edge in both — 168 and 252. A section where most pictures are missing is a very large numeral gallery, and the panel says so in those words rather than hiding the count: “3 of 4 steps have a picture.”

STEPS · the item list · outside the design’s listSteps is the P0·3 item list. Add step arrives with content — title seeded “New step”, everything else empty — so the row draws at once as a numeral and a title alone and takes the next numeral by existing. Lands last. Remove is never disabled; drag to reorder; per-item content only. Per item: title req ≤ 80 · body opt ≤ 240 · label opt ≤ 16 · duration opt ≤ 12 · image and imageAlt ≤ 60, icon and label stored. Add inherits the alternation, so adding a step flips nothing that already exists; remove and reorder re-alternate every step after them — the one edit in A13 that moves elements the user did not touch, disclosed in the repeater. Two to four; five or more → 3 Rows. At Source From posts the list is P0·3’s read-only card — no Add step, no drag handles, no Remove, one row per post.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Per step, title and body edit inline beside the picture. image carries Image focus (Centre · Top · Bottom) in the Image Picker popover — the field does its most visible work here, at 632 × 421 and 632 × 632, and imageAlt ≤ 60 sits beside it. An empty slot draws the numeral plate at the full crop, the largest type anywhere in A13. At Source From posts the step title and body are Ghost-owned: the toolbar is replaced by the plain-text lock pill and clicking says “Edit in Ghost”. The numeral is never editable — it is a rendering of position, so there is nothing to click and nothing to type.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registryNone. Alternation is :nth-child(even) with flex-direction: row-reverse — a visual reversal that leaves DOM order alone — and the plate is a template condition resolved on the server. JS off: identical at every width.
