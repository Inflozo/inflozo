Big Numbers

Two to four steps with the numerals at display size — 72 or 104 px, the only place in A13 the ladder goes past 40. The section's one display moment is spent on the figures rather than on the title, which is why A13 offers no Display title anywhere in the category.

It is the design that tests A13-0's hardest rule: the numeral is decorative and aria-hidden even at 104 px, because the <ol> already carries the order. A 104 px element a screen reader never mentions looks wrong written down and is right on the page — the figure is a rendering of position, and position is what a list announces.

Three 416 cells with a hairline in each gutter, the numeral at Display 104 on a 0.86 line height, 16 px to the title. The 0.86 is the one place in A13 a line height goes below 1: at 104 px, Georgia's default leading leaves 26 px of air under the figure that reads as a broken gap, and tightening it puts the figure's baseline where the eye expects it. The title is 24/600 here — one of two places in A13 a step title rises above 20 — and the section title is fixed at Large 40 rather than being a control, because a Display numeral and a Small title are not a composition anyone should be able to build by accident. ⚑ Mine, all three numbers.
The numeral stays text-muted at 104 px and is never accent, never an outline face, never border. Three accent figures at 104 px would be the loudest thing on the page and would say the three steps are three offers. Muted at 104 px is still 5.6:1 — the size does not change the ratio, and the ratio is checked because a sighted reader is following the sequence with it. Head to steps is 64 rather than 48, A11·15's exception carried, because a 40 px title 48 px above a 104 px figure reads as one block.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Per step, title and body edit inline beside or under the figure. The figure itself is not a field — count-up is refused with it, a numeral being a position rather than a statistic. At Source From posts the step title and body are Ghost-owned: the toolbar is replaced by the plain-text lock pill and clicking says “Edit in Ghost”. The numeral is never editable — it is a rendering of position, so there is nothing to click and nothing to type.

Member buttons are conditionalA13 draws no button anywhere. Its one destination is the section link, and where that link points at a member action it now carries the ask: it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens. Scope-tagged apart from Member visibility, which hides the section server-side.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject for the notice — this design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for a notice to replace, and no line on this frame ever promised a subscription without script. JavaScript off: identical. count-up is refused in all fifteen designs: a numeral is a position, not a statistic, and animating it would be counting up to 3.
Ghost templates cannot count, add, or rememberThe numeral is a stylesheet counter, not arithmetic: the ordered list carries a CSS counter and each item renders counter(step, decimal-leading-zero), which is where 01 to 08 comes from — no padding helper, no authored field, no template maths.
