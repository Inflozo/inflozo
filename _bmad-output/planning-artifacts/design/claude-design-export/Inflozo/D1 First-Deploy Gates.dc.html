Back your site up before we replace the theme Ghost(Pro) · orbitweekly.com
WHAT INFLOZO WRITES TO YOUR SITE Inflozo writes exactly two things to your Ghost site: your theme, and routes.yaml. It never writes posts, pages, members, tags, settings or redirects.
A full backup is still the right thing to have. A theme change is reversible only if you can put the old theme back — and that means holding a copy of it before we replace it.
On Ghost(Pro), themes, content, members, routes and redirects all download from Ghost Admin. Images and media do not — Ghost Admin has no bulk export for them, and there is no server to copy them from. Ghost's own documentation points you at your host's support for this. Ghost(Pro) also takes its own platform backups; that is a Ghost service, not an Inflozo one, and it is worth confirming with Ghost what it covers and how to request a restore.
This is not something Inflozo can fix, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.
