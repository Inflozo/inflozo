Newsletter Band

A band across the top of the footer holding one form row — field, button, and a single line of reassurance under it — with the links beneath. This is the design that settles the footer's inline form for the whole category: no heading, no body copy, no image, one row. A22 Newsletter owns the version that persuades; the footer's job is to catch someone who has already decided.

The band is one raised row on surface: a 400 px field, a 10 px gap, the accent button, and one 13 px line under the pair. No heading and no sentence above the field — the button label says what happens and the note says what you are agreeing to. The accent button is the footer's one accent; when this design is used, no other element in the footer may spend it. Below the band the footer is A3·2 at three columns, unchanged.

One letter each Thursday. Unsubscribe in a click.4
1 · A real visually-hidden <label>, always. The placeholder is never the label, and with no visible heading above the field the label is the only thing naming this form — so the form element itself also carries aria-label="Subscribe to Orbit Weekly". 2 · type="email", autocomplete="email", inputmode="email", required. Validation on blur and on submit, never per keystroke; on error aria-invalid is set and focus stays in the field. 3 · The button is a <button type="submit"> whose visible label is its whole accessible name. In the submitting state it is disabled and the label change is announced by the live region, not by the button. 4 · The note is the form's aria-describedby target and doubles as the aria-live="polite" region: error and confirmation both render here, so a screen-reader user hears the outcome in the place they were already told about the terms. Focus order · field, button, then the footer's links. The band is first in the footer at every width, and nothing in it is re-ordered by CSS. Contrast · placeholder on the field's ground 5.1:1 light and 5.9:1 dark; note 5.4:1 / 6.4:1; error at 13 px / 500 in text 12.1:1; button label on the darkened accent 4.6:1 light and 8.1:1 dark. Field 44 px at desktop, 48 px at ≤ 767.

SPEC · A3·4 NEWSLETTER BAND · full text in “A3 Footers — Spec.md”
Content fields newsletterPlaceholder (text, opt, ≤ 28, default “you@example.com”) · newsletterButtonLabel (text, opt, ≤ 16, default “Subscribe”) · newsletterNote (text, opt, ≤ 90, one line) · newsletterSuccess (text, opt, ≤ 60, default “You're in. Check your inbox to confirm the address.”) · newsletterSuccessShort (text, opt, ≤ 32 — used ≤ 767) · plus wordmark, mark, linkColumns[], social[], copyright, legalLinks[], showAttribution. There is no heading or body field — that is the settlement, not an omission. The error line is fixed copy, not a field: it must match what was actually rejected.
Controls · data · states Padding: Compact 28 · Comfortable 40 · Spacious 56 (band). Band ground: Surface · Background · ~~Contrast~~ disabled with its reason. Field width: Narrow 320 · Medium 400 · Wide 480. Band alignment: Centred · Left. Columns: Auto · Two · Three · Off. Attribution: “Built with Inflozo” → inflozo.com — disabled-on for Free, only Pro may hide. Reconciled this pass: Padding, Ground and Top edge are retired into the universal Vertical spacing, Background role and Top divider outside the design’s list; the new controls are in the updated spec.
Data: posts to Ghost's members subscribe endpoint; Ghost sends its own confirmation email. An already-subscribed address gets Ghost's response in the same slot. Members off in Ghost → the field is replaced by the button alone, linking to the subscribe page; the sidebar states it and links to the setting.
States: empty · focus (1.5 px accent border, ring suppressed) · invalid (error in the note's slot, 1.5 px text border, no red, height unchanged) · submitting (“Subscribing…”, field disabled, width reserved) · done (confirmation and a way back, height unchanged, session-lived).
Responsive · empty · a11y · flagged 1440–1081: one row, field at its named width, note under. 1080–768: field steps one value down, row holds, columns go two-across with the brand block as a cell. ≤ 767: field and button take a row each at 48 px, field text 15 → 16 px, note centred, success copy uses newsletterSuccessShort.
Empty: no note → the band is the row alone and loses 22 px. Columns: Off → the band sits directly above A3·1's bar. No columns authored and Columns: Auto → same result, and the sidebar says so.
a11y: visually-hidden label plus an aria-label on the form, since there is no visible heading; the note is both aria-describedby and the polite live region; validation on blur and submit only.
Flagged as mine: the 90-character note cap, the suppressed focus ring, the reserved button width, the session-lived done state, the shortened mobile success line, and the rule that this design takes the footer's whole accent budget.
