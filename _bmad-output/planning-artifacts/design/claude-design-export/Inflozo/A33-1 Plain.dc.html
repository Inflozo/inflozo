THIS TREATMENT’S CONTROLS AND WHAT THEY SELECT
Rules → .kg-bookmark-card, .kg-toggle-card, .kg-audio-card, .kg-file-card, .kg-product-card — the five named cards, and no others. The HTML card is not on the list because it has no element ⚑.Tinted cards → .kg-callout-card (and its colour variant class) and the call-to-action card, whose class is unverified.Emoji → .kg-callout-emoji, hidden with display:none and never with visibility, so the text takes the whole plane.Space around cards → the adjacent-sibling margin on .kg-card, inside the theme’s own wrapper around the post body. Every documented card carries .kg-card alongside its own class.Captions → figcaption inside .kg-image-card, .kg-gallery-card, .kg-embed-card and .kg-video-card. One trap: .kg-file-card-caption is the file card’s description, not a caption, and the caption rule must not reach it.Credit line → the trailing em run inside that same figcaption. This is the invented reading ⚑ — Ghost has no credit field — and it now carries a version risk: Ghost 6 strips em and strong from a feature-image caption while Ghost 5 keeps them (tested 2026-08-31). Whether the same stripping reaches a card’s figcaption is untested; the owner commissioned that test on 1 September 2026 and it stays open until it is run. One cost of the rule is accepted rather than solved (owner, 1 September 2026): a caption ending in italics for any other reason — a film title, a ship’s name — reads as a credit, and the Credit line control’s help text now says so in the editor’s own words.
THE WIDTH CLASSES · WHAT THE CARD WIDTHS ROW DEPENDS ON
.kg-width-wide and .kg-width-full sit on the card’s own element beside its card class — figure.kg-card.kg-image-card.kg-width-wide. These two are the whole of the width contract.Regular has no class on an image, gallery or embed figure — Ghost’s documentation says a normal-width card carries no extra class, so the regular rung is the card class’s own default and never a .kg-width-regular selector. The signup card is the exception: Ghost documents kg-width-regular on it. Do not write one rule for both.Two cards arrive with a width class already set ⚑: Ghost’s own markup ships .kg-header-card with kg-width-full, and its gallery example ships .kg-gallery-card with kg-width-wide. The signup card also provides .kg-content-wide at full width and .kg-layout-split when it carries an image.In this treatment: regular 720, wide 1,040, full 1,296 — the default resolution, unchanged from Ghost’s intent.
WHERE WE ARE NOT CERTAIN · SAID RATHER THAN GUESSED ⚑
Verified against Ghost’s theme documentation: .kg-card, .kg-image-card/.kg-image, .kg-gallery-card/.kg-gallery-container/.kg-gallery-row/.kg-gallery-image, .kg-bookmark-card and its eight parts, .kg-embed-card, .kg-callout-card/.kg-callout-emoji/.kg-callout-text, .kg-toggle-card/.kg-toggle-heading/.kg-toggle-heading-text/.kg-toggle-card-icon/.kg-toggle-content, .kg-button-card/.kg-btn, .kg-file-card and its parts, .kg-audio-card, .kg-video-card, .kg-header-card, .kg-signup-card, .kg-product-card/.kg-product-card-container, .kg-width-wide, .kg-width-full.Not verified, and flagged rather than guessed: the call-to-action card’s class (drawn here as .kg-cta-card) and the email-content card’s, neither of which appears in Ghost’s documented class list; the eight callout colour variants beyond .kg-callout-card-accent, whose pattern is .kg-callout-card-; the product card’s inner classes below .kg-product-card-container; and whether Ghost 6’s header card keeps the same class set. A wrong selector styles nothing silently, so each of these is written as unverified in the specification and none is used to carry a rule on its own.No class at all: the divider is a bare hr; markdown and HTML emit no wrapper; the public-preview cut leaves an HTML comment. The toggle exposes its state as [data-kg-toggle-state="close"|"open"] — a stylesheet can read the state, but only Ghost’s script ever changes it, so with JavaScript off the card stays closed.

A33 ships a stylesheet and nothing else, so the selector is the deliverable: inside a post’s body we own the stylesheet and not the markup, the ARIA attributes or the text. Nothing here assumes markup A33 would have to emit ⚑. One build fact belongs beside these: Ghost injects its own cards.min.css through ghost_head unless the theme’s package.json excludes a card in card_assets, so a treatment’s rules only take effect for cards on that exclusion list. Settled by the owner, 1 September 2026: A33 replaces Ghost's card CSS for all twelve excludable cards — nft ignored, since this category draws no NFT card — and the theme always re-includes Ghost's own cards.min.js, so A33 owns the look and Ghost keeps the behaviour. One condition, tested first and with its fallback already written ⚑: an exclusion takes a card's assets rather than its stylesheet alone, so if the served bundle turns out to be filtered by the config, the seven static cards are excluded and toggle, gallery, audio, video and signup stay on Ghost's assets, styled by a deeper selector — excluding those five would leave a card that renders and does nothing.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE FOUR STATES EVERY A33 DESIGN OWES
In the editor every card draws at its resting state ⚑ — the toggle closed, no hover, no ring. The treatment is CSS, so the edit-time picture and the published picture are the same. With JavaScript off fourteen of the twenty cards are identical ⚑. Six depend on scripts that are Ghost’s or the provider’s, not A33’s ⚑: the toggle’s open and close, the gallery’s row ratios, the audio and video players, the signup card’s post, and the embed’s provider markup. The registry has no module for any of them — a finding, not a design, and A33 declares core rather than inventing one. The treatment never restyles what is inside an iframe ⚑. The embed card’s frame, radius and caption are A33’s; the provider’s markup is the provider’s. No control in A33 styles one card of one post ⚑ — a value is written once, site-wide, and every card of that type reads it.

One hover per card and never two. The bookmark’s whole card is one link, so its hover is the surface stepping to the hover token and nothing moving ⚑ — no lift, no scale, no shadow appearing. The button card derives its hover from the accent at 93 % brightness, A32’s rule carried. Focus is A6’s 2 px accent ring at a 4 px offset in every card that is focusable, and on the toggle the focus stop is Ghost’s own button, not the heading row.

THE MARKUP A33 STYLES · GHOST’S, ANNOTATED
IMAGE, GALLERY, EMBED<figure> + <figcaption> — the caption is inside the figure, so it is read with the image, not after it
BOOKMARKone <a> around the whole card · one focus stop · the thumbnail is aria-hidden and the title is the accessible name
CALLOUTa <div> with no role · the emoji carries aria-hidden ⚑ so it is not announced as “pushpin”
TOGGLEa plain <div> holding an <h4> and a <button> — not native disclosure markup ⚑. The heading is a real h4 and a stylesheet cannot change its level; the button is the only target and the only focus stop, and no theme can add the aria-expanded it lacks. Recorded as a Ghost limitation
BUTTONan <a> styled as a button · 48 px tall, 44 px minimum target at every width
PRODUCTGhost renders the rating as stars with no text equivalent ⚑ and a theme cannot add the words · recorded as a Ghost limitation, not something this category fixes
FILEthe link names the file and its size: “Download catalogue-2026-08.csv, 1.4 MB” ⚑
HEADERthe heading is an h2 ⚑ — Ghost emits h2/h3 and A33 never promotes one to h1; the post title is the page’s only h1
THIS DESIGN’S OWN ACCESSIBILITY NOTES
The hairline is decoration and carries no role ⚑. A screen reader hears the bookmark’s title, then its description, then its publisher line — the visual boundary is not announced, and does not need to be.The callout’s emoji carries aria-hidden="true" ⚑ so the text is not prefixed with “pushpin”. If the author writes a callout that is nothing but an emoji, the glyph is the content and the attribute is dropped — a build rule, flagged.Caption Hidden hides the caption, never the alt text ⚑. image.alt is Ghost’s field and no treatment in A33 reaches it.Contrast: body 16.5 px at text on background is 12.6:1; captions at text-muted are 5.4:1; the tinted callout’s text on the hover surface is 11.8:1. All pass AA at these sizes.

The markup is Ghost’s and the treatment’s job is to not break it ⚑ — which is why every A33 design shares this frame and only the last card differs. The one thing a treatment can break is the heading outline: the toggle’s heading and the header card’s heading must stay where Ghost put them.

Where these controls live This panel is the Editor cards screen (P0·7 · S14): the Treatment picker and its controls at the top, each card’s own 4–7-control panel below it, with treatment-owned values read-only in those panels. WHAT GHOST OWNS · FIVE READ-ONLY ROWS · IDENTICAL IN ALL SIX · NOT COUNTED
Which cards appear
Whatever the author inserted🔒
Read-only. The author writes the post ⚑ — a treatment styles every card Ghost renders and cannot add, remove or reorder one. A33 has no item list.
Card widths
The author’s, per card🔒
Read-only. Regular, wide and full are authored in Ghost, one card at a time ⚑ — the treatment decides what each of the three resolves to, which is the whole of designs 4 and 5. The classes this row depends on are .kg-width-wide and .kg-width-full; regular carries no width class on an image, gallery or embed figure.
Bookmark metadata
Scraped by Ghost🔒
Read-only. Title, description, icon, author, publisher and thumbnail are all scraped from the URL ⚑ — C.1’s matrix. A treatment chooses which rows show, never what they say.
Gallery rows
Ghost’s own script🔒
Read-only. Ghost’s card script computes each row’s ratios ⚑ and no registry module covers it. With JavaScript off the images render at equal widths — a finding for the architect, not a degradation A33 can quote.
Corners
From the pack’s radius token🔒
Read-only in every treatment ⚑. C.1’s matrix lists corners as a per-card setting; A33 answers that the pack’s radius token is that value and offers no other — a card that mixed radii would fail §3·1 in eleven of twelve packs.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESSix controls, and not one of them is a colour, a font or a radius. Colour is the pack’s seven roles, type is the pack’s pairing, and the radius token answers C.1’s “corners” for all eleven cards that listed it ⚑. A treatment that offered corners would mix radii the moment a user changed pack.Rules exclude media by rule, not by taste. Every card on the page ground at the article’s measure, with no plane and no frame; a single hairline above and below the cards that must read as one object, and a tinted plane on the callout alone. In A33 the tuple’s containment and ground slots describe the card, not a section ⚑ — A33 has no section of its own; it is CSS over the cards inside A25’s. Containment none and ground page are what 2 Card, 3 Panel and 6 Contrast Band each change exactly one of.3 · Archetype. article body. Its ladder: the measure narrows with the page and nothing rearranges. Two departures — space steps to 32 at ≤ 767, and the bookmark’s thumbnail moves above its text at ≤ 767 ⚑.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 measure 720 centred in the 1,296 box on a 72 margin; wide 1,040, full 1,296; space 48; caption 13.5 at the measure. 834 measure 754, wide and full both 754 — there is no wide column below 1024 ⚑ — space 48. ≤ 767 measure 350 on a 20 margin, every width 350, space 32, bookmark thumbnail above its text, gallery one column (Ghost’s own collapse).5 · Content fields. The whole category union, every time ⚑ — a treatment cannot decline a card the author inserted. Authored: image.src (file, required), image.alt (text ≤ 125), image.caption (rich text, optional), image.width (regular · wide · full), gallery.images[] (1–9 files, ordered), gallery.caption, bookmark.url, callout.text (required) + callout.emoji + callout.colour (nine values), toggle.heading + toggle.content (both required), button.label (≤ 40) + button.url + button.align, embed.url, product.title + product.description + product.image + product.rating (1–5) + product.buttonLabel/buttonUrl, file.file + file.title + file.description, header.heading + header.subheading + header.buttonLabel/buttonUrl + header.size + header.style + header.backgroundImage, markdown.md (rich text, no wrapper class), html.html + html.visibility (public · free · paid), email.greeting + email.fallback + email.text (newsletter only), cta.text + cta.image + cta.sponsorLabel + cta.buttonLabel/buttonUrl + cta.background + cta.visibility + cta.showOn (web · newsletter · both), audio.file + audio.title + audio.thumbnail, video.file + video.poster + video.loop + video.width, signup.heading + signup.subheading + signup.disclaimer + signup.buttonText + signup.layout + signup.background + signup.label. Read-only, scraped or derived: the bookmark’s title, description, icon, author, publisher and thumbnail; the embed’s provider markup; the file’s name and size.6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Space around cards Compact 32 · Comfortable 48 · Spacious 64. Captions Under, left · Under, centred · Hidden. Credit line With the caption · Its own line · Hidden. 7 · Data. Everything comes from {​{content}​} as Ghost renders it — A33 adds no markup of its own ⚑. Zero cards → the treatment renders nothing at all: no wrapper, no padding, no trace, because there is nothing to select for. One card → it takes its own space and the article closes around it. Many → the rhythm is the space value, repeated; two cards of the same type in a row keep one space between them, not two ⚑. The same treatment renders on pages, and above a paywall cut inside A32’s preview.8 · Empty state. No caption → no <figcaption> and the space below returns to the card’s spacing value, never an empty line ⚑. No credit → the caption is one line. No scraped thumbnail → the bookmark’s text takes the full width and no grey box appears ⚑ (A19’s rule). One gallery image → one image at the card’s width. No product image → title, description, rating and button keep the card’s padding. No emoji → the callout’s text takes the full plane. No cards at all → nothing renders; in the editor the Cards panel shows the treatment’s preview against the C.4 style-guide fixture rather than an empty canvas ⚑.9 · Behaviour module. Modules: accordion for the toggle card, core for everything else — the same two in all six treatments, because a treatment is a stylesheet ⚑. Edit-safe: yes; the resting state is the only state and nothing animates, posts or pins while the post is edited. No-JS, quoted: accordion — the registry’s quoted degradation does not hold here ⚑ — Ghost’s toggle is a container with a heading and a button, not native disclosure markup, so with JavaScript off it cannot open. A33 declares accordion because it is the nearest module and records the mismatch as a finding. core — “Never runs; the .js-enabled class is never set, so all JS-conditional CSS stays in its no-JS branch.” The one gap is Ghost’s own gallery row script ⚑ — it computes each row’s ratios, the registry has no module for it, and with JS off the images render at equal widths. That is a finding for the architect, not a degradation A33 may quote.10 · Accessibility. <figure>/<figcaption> kept paired on image, gallery and embed. Bookmark: one <a>, one focus stop, thumbnail aria-hidden, title as the accessible name. Toggle: a plain div with an h4 and a button — not disclosure markup ⚑, and the heading level is Ghost’s. Button card: <a> at 48 px, 44 px minimum target. Product: Ghost gives the stars no text equivalent and a theme cannot add one ⚑ — a Ghost limitation. File: the link names the file and its size. Header: h2, never promoted to h1. The hairline carries no role; the callout’s emoji carries aria-hidden. Focus order is document order in every treatment ⚑.— · Repeating items. One authored array in the whole category: gallery.images[] ⚑. Its Add, Remove and Reorder are Koenig’s, on the canvas, not in this sidebar — the images belong to the post. Minimum 1, maximum 9 (Ghost’s limit); at 1 it renders as a single image at the card’s width, at 9 as three rows of three, and at 0 the card cannot exist — Ghost deletes an empty gallery card. No control in A33 styles one image, one card or one post ⚑: a value is written once, site-wide. Inside a card the author edits content only — text, image, link — never its layout.Flagged ⚑ — the tuple describing the card rather than a section; the hairline pair as containment, on six named cards; the callout and the call to action keeping a plane in a treatment called Plain; the radius token answering C.1’s per-card “corners”; space 32 at ≤ 767 overriding the control; the bookmark thumbnail moving above its text; Ghost’s gallery row script having no registry module; the credit being the caption’s trailing <em> run rather than a field.

WHY THIS IS THE ONLY ICON SLOT IN A33 Every other glyph in the twenty cards is Ghost's own card chrome — the toggle's chevron, the audio and video transport, the file card's download mark, the bookmark's scraped favicon, the product card's stars, the callout's emoji — and correctly out of the user's reach ⚑. The divider's centred glyph is the one ornament A33 draws itself, so it is the one slot. A33 adds no markup to {​{content}​}, so the glyph is a CSS pseudo-element on the hr, aria-hidden, and absent from the accessibility tree exactly as it is absent from the DOM outline. Button-card icons are declined with a reason: Ghost's button card has a label and a URL and no icon field, and adding one would be markup A33 may not write ⚑ — recorded in the reconciliation notes, not designed around.
