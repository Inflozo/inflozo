Four Up

Four 306 cells, two lines of excerpt, and the reading time dropped from the meta row. The densest grid in A17 that still carries an excerpt — narrower than this and the excerpt becomes a caption, which is 14 Dense's design instead.

Its own device is the row rule: an optional hairline above each row, which turns eight posts from a field of cards into something closer to an index. That, and every measure on the card being tuned for 306 from the start, is what separates it from 1 Three Up at Per row Four — where 306 is a setting the card was not drawn for. The overlap between the two is deliberate and is stated in both panels.
