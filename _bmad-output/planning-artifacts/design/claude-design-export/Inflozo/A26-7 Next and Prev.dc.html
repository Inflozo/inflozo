A destination with no feature image takes hover surface as a plate and its type returns to text ⚑ — A19's Plate, applied per tile, so a pair can be one picture and one plate and still be a pair. It is never stretched, never given a generic photograph and never dropped.
With only one destination the tile takes the full width at the same height ⚑ and keeps its own kicker and its own alignment: a lone Previous tile is still left-aligned. With neither, the section renders nothing ⚑ — no tile, no seam, no reserved height, and no other design in its place.

POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
No design ever turns into another designThe fallback is deleted in three places. “With no destinations the section renders nothing and the design hands off to 1 Author Bio” now reads nothing renders — no tile, no seam, no reserved height, and no other design in its place. On the first and last post of a one-post site the footer is simply absent. Nothing else on this frame changed: the tiles, the scrim, the mirror and Within are as drawn.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject in this design — it contains no subscribe and no sign-in form, so there is nothing for a designed notice to replace. The share links are real <a href> URLs and work unscripted; only the copy-link button is hidden.
Member buttons are conditionalNo subject at rest — this design draws no member action. At Blocks Everything the inherited subscribe bar carries the note, and the note is 12 Subscribe’s.
