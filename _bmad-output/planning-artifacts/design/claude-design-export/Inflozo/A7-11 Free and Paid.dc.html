Free and Paid

Two tiers drawn as what they are: an open door and an offer. The free tier is a block of text on the page's own ground with a text link; the paid tier is the only card. 6 Single Tier makes the same pair when the free tier is a caption to the offer — here both are full tiers with their own benefit lists, and the asymmetry is in the plane rather than the content.

Both halves carry the same six things — name, price, description, benefits, an action, and the same type sizes for all of them. What differs is the plane: the paid tier has one and the free tier does not, and that single difference does all the work the badge and the accent would otherwise share. The hairline between them is the free half's only edge; it runs the height of the taller column, 32 px from both, and it is a border token rather than an <hr>.
The free half puts its action last and the paid half puts it above the benefits. That is a real inconsistency and it is deliberate: the card's action sits above its list so a row of cards keeps its buttons on one line, and there is no row here — a text link at the foot of an unplaned column reads as the end of a paragraph, which is what the free tier is. The DOM order is identical in both halves and only the visual order differs, in one half, by one element.
