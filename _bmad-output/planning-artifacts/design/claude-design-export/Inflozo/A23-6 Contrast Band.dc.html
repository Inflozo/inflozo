The field and its results on the inverted band. The whole section changes ground — not the head alone — so the results are read on the band rather than under it.

WHAT THE BAND SETTLES
The whole section inverts, not its head. A23’s ground slot names what the section rests on, and half a section on contrast is not a ground ⚑. A publication that wants an inverted head over page-ground results is asking for two sections stacked.No accent in the design at all ⚑ — A29·3’s finding, carried: Paper’s accent on Paper’s contrast is 4.0:1. Focus border, match mark and the active state all take the carried colour. A pack whose accent passes on its band still does not get it here, because the design must survive all twelve.The band gets lighter in dark, not darker ⚑ — A22’s call, carried. #EDE7DA carrying #171511, so the inversion is still an inversion when the page is already dark.

Every state is drawn on the band, because the band is the section ⚑ — a design whose head inverts and whose results do not is two grounds and reads as two sections. The dim in Searching is opacity on the list, not a second token: on contrast there is no lighter step to move to ⚑.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SEVEN ROWS · IDENTICAL WHEREVER RESULTS ARE DRAWN · NOT COUNTED
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
Match highlight
MarkedPlain
A real <mark> at 16 % accent, 22 % in dark, in the title and the excerpt and never in the meta ⚑. At Plain it is absent from the DOM rather than untinted. Promoted here from 11 Thumb Rows, so every design that draws a result governs it in the same row ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.

Row titles, excerpts and meta are Ghost’s ⚑ — “Edit in Ghost”. countLabel, emptyHeading, emptyText, emptyLinkLabel, recentLabel and suggestLabel are edited through the P0·6 switcher: No query · Results · No matches ⚑. The archive link opens the Link Picker.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTGhost posts and pages by scope: no Add, no Remove, no drag ⚑. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — no CTA on the band; the field is a form and the archive link is navigation.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESNo accent control, because there is no accent ⚑. The value a designer would reach for — an accent button or an accent mark — is the one thing A29·3 proved fails on this ground, so the source group’s Match highlight resolves to the carried colour here rather than the accent.Row rules changes density, and says so. A control that silently changes a second quantity is how a sidebar becomes untrustworthy; this one names its side effect in its own help text ⚑.Background role is locked and the reason is shown ⚑ — not hidden. Vertical spacing resolves 0 at Full bleed, and Band padding stays as its own row because a band’s internal air is a different quantity from the space around it.
