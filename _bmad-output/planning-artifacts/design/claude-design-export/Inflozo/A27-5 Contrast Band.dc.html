Contrast Band

The set on the inverted band: edge to edge, the page ground stopping at its top line, three cards in the carried colour. The one design in A27 that changes the colour of the page.

It closes a post the way A6·5 and A26·4 close a section — the same band, the same padding ladder, the same rule that the accent is spent on nothing inside it. In dark mode Paper's band is light, so this is the only A27 design whose two modes are not variations of each other.

The band runs to both edges and the content sits on the 1,296 measure inside it, so the page margin is drawn by the band rather than by the page ⚑. The band's padding is the section's padding — one ladder, 64 · 96 · 132 — and there is no second inset to set.
Every foreground is a carried colour, not a token of its own: title onContrast, excerpt and meta at 72% of it ⚑, the plate a 7% lift of the band itself. The photographs are untouched — A19's rule, which A17 and A26 both restated: no filter, no dim, no border, on a band or anywhere else.
⚑ Mine: the 72% muted line, the 7% plate lift, and drawing this design at Heading Heading rather than Label.
