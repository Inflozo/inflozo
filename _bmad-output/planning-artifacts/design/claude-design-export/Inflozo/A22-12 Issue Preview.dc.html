ZERO, ONE AND MANY POSTS
0 posts → the rule, the label and the rows all go; the ask stands alone and the design is 1 Inline Row ⚑. Reachable on a new site, so it is drawn rather than assumed away.1 post → one row at the grid’s left, no stretch and no reflow ⚑. 2 → two of three columns, the third empty.many → the limit is Three · Four · Six and the grid wraps; the archive link is drawn whenever there is at least one post ⚑.

The issues do not change with the state ⚑ — they are the publication, not feedback. Done keeps them, so a reader who has just subscribed can start reading immediately rather than being sent to an empty confirmation ⚑. The signed-in subscribed line does the same, and is the state where the rows matter most.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label, the note, the issues label and the archive link’s label — the archive URL itself follows the source and is read-only. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”, and every issue’s title, date, thumbnail and reading time — post content is Ghost’s and says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. The native POST to /members/api/send-magic-link/ stays as the no-JS floor.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESFive controls of its own after this pass. Cut: an excerpt value (a two-line excerpt under three titles is A17’s grid and this is a preview ⚑), a visitor-facing tag filter (filter-strip is A20’s and navigates rather than filters in place), a featured-issue picker (Ghost has no editorial order for posts beyond featured ⚑), and a thumbnail-ratio value.The issues are Ghost’s posts. There is no Add and no Remove except at the Data panel’s Hand-picked ⚑, where the picks are references rather than copies; otherwise an issue appears here because it published and disappears because a newer one did ⚑. Order is publication order — Newest or Oldest in the P0·5 panel — and Hand-picked’s drag order overrides it ⚑.Where the address goes is the shared group below: which newsletter, whether a name is asked for, what a signed-in subscriber sees, who sees the section at all ⚑ new in this pass, and what happens when members are switched off. The form always posts to Ghost ⚑ — the endpoint is read-only.

No placeholder row, no empty thumbnail, no “no issues yet” chip ⚑.9 · Behaviour module. member-form. Edit-safe — nothing posts or validates while the section is being edited. No-JS, quoted from the registry: “The <form> posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint; Ghost’s own server response replaces the designed sent state.” Empty and invalid survive without script; submitting and the in-place done state are the JS path, and the note keeps its aria-describedby either way, losing only aria-live. The issue rows are server-rendered links and declare nothing; there is no load-more and no infinite scroll here — the archive link is a real page.10 · Accessibility notes. Heading h2; issue titles are h3 inside their anchors ⚑ and the label above them is a plain paragraph, not a heading — it is a caption for a list, and making it an h3 would put the titles at h4 for no gain. The whole row is the anchor ⚑. Thumbnails alt="", decorative — the title beside them is the name ⚑. A real visually-hidden <label>Email address</label>; type="email", autocomplete="email", inputmode="email", required. aria-invalid on error with focus held in the field. The note is both the aria-describedby target and the aria-live="polite" region ⚑. Light: title 13.4:1, date 5.1:1.

Repeating items — Ghost’s posts. No Add, no Remove, no reorder ⚑; order is publication order and is not selectable. Fields shown: title and date always, thumbnail and reading time when present.
