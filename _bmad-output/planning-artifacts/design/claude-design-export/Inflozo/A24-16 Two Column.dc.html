Two Column

The title in one column and the standfirst and byline in the other, side by side on the content measure, with the photograph beneath both. It is the only design in A24 where the title and the standfirst are read across rather than down — a title that has to hold its own against a paragraph beside it rather than one that leads into it.

It is the third design to reach page · one · bottom, with 1 Centred and 12 Rail, and archetype is what separates it: one axis, a fixed rail and a fluid column, and two columns of equal standing. The photograph is beneath both columns rather than in one of them, which is a distinction the responsive rule turns on.
