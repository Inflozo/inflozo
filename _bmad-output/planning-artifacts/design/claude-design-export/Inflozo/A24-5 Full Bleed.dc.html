EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Here that is readingTimeSuffix — the design's only authored string. The post title, the excerpt, the tag and author names and the caption are Ghost’s ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. The joins are theme translation-catalog strings, not fields ⚑ — the “and” between two authors and the “and 2 others” beyond three. No URL field and no icon slot exists here. The credit line is Ghost’s HTML: its link is the photographer's, and it is not inline-editable.
DATA · THE POST’S OWN · AND WHAT A PAGE CAN SWITCH OFFThe route's post and nothing else — no query, so P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere in A24 ⚑. tags[] and authors[] are Ghost's: no Add, no reorder, no per-item editing, and clicking a tag or an author says “Edit in Ghost”. On a page whose “Show title and feature image” is off the whole section does not render ⚑ — Ghost's semantics for @page.show_title_and_feature_image, stated rather than left to be discovered. Member Visibility is not here: nothing in A24 is a CTA.
WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A standing precondition is a new kind of panel element and A34's standing warning is its precedent. ⚑ It sits above the controls, it is permanent rather than conditional, and it names another section and offers to open it. The editor cannot enforce a relationship between two sections — nothing stops a user setting a solid header — so the section states the dependency where the user is already looking. A finding for the architect: the section picker should carry this too, before the design is chosen rather than after. Two block fields are changed and they are changed differently, which is the point of having five verbs. Feature image is locked — the value stays visible and cannot be moved, because the design without it is not a variant, it is another design. Standfirst is inert — the value can still be set, it simply is not drawn here, and it returns the moment the design is switched or the header hands off. Title size drops Small and adds Display 76, and it is the only design in A24 with a value above 64 outside 10 Big Type. ⚑ That is allowed because the type is not on the page — it is on a 620 px photograph, and scale on a picture is a relationship rather than a shout. 76 on 1,296 px of image is the same optical weight as 50 on the page. Cut: a "darken the whole image" toggle (Scrim already does it, honestly, and only where it is needed); a focal-point picker (Ghost's crop, not the section's); and a scroll parallax — no module covers it, the nearest is reveal, and a title that moves while a reader is trying to read it is not a title.

In dark the scrim cannot be the contrast token, because Paper's dark contrast is #EDE7DA — a pale wash over a photograph, which is fog. ⚑ So the dark scrim is the ground colour taken two steps down, #090806, at the same three percentages plus eight points — a derived value outside the seven roles, and the second overlay token the library has needed after A34·7's floating pill. A finding, not a preference: a pack that puts type on imagery needs a scrim colour per mode, and no pack records one.
The type does not change: the title takes the light mode's #F2EDE4 in both modes because it is on a photograph in both, which makes this the only element in A24 whose colour is the same in light and dark. Title 11.8:1 on the washed crop, byline 6.4:1.
