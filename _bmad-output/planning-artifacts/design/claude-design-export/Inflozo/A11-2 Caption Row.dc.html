Caption Row

A short label in a 196 px column at the left and the logos filling the 1,056 that is left, all on one line. The most common logo wall on the web, and the only design in A11 where type and artwork share a horizontal band — which makes it the design that has to answer what a 13 px label is vertically aligned to.

It reads the same field list as A11·1 Row and draws the eyebrow in a place no other design puts it. With no eyebrow authored the design is 1 Row, and the panel says so — a caption row without a caption is a row.

Two across at 163 on a 24 px gutter, box 28 at Medium, label above at 20 px, padding 64 / 20. Four logos are 2 + 2 and no row is short. State two is the wall and its caption and nothing else — no heading, no accessible name, no note, no link, no accent pixel and nothing focusable unless a logo carries a URL. Both states draw the same four logos in the same authored order.

ACCESSIBILITY · A CAPTION THAT IS NOT A HEADING The inline label is a <p>, not an <h2>, not an <h3>, and not the list's aria-label. With a title authored the section takes its name from the <h2> above the line; with no title it has no accessible name at all, like 10 Slim. Promoting a 13 px caption to a heading to fill that gap would put “Featured in” into every document outline on the page, which is worse than an unnamed region. Label side changes DOM order, and no order property is used anywhere in this design. At Label Right the caption is announced after the twelve names, which is the reading a sighted reader gets too. Below 1080 the label keeps its document position: first at Label side Left, last at Label side Right. The wall is a <ul> of <li> and each logo an <img> with its required alt, wrapped in an <a> where a URL is authored, <picture> where a dark file is. The label is outside the list, so it is not announced as an item. Contrast: label 5.6:1 light / 6.0:1 dark, title 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, note 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, link underline 3.3:1 / 6.3:1, rule 1.4:1 / 1.6:1 and decorative. A logo takes no ratio; at Muted the editor's advice is 3:1 non-text against the ground and the value is never disabled. Reduced motion changes nothing. One 160 ms transition on a linked cell, one on the link underline, both instant. At 400% the label is on its own line — above the wall at Label side Left, under it at Right — and the wall is two across; nothing overlaps and nothing scrolls sideways.
