A26 POST FOOTERS · DESIGN 3 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · EIGHT CONTROLS + THE UNIVERSAL TRIO

Card

The whole footer lifted onto A19·3's surface card at the content width — one plane holding all four blocks, a symmetrical inset the section owns, md shadow in light and a hairline in dark.

It is the only containment in A26: fourteen designs sit on the page and this one sits in a card. The card is what separates the footer from the article rather than a rule, which is why this design is the one to use under 3 Sheet — a sheet and a card read as two planes, and a sheet and a hairline read as one.

A19·3's card, verbatim: surface at the pack's radius, md shadow 0 4px 16px rgba(28,27,26,.08) in light, the shadow dropped and a hairline carrying the edge in dark. Nothing about it is new here except its contents.
The inset is symmetrical and is measured to the card's edge ⚑ — 48 all round at Comfortable — and the blocks inside keep the article's left edge only at Card width On the measure; at Content width they start 48 px inside the page margin, which is the visual cost of the plane and is stated rather than corrected ⚑.
Two hairlines inside a card is the ceiling ⚑. The tag row and the author block are separated by space; share and the pair are separated by rules, because a button row against a bio needs an edge and a bio against three pills does not.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESInline on canvas with P0·1: shareLabel, copiedLabel, authorLinkLabel, and at Blocks Everything subscribeHeading, subscribeLine, subscribeButton. Ghost-owned and never inline-editable: post titles, tag names, author names, bios and social handles — clicking one selects the section and the sidebar says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. The subscribe button accepts an optional icon from P0·2, before or after the label, with its size and colour-role popover. The member-aware ask is 12 Subscribe’s — at Blocks Everything this plane draws 12’s bar and inherits its states, and P0·4 opens there, not here ⚑. P0·3’s item controls land nowhere: A26 authors no array — the tags, the authors and the two neighbours are Ghost’s, and a Ghost-sourced list never shows an Add ⚑. P0·4 and Member Visibility land on 12 Subscribe alone in this category: a tag is navigation, a byline is attribution and a share link is not an offer. P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere — the footer reads the post’s own relations, not a query ⚑. No Preview control was removed because A26 never had one: previewing belongs to the editor, and P0·6’s state switcher is how the absent blocks, the copied confirmation and the member states are seen while editing. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: every authored label carries a default, and the strings nobody authors — the destination names, “and three others”, the ledger’s long dates — are theme translation-catalog strings.
DATA · THE POST’S OWN RELATIONS, AND NOTHING QUERIEDAs 1 Author Bio, plus the subscribe three at Blocks Everything and post.updated_at at Updated line On. {{#prev_post}}/{{#next_post}} are post-context only ⚑ — on a page the pair is simply absent, which is the category’s absent-block rule and not a new one. The card changes the ground, not the content; an empty footer draws no card. The destinations are the site setting’s.

An empty footer draws no card ⚑ — an empty raised rectangle is the failure this design has to avoid, and it is the same failure A25·3 names. Editor: the dashed rows inside a dashed card outline. A footer with one block still draws the card, because one block on a plane is composed and one block under a shadowless hairline is not.9 · Behaviour module. share, and member-form at Blocks Everything. Both edit-safe.10 · Accessibility. <footer> inside the <article>; the card is a div with no role and no label ⚑. Blocks keep the DOM order they are drawn in. The pair is <nav aria-label="Post navigation">.
