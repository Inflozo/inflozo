New in this pass ⚑, and A20’s hand-picked tags are the precedent. The shared P0·3 item controls: drag to reorder, ✕ to remove and never disabled, and Add arrives pre-filled with the next most-published writer, never blank. 1–12 references, not copies — a renamed author renames itself here, a deleted author drops out silently, and nothing about the author can be typed or overridden. Picked order is drawn order, which is why Order disables; the list is the count, which is why How many disables. At Hand-picked with more than one reference the first is the writer drawn, and the panel says so ⚑ — a founder page on a multi-author site, which order alone could not express.
How many
One▾
Three · Six · Twelve · All — the query limit. Disabled at Hand-picked, where the list is the count ⚑.
Order
Most posts▾
Most posts · Name A–Z · Newest account — count.posts desc, name asc, created_at desc. Ghost has no editorial order for authors ⚑. Disabled at Hand-picked.
Bio
From Ghost▾
From Ghost · Off. Reads {​{bio}​}; most Ghost authors have none ⚑, so absence is the normal case, not an empty state.
Role
Off▾
Ghost authors have no role field ⚑ — no job title, no desk. At Authored per writer the section keeps a role override keyed by author slug, which is A12’s Ghost-mode Role field, same mechanism ⚑: the workaround publications actually use, blessed rather than pretended away. Drawn in the meta line, ahead of the location — this is the design the override exists for.
SOCIAL HANDLES · GHOST ≥ 6.36 ⚑Nine handles and a website, not two: facebook · twitter (X) · linkedin · threads · bluesky · mastodon · tiktok · youtube · instagram · website. This corrects the category’s founding fact ⚑ — “two networks” was 5.x. Version-gated: on a site below 6.36 only Facebook, X and the website exist and the rest of the row is not drawn. The author’s own fields are read, never the sitewide fallback ⚑ — social_url falls back to the site’s Facebook and X, which would print the publication’s accounts under a writer who filled in neither. There is no loop over the handles: nine checks, and an absent handle reserves nothing.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow and, at Action label: Custom, the action label; the empty-state notice. actionUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker and defaults to the writer’s archive; the button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size and colour-role popover ⚑. The social row is nine icon slots and a website, each from the Icon Picker’s brand set ⚑. Author names, bios, counts, locations, websites and handles are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — the generated action label “Read all N posts”, with its number placeholder.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESFive controls, not six. A bio-length control was cut: the card is sized by its portrait, so clamping the bio at two lines leaves white space rather than saving any. The bio clamps at six lines and the panel does not pretend that is a choice ⚑.The action label is generated and then editable ⚑ — "Read all 68 posts" writes itself from the count and updates when the count changes, unless the user types over it, after which it is theirs. This is the first generated default in the library and it is flagged as a build behaviour, not a design one.Cut: a second action, an alignment value, a portrait radius, and a "featured writer" picker ⚑ — Ghost has no editorial order, so "featured" would mean "first by post count".
