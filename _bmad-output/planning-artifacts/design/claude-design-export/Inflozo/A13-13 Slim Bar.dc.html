Slim Bar

A 96 to 144 px band: a lead-in sentence, then three to five pills with a chevron between them. The shortest section in A13 and the only one with no heading of any kind — A12·11 Slim's position, carried, and for the same reason: a band this short is a caption on the page rather than a section of it.

It draws no body at any setting. A step here is a numeral, an optional 20 px glyph and a title, and nothing else — which makes it the design that answers “can you put the process at the top of the page in one line?” without inventing a shorter kind of step. The chevron is the one separator in A13 that is not a hairline, and A13-0's settlement 3 names it as the exception.

Padding 32 · 44 · 56 gives a band of 96 · 120 · 144 — A12·11's arithmetic, carried, because the pill is 40 px tall and the sentence is one line. A pill is 8 × 14 of padding on the pack's hover surface at the pack radius; numeral, glyph, title, at a 10 px gap, all on one line, and the title never wraps. The chevron is a 15 px glyph in border with 12 px each side. The lead-in is title at 15 px in text on a 220 measure — a sentence and not a heading, so there is no <h2> and the section has no accessible name at any setting.
Step 04 has no icon and draws its numeral in the glyph's place, at 15/600 in text-muted in a 16 px box — so that pill shows its figure twice, and both are kept, because the leading numeral is the pill's mark and the glyph's slot has to hold something or the pills stop lining up. It is the smallest numeral plate in A13. A title that would wrap is the design's one hard limit: at four steps a pill's title has about 22 characters of room, and the panel states the measured width rather than clamping the field. ⚑ Mine: the pill's padding and gaps, the chevron's size and colour, the doubled figure, and stating the width instead of truncating.

SEPARATOR NONE · A 20 PX GAP AND THE NUMERALS DO THE WORK
Separator: Chevron · Hairline · None. The chevron is the only non-hairline separator in A13 and it exists here because a horizontal band of pills is the one place a line between two items reads as a divider rather than as a join. It points forward, it is border-coloured and never accent, it is aria-hidden, and it is not an arrowhead on a connector, which A13-0 refuses — there is no line for it to sit on. ⚑ Mine, and it is settlement 3's one exception. Three to five steps and the ceiling is measured rather than chosen. Four pills at Filled with a lead-in and a link fill about 1,240 of the 1,296; five fit only with short titles. Six or more → 2 Track, named in the panel, which is this design with room for bodies. Below three → 1 Three Up: two pills and a chevron is a toggle. The pills wrap rather than scrolling. When the row runs out of width the last pill drops to a second line and the band grows 52 px, chevron and all — the one place in A13 a band changes height on its own. A horizontal scroll is refused here: 8 Rail is the design a reader moves, and a 120 px band that scrolls sideways hides steps behind an edge nobody sees. Rule and Width are one control between them and Width is the one kept. At Full bleed the band's background runs to the viewport and the content stays on 1,296; the band has a border-top and a border-bottom at both widths and always, because a band with no edges on the page ground is not a band. ⚑ Mine, and it is why this design has no Rule control where A12·11 had one.

Lead None with Pills Bare is the design at its quietest, and it is where the band centres. With no lead-in and no link the steps have nothing to sit against, so they centre in the band rather than sitting at the left — the one automatic alignment change in A13, and it is a consequence of two empty fields rather than a control. ⚑ Mine. With no title and no link authored, this section contains no interactive content and no tab stop at all, and it still has no accessible name. It is the least announced section in the library: a screen reader hears a list of four items and nothing that says what the list is. That is the stated cost of a design with no heading, and 2 Track is the neighbouring design that has one. In dark the pill's fill is the derived #2A251E and the band's borders take #332E27. At Pills Bare there is no fill at all and the band is four numerals, four titles and three hairlines — 96 px of type on a hairline ground, which is the smallest a section gets in this library.

At ≤ 767 it stops being a band at all: the pills go one per line at full width, the chevrons are dropped, and the section becomes a lead-in above a stack of four pills.
