The gate opened by a measurement: a 4 px meter across the measure, its reading time in words beneath, and the gate under that. The one design in A32 that states a quantity.

PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN The label is a static line from Ghost’s reading time; the fill is the module’s. As drawn, the label asked a template to count the words in the preview, which no template can do. The words are now one static line built from post.reading_time alone — “This piece is a 12 minute read” — and the proportional fill is drawn by reading-progress from the reader’s scroll, hidden on a very short post and hidden entirely with JavaScript off, where the line remains. {read} is deleted from the label; {total} is the only token. (Ghost’s templates cannot count, add or remember.) The fade at the cut is a fixed height, and the two-line rule is deleted. It is 96, 160 or 240 px of the page ground anchored to the end of the preview, whatever is above it. The old rule suppressed it “under two lines of visible text”, and a stylesheet cannot count lines ⚑ — so a one-line last paragraph would have faded to nothing. Suppression after a heading, a figure, a code block or an embed stays: that is an element type, which CSS can see. (CSS cannot see content.) The action is conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up window is JavaScript. The primary button does not render at all where the connected site cannot take it — self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected — and where it does render it opens Ghost’s own sign-up window, so with JavaScript off, nothing happens ⚑. The words, the fade, the measure and every drawn state are unchanged; with no button the gate is its words and the sign-in line ⚑. Both facts are now read-only rows in the panel and mono lines on every gate. (Member buttons are conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-up needs JavaScript.)

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE THREE STATES EVERY A32 DESIGN OWES
In the editor the gate draws at its resting state ⚑ — the paid gate, the fade at its control value, no hover and no ring. Nothing in A32 has a behaviour to preview, so the edit-time frame and the published frame are the same picture. With JavaScript off the section is drawn identically and every action in it is dead ⚑ — the gate is server-rendered HTML, the fade is a gradient and the suppression rules are :last-child selectors, but every action opens Ghost’s Portal, which is JavaScript. 1 Fade and 6 Split Pitch replace their email field with the no-JavaScript notice; 11 Sticky Bar hides its close button; 12 Meter keeps its static reading-time line and loses the fill — the label needs no script and the fill is the module’s. And no member button renders at all where the connected site cannot take it ⚑. A reader who has access never sees this section ⚑ — Ghost sends the whole post and A32 does not render. There is no “unlocked” state to design, and no design may draw one.

The meter is not interactive ⚑ and is not in the tab order. Hover does nothing to it; it is a reading, not a control, and a reader who drags it would expect the article to move.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe label is words, not a percentage, and that is the design’s one real opinion ⚑. “This piece is a 12 minute read” is checkable; a percentage invites the reader to argue with the arithmetic and a theme should not be doing arithmetic on a number it did not compute.Three defaults are shifted here and all three are stated: padding Compact, fade Short, and no eyebrow ⚑. Each is because the meter is about the paragraph above it rather than about the section.Segments is offered and is the value to be careful with ⚑ — twelve segments for a twelve-minute post is legible; forty-one for a forty-one-minute post is a hairline, so above 20 minutes the control resolves to Rule and the panel says so.
