10 · Lead and Rest

One topic at picture size on the left, every other topic as a ruled line on the right. The design that says a publication has a subject — and then admits, in the same section, that it has six others.

⚑ The lead is chosen by the order, not by hand: at Most posts first it is the biggest topic, at A–Z it is the first alphabetically. There is no "featured tag" picker anywhere in A20, because a tag is not an item the user authors.
