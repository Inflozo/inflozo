Columns

A brand block at the left margin, link columns filling the measure to the right, the legal line on its own row under a hairline. This is the design that settles the column rule for the whole category: how two columns behave, how four do, what happens at seven, and where they go at 390. Every other design with link columns inherits that rule unchanged.

At five or more columns the mobile stack becomes accordions, one per row, 44 px rows divided by hairlines, the count at the right end and the first group open. 1 · <footer role="contentinfo">, last landmark, one per page. The whole footer is outside <main>, so a skip-to-content link never lands in it. 2 · Lockup is one link named “Orbit Weekly, home”; the tagline beside it is plain text, not a heading. 3 · Each column is <nav aria-labelledby> pointing at its own <h2>, which is the visible heading — so the headings are real outline entries and each list is a named navigation region. Four columns means four small navs, which is correct: they are four different groupings, and a single unlabelled list of sixteen links is what we are avoiding. 4 · Legal is a <ul> labelled “Legal”, copyright as plain text in the first item, separators in CSS. Focus order · lockup, tagline links if any, columns left to right, social, legal. The accordion at ≤ 767 uses <button aria-expanded> inside the <h2>, so the heading stays in the outline whether the group is open or closed. Contrast · muted links on background 5.4:1 light, 6.4:1 dark; headings 12.1:1 light, 11.8:1 dark. Hit areas: 44 px at ≤ 767 via padding, 34 px minimum at desktop where a mouse is the input.

Responsive · empty · a11y · flagged 1440–1081: brand block 300 px fixed, columns 1fr each, 40 px gutters; two columns stay 1fr and leave the right quarter empty; five to seven put the brand block above a four-across wrapping grid. 1080–768: two columns across, brand block spanning at 260 px. ≤ 767: two to four columns become a two-across grid, open, links padded to 44 px; five to seven become accordions, one per row, first open, count at the right.

a11y: each column is a <nav> labelled by its visible <h2>; accordion headings keep their <h2> and use aria-expanded; focus order is reading order.
