Header, signup and CTA cards offer no colour controls — authors set those inline in Ghost's editor.

Two dropdowns in the template switcher's position: Treatment (the A33 whole-set style, one active per project) then Card (all twenty Koenig cards, Ghost's order). Per-card overrides sit on top of the treatment, which is why the panel shows treatment-decided values read-only. Colour controls are never offered for header, signup and CTA cards ⚑ — the post author sets their colours inline in Ghost's editor, and a control that silently loses to an inline style is worse than none; those three carry the note in the dropdown and in their panel. Flagged ⚑: this pass's brief lists "radius" as a callout control example, but A33's floor rules corners read-only from the pack token in every treatment — the frame follows A33 and shows Corners in the read-only block.

Reset is per card and scoped in words — the treatment choice and other cards' overrides are untouched. There is deliberately no "reset all cards": twenty decisions removed by one click is a footgun with no matching user intent.
