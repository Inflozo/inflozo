5 · Tag Chips

A centred field with a row of the publication’s own tags beneath it, and the results below that. The only design in A23 whose list is authored rather than queried.

It is the design for the reader who arrived without a word in mind. The chips are the publication saying: if you do not know what to type, these are the things we write about.

THE ITEM LIST · tagChips[] · THE CATEGORY’S ONLY AUTHORED LIST
These are the shared P0·3 item controls ⚑, named rather than redesigned. Add sits under the last chip as a full-width “Add a tag” row. A new chip arrives as the publication’s next most-used tag, already filled in ⚑, at the end of the order, never as a blank pill. Add is disabled at six and the row says why.Remove is an ✕ on the selected chip’s own row in the sidebar. Order is meaningful and is drag-reorderable ⚑ — it is a priority list, which is the whole reason the field exists rather than reading Ghost’s alphabetical order. Minimum two: remove the second-to-last and the section falls back to 1 Field and Results and the panel says so ⚑.Inside a chip the reader-visible content is a tag reference, not text ⚑ — the editable field is which tag, and the label is Ghost’s. A renamed tag renames the chip; a deleted tag drops it silently and the row count falls to one, then to none, then the section is 1 Field and Results ⚑. Zero chips renders the field alone, centred, exactly as 1.

The chips are the design’s empty state ⚑ — before a query they are the only thing under the field, which is why this design does not draw recent searches at all and the source group’s “Before a query” row is ignored ⚑. Typing dims the chips rather than removing them: a reader who mistyped needs the way out to still be there.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SEVEN ROWS · IDENTICAL WHEREVER RESULTS ARE DRAWN · NOT COUNTED
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
Match highlight
MarkedPlain
A real <mark> at 16 % accent, 22 % in dark, in the title and the excerpt and never in the meta ⚑. At Plain it is absent from the DOM rather than untinted. Promoted here from 11 Thumb Rows, so every design that draws a result governs it in the same row ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.
CONTENT · THE P0·3 ITEM CONTROLStagChips[] is the category’s only authored list ⚑ and takes the P0·3 controls verbatim: drag to reorder · per-row overflow with Duplicate and Remove · Add arrives with content (the next most-used tag, already filled in) · the range line reading 2–6 · 5 used. Inside a chip the only editable thing is which tag ⚑ — the label is Ghost’s, so a renamed tag renames the chip and a deleted tag drops it.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEyebrow, heading and note edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar. Tag names are Ghost’s and are not inline-editable ⚑ — clicking a chip’s label says “Edit in Ghost”; the chip’s own picker chooses which tag. countLabel, emptyHeading, emptyText and emptyLinkLabel are edited through the P0·6 switcher (No query · Results · No matches) ⚑. Chip targets open the Ghost-aware Link Picker only where a chip points at something other than its own tag route.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTChips are tag references and the results are Ghost’s posts: the chips are authored, the results never are ⚑. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — chips are navigation.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe chips are a list the user authors, and it is the only one in A23 ⚑. Everything else in this category comes from Ghost, which is why this is the only panel in the fifteen with an item row in it.A control writes one value onto the section. Chip style and Chip counts apply to all chips at once; there is no per-chip styling and asking for one is a request for two designs ⚑. Inside a chip the author picks a tag, and that is all.No “sort chips by post count” value. That is Ghost’s order, and the point of an authored list is that it is not Ghost’s order ⚑.Padding left for the trio’s Vertical spacing ⚑, and no Result meta row was added here — the chips and the count line are this design’s second signal, not the meta.
