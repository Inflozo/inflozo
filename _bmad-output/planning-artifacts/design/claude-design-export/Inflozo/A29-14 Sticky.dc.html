14 · Sticky

A full head, and under it a slim bar that pins to the top of the window as the reader scrolls the grid. The archive stays named after its name has scrolled away.

The bar is position: sticky and nothing else — no shrink, no fade, no scroll listener. It declares no behaviour module because it needs none, and that is the honest version of a sticky header.

ZERO POSTS · THE BAR RENDERS AND NEVER PINS — THERE IS NOTHING BELOW IT TO SCROLL ⚑ · IT IS NOT HIDDEN, BECAUSE THE ROUTE MAY GAIN POSTS WHILE OPEN

The pinned state is the resting state. Nothing changes on pin — same height, same ground, same hairline, no shadow appearing, no name shrinking — because every one of those needs a scroll listener, and a scroll listener needs a module ⚑. The registry's closest module is header-scroll, which belongs to A1's site header; A29 does not claim it. That is a finding for the architect, not a licence to name one.
