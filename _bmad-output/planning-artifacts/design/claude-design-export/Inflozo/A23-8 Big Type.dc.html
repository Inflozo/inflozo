WHAT THE DISPLAY LINE SETTLES
It is a

, not a heading ⚑. A reader’s query in the document outline would mean the outline changes on every keystroke. The section keeps a visually-hidden h2 reading “Search results”, so the outline is stable and the landmark is still named.The query is echoed, and echoed text is escaped ⚑ — the one place in A23 where reader input is rendered at all, let alone at 96 px. Named as a build requirement, not a design note: escape it, and cap the echo at 60 characters with an ellipsis.One display moment per section, spent here ⚑ — so this design draws no blurb at any value and holds its results at the ordinary 21 px title. A 96 px line above 28 px rows would be two display moments.Without JavaScript the display line is the heading, not the query ⚑ — the earlier claim that the route renders the submitted query into the line is struck: a Ghost template cannot read ?q=. The mechanism is the module’s, and the no-JS floor is the heading, the field and the archive link.

The display line has three jobs and one slot: the heading before a query, the query while there is one, and the query again in the no-match state — where the no-match block drops its own heading because the word is already 96 px tall ⚑. A long query steps the size down rather than wrapping ⚑: 96 → 76 → 56 by character count, so the line never becomes a paragraph.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SEVEN ROWS · IDENTICAL WHEREVER RESULTS ARE DRAWN · NOT COUNTED
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
Match highlight
MarkedPlain
A real <mark> at 16 % accent, 22 % in dark, in the title and the excerpt and never in the meta ⚑. At Plain it is absent from the DOM rather than untinted. Promoted here from 11 Thumb Rows, so every design that draws a result governs it in the same row ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESThe eyebrow and the heading edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — and the heading is what the display slot draws before a query, so it is edited at display size, in place ⚑. The reader’s query is not editable content at all ⚑: it is reader input, escaped and capped at 60 characters. emptyText, emptyLinkLabel, countLabel, recentLabel and suggestLabel are edited through the P0·6 switcher (No query · Results · No matches) ⚑, which is the only way to reach them — they never show at rest.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTGhost posts and pages by scope: no Add, no Remove, no drag ⚑. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — no CTA in this design.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESNo excerpt control ⚑ — the display line is the emphasis, and rows with excerpts under a 96 px word make the section two competing weights. The rows are title and meta, always.No alignment control. The line is flush left at every value: a centred 96 px word above a 520 px field centres nothing ⚑, because the two objects have no shared axis.Four controls, one fewer than before. Padding retired into the trio’s Vertical spacing; Match highlight moved into the source group; no Result meta row was added here ⚑ — with a 96 px line above them the rows keep one meta grammar and the display slot does the differentiating.The substitution stays unconfigurable ⚑ — heading before a query, query after it. Making it optional would leave a design with no reason to exist.
