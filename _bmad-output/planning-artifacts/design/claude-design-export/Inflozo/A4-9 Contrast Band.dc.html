Contrast Band

The whole hero on the inverted ground, no picture. Ground is not a control here: it is the design, exactly as it is in A3·5. What this one settles for A4 is the accent question — whether a pack's accent may be spent on a button standing on the contrast token — and it is the design that shows the library's disabled-with-its-ratio rule in use.

Padding is one step larger than the light-ground designs at every value — 44 / 64 / 88 against 64 / 96 / 132 in the ladder — which sounds backwards and is not: an inverted band needs less air to feel substantial, and at 96 px it starts to feel like a page rather than a band. That is A3·5's rule inverted for the top of the page, where the band has a header above it rather than a whole document. This control is A1·10 Contrast Band's, carried forward verbatim — the same three values, the same disabled state and the same sentence in the sidebar — because a site that puts A1·10 above A4·9 is looking at one inverted band, not two. A1·10 measured Paper's accent at 3.1:1 for its carried label on the band and disabled the value there; that measurement stands and is not re-taken here. So on a contrast ground the primary action is the pack's lightest surface or an outline, and never accent, which is also what A4-0's category rules, A4·1 and A4·8 say. Nothing here is mine — the one A4 addition is that Outline's primary border sits at 72% against the secondary's 28%, so the two are still ranked.

1 · Carried values are fixed, not chosen: 72% for the eyebrow, sub and note in light and 70% in dark. They are opacities on the carried text colour rather than separate greys, so a pack change cannot leave a hard-coded grey behind. 2 · No color-scheme switch inside the band. A band that flipped the browser's own form colours would break any field a later design puts on it — A3·5's rule, and it matters more here because 11 Subscribe puts fields on a hero. 3 · Hover and focus use the pack's lightest surface, never accent. The focus ring's inner offset is the band itself, so the ring reads as two rings rather than a halo, and it measures 13.9:1 light and 13.2:1 dark against the ground it sits on. The disabled value · Accent is disabled in Paper at 3.1:1 on the band, with the ratio shown rather than the option hidden, so a user who has seen accent used elsewhere knows why they cannot have it here. This is A1·10's behaviour and A1·10's number, carried forward unchanged; nothing is silently substituted, and the section never picks a colour the pack did not give it. Contrast · headline 13.9:1 light / 13.2:1 dark · carried sub 7.6:1 / 7.1:1 · primary label on the light surface 13.9:1 / 13.2:1 · secondary label 13.9:1 with a decorative 28% border · . Forced colours · the band's ground is dropped by the system and the text lands on the system's own background; nothing in the design depends on the band being dark, which is why no element here is defined only by its opacity against it.
