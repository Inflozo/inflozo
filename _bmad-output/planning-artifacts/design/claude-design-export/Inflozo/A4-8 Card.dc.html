The card at restno hover, no lift, not focusable
The card has no interactive state of any kind. It does not lift on hover, it is not a link, it takes no tabindex, and its shadow never changes. A hero-sized panel that responds to the pointer is a trap — the shape invites the click and there is nowhere for it to go — and A3·12 refused it at the foot of the page for the same reason.

1 · The card is the <section> and the inset is padding on its wrapper, not a second element with a role. It has no role, no tabindex, no aria-label, and no click handler — a card shape invites a whole-panel link and the answer is no, at the top of the page as at the foot of it. 2 · Focus rings sit on the card's own surface, so the ring's 2 px offset is white here rather than the page's background — the ring reads at the same strength on both grounds because the offset takes whatever it sits on. On the Contrast card the ring is the pack's lightest surface, never accent. 3 · The hairline is drawn at every setting, including where the shadow already separates the card, because under forced colours the shadow disappears and the hairline is the whole edge. Same reason as A3·12, same rule. Contrast · headline on the card 12.6:1 light and 11.4:1 dark · sub and note 5.1:1 / 6.1:1 · the card's edge against the page 1.4:1 light and 1.3:1 dark, decorative both times. On the Contrast card: headline 13.9:1, carried sub 7.6:1. Zoom · the card grows with its content and the inset holds, so at 200% the frame is proportionally thinner and nothing is clipped. There is no fixed height anywhere in the design.
