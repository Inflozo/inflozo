Contrast Band

1 Split's arrangement on the pack's contrast colour. Same measures, same proportion, same components — one token different, and that is the point: it is the pair A19-0 uses to show what the tuple's ground slot is for.

A site reaches for it to break a long page. A featured post on the page ground sits in the flow; the same post on an inverted band stops it, which is worth doing once on a page and never twice. Everything on the band — muted text, hairline, plate — is derived from the band's own two colours by A17·7's mix rather than picked, so the design survives a pack that has no on-contrast values of its own.
