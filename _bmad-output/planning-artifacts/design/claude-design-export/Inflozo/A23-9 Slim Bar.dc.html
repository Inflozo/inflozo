9 · Slim Bar

One line: a label, a field, and three tag links at the right. The only design in A23 that renders no results of its own — it hands the query to the search route and gets out of the way.

It is the strip a publication puts between two sections of a long index page, or under an archive header, where a full search section would be a second page inside the first.

WHAT THE HANDOFF SETTLES
A section may end at the query ⚑ — 9 Slim Bar and 12 Cover both do. The spec names the destination rather than inventing a truncated results list, and the editor warns when no search route exists, exactly as A4·15’s panel does.Three tag links, not chips ⚑ — A17·15’s pill is 36 px and would make the bar 88. These are plain links at 14 px in the muted token, and they are to Ghost routes, so they work with everything off. Which three is now a stated selection ⚑: Trailing tags — Most posts, or two to three hand-picked tag references on the category’s own chips pattern with the P0·3 controls.No results means no live region and no count ⚑. A34’s rule: the navigation announces itself. This design has the smallest accessibility surface in the category and that is the point of it.

Four states, not seven ⚑ — before a query, typing, the browser’s own load, and no-JS, which is identical to the first. The other three are the destination’s: results, one result and no matches all happen on the search route, drawn by whichever design that route carries. This is the handoff A1·11 established, used deliberately and named here.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SIX ROWS · NO MATCH HIGHLIGHT: THIS DESIGN DRAWS NO RESULTS ⚑
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESThe label and the placeholder edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — they are the only visitor-facing strings this design draws. Tag names are Ghost’s ⚑ — “Edit in Ghost”; at Hand-picked the row chooses which tag, never its text. Every result and empty-state string is stored and not drawn, so the P0·6 switcher offers this design no states ⚑ — it has one. The form’s aria-label at Label: Hide is @site.title, Ghost’s own, plain-text-locked.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTGhost tags for the trailing links and nothing else ⚑. At Most posts the order is Ghost’s; at Hand-picked it is the author’s. Fewer than three tags → those that exist; none → the trailing group is absent and the field takes the room. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — a search strip is not a CTA.
