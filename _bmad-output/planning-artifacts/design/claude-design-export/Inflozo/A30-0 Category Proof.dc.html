A30 Members Pages — the category

Thirteen full-page membership designs. Each one draws three routes: the signup page, the sign-in page, and the account page — one design, one field list, three arrangements, because a publication picks a design and gets all three.

The category’s boundary is the thing to read first. Signup and sign-in are themeable; the account page is display-only; everything that changes a subscription is Ghost’s Portal. Every frame in the category carries that line, and no control in any design moves it.

This frame carries what belongs to the category rather than to any one design: the four questions §8 asked, the tokenisation proof, the stress frames, the roster with its thirteen structural descriptors, the shared field list, the component inventory and the findings for the architect. The per-design frames are A30-1 … A30-13, and the written specification is A30 Members Pages - Spec.md.

THE FOUR QUESTIONS §8 ASKED, ANSWERED
1 · WHICH PAGES ARE THEMEABLE, AND WHICH ARE PORTAL’S
Themed: /signup/, /signin/ and /account/ — Ghost pages on custom-signup.hbs, custom-signin.hbs and custom-account.hbs ⚑. A30 draws all three, and each frame in the category names which one it is. Portal’s: Stripe checkout, plan changes, billing and card detail, email changes, newsletter preferences, and the account panel Ghost opens from its own floating button. None of it is themeable and none of it is drawn here ⚑ — the theme draws the trigger and Portal draws the rest, over the page. So the account page is a summary with hand-offs, and that is a platform fact rather than a design choice ⚑. Every action on it that would write something is an outline button with PORTAL ↗ beside it, in all thirteen designs. And Portal is JavaScript ⚑ (patch pass). Every button that opens it — every hand-off on the account page, every tier deep link, every Sign up that raises Ghost’s own pop-up — does nothing with JavaScript off, and the theme cannot substitute anything for it. The line is on every account page’s Portal note and in every panel. Paid tier cards and their deep links are conditional too ⚑: they render only where a payment provider is connected, and Ghost seeds a $5 tier at install, so an unconnected site would otherwise ship a card whose checkout cannot complete.
2 · THE FORM STATES, INCLUDING SENT AND EXPIRED
Eight states, drawn by every design: sign-in · sent · expired · invalid · focus · already-signed-in · no-JavaScript. Sending is an eighth and is the button’s label change alone ⚑. Sent replaces the field in place with a panel carrying the address back and “Send another link” ⚑ — the address the visitor typed, never a field Ghost hands the theme; never a disabled field with a message under it. Expired is read from the query string (?action=signin&success=false), so it is JS-only ⚑, and without JavaScript P0·4’s notice stands in the form’s place ⚑, which is the same recovery in one step fewer. Invalid is the browser first — type="email" — then Ghost’s reply; no theme-side domain checking ⚑. A signed-in member never sees a signup form: the route draws the membership summary instead ⚑.
3 · THE ACCOUNT PAGE FOR FREE, PAID AND COMPED
Paid: email address (handed off) · plan and price · renews · payment (Stripe) · newsletters (a link out). Member since is gone and so is the newsletter list ⚑ — Ghost’s member object gives a theme neither, so both rendered empty on every live site. Free: the same list without price, renewal or payment — four rows, and the absent rows are absent, not empty ⚑. Comped: plan reads “Member · complimentary” with no billing hand-off at all ⚑, because there is nothing to bill. Cancelled but still running is the fourth member every account page meets ⚑ — “Renews” becomes “Ends”, the action becomes Resume, and access is stated as full until the date. And a fifth visitor, drawn in the patch pass: someone signed out ⚑ — Ghost serves /account/ to an anonymous reader exactly as it stands, so every design draws a signed-out panel and no member row renders. cancel_at_period_end is the flag, and it is the one subscription field a theme must read rather than print. No card detail ever reaches a theme ⚑, so “Payment · Managed by Stripe” is the whole row. Every design draws these rows; only their arrangement differs, which is what makes switching designs safe.
4 · THE A1·14 ASSUMPTION · TIER AND RENEWAL DATE ARE READABLE
Confirmed, with one qualification. A1·14’s member pill assumed a theme can read the member’s tier name and renewal date. The tier name is readable ⚑ — it is on the member’s subscription — and so is the current period’s end date, which is what “renews on” means. The qualification: “renews” and “ends” are the same field ⚑. Which word is correct depends on cancel_at_period_end, so a theme that prints “Renews” unconditionally will lie to every cancelling member — the commonest members-page defect, and the reason this is a settlement rather than a note. Also readable: status (free · paid · comped), price and interval, newsletter subscriptions, join date. Not readable: card detail, invoices, Stripe customer state, and the number of days left ⚑ — that is arithmetic a theme should not do on a date it did not compute.

TUPLE UNIQUENESS · THE HONEST STATEMENT
All thirteen are distinct on the five closed slots ⚑, and two slots do most of the work. Containment separates 1, 3 and 10 — none, card, box — and ground separates 1, 4 and 5 — page, surface, contrast. Those five designs are deliberately the same components five ways, which is the case the ground and containment slots exist for. Media separates the two picture designs: background in 6 Cover against edge in 7 Image Split. Item-count separates the three that repeat something — few in 8 Tiers and 11 Rail, many in 12 Ledger — and archetype separates those two fews. Containment is none in ten of thirteen ⚑. 8 Tiers’ cards and 13 Steps’ tier boxes are the items’ geometry, not the section’s — A21·2’s rule, and the slot most easily got wrong.
WHAT THE CHECK CANNOT PROMISE
9 Big Type and 12 Ledger are both stacks on the page ground and are separated by item-count alone; what actually distinguishes them is scale against enumeration, and no machine reads that. 2 Split Pitch and 7 Image Split are the same split with prose or a photograph in the second column. 13 Steps and 4 Panel are both forms on a plane — the tuple separates them on archetype and item-count, and the emphasis phrase carries the rest. 1 Centred and 3 Card are one design in two containments, which is a real and visible difference at rest and a thin one written down. Each design’s panel names the ones it is closest to, by number ⚑, and says which control value would turn it into its neighbour. That is the honest version of a uniqueness claim.

FINDINGS FOR THE ARCHITECT · FOURTEEN · FOUR NEW IN THE PATCH PASS
A themed members page is a Ghost page on a custom template, not a route ⚑. There is no /signup/ route in Ghost: the publication creates pages and the theme supplies custom-signup.hbs, custom-signin.hbs and custom-account.hbs. Ghost’s Portal will intercept membership links unless it is configured not to, so the generator has to write those pages and set Portal’s links — a product step, not a design one.The 24-hour magic-link expiry and “works once” are asserted from Ghost’s member auth behaviour ⚑, and both appear in reader-facing copy (sentText, expiredText). If the interval differs, the strings are fields and change without a redesign — which is why they are fields.“Renews” and “Ends” are one date and two words. cancel_at_period_end decides which ⚑. Confirm a theme can read that flag: if it cannot, every design in A30 must drop the word and print the date alone, and thirteen frames change.Portal can be opened at a named panel — account/plans, account/newsletters, account/profile — and 11 Rail is wired to all three ⚑. Answered in the reconciliation pass: this was the open question the rail’s two rows assumed, and the rows now name their targets. The remaining dependency is product, not theme — Portal has to be configured not to intercept the themed pages.Ghost gives a theme no member join date and no list of a member’s newsletters ⚑ — new. Both were drawn in every account design and both rendered empty on every live site. Member since is deleted; the newsletter row is a link out to Ghost’s own account screen. Nothing replaces them, and no field was invented to carry them.Ghost does not redirect an anonymous visitor away from /account/ ⚑ — new. The page is served exactly as it stands, so every design now draws a signed-out state. The previous spec said Ghost redirects before the theme renders; it does not.Ghost’s signup endpoint will not accept a plain form submission ⚑ — new, tested against both live servers. The library’s "the form posts natively" line is withdrawn in all thirteen designs and P0·4’s notice replaces the form. Sending, sent, expired and invalid are Ghost’s own script and are unchanged.A paid tier card is only renderable where a payment provider is connected ⚑ — new. Ghost seeds a $5 tier at install, so an unconnected site would ship a card whose checkout cannot complete. 8 Tiers and 13 Steps hide the cards, the toggle and every deep link; the panel says why.
A tier’s benefit list may not be exposed to themes in the shape 8 Tiers draws ⚑ — name, price and description are certain; benefits are the doubt.
