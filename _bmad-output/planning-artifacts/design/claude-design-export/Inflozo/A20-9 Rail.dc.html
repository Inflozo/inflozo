EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading and the introduction. Tag names and counts are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. The arrows’ labels — “Previous topics” and “Next topics” — are theme translation-catalog strings ⚑, not authored fields: they are read by assistive technology only and belong with the site’s language, not with this section. A20 has no authored image field ⚑ — the pictures are Ghost’s feature_image, so rule 10’s Image focus has nothing to attach to and the crop stays centred; a focus control here would be a fake one.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe Controls field names what JavaScript adds, not what the reader needs. With the module absent the strip is a native overflow-x scroll-snap track — drag, swipe, shift-scroll and keyboard all work. The registry's wording is the acceptance criterion and is quoted in the spec ⚑.No autoplay, and no loop. A rail of topics that moves on its own is a rail nobody can read, and A19·15 refused the same thing for the same reason.Cell width changes how many tiles are visible and never how many are fetched. Show in the Data group is the only count ⚑.
