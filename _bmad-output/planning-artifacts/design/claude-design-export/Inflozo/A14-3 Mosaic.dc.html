WHY THIS IS A DESIGN AND NOT A CONTROL ON 1 GRID
A “make one frame bigger” control would be per-item styling ⚑, which A14 does not have anywhere, by construction. Making it a design instead means the rule is structural: the first image is the lead, always, in every pack and at every count.The lead is the first image, so reordering is how you choose it ⚑ — drag a frame to the top of the images block and it becomes the lead. The panel says this in the images block's help line rather than adding a “Lead image” picker.Lead position is a mirror, not a size ⚑ — Left or Right, and both are the same tile flipped. It is the one control here that is purely visual.

The mosaic needs three images to exist ⚑. At 1 it draws the lead alone at 856 × 648 — the tile does not collapse to a full-width frame, because that is 10 Lead and Grid. At 2 the lead keeps its size and one cell sits beside it, the second cell left empty rather than stretched ⚑. At 4 and 5 the bottom row is short and left-aligned; at 7 or more the extra frames continue in rows of three under it ⚑.
