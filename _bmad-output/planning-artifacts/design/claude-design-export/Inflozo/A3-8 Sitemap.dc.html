Compact closes the link rhythm 9 → 6 px and the row gap 32 → 24, and nothing else: type sizes are the same, because 13 px is the pack's floor for a tracked heading and 14 px is the floor for a link. Seven columns at Compact is 208 px of grid — a sitemap that reads as one block rather than seven lists, which is why Comfortable is the default and Compact is for sites that genuinely have this much. The seventh column here is a legal group, which does not remove the legal line: the line is still the footer's own and still carries the copyright.

ACCESSIBILITY · annotated · mobile 390 · the accordion is what needs annotating

1 · Each group heading stays an <h2> at every width; at ≤ 767 it contains a <button aria-expanded aria-controls>. The heading is in the outline whether the group is open or closed, so the page's structure does not change with a tap. 2 · The list inside is the same <nav aria-labelledby> markup as the desktop column — one structure, two presentations. A closed group is hidden, not merely clipped, so its links are out of the tab order. 3 · The count is decorative and aria-hidden: the number of links is announced by the list itself. The row is a 48 px target — heading plus count plus padding — and the whole row is the button, not just the word. Motion · opening is a 160 ms height transition; under reduced-motion the group appears with no transition and the scroll position is held, so a group opening below the viewport does not move what is being read. Desktop · identical to A3·2's structure: six <nav>s labelled by their visible headings, focus order left to right then the legal line. Six small navs is correct; one unlabelled list of 28 links is not. Contrast · 14 px links at 5.4:1 light and 6.4:1 dark — the smallest type in the category, and the reason 14 px is the floor rather than 13.

DECLARED WIDTH · “COLLAPSES INTO SECTIONS UNDER 768” · UNCONDITIONAL — FIVE TO SEVEN GROUPS IS THIS DESIGN’S FLOOR Without JavaScript, at 768 and above: no accordion exists — five to seven columns across at 1440, three-across in authored order at 1080, every heading and every link open and server-rendered. Both Density values are CSS. Without JavaScript, below 768: native <details> throughout: first group server-open, 48 px rows on hairlines, counts static and aria-hidden, links back up to 15 px, Density not applied. Every link is reachable; only the 160 ms height transition is lost. Unlike A3·2 the condition is not a column count. This design never draws fewer than five groups, so the width alone decides, and the declaration carries no “and only at …” clause.
