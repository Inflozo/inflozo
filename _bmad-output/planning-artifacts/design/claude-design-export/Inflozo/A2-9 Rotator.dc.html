Rotator

The same message list as Ticker, one at a time, with dots at the right end. Nothing moves horizontally: a message crossfades into the next every few seconds, and pressing a dot stops the rotation for the rest of the session. It is the quieter answer to having three things to say, and on surface it is the only multi-message bar that does not read as a news channel.

Under prefers-reduced-motion there is no auto-advance and no crossfade: message 1, dots, and an instant swap when a dot is pressed. Indicators: None is overridden to Dots in that case — with no rotation and no dots there would be no way to reach messages 2 and 3 at all. This is the state that makes Ticker and Rotator identical for these readers, flagged in A2-0.

The stop mechanism, and my reading of it Auto-updating content needs a way to pause, stop or hide it. Here that is: hover pauses, focus pauses, pressing a dot stops the rotation for the session, and the close hides the bar entirely. Flagged: treating the dots as the stop control is my interpretation — it is discoverable and it is what readers already reach for, but it is not a labelled “pause” button like Ticker's. If you would rather Rotator carried an explicit pause, it costs the Indicators control.

The inactive two are visually hidden but present and readable — not display:none — so a screen-reader user gets all three announcements in order without waiting for a rotation. aria-live="off"; the crossfade is never announced. 3 · Each message carries its own link with its own visible text. Only the active message's link is focusable, so tabbing never lands on something invisible; the hidden messages' links are removed from the tab order and their text remains readable. 4 · Dots are buttons: “Show announcement 2 of 3”, with aria-current on the active one. 7 px dot in a 24 px target at desktop (17 px gap, 24 px pitch), 32 px wide at ≤ 767 (25 px gap) — under the 44 px floor, stated on that frame, with swipe as the alternative. SPEC · A2·9 ROTATOR · full text in “A2 Announcement Bars — Spec.md”
Content fields messages[] (list of { message ≤ 120, messageShort ≤ 48, linkLabel ≤ 24, linkUrl }, req, 2–6 items — the same list Ticker uses, so switching between the two keeps everything) · dismissible · audience · dismissMemory. Every message must fit one centred line above 767; the editor measures the longest against the available width rather than letting one item wrap where the others do not.
Controls · data Interval: Slow 8 s · Medium 6 s · Fast 4 s. Transition: Crossfade · Slide up · None. Indicators: Dots · Numbers · None (None requires Interval: Slow; only Dots can be pressed to stop the rotation, and the sidebar says so). Background role (universal): Surface · Contrast · Background. Vertical spacing (universal): Compact 44 · Comfortable 52 · Spacious 64. No Placement control — this design is In flow only: a bar that changes its text every six seconds should not follow the reader down the page. Universal trio outside the list: Background role · Vertical spacing · Top divider (locked). Visibility gains Schedule (Always · From · Until · Between dates) and Shows on (All pages · Home only · Posts · Pages); Preview as is removed — previewing is the editor’s View as.
Data: member state only. Fewer than two messages → renders as A2·1 Rule with item 1.
Responsive · motion · a11y · flagged 1440–768 centred message, dots at the right end, side padding 200 → 120, short forms used where authored. ≤ 767 message left-aligned, dots below at 25 px spacing in 32 px-wide targets, link label dropped and the whole bar becomes the link; bar 95 px, or 66 px with Numbers, which sits inline as “1/3”. Swipe advances at every mobile width. Motion: rotation pauses on hover and on focus entering the bar, stops for the session when a dot is pressed, and does not run at all under prefers-reduced-motion, where dots are forced on and the swap is instant. Height is fixed by the control, never by the longest message.
a11y: all messages in the DOM and readable, only the active one focusable, dots are buttons with aria-current, aria-live off.
Flagged as mine: the 32 px-wide mobile dot target (under the 44 px floor, with swipe as the alternative), reading the dots as the WCAG 2.2.2 stop mechanism, the dark inactive-dot value #453E35 that no pack defines, the 160 ms crossfade ceiling, and dropping the Placement control.
