EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: eyebrow, heading, blurb, ctaLabel, note, legal, benefits[], imageAlt and every state string. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the member's name and email, the tier name, the price, the renewal date, the newsletter names and a tier's description and benefits all answer "Edit in Ghost" or show P0·1's lock pill. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, Portal actions first. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the rescued literals above are fields with defaults, and the badge vocabulary and sending word are catalog strings. There is no Preview control in this panel and there never was one to remove — states are P0·6's switcher, audiences are View as.benefits[] is the P0·3 authored list ⚑ — drag to reorder, per-row overflow with Duplicate and Remove, Add arrives with content rather than an empty shell, Remove is never disabled, and the range line reads 0–6 · 4 used. Add disables at six with its reason in a sentence. Per-item content only: a name and a detail edit inline, and no per-item layout control exists ⚑. Tiers are the other list and are Ghost’s — no Add, no Remove, no drag.The one icon slot is the primary button’s ⚑ — P0·2's button-icon rules: Before or After the label, always Small, always the label's colour, its Size and Colour rows hidden. The list marker is the second where a design draws ticks, and it is whole-list.Member Visibility lands nowhere in A30 ⚑, and it is a judgement rather than an omission: the route is the member state. A signed-in member never sees a signup form — the Data group's "A signed-in member on this page" decides what they see instead — so a section-level Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid row would hide a page from readers the route has already sorted. P0·4’s member-aware action editor never opens here for the same reason. Recorded in the Reconciliation notes.
