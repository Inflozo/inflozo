Thumb Rows

A17's card laid on its side: a small picture at the left of every row, the title beside it, the date at the far end. Three related posts in 300 px of page instead of 500.

It is the design for the end of a long article, where a row of tall cards restarts the page and a picture-free list looks like a footnote. The thumbnail is the only picture size in A27 that survives a phone unchanged — which is the argument for it and the reason its collapse rule is one line long.

Thumbnail has two values and neither is smaller than 96 ⚑ — below that a 3:2 crop is 64 px wide, which is a favicon of a photograph. Square is offered because a square crop survives a portrait photograph; 16:9 is not, because at 128 wide it is 72 tall and the row is then taller than its picture.
The row is one link and one target, 133 px at Medium and 101 at Small, and the picture is inside the link rather than beside it — there is no second tab stop per row anywhere in A27.
