WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A gap control is only worth a slot where the gap is the design. Two designs in A24 have one — this and 6 Edge to Edge — and in both the space between a photograph and a title is the whole relationship between them. Everywhere else the gaps are fixed by the floor, because a user given a spacing control for every element will produce a page with fourteen different rhythms in it. The panel names the other design rather than hiding the overlap. "Without a picture this is 1 Centred" is written in the Feature image row, and this is the pattern for every A24 design whose distinction depends on a field the post may not have: say which design it becomes, name it, and let the user decide whether to switch permanently. Cut: an image-height control in pixels. The ratio ladder already sets the height and does it in a way that survives a re-crop; a pixel height plus a ratio is two controls fighting over one number, and the loser is always the picture. Cut: a caption-position control, again — the caption's position is this design's departure, not the user's. Offering "under the picture" would put grey type in the gap the whole design is built around.

Ghost finding · the feature-image caption renders differently on the two Ghost versionsReaches this design. The caption is Ghost's post.feature_image_caption, rendered as HTML, and the two supported versions do not render it alike: Ghost 6 strips <em> and <strong>, Ghost 5 keeps them, links and <b> survive on both. So no caption here may depend on italics or bold — the photographer's credit link, which is what the field exists for, is safe on either.

Recorded because the shape is easy to get wrong: the count's element is never created without JavaScript, Ghost writes the number in with a script and prepends it to a bare noun, and a design that reads it aloud labels the surrounding element rather than the count.
