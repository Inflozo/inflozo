15 · Filter Bar

The topics as a bar of chips on a raised surface, with the current one filled in accent and an All chip at the head of it. The design that sits directly above a post grid and says what the grid is showing.

It declares filter-strip, the module that needs JavaScript least of all: every chip is a real link to a Ghost tag archive, so the bar works completely with the script absent. A17·15 Filtered draws the same strip inside its own section; ⚑ this is the standalone version, for a grid that does not carry one.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESSticky was cut. A bar that follows the reader down a tag archive is a different component with its own scroll behaviour, and the registry's header-scroll belongs to A1. That is a finding, not a control ⚑.The active chip is decided by the route, never by the user ⚑ — Ghost knows which tag archive is being viewed. On a home page no chip is active except All.This bar filters by navigating, not by hiding cards. The grid below it is a fresh page from Ghost, which is why the module's no-JS behaviour is not a degradation at all.
