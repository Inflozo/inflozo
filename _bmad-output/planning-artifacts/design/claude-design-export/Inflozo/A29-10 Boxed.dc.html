A hairline box on the page ground, the head centred inside it. No fill, no shadow — the box is a boundary, not a surface, and the page shows through it.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESReconciled. Padding renamed Box padding ⚑ — the box's inside is a different ladder from the space around a section — and the universal Vertical spacing now owns that outside space, which this design had fixed at 96 with no control ⚑. Eyebrow: Show · Hide added; catalog zero count; Content and Data groups drawn. Seven controls of its own.This design inverts 4 Panel's padding rule, and the inversion is the point: here the space inside the box is what the user is judging, and the space around it is fixed. In Panel the plane's inside is fixed and the space around it moves ⚑.No Fill control. A filled box is 4 Panel; offering the fill here would make two designs one and break the tuple's ground slot ⚑. The panel says which design to switch to, in the hint under Alignment.No Corners control. Radius comes from the pack token and a Square value would be the one place in 485 designs where a section overrode it ⚑.
