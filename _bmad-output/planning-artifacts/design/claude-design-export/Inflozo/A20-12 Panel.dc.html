WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no fill control and no shadow control. With a fill this design is 3 Cards; with a shadow it is a card floating on the page. The box is a hairline and stays one ⚑.Columns fill down then across and are never balanced by height ⚑ — a name that wraps to two lines makes its column one line taller than the others, which is correct, because balancing would put topic seven in a different place every time an editor renames topic two.The hand-off is not a control and cannot be turned off ⚑. The threshold is four.
