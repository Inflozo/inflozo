A thumbnail list that grows in place: a batch of rows, a button, another batch appended below it. One of the two designs in A18 that declare a module — load-more, from the fixed registry, beside 5 Grouped’s group-headings — and the only one that appends content: how much of the feed a reader sees is decided by the reader rather than by the site.

The button, its three states and its no-JS branch are A17·16's, reused verbatim. What is new here is what it appends to: rows rather than cells, so a batch boundary is invisible in a way it never is in a grid — a new row simply continues the list, where a new grid row starts a new line.

The button is centred, 45 px tall, 24 px radius, in one of A17·16's three styles. Outline is the default here as there: a solid accent button at the foot of a list of links is the loudest thing on the page and it is not the page's purpose. Under it, “5 of 15” — a count rather than a page number, because a growing list has no pages.
The rows are 1 Rows' rows with 2 Thumb Rows' smallest thumbnail. Nothing new is drawn: 96 × 64 at the leading edge, 20 px gap, text to 760, meta hung at 200. The picture is here because a batch boundary needs something to make the list feel continuous — a run of pictures reads as one column whatever the batches, where a run of bare titles can look like two lists stacked.
The button sits 44 px below the last row's rule, which is 4 px more than the gap between the head and the list. It belongs to the list rather than to the section, and the space says so; at Rule Boxed the last row's rule is the line it hangs from.
⚑ Show means the whole set here, not the drawn count. Every other design in A18 draws Show rows; this one draws Batch rows and holds the rest.
