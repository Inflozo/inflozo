THE PICTURE, AND WHAT IT COSTS
No image authored → the design hands off to 5 Panel ⚑, drawn in place, flagged in the editor, invisible on the site. A22’s only hand-off besides 7 Cover’s.The alt is drawn as an alt ⚑ — the editor asks what the picture adds rather than defaulting it to the heading. A6·8’s rule, carried.Picture side is grid placement, never a source reorder ⚑. Words come first in the DOM at both values.

The picture never changes with the state ⚑ — it is authored content, not feedback. The words column holds 118 px across all seven states, and because the picture’s height follows the words at 1440, a submit does not resize the photograph.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label and the note, and the picture’s alt text in the Image Picker — 6 draws a real alt, not an empty one. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. The newsletter is chosen in the markup too ⚑ — data-members-newsletter, its value the letter’s name, not its id; errors land in a data-members-error child, which is the invalid state’s own slot. Ghost’s form creates a free member and never asks for payment ⚑ — checked against Ghost’s theme documentation, 28 August 2026. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. There is no no-JavaScript floor ⚑ corrected in this pass — /members/api/send-magic-link/ refuses a plain POST, tested against both live Ghost servers, so the form is replaced by P0·4’s no-JavaScript notice at the same height: “Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.” The sent, error and loading states are untouched. The ask is conditional on the connected site ⚑ — it does not render with self-signup switched off or members disabled, and a paid ask needs a payment provider connected; any button that opens Ghost’s own sign-up pop-up does nothing with JavaScript off.

1 · Descriptor. A photograph holding one half of a surface plane and the ask the other, the picture bleeding to three of the plane’s edges. The only design in A22 with an authored image and a drawn alt.2 · Structural descriptor. split · none · surface · none · left · a photograph holding one half

6 · Controls. Picture side Left · Right — Split Even · Wide picture · Wide words — Picture treatment Bleed · Inset — Image focus Centre · Top · Bottom ⚑ new — Plane Raised · Flat — Field Stacked · In a row (In a row disabled at Wide picture ⚑) — Social proof Off · Member count ⚑ new. Seven. Padding is retired into Vertical spacing; Background role is locked at Surface ⚑. As 1. No image authored → hands off to 5 Panel ⚑ — the plane and the words survive, and the editor flags it. Members off → the picture stays and the words half holds the button-as-link ⚑.8 · Empty state. No blurb → the words column shortens and, at 1440, the picture shortens with it ⚑ — the two halves are one height by construction. No alt → the editor asks for one and the picture publishes with alt="" rather than a guessed sentence ⚑. No image at any width → the hand-off is the empty state.9 · Behaviour module. member-form. Edit-safe — nothing posts or validates while the section is being edited, which is why the seven states are drawn rather than reached. No-JS, corrected in this pass ⚑: the registry line “the <form> posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint” is withdrawn — tested against both live Ghost servers, Ghost’s signup endpoint refuses a plain form submission, so a scriptless field would take an address and lose it. The form is replaced by the no-JavaScript notice (P0·4) at the form row’s own height, with no field and no button: “Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.”, the note kept beneath it. The sent, error and loading states are untouched — they are Ghost’s own script doing its job, and only the promise that any of it survives without script was wrong. Nothing of the form survives without script ⚑ — the field is not drawn at all, so there is no address to lose, and the notice is plain text rather than an alert. Members off is a separate question and is not about JavaScript: that substitution happens at the server and holds either way. The picture is a CSS background on a real {{img_url}} and declares nothing.10 · Accessibility notes. Heading h2. The picture carries a real alt, not an empty one ⚑ — it is content at this scale, the opposite call from 7 Cover’s background. A real visually-hidden <label>Email address</label>, never a placeholder standing in for one; type="email", autocomplete="email", inputmode="email", required. aria-invalid on error with focus held in the field. The note is both the aria-describedby target and the aria-live="polite" region ⚑. DOM order is words then picture at every width and at either side value ⚑. Light: heading 14.0:1, blurb 5.3:1.
