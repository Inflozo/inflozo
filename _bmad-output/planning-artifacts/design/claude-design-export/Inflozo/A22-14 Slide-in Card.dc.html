14 · Slide-in Card

A 380 px card that arrives in the page’s bottom corner after a trigger, carrying a short ask and a stacked form. The only design in A22 that is not in the document’s flow.

It is drawn here at rest, arrived and undismissed, because behaviours do not run while editing and the resting state is the primary frame. What sits behind it on this frame is another section, at low opacity, and is not part of this design.

TWO MODULES, AND A GAP IN THE REGISTRY
slide-in-card — no-JS, quoted: “The card renders statically in the document flow near the page end rather than sliding in.” It becomes an ordinary section at the page foot ⚑, which is why this design still works with script off.member-form for the ask, as every other design in A22.Dismissal memory is a gap ⚑. slide-in-card does not say whether a dismissal is remembered; dismiss is the closest module and belongs to A2’s bar. Named as a finding for the architect, not resolved here.

The card holds one height through empty, focus, invalid and submitting ⚑ and shrinks for done and subscribed, because a card in the corner has nothing beneath it to push. Done auto-dismisses after eight seconds ⚑ — the one timed behaviour in A22, and it is stated rather than assumed: a confirmation that sits in the corner for the rest of a reading session is a nag. The close button is 32 px in a 44 px target ⚑.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the heading (capped at 40), the blurb (capped at 90), the button label and the note. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. The newsletter is chosen in the markup too ⚑ — data-members-newsletter, its value the letter’s name, not its id; errors land in a data-members-error child, which is the invalid state’s own slot. Ghost’s form creates a free member and never asks for payment ⚑ — checked against Ghost’s theme documentation, 28 August 2026. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. There is no no-JavaScript floor ⚑ corrected in this pass — /members/api/send-magic-link/ refuses a plain POST, tested against both live Ghost servers, so the form is replaced by P0·4’s no-JavaScript notice at the same height: “Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.” The sent, error and loading states are untouched. The ask is conditional on the connected site ⚑ — it does not render with self-signup switched off or members disabled, and a paid ask needs a payment provider connected; any button that opens Ghost’s own sign-up pop-up does nothing with JavaScript off. Two modules ⚑ — slide-in-card as well, both edit-safe. With script off this is an ordinary bordered card at the page foot with a working form in it.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESSix controls of its own. Cut: an animation-direction value (fast and springy from the corner it is pinned to, 160 ms ease-out, always ⚑), a scrim value (this card never dims the page behind it — it is not a modal ⚑), a repeat-frequency value, and a heading-size value.It is not a modal and never becomes one. No focus trap, no page scrim, no background inert ⚑ — a reader can keep reading with the card on screen, and the registry has no modal module to declare if it could not.Where the address goes is the shared group below: which newsletter, whether a name is asked for, what a signed-in subscriber sees, who sees the section at all ⚑ new in this pass, and what happens when members are switched off. The form always posts to Ghost ⚑ — the endpoint is read-only.

As 1. Members off → the card does not render at all ⚑ — this is the one design that hides rather than substituting, because a card that interrupts a reader to show them a link they did not ask for is worse than nothing. A signed-in subscriber never sees it ⚑, at either value of the shared control: the subscribed line is not worth an interruption.8 · Empty state. No blurb → the card falls to 168 px ⚑. No heading is not supported — the card is 22 px of padding around a field otherwise, and the editor says so ⚑. No note → the card loses 20 px.9 · Behaviour module. Two ⚑ — slide-in-card and member-form. Both edit-safe: neither runs while the section is being edited, which is why the card is drawn arrived. No-JS, corrected in this pass ⚑: slide-in-card — “The card renders statically in the document flow near the page end rather than sliding in.” member-form — the registry line “the <form> posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint” is withdrawn — tested against both live Ghost servers, Ghost’s signup endpoint refuses a plain form submission, so a scriptless field would take an address and lose it. The form is replaced by the no-JavaScript notice (P0·4) at the form row’s own height, with no field and no button: “Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.”, the note kept beneath it. The sent, error and loading states are untouched — they are Ghost’s own script doing its job, and only the promise that any of it survives without script was wrong. Together: with script off the card renders statically at the page foot and carries the no-JavaScript notice where the form would be ⚑ corrected in this pass — a real section that tells the reader the truth, rather than a field that would take an address Ghost cannot accept without script.10 · Accessibility notes. The card’s heading is an h2 ⚑, as every A22 heading is. It is not a dialog ⚑ — no role="dialog", no focus trap, no aria-modal: it does not block the page, so claiming it does would strand a screen-reader user. It is a <section aria-labelledby> that becomes visible; focus is not moved to it on arrival ⚑, and it is announced with aria-live="polite" once. The close button is a real <button> with aria-label="Close" in a 44 px target ⚑. A real visually-hidden <label>Email address</label>; type="email", autocomplete="email", inputmode="email", required. aria-invalid on error with focus held in the field. The note is both the aria-describedby target and the aria-live="polite" region ⚑. Motion: 160 ms ease-out from the pinned corner; under reduced-motion it appears without translation ⚑, the threshold unchanged.

Repeating items — none.
