WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESFive controls, and the fifth is nearly a sixth. A portrait toggle was cut: with faces this is 8 Faces, and two designs that differ by one control value are one design. The Ground control keeps the overlap visible instead of hiding it — the panel names 8 Faces by number.How many is doing two jobs here and the panel says which: it sets the query limit, and the limit is what the remainder is drawn from ⚑. The remainder carries no number ⚑ 29 August 2026 — it reads "and others" — because a theme cannot subtract the names it drew from the total it holds; a template has no arithmetic. The whole writer count is still available as its own line, and the masthead link is where the rest are.Cut: portraits, a heading, an alignment value, a second link, and a "since" or founding date ⚑ — Ghost stores no publication founding date, and inventing one is exactly the kind of authoritative fiction the brief forbids.
