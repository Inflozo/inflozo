2 · Tiles

Four tiles across, each a picture at a named ratio with the topic under it. The design for a publication whose topics look like something — food, photography, cities — and the one that fails hardest without pictures, which is why the tag plate is drawn on the primary frame rather than hidden in a state.

A20 inherits A17's named ratio ladder and A17's tag plate verbatim. ⚑ The plate here carries the tag's own name rather than a post's primary tag, because there is no post to read from.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the introduction and the link label. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the all-topics link takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover ⚑. Tag names and counts are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. A20 has no authored image field ⚑ — the pictures are Ghost’s feature_image, so rule 10’s Image focus has nothing to attach to and the crop stays centred; a focus control here would be a fake one.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no focal-point control, and no per-tag ratio.
