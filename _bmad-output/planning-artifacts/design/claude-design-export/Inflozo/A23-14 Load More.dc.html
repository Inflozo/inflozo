WHAT THE BUTTON SETTLES
Load-more, not infinite scroll ⚑. A34·7 owns infinite-scroll and owns no query; a search result list has a cap in the source group, and auto-loading past a stated cap makes the control a lie — A17 and A18’s refusal, carried.The batch is the cap’s first slice, not a second number ⚑. “How many results” in the source group is the total the section will ever show; Batch size is how much of it arrives at once. At Batch ≥ cap the button never renders.Focus moves to the first new result’s link after a press ⚑ — A18·15’s rule, and the visually-hidden aria-live="polite" region says “Four more results loaded. Showing 8 of 12.” Not the titles: a live region reciting four headlines is worse than silence.

Three states this design owns alone: loading (the button keeps its width and its name becomes “Loading”), exhausted (the button is replaced by “That is all 12” ⚑ — never left inert, A18·15’s rule), and the no-JS branch, which is Ghost’s numbered pagination. A new query resets the batch to one ⚑ — appending a second query’s results under a first query’s is the defect this control invites.

What it searches

Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.

Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.

MarkedPlain

What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.

Marks are allowed in them through the P0·1 toolbar; the button’s label takes an icon from the P0·2 picker, before or after, Small and label-coloured. Row content is Ghost’s ⚑ — “Edit in Ghost”.
