The share destinations alone, on a surface bar at the content width — the label on the left, the links on the right, one 44 px row, nothing else in the section.

This is the design A25 asked for: its finding was that a post with the article's share rail set to Under and a footer with a share block shows the same three buttons twice. The rail's Under value now resolves to Off wherever an A26 share block is on the route ⚑, and this design is the canonical place that block lives.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The share destinations alone on a surface bar at the content width, label left and links right, one 44 px row. The canonical home of the share block, and the reason A25's rail Under resolves to Off.2 · Structural descriptor. bar · none · surface · few · none · share bar on its own ground
Ground surface is the slot doing the work — it is what separates this from 6 Slim, which draws the same links on the page. Count few: two to four destinations.3 · Archetype. bar. One departure: it does not become a stack below 767, it becomes two rows inside the same bar ⚑ — the ground is what makes that legible.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 bar 1,296 or 720 at the pack radius, padding 20/28/36 vertical and 32 horizontal, label 15 muted left, links 15/600 at 28 px gaps on 44 px targets right. 834 bar 754, unchanged. ≤ 767 bar 350, padding 20/20, label above the links and both left-aligned whatever Alignment says ⚑.5 · Content fields. Read: post.url, post.title. Authored: shareLabel (24 ch, "Share this piece"), copiedLabel (24 ch, "Link copied"). Two read and two authored — the shortest field list in A26 ⚑. Unread: everything else in the union, including tags[] and authors[].6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Links a number picker, 2 to 4, default 4 ⚑. Link style Words · Words and glyphs. Alignment Split · Left · Centre. Bar padding Compact 20 · Comfortable 28 · Spacious 36. Edges Content width · On the measure. Five. Cut: a glyph-only value, share counts, a native share trigger, a sticky value, a bar colour.
7 · Data. Destinations: the count is the control and the list is a site setting ⚑; there is no zero case, because a site always has Email and Copy link. Nothing else is read — a post with no tags, no author bio and no neighbours draws this design unchanged, which is what makes it the safe fallback ⚑. The route rule: where this design is on a route, A25's Share rail Under resolves to Off and A26's other share blocks are suppressed ⚑ — one share row per route.8 · Empty state. There is none in the usual sense ⚑ — the design's content cannot be absent. An empty shareLabel falls back to the default rather than rendering an unlabelled row ⚑; an empty copiedLabel likewise. Editor: the bar as drawn, with the copy confirmation previewable from the panel.9 · Behaviour module. share. No-JS, quoted: "Share links are real <a href="https://…"> URLs and work normally; only the copy-link button is hidden." Edit-safe. With JavaScript off this design loses one of its three links and keeps its bar, its label and its ground ⚑ — the smallest no-JS loss in the category.10 · Accessibility. <footer> inside the <article>; the links are a <ul> labelled by shareLabel ⚑, so the label is a real accessible name and not decoration. Copy link is a <button> and the other three are <a> ⚑ — different elements because they do different things. The confirmation is announced aria-live="polite" and focus never moves. Measured light: links 13.1:1, label 5.4:1, confirmation #D96C3F 4.6:1 on surface. Print: nothing prints ⚑ — the only A26 design that prints nothing at all.Repeating items. One, and it is the site's: the destinations. No Add, no Remove, no reorder, and no choice of which — Links picks a count from a fixed list.Reconciled. The destinations become one site-wide ordered setting and the glyphs fixed brand icons ⚑; Links draws the first N enabled, which pays off this design’s admitted LinkedIn cost. Background role is locked to Surface with the reason shown, Bar padding keeps its own name, and Line draws above the bar rather than inside it. Five controls plus the universal trio. The conflict with A25’s native-share pass is recorded: A25 reads no list, A26·9 and A24·14 still do.Flagged ⚑ — surface as the slot that makes this a design; four links wrapping at the measure where 6 Slim disables the same combination; horizontal padding fixed at 32; the label above the links at 390 overriding Alignment; the one-share-row-per-route rule; A25's rail Under resolving to Off; Copy link as a <button> among anchors; nothing printing.
