The featured post's title at 64 px on an 1,100 px measure, a hairline above it, the excerpt at 20 and the meta beneath. No picture at any setting — which makes it the one design in A19 that cannot be broken by a missing one, and the reason seven other designs name it when theirs is gone.

It is also the category's ceiling on scale. A19's spotlight ladder stops at 40 and this design starts at 52, with nothing between them: a site that wants a bigger title changes design rather than pushing a split past what its column holds. One display moment per section is the design system's rule, and here the title is it — there is nothing else in the section to compete.

Rule is Off · Above · Above and below, and it is A18's separator vocabulary adapted: a section rather than a list has no “between”, so the three values are the three places a hairline can be. Above is the default because a spotlight needs a top edge and rarely needs a bottom one — the next section's padding supplies that. This is the design a text-only publication builds its home page on. No picture, no plate, no ratio, no scrim: two type colours and a hairline is the whole of what it spends, which also makes it the cheapest design in the category to re-skin and the one that looks most different between packs — the heading face is doing all of the work at 64 px. ⚑ Seven designs name this one when their picture is missing, and none of them becomes it. Reflow produces this composition at the reflowing design's own title size — 34, not 64. A user who wants the 64 has to choose it, because a section that quadruples its title size on its own is a section that has changed design without being asked.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES The control set is the shortest useful one in A19, and every control here changes something a user can see from across the room. Two of the six are the type's size and place, one is a hairline, one is how much prose there is. There is nothing to tune because there is nothing in the section but words — which is the point of drawing it, and the argument for keeping a text-only design in a category about pictures. Excerpt has three values here, not four. Three lines of 20 px type under a 64 px title is a paragraph and the section stops being a spotlight. The excerpt's size is not a control and is 20 at every title size, because a second size ladder inside a design that is already about size is one dial too many. Cut: a background control (5 Contrast Band, one click away). Cut: a tag chip — the tag joins the eyebrow with a middle dot, as in 4 Poster. Cut: a quotation option — setting the excerpt at display size instead of the title is 13 Quote, and it is a different structural claim rather than a value of this one.
