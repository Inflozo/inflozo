A9 FAQ · DESIGN 14 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · THE ONE FILLED BUTTON IN THE CATEGORY

Ask

The accordion with a contact panel at its foot: the section's note as one sentence and its link as the only filled accent button in A9. It is the design for the page where the FAQ is the last thing before the footer, and the place where the category admits that a list of answers ends without answering somebody.

The button needs a reason, because A9-0 refuses one everywhere else. A6 CTA Banners owns the ask, and a FAQ that ends in “Subscribe” is a banner with questions as its copy. This button asks for nothing except the reader's own question, which is the one thing a FAQ is allowed to want: it is a mailto: or a contact page and never a subscribe, a trial or a purchase, and the editor says so at the field.

NONE · THE CATEGORY'S ORDINARY FOOT · MUTED NOTE AND A TEXT LINK, NO BUTTON
Ask panel None is not an empty state — it is this design becoming 1 Accordion's foot, a muted note and an underlined text link, no plane and no button. It exists so that a site can keep the design while a campaign is running and take the button off afterwards without switching designs and losing its Rows shown value. Rule above is the value for a page that already has a card in it. The hairline is the row's hairline, 24 px above the note, so the panel reads as the last item in the list rather than as an object on top of it — which is the same argument A8·6 made for its foot, arriving at the opposite answer because here the foot is asking for something. The button is the only filled accent in A9 and it appears at two of the three values. At None the accent is back to an underline, which means a site can turn the category's single filled button off with one control. The note and the link are a pair here in a way they are not elsewhere: with a note and no link the panel is a sentence in a box, so the panel is not drawn at all unless the link pair is authored — the note then falls back to the ordinary muted foot. ⚑ Mine.

Nothing about it was redrawn for A9, and its label is linkLabel, capped at 20 characters like every other link in the category. What it may point at, stated at the field: a mailto:, a contact page, or a help address. The editor warns on a link to a checkout, a signup or a trial and names A6·11 Reasons, which is the design for a FAQ-shaped list that ends in an ask. The warning does not block the save: a theme cannot know every URL, and a section that refused a link would be guessing. ⚑ Mine, warning and wording. The button is a link, not a <button> — an <a> styled as one, because it navigates. The only <button>s in this design are the six rows. Contrast: carried text on the accent fill is 4.6:1 in light and 4.9:1 in dark, both above AA for 14 px at 600; the ring against the card is 3.3:1 light and 6.3:1 dark. The accent fill is the only place in A9 where accent carries text, which is why it is the one ratio in the category that had to be checked against the fill rather than the ground.

ACCESSIBILITY · ONE MORE STOP THAN 1 ACCORDION 1 Accordion's structure with one addition: the panel is a <div> after the <ul> holding a <p> and an <a>. No role, no label, no heading — it is a paragraph and a link at the foot of a section, and giving it a landmark would put a region between the questions and the page's footer. The button is an <a> with a real href, not a <button> and not a <div> with a handler: it navigates, so it is a link, and a mailto: announced as a button would promise something that happens on the page. Tab order: one stop per row, then the button, last. At Ask panel None the stop is still there — the link is just not filled — so switching the value does not change the number of stops. Contrast: carried text on the accent fill 4.6:1 light / 4.9:1 dark; the note 15.4:1 / 14.6:1 on the card at the two drawn values and 5.6:1 / 6.8:1 muted at None; question and answer 15.8:1 / 15.1:1. The ring on the button takes the card as its inner gap, not the page, so it is 3.3:1 against surface rather than background. Forced colours: the fill is dropped and the button takes the system's own button colours with a 1 px border, which is the one element in A9 that gains a border in that mode. Print: the panel is kept, the fill is not printed, and the label prints as text with its URL beside it — a printed accent rectangle is ink for nothing, and a printed “Email the editors” with no address is a dead end. ⚑ Mine.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Questions and answers edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The answer raises the marks toolbar on selection with bold · italic · link · inline code and no underline — an underline inside prose is a link that is not one. Recorded as a PRD delta. Nothing in A9 is Ghost-owned, so nothing here says “Edit in Ghost” — every word on the page is the site’s own. Questions are the P0·3 item list: Add question arrives with content, Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. The button is the P0·4 member-aware action editor: its URL opens the Link Picker — a mailto:, a contact page or a help address picked rather than typed — and it takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, from the Icon Picker with its size and colour-role popover, off by default (the frame is drawn without one; an icon takes the label’s carried colour, never the accent). The warning stands: a link to a checkout, a signup or a trial is flagged with A6·11 Reasons named, and it does not block the save — a theme cannot know every URL.
