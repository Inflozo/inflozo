What the category owes, before any of the fifteen.

Four settlements the brief asked A2 to make, the stack checks against three A1 headers, the tokenisation proof, the stress frame, the shared field list, and the roster. All fifteen designs are drawn. Rule, Split, Badge, Two-Line, Capture, Countdown, Dateline, Ticker, Rotator, Pill, Toast, Takeover, Consent, Edge, Triple — each with its own file, frame set, control panel and spec card, and the written specification in A2 Announcement Bars — Spec.md.

1 · THE FOUR SETTLEMENTS · CONFIRMED WITH YOU BEFORE DRAWING

Each of these is stated once here and repeated verbatim in every design's spec card and in the spec doc. Where a value is mine rather than yours, it says so.

Stacking · one bar, always first The bar is the first element in the body after the skip link, above <header>, on every template. One A2 per site — the category is site-wide furniture, not a message queue; a second simultaneous message is what A2·8 Ticker and A2·9 Rotator exist for. The two bottom-anchored designs (A2·11 Toast, A2·13 Consent) are the exception: they are not in the top stack and may coexist with one top bar. The bar never draws a bottom hairline when its ground differs from the header's; when the grounds match, the bar owns the hairline and the header keeps its own.
Dismissal · 30 days, keyed to the message Close sits at the right end of the bar: a 32 px box in a 44 px hit area at desktop, a full 44 px target at ≤ 767, moving to the block's top-right whenever the bar is more than one line. Dismissal collapses the height to zero over 160 ms; the sticky offset and every anchor's scroll-margin recompute in the same frame, and if the reader has scrolled, the scroll position is reduced by the bar's height so the words under the pointer do not move. Comes back after 30 days is the default, stored per browser against a hash of the bar's content — editing the message brings it back immediately, which is the behaviour authors expect and the one that makes “dismissed forever” safe to offer. Values: This session · 30 days · Never comes back · Always shows.
Audience · four values, and a Preview as switcher Audience: Everyone · Logged out · Free members · Paid members. Member state comes from the same Ghost source as A1·14's member block, so a site with members disabled shows the control collapsed to Everyone with the reason given. In the editor the sidebar carries a Preview as switcher — Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid — and the canvas renders exactly what that reader would get. When the current preview audience is not the targeted one, the bar renders normally with a dashed outline and the line “Not shown to free members” in the editor chrome; it does not dim the bar, because a dimmed bar reads as a disabled bar. Audience and dismissal live in a shared Visibility group below each design's own controls and do not count against the six.
Height and reflow · in flow, with a Sticky value Every top bar is in flow: it pushes the page and the header down, and it scrolls away with them. Placement: In flow · Sticky is a control on ten of the thirteen top designs — A2·9 Rotator, A2·12 Takeover and A2·15 Triple are In flow only, each for a stated reason; Sticky pins the bar above a sticky header, and the two heights sum into one offset that every in-page anchor uses as its scroll-margin. The bar's height is measured, never a constant — no design assumes 44 px. Height is reserved on first paint and the dismissal state is read before paint, so a dismissed bar never flashes. The bar itself never changes height on scroll; only the header shrinks, exactly as A1 already specifies.

The one thing the packs disagree about Whether accent may be used for the bar's inline link. On a contrast band, Paper's accent measures 4.85:1 against near-ink — AA at 14 px, but only just. Ink's dark-mode accent on its pale contrast measures 2.9:1 and fails. So the rule is set by the worst pack, not the best: on a contrast or accent ground, the inline link is the carried text colour with a 50%-opacity underline, never accent. Accent links are permitted on background and surface grounds only. The Link style control shows the disabled value with its measured ratio, per §7.4.
Flagged: invented here Tangerine's and Ink's contrast pairs are mine — the brief gives contrast for Paper only. Tangerine: #2B1A12 carrying #FFF4EA in light, #F7E2D2 carrying #1F1410 in dark. Ink: #17171C carrying #F5F5F7, and #E6E6EE carrying #101014. The 2.9:1 Ink figure follows from that invented value; if you set a different Ink contrast, re-check it before the rule above is treated as settled.

5 · THE CATEGORY'S SHARED FIELD LIST · THE CONTRACT THAT MAKES SWITCHING SAFE

The union of everything the fifteen designs need. Any design may use fewer; none may need a field that is not on this list. Switching design keeps every value — a field with nowhere to live is kept, not cleared, and reappears when the user switches to a design that shows it.

Message and action
message — rich text, required, ≤ 120 chars. Bold and one inline link allowed. Every design. messageShort — text, optional, ≤ 48. Used ≤ 767 wherever a design says so. label — text, optional, ≤ 14. Never truncates. A2·3, ·7, ·10, ·12. headline — text, optional, ≤ 60. A2·4 Two-Line, A2·12 Takeover. body — text, optional, ≤ 160. A2·4, ·11, ·12, ·13. linkLabel + linkUrl — optional pair, label ≤ 24. Inline text link. ctaLabel + ctaUrl — optional pair, label ≤ 18, plus ctaLabelShort ≤ 12. Button. secondaryLabel + secondaryUrl — optional pair. Second action; A2·13 Consent uses it for Decline.
Multiples, time, media, visibility
messages[] — list of { message ≤ 120, messageShort ≤ 48, linkLabel ≤ 24, linkUrl }, optional, 2–6 items. A2·8 Ticker, ·9 Rotator, ·15 Triple, and shared between them so switching keeps the order. When a design that shows one message is chosen, item 1 is shown and the rest are kept; fewer than two items renders as A2·1. endsAt — datetime, optional, site time zone, with expiredBehaviour (Hide bar · Keep bar, drop the clock), expiredMessage ≤ 120 and expiredCtaLabel ≤ 18. A2·6 Countdown, ·12. image — image, optional, with imageAlt ≤ 120 (empty means decorative). A2·12 Takeover only; the one design in A2 that carries imagery. emailPlaceholder ≤ 28, submitLabel ≤ 14, successMessage ≤ 60 — A2·5 Capture. metaText — text, optional, ≤ 28. The right-hand meta slot. A2·7 Dateline; its Meta control can instead read Ghost's published_at, the only content binding in the category. chipLabel — text, ≤ 14, required by A2·14 Edge; the only visible text in its collapsed state. policyUrl — url, optional, A2·13 Consent's inline policy link. askAgainAfter — enum (Never · 6 months · 12 months), A2·13 only, replacing dismissMemory there. audience — enum: Everyone · Logged out · Free members · Paid members. Every design. dismissible — bool, default on. dismissMemory — enum: This session · 30 days · Never comes back · Always shows. Default 30 days. placement — enum: In flow · Sticky. Twelve top designs offer it; A2·9 Rotator is In flow only, and A2·11 and ·13 are bottom-anchored and read offset instead.

Open for you, before batch 2
1 · Hand-offs are retired. Part A·A8, ruled to apply in all 33 categories. The two A2 used are redrawn as the designs themselves: Badge at Label style “None” hides the badge and lets the sentence hold the band; Triple draws its first slot alone below 768 and hides the other two. Dateline loses the same offer. No design in A2 names another design as a state. 2 · The Compact ceiling above multi-row headers. A2 disables Spacious and defaults to Compact when the header below has more than one row. That rule is mine, and it means the same bar looks different under different headers — the one place in the library where a section's control set depends on its neighbour. Say if you would rather it be a warning than a constraint. 3 · Consent is a legal surface. A2·13 will be drawn as a design, but cookie consent that actually gates scripts is product behaviour, not a section control. I will draw it and flag that the blocking logic is out of scope for A2. 5 · Batch 2 changed the field list twice. Countdown added expiredMessage and expiredCtaLabel, and it has no Height control — height follows Clock style, 56 with boxes and 44 inline. Capture spends its sixth control on Submit style rather than Height. Both are trades I would make again, and both mean two designs in the category do not offer Height. 4 · Ticker and reduced motion. A2·8 stops entirely under reduced-motion and shows message 1 with the dots from A2·9. That makes Ticker equal to Rotator for those readers, which I think is correct; it does mean two designs converge. 6 · Batch 3 needs two things the packs do not have. A2·9's inactive dots are invisible in dark on the border token, so they take an invented step between border and muted (#453E35 in Paper); and its mobile dots sit in 32 px targets, under the 44 px floor, with swipe as the alternative. Both are drawn and flagged on the frame. A2·7 also introduces the category's only Ghost content binding — the latest post date — as a named control value rather than a silent lookup. 7 · Batch 4 needs a dark shadow pair. A2·10 Pill and A2·11 Toast are the two designs that must look lifted, and the brief's warm shadows are both tuned for light grounds — they vanish on a deep background. Both frames use an invented 0 4px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.32) in dark; every pack will need its own pair. Batch 4 also adds imageAlt to the field list, and settles that Toast stacks 12 px above A2·13 Consent — consent first, because it gates everything else. 8 · Batch 5 closes the category, and three things want your ruling. A2·13 Consent has no close control and does not trap focus — both departures from every other design here, and the second is a departure from how most consent bars behave. It also disables Accept only, which is a design judgment and not legal advice. A2·14 Edge disables Chip: None, because a 4 px hover strip cannot be reached by keyboard or thumb, and uses an overlapping hit area at 390 to reach 44 px without a 44 px chip. A2·15 Triple clips every slot to one line on desktop and draws slot one alone below 768 (Part A·A8, owner's ruling) — the rejected alternative, all three stacked at 212 px, is drawn on its frame at 62% so the trade stays visible. #453E35 is now needed by three designs; it should become a pack token.
