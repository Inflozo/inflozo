Subscribe

A field that cannot subscribe anyone is worse than no field. With members disabled in Ghost, this design renders as A2·2 Split at 56 px: the sentence holds, the field is replaced by a button carrying submitLabel, and the button links to the site's own subscribe page. The editor states the substitution in the sidebar with a link to Ghost's members setting. Nothing is lost — emailPlaceholder and successMessage are kept and return the moment members are switched on.
