The domains are the tell. Fifteen years on the same address, renewed every October by someone who never expected an audience and got one anyway. I have been keeping a list since 2019, and it has never once grown.

What they share is not taste. Some are beautiful and most are not. It is a refusal to move — off the address, off the software, off the habit — which turns out to be the rarest thing on the web.

Who is actually paying

Three of the four hundred are paid for by estates. Two are paid for by universities that forgot they were paying. The rest are individuals, and their renewal dates cluster in October for a reason nobody has explained to my satisfaction.

A site that outlives three platform migrations is not nostalgic. It is just paid for.Kicks Condor, 2019

I asked eleven of them why October. The answers divided cleanly:

·Four said the date meant nothing at all, and were mildly annoyed to be asked.
·Three said it was simply when they had money, which is the honest version of the same answer.
·Two said October was when their first host used to bill them. That host closed in 2013.

The renewal arithmetic

A domain costs about twelve dollars a year. Hosting a static site costs nothing at three of the four common hosts. The whole obligation, then, is one dollar a month and one afternoon a decade.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESInline on canvas: shareLabel (“Share this piece”), which is the share link's own text. copiedLabel is deleted from the category ⚑ — Ghost's modal owns copying the link. The share mark takes one icon from P0·2’s Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The article is Ghost’s ⚑ — paragraphs, headings, captions, quotes, lists and every card: clicking one on the canvas selects the section and the sidebar says “Edit in Ghost”. A25 has no authored URL field ⚑, so the Ghost-aware Link Picker never opens in this category, and no image field, so Image focus lands nowhere — the pictures are the author's and the Cards module sets their width. No authored array anywhere in A25 ⚑, so P0·3's item controls land nowhere, and P0·4 and Member Visibility land nowhere either — an index entry is navigation and a share link is not an offer. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: the authored labels carry defaults and the rest are theme translation-catalog strings — “Link to this section”, the disclosure's heading count, and 8 Index Top's “{n} sections” and “min read”. The share strings are gone with the destinations ⚑: Ghost’s own share modal supplies the platform names and the copy confirmation, so the theme ships neither.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESShare rail has two values here and three in 1 Measured, and the missing one is Rail. ⚑ There is no margin to put a mark in — the sheet is the content width — so the value is absent rather than offered and disabled. That is the category's rule for furniture a design has no room for, and it is the opposite of A24's disabled-with-its-reason convention, which covers values that exist but would fail.There is no Table of contents control at all, for the same reason and one more: an index inside the sheet would sit on surface beside the article and need a rule between them, which is 11 Ruled's design rather than this one. A site that wants an index and a plane picks 7 Sticky Index, whose index is outside the sheet's ancestor entirely.Cut: a sheet edge control, hairline against shadow. It was drawn and refused — the pack decides whether its surfaces carry a shadow or a hairline, Paper carries a shadow in light and a hairline in dark, and a per-design override would be the first place in the library where a section out-ranks its pack. Cut: a sheet width — 1,296 is the content width, and a sheet narrower than the content is A19·3's card at its ordinary size. Cut: a sheet radius, which is the pack's radius token and nothing else.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The article on a surface sheet at the full content width, the measure unchanged inside it, a symmetrical inset the section owns, images able to run to the sheet's edges with their captions returning to the measure, and the share row inside the plane. The category's one raised design. 2 · Structural descriptor. article body · card · surface · none · edge · the article on a raised sheet
The only design in A25 whose containment is card — the section itself sits in it, which is what the slot means. Ground surface follows: the article rests on the sheet, not on the page. Media edge is the sheet's edge, not the viewport's; the viewport is 5 Full Bleed. 3 · Archetype. article body. One departure: the sheet is not drawn below 767 ⚑ and the section reverts to the page ground. The archetype's ladder has no vocabulary for a container that leaves at a width, so it is stated here and in the panel. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 sheet 1,296 on the 72 margin, inset 64/96/132 symmetrical, measure 720 centred inside it, images at 720 or at 1,296 clipped to the radius, share row above the lower inset. 834 sheet 754, inset 64 with 32 px horizontally, so the measure inside is 690 rather than the page's 754 ⚑. ≤ 767 no sheet: ground reverts to background, measure 350, share row kept, images break to the screen edges. 5 · Content fields. Read: {{content}} whole, post.url and post.title for the share row, post.access. Authored: shareLabel (optional, 24 ch, default "Share this piece"). Unread: indexLabel, indexNote. 6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Measure Narrow 620 · Comfortable 720 · Wide 840. Type scale Compact 17 · Comfortable 19 · Spacious 21. Drop cap Off · Drop — three lines · Raised. Paragraph rhythm Spaced · Indented. Sheet inset Compact 64 · Comfortable 96 · Spacious 132. Media in the sheet In measure · To the edge. Share rail Off · Under — one link to Ghost’s share modal ⚑. Seven, and the universal trio outside them. Cut: a sheet edge value, a sheet width, a sheet radius.
7 · Data. {{content}} whole. Headings: any number; none of them leave the flow, so the design is indifferent to them. Images: one marked wide or full in Ghost takes the sheet's edge at To the edge and 720 at In measure ⚑ — this design overrules the Cards module's "wide and full break out to" setting, the only place in A25 a section does, and a finding for the architect. At the paywall cut the sheet closes above the cut ⚑ with its lower inset intact and A32's section draws its own ground beneath: a sheet that ran under a paywall would suggest the rest of the article was inside it. 8 · Empty state. An empty {{content}} renders nothing — and in particular no sheet ⚑, which is the empty state that matters here: a blank plane 1,296 wide and 192 tall is the most conspicuous way this section can fail. In the editor, the greyed line inside a dashed sheet outline. Share rail Off removes the row and its 24 px; the inset does not change. 9 · Behaviour module. None ⚑ — the share module is gone with the destinations: the row at Share rail Under is one <a href="#/share"> and Ghost's own modal does the rest. The share link declares nothing ⚑ — <a href="#/share"> is an ordinary anchor, so there is no module to degrade; Ghost’s modal is Ghost’s own client-side flow, and with JavaScript off the link opens nothing — a platform limitation the theme cannot fill. core is assumed at both values. Edit-safe — nothing in it runs while editing. 10 · Accessibility. <article> with no h1; heading levels as Ghost emitted them; scroll-margin-top: 96px. The sheet is a div with no role and no label ⚑ — it is a surface, not a region, and announcing it would put a landmark between the reader and the first sentence. An image at the sheet's edge keeps its <figure>/<figcaption> pair even though the caption is indented back to the measure. Measured light: body 13.4:1 on surface, headings 13.4:1, captions 5.6:1; the sheet against the page is 1.05:1 — decorative, carried by the shadow in light and the hairline in dark. Print: the sheet is not printed and the article prints as 2 Plain ⚑. Repeating items. None authored. No index, no item list, no per-item control of any kind. Selecting the section gives shareLabel, optional and falling back rather than rendering empty. The sheet is not separately selectable — it is the section's own geometry, and clicking it selects the section. Reconciled. Paragraph rhythm joins the quartet; seven controls. The universal trio sits outside the list with Background role locked to Surface and Vertical spacing inert — Sheet inset keeps its own name because it is padding inside an object, not the space above the section. Sharing becomes Ghost’s native modal ⚑ — the row is one link to #/share, copiedLabel, post.url and post.title leave the field list, and the design now declares no module at all. Flagged ⚑ — the sheet as the library's largest use of A19·3's card; the inset measured to the sheet's edge and symmetrical; captions returning to the measure under an edge-wide image; the 690 measure at 834; the sheet leaving below 767; no sheet at all when the body is empty; the sheet closing above the paywall cut; the design overruling the Cards module's breakout setting; the hairline running behind the image in dark; the sheet omitted in print.
