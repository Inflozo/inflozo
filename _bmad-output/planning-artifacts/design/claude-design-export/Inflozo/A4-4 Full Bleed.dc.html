The picture is the section: 16:9, edge to edge, no radius, no margin. The set sits in the lower third on the page's own 72 px margin, which is what lets one gradient family serve every arrangement in the category — text over the optical centre would need a wash, and a wash over a photograph is the thing this pack is trying not to do. The accent is absent from the whole frame, header included: a pack accent is not guaranteed to hold against an arbitrary photograph, so the primary action takes the pack's lightest surface and A1·4's own rule for its transparent state is followed rather than re-decided.

In dark both weaken: the text scrim goes to 48% in the background token and the header scrim to 24%, because the picture is already darker than the page around it. 1 · The photograph is decorative — alt="" by default — because the words over it say what it is for. This is the opposite default from 3 Split, and the split is the category's rule: over the picture, decorative; beside it, asked for. Explicit dimensions, loading="eager", fetchpriority="high", and the 16:9 box reserved before the file arrives. 2 · Two scrims, two aria-hidden siblings, never a blend mode on the image: under forced colours both disappear and the text lands on a solid ground rather than on a picture nobody can see through. 3 · The headline is the page's <h1> and is plain text, not a link — a large silent target over a photograph invites a click that goes nowhere. Contrast is measured against the scrimmed picture, exactly as A1·4 measures the header's. 4 · Both actions are links, 46 px, focus rings in the pack's lightest surface rather than accent so the ring is visible over any photograph. Focus order is header, then headline, then the two actions. The honest limit · the scrim guarantees 4.5:1 for the headline, sub and both labels against the darkest reasonable photograph, not the average one; against a bright sky the same scrim gives about 7:1. Where a site's own picture defeats it the editor flags the hero and offers 3 Split or the flat panel. Flagged — a floor, not a guarantee, and the same limit A3·14 states in the same words. Motion · none. No parallax, no Ken Burns, no entrance. The hero is the largest thing on the page and the first thing a reader lands on, and the category spends its motion budget on 10's play control and 14's cue instead.

The scrims were fixed at 60/30; a user with an already-dark photograph had no way to lighten them, and a pale photograph no way to hold its text. Subtle · Standard · Strong names the three pairs — bottom scrim then top scrim, each over the same geometry as before (100 px top, 70% of the height bottom). Dark mode steps each pair down as the fixed values did (Standard: 24/48).
