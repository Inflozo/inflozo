ZERO, ONE AND MANY NEWSLETTERS
One newsletter → the rows are not drawn ⚑ and the design is 5 Panel with a head and a field. The editor flags it and names 5 Panel; the section is not switched.Two to four is what the layout is designed for; five or more and the list scrolls the page rather than the section — no inner scroll, no “show more” ⚑.Zero newsletters is not reachable ⚑ — Ghost ships every site with one and it cannot be deleted. Stated because every other count in A22 is drawn, and this one cannot be.

The rows do not change with the state ⚑ — they are the reader’s choice, and clearing them on submit would lose it. Done names what was chosen: “Check your inbox — we sent a link for The Friday edition and Field Notes.” ⚑ Invalid with nothing ticked is a second error the field cannot show, so it goes in the same note slot: “Choose at least one letter.” ⚑ — one live region, two possible messages, never both at once.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label and the note. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”, and every newsletter’s name, description and visibility — the rows are Ghost’s and say “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. The newsletter is chosen in the markup too ⚑ — data-members-newsletter, its value the letter’s name, not its id; errors land in a data-members-error child, which is the invalid state’s own slot. Ghost’s form creates a free member and never asks for payment ⚑ — checked against Ghost’s theme documentation, 28 August 2026. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. There is no no-JavaScript floor ⚑ corrected in this pass — /members/api/send-magic-link/ refuses a plain POST, tested against both live Ghost servers, so the form is replaced by P0·4’s no-JavaScript notice at the same height: “Signing up needs JavaScript — turn it on to subscribe.” The sent, error and loading states are untouched. The ask is conditional on the connected site ⚑ — it does not render with self-signup switched off or members disabled, and a paid ask needs a payment provider connected; any button that opens Ghost’s own sign-up pop-up does nothing with JavaScript off. Without JavaScript the checkboxes are real inputs in the same form and post with the address ⚑ — this design loses nothing at all.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESSix controls of its own after this pass. Cut: a per-newsletter visibility list (the rows are Ghost’s and the section does not curate them ⚑), a radio-instead-of-checkbox value (Ghost allows several subscriptions and a radio would lie about that), an order value (Ghost’s own newsletter order is the order ⚑), and a checkbox-shape value.There is no Add and no Remove. A newsletter appears here because the publication created one in Ghost, and disappears because it was archived. The section cannot create, hide, reorder or rename them ⚑ — it reads their visibility for the Members badge and the members link — both only where that value is paid ⚑ — and nothing else.Where the address goes is the shared group below: which newsletter, whether a name is asked for, what a signed-in subscriber sees, who sees the section at all ⚑ new in this pass, and what happens when members are switched off. The form always posts to Ghost ⚑ — the endpoint is read-only.
