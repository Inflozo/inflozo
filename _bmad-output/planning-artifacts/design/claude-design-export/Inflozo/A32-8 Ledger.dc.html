What a membership includes, enumerated: six ruled rows across the content box, each a mono ordinal, a name and one line of detail, with the heading above them and the action at the foot.

It is the design that argues by listing rather than by asserting, and the only A32 design whose item count is many. The rows are authored, not queried — which is what separates it from 7 Tiers.

PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN Rows shown is a number picker. It drew Four · Six · All authored. It is now a number picker, 1 to 6, default 6, capped at six because the authored list itself is capped at six, the + drawn greyed with that reason visible at the cap. (Item counts are a number picker.) No rows no longer hands off to 1 Fade. At 0 rows the rows and their hairlines are hidden and the design draws its heading, sentence and action ⚑, and the editor says the list is empty. (No design ever turns into another design.) The fade at the cut is a fixed height, and the two-line rule is deleted. It is 96, 160 or 240 px of the page ground anchored to the end of the preview, whatever is above it. The old rule suppressed it “under two lines of visible text”, and a stylesheet cannot count lines ⚑ — so a one-line last paragraph would have faded to nothing. Suppression after a heading, a figure, a code block or an embed stays: that is an element type, which CSS can see. (CSS cannot see content.) The action is conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up window is JavaScript. The primary button does not render at all where the connected site cannot take it — self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected — and where it does render it opens Ghost’s own sign-up window, so with JavaScript off, nothing happens ⚑. The words, the fade, the measure and every drawn state are unchanged; with no button the gate is its words and the sign-in line ⚑. Both facts are now read-only rows in the panel and mono lines on every gate. (Member buttons are conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-up needs JavaScript.)

Four gates, one arrangement. Free-signup · paid · upgrade (a paid post, a signed-in free member) · specific tier ⚑. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE THREE STATES EVERY A32 DESIGN OWES
In the editor the gate draws at its resting state ⚑ — the paid gate, the fade at its control value, no hover and no ring. Nothing in A32 has a behaviour to preview, so the edit-time frame and the published frame are the same picture. With JavaScript off the section is drawn identically and every action in it is dead ⚑ — the gate is server-rendered HTML, the fade is a gradient and the suppression rules are :last-child selectors, but every action opens Ghost’s Portal, which is JavaScript. 1 Fade and 6 Split Pitch replace their email field with the no-JavaScript notice; 11 Sticky Bar hides its close button; 12 Meter keeps its static reading-time line and loses the fill — the label needs no script and the fill is the module’s. And no member button renders at all where the connected site cannot take it ⚑. A reader who has access never sees this section ⚑ — Ghost sends the whole post and A32 does not render. There is no “unlocked” state to design, and no design may draw one.

No row is a link and no row is a target ⚑ — the ledger is prose in a grid, and the only interactive things in the section are the button and the sign-in line.

A row with no detail → the row closes up to 40 px and its neighbours do not ⚑.8 · Empty state. No benefits → the rows and their hairlines are hidden and the design renders as itself, heading then sentence then action ⚑.
