SOCIAL HANDLES · GHOST ≥ 6.36 ⚑Nine handles and a website, not two: facebook · twitter (X) · linkedin · threads · bluesky · mastodon · tiktok · youtube · instagram · website. This corrects the category’s founding fact ⚑ — “two networks” was 5.x. Version-gated: on a site below 6.36 only Facebook, X and the website exist and the rest of the row is not drawn. The author’s own fields are read, never the sitewide fallback ⚑ — social_url falls back to the site’s Facebook and X, which would print the publication’s accounts under a writer who filled in neither. There is no loop over the handles: nine checks, and an absent handle reserves nothing.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the action label and the empty-state notice. actionUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the action takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size and colour-role popover ⚑. Author names, bios, counts, locations, websites and handles are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESRoster columns: Three writes two values — three columns and no bio. The control says so in its hint rather than letting a user discover that the bio silently vanished. This is the same one-control-two-values pattern as 3 Rows' Divider, and both are stated.The action defaults to Link, not Button. Accent appears at most twice in a section; on this design the only candidate is the action, and a filled button beside a list of colleagues reads as an advertisement. A user who wants the button has it in one click.Cut: a sticky head ⚑ (the registry has no module for it and CSS stickiness inside a split column fights the page's own scroll), a second action, an alignment value, and a rule between the columns.
