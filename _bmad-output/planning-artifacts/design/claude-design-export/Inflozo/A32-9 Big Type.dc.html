The promise at display size: the heading at 84 across the content box, one line under it, and the button. Nothing else — no list, no card, no plane, no rule.

PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN The fade at the cut is a fixed height, and the two-line rule is deleted. It is 96, 160 or 240 px of the page ground anchored to the end of the preview, whatever is above it. The old rule suppressed it “under two lines of visible text”, and a stylesheet cannot count lines ⚑ — so a one-line last paragraph would have faded to nothing. Suppression after a heading, a figure, a code block or an embed stays: that is an element type, which CSS can see. (CSS cannot see content.) The action is conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up window is JavaScript. The primary button does not render at all where the connected site cannot take it — self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected — and where it does render it opens Ghost’s own sign-up window, so with JavaScript off, nothing happens ⚑. The words, the fade, the measure and every drawn state are unchanged; with no button the gate is its words and the sign-in line ⚑. Both facts are now read-only rows in the panel and mono lines on every gate. (Member buttons are conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-up needs JavaScript.)

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE THREE STATES EVERY A32 DESIGN OWES
In the editor the gate draws at its resting state ⚑ — the paid gate, the fade at its control value, no hover and no ring. Nothing in A32 has a behaviour to preview, so the edit-time frame and the published frame are the same picture. With JavaScript off the section is drawn identically and every action in it is dead ⚑ — the gate is server-rendered HTML, the fade is a gradient and the suppression rules are :last-child selectors, but every action opens Ghost’s Portal, which is JavaScript. 1 Fade and 6 Split Pitch replace their email field with the no-JavaScript notice; 11 Sticky Bar hides its close button; 12 Meter keeps its static reading-time line and loses the fill — the label needs no script and the fill is the module’s. And no member button renders at all where the connected site cannot take it ⚑. A reader who has access never sees this section ⚑ — Ghost sends the whole post and A32 does not render. There is no “unlocked” state to design, and no design may draw one.

The button is the only accent and the only target ⚑; at this scale a text link beside it is invisible, so the sign-in line is drawn at 14 px in the text colour with a rule, not at muted ⚑.
