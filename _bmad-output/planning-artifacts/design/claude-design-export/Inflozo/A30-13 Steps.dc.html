WHAT THIS DESIGN IS NOT · AND THE FINDING THAT COMES WITH IT
It is not a wizard. A real multi-step signup — one panel at a time, forward and back — needs behaviour no module in the registry covers ⚑. The closest is tabs, and this design deliberately does not declare it: tabs would render all three panels stacked with their labels as headings, which is what this design already is at rest ⚑.So the steps are typography, not state ⚑. That is a finding for the architect rather than a request: if a stepped members flow is wanted, it is a new module, and no design in A30 assumes one exists.Step 1 is a choice a theme cannot complete ⚑ — picking a paid tier hands to Portal’s checkout immediately. The chip on the chosen tier says so on the frame, in the sidebar, and in the spec.

Every state replaces step 02’s contents ⚑ and the other two steps hold. Sent moves the filled ordinal from 02 to 03 ⚑ — the one place in A30 where a state changes something outside the form block, and it is drawn because the numbering would otherwise be lying about where the reader is.

Which page this is

Read-only. The template decides — the section reports whether it is drawing custom-signup, custom-signin or custom-account ⚑, and draws the matching arrangement.
Form wiring
data-members-form🔒
Read-only, new in this pass ⚑. The form carries data-members-form="signup" or "signin" and the drawn states are Ghost's own: Ghost writes loading, success and error onto the form element, which are sending, sent and invalid ⚑. Expired stays the query string's and already-signed-in is server-side. The sign-out button carries data-members-signout ⚑ — Ghost's only sign-out mechanism, and the answer to the "verify the attribute" note the frames carried.

Membership summary · Send them to the account page. Never the signup form ⚑ — a member who lands on /signup/ has nothing to sign up for. This is the category’s member-state model, and the reason no design carries a Member visibility row ⚑.
Everything that changes a subscription
Ghost Portal🔒
Read-only. Plan changes, checkout, billing, email changes and newsletter preferences are Portal’s ⚑ — the theme draws the trigger and Portal draws the rest. This is the category’s boundary and no control moves it.
Step 01’s tier links are Portal tier deep links ⚑, new in this pass — signup/{tierId}, the same as 8 Tiers' card buttons. They are links and not radios, because choosing one navigates to Portal. Prices render through Ghost’s {​{price}​} helper ⚑ for the same smallest-currency-unit reason.Member buttons are conditional ⚑ — every Sign up, Subscribe and paid-tier button is absent where the connected site cannot support it: self-signup switched off, members disabled, or no payment provider connected. Ghost seeds a $5 tier at install, so an unconnected site would otherwise ship a card whose checkout cannot complete. The rows stay in this panel, disabled, with the reason readable.Ghost’s own pop-up needs JavaScript ⚑ — every button that opens Portal, including every tier deep link and every hand-off on the account page: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. The theme cannot substitute anything for it, so the notice is the panel’s, not the page’s.Nothing identifying is printed ⚑, new in this pass — no member name, no member email, and no join date on any of the thirteen. Ghost gives a theme no join date and no list of a member’s newsletters, so both rows are gone: the newsletter row is a link out to Ghost’s own account screen, and the email row hands off the same way. Layout and actions are ours; anything that names the reader is Ghost’s account panel’s.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: eyebrow, heading, blurb, ctaLabel, note, legal, the three stepLabels, stepThreeText and every state string. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the member's name and email, the tier name, the price, the renewal date, the newsletter names and a tier's description and benefits all answer "Edit in Ghost" or show P0·1's lock pill. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, Portal actions first. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the rescued literals above are fields with defaults, and the badge vocabulary and sending word are catalog strings. There is no Preview control in this panel and there never was one to remove — states are P0·6's switcher, audiences are View as.The one icon slot is the primary button’s ⚑ — P0·2's button-icon rules: Before or After the label, always Small, always the label's colour, its Size and Colour rows hidden. The list marker is the second where a design draws ticks, and it is whole-list.Member Visibility lands nowhere in A30 ⚑, and it is a judgement rather than an omission: the route is the member state. A signed-in member never sees a signup form — the Data group's "A signed-in member on this page" decides what they see instead — so a section-level Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid row would hide a page from readers the route has already sorted. P0·4’s member-aware action editor never opens here for the same reason. Recorded in the Reconciliation notes.The tier boxes in step 01 are the P0·3 Ghost-sourced list ⚑ — no Add, no Remove, no drag; Step one and Order are its whole control surface. benefits[] is stored and not drawn, so the authored list never opens here.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESNo “one step at a time” value ⚑ — that is the module that does not exist, and offering it as a control would promise behaviour the build cannot compile. The panel says so where a user would look for it.Step one is tiers, and it is a read-only offer ⚑: two tiers or all of them, from Ghost, in Ghost’s order. No Add, no Remove, no reorder — the same rule 8 Tiers states, restated here because this is the second design that draws them.Renumbering is automatic ⚑. Hide step one and the remaining two are 01 and 02, not 02 and 03 — a numbered list with a missing first item is the fastest way to make a page look broken.Reconciled. Step 01’s tier links are wired ⚑ — signup/{tierId}, the same Portal deep links as 8 Tiers, with prices through {​{price}​}. Order arrives and matches 8 and A32 ⚑. Background role is locked at Background with the plane-is-the-ground reason. Sign-in link, Eyebrow, Note line and CTA icon arrive; Padding retires into Vertical spacing. Ten controls of its own.This pass · nothing identifying, and two rows deleted. Member since is gone and so is the newsletter list ⚑ — Ghost’s member object carries neither a join date nor a theme-readable list of newsletters, so both rendered empty on every live site. The newsletter row is now a link out to Ghost’s own account screen, and no design prints a member’s own name or email: the head is the authored account heading, the email row hands off, and the avatar goes with the name it was derived from.This pass · the no-JavaScript notice. Ghost’s signup endpoint will not accept a plain form post ⚑ — tested on both live servers — so P0·4’s notice replaces the form rather than the form standing scriptless. Sending, sent, expired and invalid are Ghost’s own script and are unchanged.This pass · the signed-out account page. Ghost does not redirect an anonymous visitor away from /account/ ⚑, so the fourth account tile is a designed signed-out state: a heading, one sentence, a link to /signin/, and no member row at all.
