Constraints stated once, for every category ⚑: the Count stepper caps at 100. Hand-picked has no maximum at all — it accepts as many posts as the user cares to pick and warns past 25, drawn in the next frame. No relative-date filters — "this month" is route vocabulary, not section vocabulary. A secondary feed renders nothing at zero items, heading and container together — an empty "More essays" band is a defect the panel prevents by construction. Meta toggles are the design's own: a design with no excerpt slot never offers an Excerpt toggle. The tag and author selects live-search past ten entries with the same field grammar as the Link Picker.

A cap would be easier to build and harder to justify. Hand-picking is how an editor curates, and the honest answer to a long list is the cost, stated where the picking happens. The warning names the unit that actually bills — the page — because a user counting sections will underestimate it by however many sections they placed, and it now names the measured figures so the cost is a quantity rather than a mood.
