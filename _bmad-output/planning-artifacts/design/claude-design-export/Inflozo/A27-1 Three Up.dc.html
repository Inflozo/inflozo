DESIGN PATCH PASS · 30 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Item counts are a number pickerShow became a number picker, 1–4, default 3 — it was a row of three fixed buttons. 4 is the ceiling, greyed with its reason: the divisions are 2 → 636, 3 → 416, 4 → 306 and there is no fifth. At 4 the row still steps to two across at 834.
No design ever turns into another designNothing to delete. This design never named another design: fewer posts than the number keeps the cell width at the left, and a post with no picture takes the plate. The panel advises at 1 — “at one related post, 12 Next Up reads better” — and never switches.
Avatars with no photographOne letter, not two. Where a post's author has no photograph this design draws A1's initials circle, and every author in A27 comes from Ghost — which cannot make two initials out of a name. “Jane Doe” shows J. No list in this category is typed by the site, so the two-initial form has no subject in A27 at all.
A design may offer fewer choices, and must say whyRe-checked. Portrait 4:5 already carried its reason (three 416 portraits are 520 px tall each). The swatch row is Base; there is no “Inherit” value here and none was removed, because none existed.
Ghost’s templates cannot count, add, or rememberNothing here is computed. No total, no “and 2 others”, no saving and no heading that appears only when a value changes. The set is one {{#get}} loop at the chosen number, and the editor's count line (“4 posts matched. 3 drawn.”) is editor text that never reaches a reader.
CSS cannot see contentEvery clamp is by line, never by character count — the excerpt is line-clamp and a long title wraps and pushes nothing. No stylesheet here measures a caption, counts a word or reorders the DOM at a breakpoint.
The Remove button never greys outThe category's only repeating list is the Hand-picked post picker in the Data group, and Remove was never dimmed on it. There is no minimum to defend — unpicking the last post empties the list, When empty then decides, and the panel says that at the click rather than greying the control. Nothing else in this design is a list: no Add creates a post and no Remove deletes one.
Slider labels · gap namesNo subject. No slider and no gap control: Vertical spacing reads Compact · Comfortable · Spacious, and every fixed measure — gutters, row gaps, insets — is part of the arrangement. No Tight/Even/Airy or Tight/Standard/Wide survives anywhere in A27.
Member buttons, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-upNo subject. Every action here is a link to a post or to an archive route. There is no Sign up, no Subscribe, no paid tier and no Portal link, so nothing renders conditionally on self-signup or a payment provider and nothing opens Ghost's pop-up. Member visibility is offered nowhere in A27: a related post is navigation.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject, and no promise to withdraw. This design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for the notice to replace. Its JavaScript-off line is unchanged: pixel-identical — hover and focus are CSS over server-rendered markup.
Some fields we drew do not existNothing missing is read. This design reads title, url, feature image and its alt, primary tag, custom excerpt, published date, author with profile image, reading time — all of them fields Ghost gives a theme. No member join date, no member newsletter list, no newsletter cadence, no site address and no “posts per page” setting appears anywhere in it; When empty is this section's own field, not a Ghost admin setting.
Inside a blog post’s body we own the stylesheet and nothing elseApplies nowhere. This section is placed after the article, not inside its body — its markup, its ARIA and its words are ours end to end. A33 is the category for what Ghost renders inside a post.
