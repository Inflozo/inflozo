WHAT THE CARDS SETTLE
A result card is A17’s post card, unchanged ⚑ — same image ratio, same title size, same meta line, same “the whole card is the link”. A search result and a post grid item are the same object, and a reader should not have to learn two.A post with no feature image is the hard case. It draws as a card with no image, title at the top of the column ⚑ — not a placeholder, not a letter-tile. Mixed grids are expected: at Image: Off the whole grid is title-and-meta cards, which is the value for archives that do not run pictures.The excerpt is the card’s optional third line, clamped to two. It is off by default ⚑ — the image is doing the work the excerpt would do, and three columns of prose is a page of prose.

The grid does not re-column by result count ⚑ — one result is one card at 405 at the grid’s left, not a stretched card and not a centred one. A17·1’s rule, and the reason this design survives the ugly test. Searching dims the previous grid; there are no skeleton cards ⚑, because a grid of grey rectangles is indistinguishable from a grid of missing images.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SEVEN ROWS · IDENTICAL WHEREVER RESULTS ARE DRAWN · NOT COUNTED
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
Match highlight
MarkedPlain
A real <mark> at 16 % accent, 22 % in dark, in the title and the excerpt and never in the meta ⚑. At Plain it is absent from the DOM rather than untinted. Promoted here from 11 Thumb Rows, so every design that draws a result governs it in the same row ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESThe sort note and the state strings are the only authored text this design draws ⚑ — sortNote edits inline with the P0·1 toolbar; countLabel, emptyHeading, emptyText, emptyLinkLabel, recentLabel and suggestLabel are edited through the P0·6 switcher (No query · Results · No matches). Eyebrow and heading are stored and not drawn, and remain editable in the designs that draw them. Card titles, meta and images are Ghost’s ⚑ — “Edit in Ghost”.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTGhost posts and pages with feature_image: no Add, no Remove, no drag ⚑. A post with no feature image draws as a card without one ⚑ — never a placeholder. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — a card is a link, not a CTA.
