Index

Four related posts as an index: a number, a title, its tag, its date, ruled between. The columns line up because they are a grid rather than a row of floated pieces, which is the only thing that makes a four-line list read as a table.

It is the archive-minded design — for publications whose readers arrive by date and section rather than by picture — and it is the densest thing in A27: four posts in 240 px.
