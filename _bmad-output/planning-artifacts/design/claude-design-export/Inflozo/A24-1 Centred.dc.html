Centred

Tag, title, standfirst and byline on one axis, the picture beneath them at the content width. It is the arrangement most readers have already seen a thousand times, which is the reason it is the default: a post header's job is to be recognised in the half second before anyone decides to read, and recognition is not a place to be original.

It is also the design that settles the category's numbers. The 820 title measure, the 24 px gap under the eyebrow, the 20 under the title, the 40 above the picture and the 64 to the article are all set here and inherited by fifteen designs. When a design switch has nowhere obvious to land, it lands here, and it is the frame the stress test and the tokenisation proof are both drawn on.

The gates close to lorries at ten and open again at five, and the eight hundred people who work the hours in between call it the second day.

Four measures, three of them different, and that is deliberate. The title takes 820, the standfirst 720, the article 720 and the picture the full 1,296 — so the composition narrows toward the reader and then opens once. A title on 720 would set at 41 characters a line and read as a paragraph in a big font; on 1,296 it would set at 74 and stop being a title at all.
The gaps are the 8 px grid, four values only: 24 under the eyebrow, 20 under the title, 24 above the meta, 40 above the picture. Every absent element takes its own gap with it — no reserved space, anywhere, in any state — which is what keeps the design composed when three of its five parts are missing.

Centred is the harder alignment and it is the default anyway. Centred type needs every line to be a deliberate length, which is why the standfirst is capped at 720 rather than sharing the title's 820 — two centred blocks at the same width read as one ragged shape. Alignment Left keeps every measure and every gap and moves the axis; it is one control value and it changes nothing else, which is the test of whether a composition was built on a grid or on a look. The picture is 16:9 by default rather than 3:2 ⚑ because a 1,296-wide 3:2 crop is 864 px tall and pushes the article's first line below every laptop's fold. Portrait 4:5 is offered and is the value to avoid here; the panel says so, and 4 Image Top is named as the design that holds a tall crop properly.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Here that is readingTimeSuffix — the one authored string this design draws. The post title, the excerpt, the tag and author names and the caption are Ghost’s ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. The joins are theme translation-catalog strings, not fields ⚑ — the “and” between two authors and the “and 2 others” beyond three. No URL field and no icon slot exists in this design, so the Link Picker and P0·2 never open.
DATA · THE POST’S OWN · AND WHAT A PAGE CAN SWITCH OFFThe route's post and nothing else — no query, so P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere in A24 ⚑. tags[] and authors[] are Ghost's: no Add, no reorder, no per-item editing, and clicking a tag or an author says “Edit in Ghost”. On a page whose “Show title and feature image” is off the whole section does not render ⚑ — Ghost's semantics for @page.show_title_and_feature_image, stated rather than left to be discovered. Member Visibility is not here: nothing in A24 is a CTA.
WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Six controls and none of them is a colour, a size in pixels or a font. Alignment, Title size, Standfirst lines, Image ratio, Rule, Padding — and the whole panel is arrangement. The pack decides what the type looks like and the post decides what it says; the user decides where it sits and how much of it there is, which is the only decision the section can safely offer across twelve packs. The Post block is the category's replacement for the Posts block, and this is the design where it is drawn at rest. Fifteen designs narrow, set, lock, ignore or interpret one of its four fields; this one takes all four as they come, which is what makes it the safe destination for a design switch — no field arrives here with nowhere to live. Cut, and named so nobody re-proposes them: a title-colour control (a coloured h1 is a pack decision and usually a mistake); an eyebrow-style control offering a filled chip (settlement 4 refused it category-wide); a "show updated date" toggle (A26); and a caption-position control — the caption sits under the picture in every design that has one, because a caption above a photograph is a standfirst that lies about which thing it describes. Rule has no "Above and below" value here and 2 Flush Left has one. ⚑ A rule under a centred meta row and another above a centred eyebrow makes the header a box, and a box is 7 Card. The two designs' Rule controls therefore differ, which is correct: the same-named control does not have to carry the same values in two designs, only the same meaning.

One decision, and it is the avatar. The initials circle takes border — #332E27 — with text initials, which is a 1.6:1 disc on the ground and reads as a shape rather than a badge; the initials themselves are 12.4:1 and are what has to be legible. Filling it with accent would have made it the brightest object in a section whose subject is a title.
Nothing else is re-decided. Ground, hairline and stripe are A17's inherited step; the title is text at 13.9:1 and the standfirst muted at 6.1:1; no shadow is drawn in either mode, this design having nothing raised in it. A 50 px serif in dark looks lighter than the same serif in light ⚑ and the pack cannot compensate without a per-mode weight — noted here, drawn at its worst on 10 Big Type.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. Tag, title, standfirst and byline centred on one axis at three narrowing measures — 820, 720, 720 — with the feature image beneath at the full content width. The category default, the design a switch falls back to, and the only one that changes nothing in the Post block. 2 · Structural descriptor. stack · none · page · one · bottom · centred column
Containment none: the section has no box; the picture's radius is the picture's. Separates from 16 Two Column on archetype (one axis against two columns) and from 4 Image Top on media placement alone — above the words and below them are two designs, and this slot is what says so. 3 · Archetype. stack. No departure. It narrows and re-sizes; nothing reorders, nothing moves column, and the DOM order is the reading order at every width. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 margin 72, title 50 on 820, standfirst 19 on 720, meta 13, picture 1,296 at 16:9, gaps 24/20/24/40, padding 96 above, 64 to the article. 1080 unchanged but for the margin. 834 margin 40, title and standfirst both take the 754 measure, title 42, standfirst 18, padding 80. ≤ 767 margin 20, title 32, standfirst 17, gaps 20/16/20/32, reading time leaves the meta (A18's rule) and is not drawn elsewhere, padding 64. Alignment never changes with width ⚑. 5 · Content fields. Read from the post: title, custom_excerpt, feature_image + feature_image_alt, tags[], authors[], published_at, reading_time. Authored: post.feature_image_caption (text, optional, 90 chars) and readingTimeSuffix (text, optional, 16 chars, default "min read"). In the union, unread: shareLabel, copiedLabel, bylinePrefix, post.url. 6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Alignment Left · Centre. Title size Small 40 · Medium 50 · Large 64. Standfirst lines Two · Three · Full. Image ratio Landscape 3:2 · Wide 16:9 · Square 1:1 · Portrait 4:5. Rule None · Above the tag · Below the meta. Padding Compact 64 · Comfortable 96 · Spacious 132. Six, then the block's four, all at their defaults and none narrowed, set, locked, ignored or interpreted. Cut: title colour, an eyebrow chip, an updated-date toggle, caption position.
7 · Data. The route's post, on any post or page template. There is no zero case — no post, no route, no section — which is the one respect in which A24 is easier than every collection category. Tags: 0 → no eyebrow; 1 → as drawn; 3 → three at All tags, dot-separated; 4+ → the first three in Ghost's order and the rest dropped, with no counter ⚑. Authors: 1 → as drawn; 2 → two overlapping avatars and "and"; 3+ → three avatars and "Name and 2 others", each name its own link. On a page with no author, Meta resolves to Date only. 8 · Empty state. No picture → Reflow: the picture's box and its 40 px gap are both removed and nothing replaces them — no plate, no placeholder, no striped rectangle on a live page. No standfirst → its 20 px goes with it. No tag → the eyebrow slot is removed, not filled. All four absent leaves a title and a byline, and that is still this design, drawn in the stress frame. In the editor an absent picture draws the box greyed with "This post has no feature image. Add one in Ghost, or set Feature image to Off." — the only place a placeholder appears anywhere in A24. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical — every element is text, an <img> or an <a>, and the two links go to routes Ghost already serves. Edit-safe. reveal was considered and refused: a title that fades in is a title that is not there when the page paints, and it is the page's h1. 10 · Accessibility. <header> holding the page's one <h1>; nothing else in the section is a heading. DOM order tag → title → standfirst → byline → picture, matching the visual order at every width. The tag is an <a> to its archive and each author name an <a> to theirs, both on 44 px targets; the date is <time datetime> and the reading time plain text. The avatar is alt="" ⚑ — the name is beside it — and the picture takes feature_image_alt, falling back to an empty alt and never to the title. A6's ring at 4 px. Title 13.1:1, standfirst 5.4:1, eyebrow 5.4:1. Print: as drawn at 26 pt, picture kept, links absent. Repeating items. None authored. The two Ghost arrays are covered in field 7; there is no Add, no Remove, no reorder and no per-item control, and selecting the header on the canvas gives the user post.feature_image_caption and readingTimeSuffix and nothing else. Reconciled. Field 6's Padding row is struck and is the universal Vertical spacing (64 · 96 · 132), leaving five controls. Field 5's post.feature_image_caption is replaced by Ghost's post.feature_image_caption, drawn as HTML, governed by Caption: From Ghost · Hide; field 8's caption sentence and field 10's figcaption follow it. The Post block gains Avatar (Off · Small 24 · Medium 36 · Large 44) and Image focus (Centre · Top · Bottom — the category's focal-point refusal withdrawn ⚑), and Meta gains Author only and Author, updated date and reading time (updated_at, past a day's gap). Field 7 gains one line: on a page with “Show title and feature image” off, the section does not render. The joins in field 7 — “and”, “and 2 others” — are catalog strings, and readingTimeSuffix edits inline with P0·1.Flagged ⚑ — the 820 title measure and its ladder; the avatar filled with border rather than accent, against the calibration reference; three tags capped with no counter; the two-author join and "and 2 others"; 16:9 as the default ratio; Alignment never switching by width; the picture's alt never falling back to the title; the editor's greyed picture box as A24's only placeholder.
