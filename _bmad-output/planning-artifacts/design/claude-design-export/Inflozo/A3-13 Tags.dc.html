The chip: surface fill, one hairline, the pack's radius, 15 px label in text with an optional 12 px muted count, 7 × 13 padding for a 35 px box. Every chip is the same size regardless of its count — a weighted tag cloud is a data visualisation, and a footer is not the place for one. Tags are ordered by post count and truncated at the chosen number, with the overflow as a plain link, never a thirteenth chip.

1 · A <nav> labelled by its visible <h2>, holding a <ul> — these are links to archives, so unlike A3·9's post feed this genuinely is navigation. 2 · Each chip is one link whose accessible name is the tag alone; the count is inside the link but marked so it reads as “Essays, 64 posts” rather than “Essays 64”. Flagged as mine: the wording. 3 · The current tag keeps aria-current="page" and stays a link. Its contrast ground is not the only signal — it is also the only chip whose label is the carried colour, and aria-current is what carries it to a screen reader. 4 · The overflow is the list's last item, a plain link named “All 34 tags”, pointing at the site's tag index. Its number is generated, and if the site has no tag index page the link is not rendered rather than pointing nowhere. Targets · 35 px at desktop where a mouse is the input, 41 px at ≤ 767 with an 8 px gap giving 49 px of rhythm. Chips never rely on the border alone: the fill differs from the ground at every ground.
