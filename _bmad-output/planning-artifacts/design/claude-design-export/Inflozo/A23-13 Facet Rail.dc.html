THE FINDING THIS DESIGN RAISES
Two facets at once is not expressible in a Ghost theme ⚑ — routes give a theme one taxonomy at a time, so “Reporting, by Ida Brandt, in 2026” has no URL. The design is single-select and says so rather than drawing checkboxes that cannot combine.A finding for the architect, not a module request. The closest registry module is filter-strip, which this design declares; multi-facet filtering would need a query the platform does not expose, and naming a new module would not create one.Year is the shakiest facet ⚑ — Ghost has no year archive route by default; a publication must add one in routes.yaml. At Facets: Sections and writers — the default — no year rows are drawn, and the editor says why.

The counts beside the facets are the archive’s, not the query’s ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme a tag’s post count cheaply and the intersection of a tag and a query not at all. Stated on the frame and in the rail’s own footnote, because a reader who reads “Interviews 31” and then sees no matches has been told something untrue unless the design says which number it is.

THE SEARCH SOURCE · SEVEN ROWS · IDENTICAL WHEREVER RESULTS ARE DRAWN · NOT COUNTED
What it searches
Posts and pages▾
Posts · Posts and pages · Posts, pages and tags · Everything, including authors. Ghost decides what a theme can reach; this row says which of it this section asks for ⚑.
Search in
Titles and excerpts▾
Titles and excerpts · Full content. Full content is available here and is not in Ghost’s own bundle ⚑ — A23’s index is client-side over the Content API, which can return plaintext. It costs index size: roughly 4–8 kB a post, so a 400-post archive fetches a few megabytes before the first query. Titles and excerpts is the default, and this row is what the read-only line below reports.
Before a query
Recent searches, then featured▾
Recent searches, then featured · Featured posts · Latest posts · Nothing. Recent searches are the reader’s own and never leave their browser ⚑; a first visit falls through to the second value. “Most read” is gone ⚑ — Ghost gives a theme no view counts, so it was a promise the platform cannot keep.
Start searching after
3 characters▾
2 · 3 · 4 characters. A1·9’s three-character minimum and 200 ms debounce, carried ⚑.
How many results
20▾
10 · 20 · 50 · All matches. The cap is the section’s, not Ghost’s — the count line always names the true total ⚑.
Match highlight
MarkedPlain
A real <mark> at 16 % accent, 22 % in dark, in the title and the excerpt and never in the meta ⚑. At Plain it is absent from the DOM rather than untinted. Promoted here from 11 Thumb Rows, so every design that draws a result governs it in the same row ⚑.
What is indexed
Title, excerpt and slug🔒
Read-only, and it follows Search in ⚑. At Titles and excerpts it reads title, excerpt and slug; at Full content, title, excerpt, slug and body text. emptyText’s default follows the same value, which is why that sentence is a field and not a string.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESfilterHeading, sectionLabel, writerLabel and yearLabel edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — they are the rail’s own words. The facet rows are Ghost’s tags, authors and dates and their counts ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. countLabel, emptyHeading, emptyText, recentLabel and suggestLabel are edited through the P0·6 switcher (No query · Results · No matches) ⚑.
DATA · GHOST’S OWN, AND NEVER AN AUTHORED LISTGhost tags, authors and — where the site has the route — dates: no Add, no Remove, no drag ⚑, and a facet group with fewer than two rows is not drawn. No Member Visibility row ⚑ — facets are navigation.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESNo multi-select value ⚑ — not a simplification but a platform limit, stated in the panel: two facets at once has no Ghost route, so a checkbox here would be a control that cannot be honoured.No “sort” control.
