4 · Contrast Band

A full-bleed inverted band carrying the head and one form row, both centred. The section’s own vertical padding is 0; the band carries it.

It is the loudest arrangement in the category and the only one that spends the section’s whole colour budget on its ground — which is why the button here is the carried colour and not the accent.

WHAT THE BAND COSTS AND WHAT IT BUYS
The section’s own vertical padding is 0 at every width ⚑ — the band carries 48 · 72 · 104 inside itself instead, so two bands stacked never draw a gap of page ground between them.No accent appears anywhere on this design ⚑ — A29·3’s ratio finding. The one place the pack’s accent would go is the button, and the button takes the carried colour.In dark the band inverts the other way: contrast is #EDE7DA carrying #171511, so a dark page gets a light band ⚑.

Focus on the band is the carried colour, not the accent ⚑ — the 1.5 px border goes to carried because accent on contrast is 4.0:1 and a focus border that fails contrast is not a focus indicator. Invalid keeps the same substitution: border and message in the carried colour at 100%, note at 72%. The band’s height is identical in all seven states.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label and the note. The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost” — and on the band the note, the error and the focus border take the carried colour, not the accent. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
