Lead + Grid

One post given two columns and the rest arranged beside and beneath it. The design where the span is not a control — every other design either offers it or ignores it; here it is the structure, and turning it off would leave 1 Three Up.

Which makes it the one design in A17 that promotes a post without being told to. The lead is the first post in the set’s order ⚑ 29 August 2026 — position, not the featured flag, because a Ghost template cannot look across a loop to find the flagged post. A design whose subject is the lead cannot have no lead, and by position it always has one. A17-0's settlement 3 now reads position rather than flag, and the panel repeats it on the frame.

It carries five controls rather than six. The lead is a fixed relationship — 2fr to 1fr, excerpt on the lead only — and once that is settled there are five things left worth deciding. A sixth would be a control over the lead's size, which is the design.

WHY SIX, AND WHY NO FIRST CELL CONTROLTag and “View all” link added; Padding retired into Vertical spacing.With photograph applies to every cell, lead included — one value, one section, as A17-0 requires; the lead does not get its own meta. The brief allows four to seven and this design has five, which is a finding rather than an omission. Once the lead's proportion, its excerpt and its promotion rule are fixed by the design, the remaining decisions are: which side it sits on, how many follow beneath, the ratio, the meta, and the section's padding. A sixth would have to be a control over the lead's size or its excerpt, and both of those are the design. Padding out to six would be worse than stopping at five. No First cell control, and the reason is the inverse of 4 Cards'. There, a span was possible and wrong; here it is compulsory. This is the only design in A17 where a First cell control would have no meaning — the closest thing to it would be “lead the newest instead of the featured”, which is what the design already does when nothing is featured.
