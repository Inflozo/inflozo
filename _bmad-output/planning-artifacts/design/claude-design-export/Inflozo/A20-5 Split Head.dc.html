5 · Split Head

The head stands in a 320 column on the left and the topics run as two ruled lists on the right. A8·6's split layout, carried into A20 verbatim: a grid-column move rather than an order swap, so the DOM keeps reading head-then-list.

This is the design for a long introduction. The head column can carry three lines of explanation without pushing the topics down the page, because it is beside them rather than above them.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESFour controls, not five. There is no Heading control: the head column exists to carry a heading and an introduction, and a design whose left column can be turned off is 1 Chips with extra steps ⚑.Head width and List columns interact and are not guarded.
