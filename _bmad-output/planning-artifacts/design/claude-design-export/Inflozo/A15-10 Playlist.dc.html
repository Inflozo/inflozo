10 · Playlist

The player on a surface plane with the rest of the set queued beside it: one row per film, the current one marked, the whole list reachable without leaving the section.

It is the section for a course, a season or an archive — where the reader picks one film, then another, and the list is as much the point as the picture.

THE MODULE, AND WHAT IT COSTS
This design declares tabs as well as video-facade ⚑. Each queue row is a tab and the player is its panel — which is what a playlist is, structurally, and it is the closest thing in the registry to a queue.No-JS, quoted: “All panels render stacked and visible, each preceded by its tab label as a heading.” A15 reads that as: the plane renders every film stacked ⚑, each with its own poster, title and meta line, in authored order — six frames instead of one player and a queue. Longer, and everything is reachable.There is no autoadvance and no “play all” ⚑ — the queue is a list of choices, not a schedule, and a section that starts the next film on its own is autoplay by another name.

Choosing a row swaps the player and nothing else moves ⚑ — the plane, the queue and the head all hold their positions, which is the whole reason the box is reserved. The queue does not scroll to follow the current film ⚑ unless the reader scrolls it. No third-party request is made until the reader acts ⚑ — settlement 3, and there is no “load with the page” value anywhere in A15 ⚑. — and the notice is drawn once, on the first film the reader plays, not on every swap ⚑.

No layout, spacing, alignment or emphasis is editable inside an item: every design control writes one value onto the section, so “make film 3 bigger” is not expressible by construction ⚑.

A15 reads nothing from Ghost but @site.title ⚑, so nothing here is Ghost-owned and no line shows the “Edit in Ghost” lock — the one Ghost value is the aria-label fallback, which is the site title and not a field. Every URL field opens the Link Picker — transcriptUrl and the per-item URL included. Buttons accept an optional icon before or after the label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size / colour-role popover, off by default ⚑; the play control is not an icon slot ⚑ — it is A15’s own component and its glyph is not swappable. Upload only ⚑ — an embed is never asked to autoplay; the facade exists to prevent the request. Ambient loop is muted, looped and playsinline ⚑ — muted is what makes autoplay browser-legal, which is why the value cannot be unmuted. The poster stands in under reduced motion and with no JavaScript ⚑.
ARCHITECT: REGISTRY ADDITION (ambient loop)Gating an autoplaying muted loop on prefers-reduced-motion is behaviour no registry module covers ⚑. The no-JS and no-module state is designed anyway — the poster with the native controls, which is Click to play — and no module name is coined.
Editor-side seeding, new in this pass ⚑: pasting a recognised URL fetches the oEmbed title and duration once, in the editor, and seeds both fields; “Use provider thumbnail” downloads the image and re-hosts it as the poster ⚑. The reader’s browser still never touches the provider — settlement 3 protects the reader, not the editor — and the typed-duration chore 10 Playlist’s running total depended on is gone.
