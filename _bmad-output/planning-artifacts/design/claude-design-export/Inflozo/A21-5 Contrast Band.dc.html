5 · Contrast Band

The roster as a full-bleed inverted band: heading and blurb in the band's own ink, then six portraits in a row with names and counts. The band carries its padding and the section carries none.

It is the design that ends a page. Six faces on the contrast token read as a colophon, and the band gives the masthead a boundary that a grid on the page ground cannot.
