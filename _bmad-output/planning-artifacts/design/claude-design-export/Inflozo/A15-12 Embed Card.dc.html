12 · Embed Card

One embed inside a hairline box that names what it is and where it loads from, with the notice above the frame rather than on it. The design for everything that is not a film.

It is the section for an audio episode, a map, a form, a data frame — the embeds a publication ends up with — and the place A15 says out loud what a third-party frame costs the reader.

No poster, no title and no duration each close up exactly as drawn. Embeds are unaffected. YouTube, Vimeo and the audio providers keep this design, its facade, its click-to-load notice and their refusal to autoplay. No layout, measure, type size, colour or spacing changed on this frame ⚑, and the design’s number is unchanged. This design’s own delta. A SECOND FINDING FOR THE ARCHITECT ⚑
