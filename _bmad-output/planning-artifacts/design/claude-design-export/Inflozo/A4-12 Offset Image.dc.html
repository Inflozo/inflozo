THE HEADER FINDING · why this design cannot take A1·4 Overlay
Orbit Weekly Essays Sign inSubscribe
half the bar on the page's ground, half on the picture
A1·4 Overlay has one colour setting for the whole bar — Light, Dark or Match pack — and this design's picture covers only its right-hand end. Either the nav is illegible on the ground or the actions are illegible on the picture, and no single value fixes both. So A4·12 declares header mode Below: A1·4's own rule then applies, it falls back to A1·1 Rail on surface, and the picture starts under the Rail's hairline. This corrects A4-0's settlement 1, which listed five Overlap designs on the assumption that any hero with a picture at its top edge could carry a transparent bar. The correct condition is narrower: the picture must span the header's full width. Four designs do — 4 Full Bleed, 10 Video Poster, 14 Full Height, 18 Overlap Card — and A4-0 has been amended to say so. Flagged as mine, and flagged as a correction rather than a design decision.

The section's height is the taller of the picture and the text block plus its padding, and the shorter one centres against it — which is why the three values change the composition rather than just the picture. At Short the picture reads as an inset panel beside the words; at Tall it reads as the page's edge. Flagged as mine: the three heights and the centring rule.
