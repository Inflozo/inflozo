Eighteen arrangements of a post

A17's content is posts, and the user does not author them. There is no repeater, no Add, no Remove and no drag handle in this whole category: the section is handed a query, Ghost answers it, and the eighteen designs draw whatever comes back. That single fact is where most of A17's decisions come from — the count is a count rather than a list, the order is a rule rather than a sequence, and every optional field is optional on a per-post basis, decided by an author who was writing a post rather than filling a section.

It is also the category that sets the post card, and the post card is the most reused component in the library: A18 Post Lists, A19 Featured, A20 Tag Collections, A21 Author Showcases and A27 Related Posts all inherit it verbatim. Nine categories depend on the six decisions on this page, which is why the card is settled here in more detail than any earlier component.

This file carries the four settlements §8 asks for, the post card and its anatomy, the ratio and cell ladders, the rules all eighteen share, the Data group that replaces A12's repeater, the twenty-field shared list, the roster, the nine posts every frame draws, the tokenisation proof across three packs and the stress frame. The prose companion is “A17 Post Grids — Spec.md”. Where the two disagree, the drawn panel is the authority. Every invented decision is marked ⚑.

⚑ Sequencing note. A13–A16 are briefed and not drawn; this category was run ahead of them at the manager's instruction. Nothing in A17 depends on A13–A16, and the components it inherits are all from A1–A12. Where a later category would have supplied something — a gallery's crop ladder, a video poster — A17 states its own and flags it.
