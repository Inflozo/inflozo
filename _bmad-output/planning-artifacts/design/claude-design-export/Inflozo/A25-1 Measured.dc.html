The article on its measure with the index in one margin and the share rail in the other — the reading page as most publications draw it, and the design every other one in A25 is a departure from. It is the only design in the category that offers both pieces of furniture at full strength.

Everything here is drawn in margin the measure already had. The rest of the category spends those margins differently or refuses to spend them at all.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Measure, Type scale and Drop cap are the category's three quick controls and every one of the twelve carries them, in that order, at the top of the panel. What a design adds below them is its own argument — here it is the two pieces of furniture, which is why this design is the default: a user who wants the ordinary reading page gets it, and both margins are live if they want them. Table of contents and Share rail are the only controls in A25 that add anything outside the measure. They are drawn from C Post Body's six unchanged, and the two values that collide — index Right and rail Rail — resolve the way C drew them rather than by disabling either. No value in this panel is ever greyed out ⚑: every combination of the six produces a page, and the two that produce less than the user expects (index under three headings, rail Off) say so in their own line rather than in a disabled state. Cut: an index width control. 240 is the width A24·12 Rail set and this is the same margin on the same grid; a 180 index wraps three of the fixture's four entries and a 300 index eats the gap. Cut: a rail position control — the rail follows the index to the opposite margin, so its position is already decided by a control the user can see. Cut: an index depth control (H2 · H2 and H3). It was drawn and refused: the fixture's H3s are sub-steps of their H2, and an index that indents them is a second structure to read before the first line of the article. 6 Hanging Heads is the design where heading depth is visible, and it is a control there because the headings are the layout. The Cards note is not a control and is in every A25 panel. A callout that changed shape per post design would be unshippable as CSS, which C settled; the panel sends the user to the Cards module instead.

One departure: the archetype's ladder collapses side furniture at 834, and this design collapses it at 1,200 ⚑ because its furniture needs 288 px of margin rather than a breakpoint's worth. No-JS, quoted: toc — "No TOC renders. Because it is built from {{content}} client-side there is no server-side equivalent — the article itself is unaffected, which is why FR-G4 is still satisfied." The share link declares nothing ⚑ — <a href="#/share"> is an ordinary anchor, so there is no module to degrade; Ghost’s modal is Ghost’s own client-side flow, and with JavaScript off the link opens nothing — a platform limitation the theme cannot fill. Edit-safe: neither runs while editing, and the resting state drawn here is what the editor shows. 10 · Accessibility. The section is <article>; it carries no h1 — A24's header owns the page's only one — and Ghost's own h2/h3 keep their levels and never skip one ⚑. The index is a <nav aria-label="On this page"> holding an ordered list of in-page links, placed after the article in the DOM and before it visually ⚑, so a screen reader reaches the words first.
