THE RAIL’S ITEMS · AUTHORED ON /SIGNUP/, FIXED ON /ACCOUNT/
On /signup/ the rail is the benefits field — 0 to 6 authored lines, and with none the rail is absent and the content column takes the content box ⚑. That is the same field 1 Centred draws as a tick list.On /account/ the rail is fixed and is not authored ⚑: Membership, Newsletters, Billing, Sign out. There is no Add or Remove — three of the four are Ghost’s own surfaces and one is a link. Only their labels are authored, at 24 characters each.Rail sticks uses position: sticky and declares no module for that ⚑; scroll-spy is declared for the active-row marking alone, and with JavaScript off the first row is marked and nothing tracks — the registry’s own words.

The rail is unaffected by every form state ⚑ — it is the design’s fixed element, which is why this arrangement suits an account page more than a signup one. At ≤ 767 the rail is above the form, so the states sit below a wrapped row of links.

Everything that changes a subscription
Ghost Portal🔒
Read-only. Plan changes, checkout, billing, email changes and newsletter preferences are Portal’s ⚑ — the theme draws the trigger and Portal draws the rest. This is the category’s boundary and no control moves it.
The rail rows open Portal’s named panels ⚑, new in this pass — account/plans, account/newsletters and account/profile. They exist, which answers the spec's open question; the row's accessible name still ends "opens in Ghost's Portal" and Billing is absent on a comped membership.Member buttons are conditional ⚑ — every Sign up, Subscribe and paid-tier button is absent where the connected site cannot support it: self-signup switched off, members disabled, or no payment provider connected. Ghost seeds a $5 tier at install, so an unconnected site would otherwise ship a card whose checkout cannot complete. The rows stay in this panel, disabled, with the reason readable.Ghost’s own pop-up needs JavaScript ⚑ — every button that opens Portal, including every tier deep link and every hand-off on the account page: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. The theme cannot substitute anything for it, so the notice is the panel’s, not the page’s.Nothing identifying is printed ⚑, new in this pass — no member name, no member email, and no join date on any of the thirteen. Ghost gives a theme no join date and no list of a member’s newsletters, so both rows are gone: the newsletter row is a link out to Ghost’s own account screen, and the email row hands off the same way. Layout and actions are ours; anything that names the reader is Ghost’s account panel’s.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: eyebrow, heading, blurb, ctaLabel, note, legal, benefits[], the four railLabels and every state string. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the member's name and email, the tier name, the price, the renewal date, the newsletter names and a tier's description and benefits all answer "Edit in Ghost" or show P0·1's lock pill. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, Portal actions first. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the rescued literals above are fields with defaults, and the badge vocabulary and sending word are catalog strings. There is no Preview control in this panel and there never was one to remove — states are P0·6's switcher, audiences are View as.benefits[] is the P0·3 authored list ⚑ — drag to reorder, per-row overflow with Duplicate and Remove, Add arrives with content rather than an empty shell, Remove is never disabled, and the range line reads 0–6 · 4 used. Add disables at six with its reason in a sentence. Per-item content only: a name and a detail edit inline, and no per-item layout control exists ⚑. Tiers are the other list and are Ghost’s — no Add, no Remove, no drag.The one icon slot is the primary button’s ⚑ — P0·2's button-icon rules: Before or After the label, always Small, always the label's colour, its Size and Colour rows hidden. The list marker is the second where a design draws ticks, and it is whole-list.Member Visibility lands nowhere in A30 ⚑, and it is a judgement rather than an omission: the route is the member state. A signed-in member never sees a signup form — the Data group's "A signed-in member on this page" decides what they see instead — so a section-level Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid row would hide a page from readers the route has already sorted. P0·4’s member-aware action editor never opens here for the same reason. Recorded in the Reconciliation notes.The /account/ rail’s rows are fixed and are not an item list ⚑ — their labels are editable, their targets are Portal's named panels, and Billing is absent on a comped membership. Their accessible names end "opens in Ghost’s Portal".

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe rail’s contents are not a control ⚑ — they are the route: the benefits field on /signup/ and four fixed rows on /account/. A control offering to put the account sections on the signup page would be offering to link to pages a visitor cannot reach.“PORTAL ↗” is not a control either. It is drawn wherever a row leaves the theme, in every design in A30 ⚑, and a user cannot turn it off — hiding it would be hiding where the reader is about to go.Six controls and no seventh. Cut: a rail heading, a counts value (there is nothing to count), and a rail-on-mobile value — the wrapped row is the only arrangement that works ⚑, so it is a rule and not a choice.Reconciled. The rail’s rows are wired ⚑ — account/plans, account/newsletters and account/profile, Portal's named panels, which exist: the spec's open question is answered and the two rows that assumed it are no longer assuming. Padding retired into Vertical spacing; Sign-in link, Eyebrow, Note line and CTA icon arrive; the /signup/ rail is named as the P0·3 list. Nine controls of its own.This pass · nothing identifying, and two rows deleted. Member since is gone and so is the newsletter list ⚑ — Ghost’s member object carries neither a join date nor a theme-readable list of newsletters, so both rendered empty on every live site. The newsletter row is now a link out to Ghost’s own account screen, and no design prints a member’s own name or email: the head is the authored account heading, the email row hands off, and the avatar goes with the name it was derived from.This pass · the no-JavaScript notice. Ghost’s signup endpoint will not accept a plain form post ⚑ — tested on both live servers — so P0·4’s notice replaces the form rather than the form standing scriptless. Sending, sent, expired and invalid are Ghost’s own script and are unchanged.This pass · the signed-out account page. Ghost does not redirect an anonymous visitor away from /account/ ⚑, so the fourth account tile is a designed signed-out state: a heading, one sentence, a link to /signin/, and no member row at all.

Containment none; the rail draws no box of its own, which is what keeps it from reading as a sidebar card.3 · Archetype. edge rail. One departure: held at 834 rather than collapsing ⚑; the collapse is at 767, to a wrapped pill row.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 rail 240 + gap 48 + content 1,008; rail sticks at 96; form 480 in the content column; padding 96. 834 rail 200 + gap 32 + content 522; padding 80. ≤ 767 rail is a wrapped pill row above the content ⚑, sticking ignored, padding 64.5 · Content fields. 6 · Controls. Rail side Left · Right — Rail width Narrow 200 · Medium 240 — Rail sticks On · Off — Rail marks Show · Hide — Fields — Sign-in link (new ⚑) — Eyebrow (new ⚑) — Note line (new ⚑) — CTA icon (new ⚑).
