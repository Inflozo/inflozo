Thumb Rows

1 Rows with a landscape thumbnail at the leading edge of every row: a 144 × 96 picture, a 24 px gap, then the same tag, title, excerpt and hung meta. This is A18's answer to §8's second question — a thumbnail belongs at the leading edge, before the text, and it is a fixed size rather than a fraction of the row.

A17·13 Thumb Side is the nearest design in the library and the differences are deliberate: that design is a square thumb in two 636 columns with no excerpt; this one is a 3:2 thumb in one 1,296 row with one. Square identifies, landscape shows. A row wide enough to carry a sentence can afford a picture with a shape, and 3:2 is the shape Ghost's feature images already are.
