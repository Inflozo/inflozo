Split

Two columns of unequal standing across the content width: who wrote it on the left — tags and the author block — and where to go next on the right — the share row above the two destinations, one under the other.

The split is the argument: the two halves of a footer answer different questions, and stacking them makes the reader scroll past the first to reach the second. At 834 and below the columns become the stack that 1 Author Bio already is, in the same order.

THE SPLIT COLLAPSES TO ONE COLUMN AT THE MEASURE · NOT AN EMPTY HALF ⚑

When the right column has nothing in it the design stops being a split ⚑: the left column takes the article's measure, the hairline goes, and the section reads like 1 Author Bio without its share row — the same design with an empty column hidden. A hairline with nothing on one side of it is the failure this design has to avoid.
Five and seven is for sites whose authors have no bios — the weight moves to the destinations — and Half is the value that reads as a table, offered because a site with one tag and a one-line bio genuinely balances.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no side control ⚑ — the author is always on the left. A footer whose byline moves side to side between posts of the same site is the thing rule 1 is protecting against, and the two halves are not interchangeable: one is about the piece behind you and one is about the page in front of you.There is no Width control, the only design in A26 without one: a split at the 720 measure is two 336 columns, and a 336 column cannot hold a bio and a 56 px avatar ⚑. This design is the content width; at the measure the panel advises 1 Author Bio instead, and never switches to it.Cut: a column side value, a width value, per-column padding, a vertical alignment value ⚑ — both columns start at the same line and end where their content ends.

POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Bylines carry no number“Rosa Menendez and three others” is now “Rosa Menendez and others”. A template cannot subtract the drawn names from the author count, so the number could only ever have been hard-coded or wrong. The row, the avatars and the type are unchanged.
“More from” uses the full nameThe archive link reads “More from Rosa Menendez”, not “More from Rosa”. Ghost gives a theme one display name and no separate first name on the versions we support, so a first name was never available to interpolate. The default of authorLinkLabel changes with it.
Avatars with no photographThe avatar fallback draws one letter — “Rosa Menendez” shows R. Every person here comes from Ghost, which cannot produce two initials from a display name, so two initials were never available. The circle, its sizes and its colours are unchanged.
Item counts are a number pickerShare links is a stepper, 2 to 4, default 4 — new in this pass, where the row drew Two · Three · Four — drawing the first N of the site’s enabled destinations in the site’s own order. The + is greyed at four with the reason visible: a fifth link takes the row onto a second line. Superseded 1 September 2026: the stepper is cut and sharing opens Ghost’s own share panel.
No design ever turns into another designTwo phrases reworded. An emptying right column previously made the section “1 Author Bio without its share row”; it now reads like it — the same design with an empty column hidden. “This design is the content width or it is 1 Author Bio” becomes advice: at the measure the panel recommends 1 Author Bio and never switches to it.
The no-JavaScript noticeThe claim that the form “posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint” is withdrawn — tested against both live servers, Ghost’s signup endpoint cannot accept a plain form submission. At Blocks Everything the inherited subscribe bar now replaces the form with a designed notice when JavaScript is unavailable: one sentence in text-muted at 15, in the space the field and the button occupied, the bar keeping its ground, padding and words. The sent, error and loading states are unchanged and still apply. Drawn in full on 12 Subscribe.
Member buttons are conditionalThe inherited Subscribe button does not render when the connected site cannot support it — self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected for the paid ask — and the editor says which. Any button that opens Ghost’s own sign-up pop-up carries the line: with JavaScript off, nothing happens.
POST FOOTERS PATCH · PASS TWO · 1 SEPTEMBER 2026 · INCLUDING THE NATIVE-SHARE RULING · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Sharing is Ghost’s own share panel, everywhereOne trigger, and Ghost’s panel owns the destinations. The drawn share block is now a label and a single Share control; no platform links, no brand glyphs, no per-section or site-wide destination list. Where the app reports an older Ghost, that panel does not exist — the same link opens the sign-in box — so the trigger is replaced by Copy link and Email, two links in the row the destinations used to fill, and the editor panel greys its Share destinations row with the reason beside it. The version comes from a capability flag the app sets when the site is connected ⚑, because a theme cannot ask Ghost its version. The site setting is reduced to two switches, Copy link and Email. This supersedes the earlier ruling of the same day that we would not use Ghost’s panel at all.
A control switched off by another is greyed, with the reason beside itThe Share destinations row is greyed with the reason beside it — Ghost’s panel owns the destinations — and the old 2-to-4 stepper is cut outright rather than greyed: a control with nothing to do is not a disabled control. On an older Ghost the row stays greyed and says which. There is no Width row at all — a split at 720 is two 336 columns — and the panel says why rather than greying one.
Avatars with no photograph show initials, and the two forms are not interchangeableOne letter, and it does not change. “Rosa Menendez” draws R, because every person in A26 comes from Ghost and Ghost gives a theme one display name it cannot promise is splittable. Two initials belong to a list the user types, and this design has none, so the two forms are never mixed here. No circle, size, colour or crop changed.
A count that picks between drawn layouts is a named set, not a number pickerThe 2-to-4 stepper is cut, so the rule has one fewer subject here — nothing in this panel counts destinations any more. Blocks stays a named set: five values, each an arrangement that has a frame.
A design may declare the width below which its script runsNo width is declared — share was never width-dependent — but the unscripted state changes: the trigger opens Ghost’s panel, which needs JavaScript, so unscripted only Email renders, a mailto: link, and Copy link is hidden with it. The collapse at 834 stays a media query.
Superseded elsewhere on this frameEvery older mention on this frame of platform destinations, a site-wide ordered list, a 2-to-4 stepper or per-platform brand glyphs is superseded by the row at the top of this panel. The drawn share block, the panel’s destinations row and the no-JavaScript quote are already changed; the surrounding prose from earlier passes is left in place rather than half-rewritten, so a reader can see what the ruling replaced.
POST FOOTERS PATCH · PASS FOUR · 3 SEPTEMBER 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
The all-tags link is authored, not assumedNo subject on this frame — the archive link lives on 8 Tag Row alone, and it is the category's only authored URL. The ruling is recorded here because the field list is shared: the destination is authored, has no default, and the row does not render until it is set.
An author’s post count may be shown, and a design may turn it offNo subject — the post count is 10 Big Type's alone, and no other design in A26 reads a derived figure. The ruling is recorded here because the field list is shared: the count stays, with an off switch, defaulting to Hide.
The pack supplies its contrast colours, and they are COMPUTEDEvery pack value is marked COMPUTED or AUTHORED, and on-contrast text, accent-on-contrast, dark elevation, dark hover-surface and tabular figures are all COMPUTED — the pack supplies them, so no design works out its own readable text colour for a coloured band. No subject on this frame: nothing here derives a colour for itself.
A control’s dependency is declared in its own definition, with its reasonThe greyed Share destinations row keeps its reason, and the reason is no longer drawn by hand: the dependency is declared in the control’s own definition and carries its reason, so the panel, the checker and the compiler read one source.
The answers with no subject in this designThree of the answers carried into this pass have no subject here. Which section is the main feed — the project file stores it, and A26 owns no feed, no query empty state and no page-number control. Hand-picked posts keep the order the user dragged them — A26 authors no array at all, and the tags, the authors and the two neighbours are Ghost's in Ghost's order. A design may declare more than one behaviour module — this design declares two — share and member-form — and the compiler emits the union, which has been true since the inventory merge.
