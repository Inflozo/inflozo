Sticky Index

An index in one margin that holds at 96 px from the top of the viewport and marks the section the reader is in, with the share mark travelling on it — one column of furniture that follows, rather than two pieces pinned to two margins.

It is the only element in A25 that leaves the document flow, and the only design in the library that declares three behaviour modules. The resting state is the primary frame, because behaviours do not run while editing and a design that is only composed once it moves is not composed.
