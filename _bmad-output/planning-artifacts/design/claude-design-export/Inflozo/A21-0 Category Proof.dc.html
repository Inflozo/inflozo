1 · The writer block. Portrait, name, post count, two lines of bio and a link to the writer's archive — A1·6's avatar with its initials fallback, A29·12's meta line, A1's ghost action. Established in 1 Grid and reused verbatim in 2, 3, 4, 7, 12 and 13. Social links are drawn in 6 Founder and, at Social links: On, in 1 Grid, 2 Cards, 12 Carousel and 13’s lead ⚑. Most authors still fill in none, so every absent handle drops and nothing is reserved.2 · One writer against twelve. 6 Founder is built for exactly one — portrait at picture scale, the full bio, one accent action — and at many it draws the first in the chosen order and says so. 11 Rail, 10 Directory and 7 Panel are built for twelve and hold their arrangement down to one. The other eleven degrade to one without changing arrangement, and four of them (1, 2, 4, 10) suggest 6 Founder in the panel without switching to it ⚑ — the user's chosen design is never silently changed.
3 · No bio and no picture. Absence is the normal case, not an empty state ⚑: most Ghost authors have neither. No picture → A1·6's initials on hover, at 14% white on a contrast band and 18% white over an image ⚑. No bio → the lines go and the block closes up; nothing is reserved anywhere in A21. 11 Rail draws no portraits at all and is the design for a publication with no photographs on file, and 9 Big Type's Portraits: Hide is the same answer at display scale.4 · Zero, one and many, and the link out. Every design states all three. Zero is reachable — a site whose only posts are drafts returns no authors — and the shared source group offers Head and notice or Hide the section ⚑. Hiding is offered here and was refused in A29: an archive route with a hidden head is a blank page, but A21 is never the only section on its route. Every writer links to their A29 archive via Ghost's {​{url}​}, and the section cannot repoint it ⚑.

Fourteen authored fields and nine read from Ghost, after the pass. A design may use fewer — 15 Slim draws three — but none needs a field the category does not have, so switching between any two of the fifteen preserves everything the user typed. Eight designs store a bio they never draw ⚑ and four store a heading they never draw ⚑: the field lives on the section, not on the design.

Repeating items, for the whole category, in one place. A21's items are Ghost's authors and no design shows an Add or a Remove control ⚑ — there is no authored array anywhere in this category, which is the difference between it and A20 and A29. How many is a query limit (Three · Six · Twelve · All), Order is one of Ghost's three sorts (Most posts · Name A–Z · Newest account ⚑), and there is no reordering, no per-item styling and no per-item picker — a control writes one value onto the section and the stylesheet reads it. Inside an item the user edits nothing: every field is Ghost's, including the link ⚑.

FINDINGS FOR THE ARCHITECT · EIGHT, FOUR OF THEM NEW
1 · Ghost returns only authors who have published. {​{#get "authors"}​} excludes staff accounts with no published post ⚑, so a masthead cannot show a new hire until their first piece ships and cannot show an editor who commissions rather than writes. Every design in A21 inherits this. It is a data decision, not a layout one.2 · There is no per-author latest post. Drawing "their most recent piece" beside each name needs a {​{#get "posts"}​} inside the author loop — one query per writer ⚑. No design draws it, and 10 Directory's cut column is the visible consequence.3 · There is no authors index route. Ghost has /author/:slug/ and nothing above it, so every "The full masthead" link in 8 and 15 is an authored URL ⚑ pointing at a page the publication built. A20 found the same gap for tags; it is the same missing route.4 · Nothing in the registry sorts a table. 10 Directory's header row is labels, not controls ⚑ — the closest module is filter-strip, which navigates rather than sorts. If a sortable masthead is wanted, that is a registry change.
5 · Ghost authors have no role. There is no job title, no desk and no "Editor" field ⚑ — A12 About and Team draws roles because its people are authored, and A21's are Ghost users. The workaround publications actually use is the first clause of the bio, which is why the bio is drawn in seven designs and stored in the other eight. A role field is a platform decision.6 · count.posts and pagination.total can disagree. A21 draws count.posts; A29 draws the archive's own total ⚑. On a site with members-only posts a reader can see "68 posts" on a writer's card and a shorter archive behind it. Raised in A29 as finding 4 and unchanged here.7 · Ghost stores nine social handles and a website — corrected. facebook, twitter (X), linkedin, threads, bluesky, mastodon, tiktok, youtube, instagram and website on Ghost ≥ 6.36 ⚑; the two-network fact this category was drawn on was 5.x, and the reconciliation pass corrects it. Three consequences for the build: the row is version-gated ⚑ — below 6.36 only Facebook, X and the website exist; Ghost’s own helper is the loop ⚑ 29 August 2026 — it iterates the author’s connected accounts and skips the rest; and the author’s own fields must be read rather than social_url ⚑, which falls back to the site’s Facebook and X and would print the publication’s accounts under a writer who filled in neither. A fourth, new in the design patch pass and now settled against Ghost’s own documentation ⚑ 29 August 2026: the row is drawn with Ghost’s social_accounts helper, passed the author, and the theme assembles no address. Ghost keeps handles in several shapes (bare handle, @handle, full URL, a server for Mastodon and Bluesky), and the helper hands over the finished address, the platform’s own label and a platform key per connected account, skipping any platform with nothing set. The nine are X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon, TikTok, YouTube and Instagram; the website is not among them — it is the author’s own website field, drawn beside the row. The icon is picked from the platform key with a fallback, so a platform Ghost adds before this theme has an icon renders rather than erroring. This closes the question, and there is nothing left for the architect here. 6 Founder draws the row always; 1, 2, 12 and 13 draw it at Social links: On.8 · There is no editorial order for authors. The three sorts are the whole vocabulary ⚑, so "featured writer" is not expressible — 13 Lead and Rest's lead is whoever the sort puts first, and no design offers a picker that would hard-code an author ID into a section.
