This design draws no form ⚑, so it declares no behaviour module and there is nothing in it to degrade: every element is server-rendered text, a link, or a static image. The delivery group is absent from the sidebar and the form fields are kept on the section ⚑, returning the moment the user switches to one of the ten designs that has a form. The address never shrinks to fit ⚑ — a 34-character address at Display 76 wraps at the @ onto two lines and the section grows by 78 px. Type that resizes itself to its content is type the user cannot predict, and the panel offers Large 64 as the value for a long address instead. THE DELIVERY GROUP · ABSENT HEREThis design draws no form ⚑, so the delivery rows are not shown, and the mailto: honesty fix does not reach it: the address at 76 px is itself the link. The rows are kept on the section and return on switching.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesEvery visible text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the small heading line, the address itself and the social row’s label — socialsLabel, default “Also on”. The field labels and the placeholders are edited by clicking them on the canvas ⚑ — this panel holds no label boxes. Ghost-owned values are never inline-editable: at Social accounts: From Ghost a row’s URL is read-only and clicking it says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker. Buttons accept an optional icon before or after the label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size / colour-role popover, off by default ⚑. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: headingSmall, socialsLabel and the address are authored fields with defaults, and “Sending…”, “Send another message”, the failed banner’s first line, “Email us at {address}” and the honeypot’s hidden label are theme translation-catalog strings.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registryNone ⚑ — text and real links: mailto:, tel: and the social URLs. There is nothing to degrade, which is why the form panels offer this design as the fallback A16 always has. No module name is coined, and the contact-form registry addition the form designs ask for does not touch this one.
DATA · Ghost’s surface, and what A16 cannot readNothing is read from Ghost’s content API ⚑ — no Show ladder, no filter, and the P0·5 “Populate from…” panel is not offered anywhere in A16 ⚑, because no design reads a Ghost list. This design draws no action, so Member visibility is not offered ⚑ — recorded rather than invented; a members-only page is A32 Paywall’s ground.
Social accounts

From Ghost reads the site’s own social settings — version-gated ⚑: Facebook and X only on Ghost 5.x, all nine platform fields on ≥ 6.36, each read-only and linking to the setting that owns it. Authored is this section’s own 0–6 list and is the drawn default. Ghost’s rows render regardless and do not count against the six ⚑ — A3’s ruling, carried.
