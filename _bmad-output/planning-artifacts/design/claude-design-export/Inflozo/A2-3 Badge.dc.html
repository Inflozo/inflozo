MEMBER ASK · PART A · A9 · THE ARROW LINK It does not render when the connected site cannot honour it — signup closed, members disabled, or no payment provider for a paid ask. The band closes up: no reserved gap, no empty slot. It opens Ghost’s Portal, so with JavaScript off nothing happens. Nothing in this bar is subscribable without script. It renders when members are on and signup is open; a paid ask needs a payment provider connected and a live paid tier as well; a sign-in link needs members on and nothing more. Withdrawn, label and sentence hold the band and the whole-bar tap target at ≤ 767 goes with it — a bar-wide link to a Portal that cannot open is the dead button the empty-data floor refuses.
