Big Type

Post titles at Display scale on a bare ground, two to a row, and nothing else but a date. The one design in A17 whose display moment belongs to the posts rather than the section — every other design keeps its head a step above the cards; here the titles are the largest type on the page and the head sits below them in size.

It is also the design a site with no photographs chooses. Three designs are drawn around photography — 8 Overlay, 11 Masonry and 18 Edge to Edge — and all three may advise this one when a set has no feature images. Which is the whole reason it draws no images at any setting: a fallback that could show an image is not a fallback.

Its risk is the opposite of every other design's. With four posts it is a statement; with twelve it is a wall. The panel states the count where it stops working and does not pretend the design scales past it.

THREE ACROSS · 416 · LARGE 40 ONLY · DISPLAY UNAVAILABLE · 65% OF THE MEASURE IT NEEDS
Display 48 is unavailable at Per row Three, and this is the one hard dependency between two controls in this design. The disable does not rest on a line count — measured, the 69-character title wraps to four lines at 48 px and to four at 40 px alike in a 416 column, so nothing is saved there. It rests on the cell and the measure. A 416 column is 65% of the 636 the design is drawn for, and at 48 px the title block in it runs 20% taller than at 40 px for no gain in emphasis — which follows directly from 48/40, since the line count is the same — a taller cell saying the same thing. The value is disabled with the reason at the control, as 1 Three Up disables its three-line excerpt at Four. Measured on the primary frame, the four titles' first lines hold 31, 28, 25 and 29 characters. That band — 25 to 31 characters a line — is the design's real measure, and it is read off the render rather than computed, because a character count depends on which characters. Below about 20 big type stops being typography and becomes a stack of fragments, which is what the 416 column would produce. There is no size above Display for the same reason in reverse: a wider line stops reading as a headline. Both sizes tighten their tracking as they grow — Large 40 at -0.025em, Display 48 at -0.03em — and both drop their line height to 1.06 against the card's usual 1.22. A 48 px title on a 1.22 line height reads as a list; on 1.06 it reads as a headline. ⚑ Both values are mine.
