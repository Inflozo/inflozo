6 · Cover

The poster is the section: full bleed at 620, the head over it on A14·11’s warm wash, the play control at the centre. The film opens in the theatre rather than under the words.

It is the section for the film a page is built around — a trailer, a title sequence — and the only design in A15 where type sits on the picture.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND WHY
There is nowhere to upload to, so the Upload source is deleted. Ghost’s public interface has no media library a theme can read, Inflozo is not permitted to put files on a customer’s Ghost, and Inflozo’s own asset system handles images only. Every film in this design is a pasted URL ⚑ — a provider embed, or a media file already hosted on the customer’s own Ghost, which stays first-party and keeps its native <video controls playsinline preload="none" poster>. The ambient loop goes with it. The Playback row — Click to play · Ambient loop — is deleted from the panel: the loop had no source of files. Nothing autoplays anywhere in A15 again, and the reduced-motion registry addition the loop needed is withdrawn — the category is back to four findings. Nothing reads a duration off a file. duration ≤ 8 is typed by the author again, seeded from the provider on paste where the provider supplies one. No poster, no title and no duration each close up exactly as drawn. Embeds are unaffected. YouTube, Vimeo and the audio providers keep this design, its facade, its click-to-load notice and their refusal to autoplay. No layout, measure, type size, colour or spacing changed on this frame ⚑, and the design’s number is unchanged. This design’s own delta. The ambient loop that played under the warm wash is deleted; the poster is what sits under the wash, and the play control opens the theatre on the reader’s action as drawn.
