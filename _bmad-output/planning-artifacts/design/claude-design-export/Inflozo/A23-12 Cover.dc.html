12 · Cover

One field over a full-bleed photograph, with a warm scrim behind the text. The archive’s front door, and the second of the two designs that render no results.

It is the design for the top of a search route or a section index: a picture, a line, a place to type, and a handoff to whichever design draws the results below or after it.

WHAT THE COVER SETTLES
Image focus is a control and a picker popover, not a hidden field ⚑ — changed in this pass: Centre · Top · Bottom, reachable from the sidebar and from the Image Picker, because a cover crop that loses its subject at Height: Short is the commonest complaint about a design like this.The scrim is the pack’s text colour, not black ⚑ — A20·13’s warm scrim. A black wash over a warm photograph greys it, and the imagery rule in the brief is warm and editorial.The image is required and the design is not offered without one ⚑ — unlike every other image in the library, there is no “no image” fallback here, because a cover with no cover is 1 Field and Results with extra padding. The editor says so at the picker.

Four states, and the picture never changes in any of them ⚑ — no scrim deepening on focus, no zoom, no parallax. Behaviours do not run while editing and the resting state is the design, which on a photograph means the resting state is all there is. The other three states belong to the route this field submits to, as in 9 Slim Bar.
