Why a plate and not a reflow. 9 Alternating reflows a pictureless band because a band is alone on its line. Here a reflowed cell would start its title 358 px above its neighbour's and the pair would stop being a pair — so the plate holds the space at the picture's exact ratio and the two titles keep their shared line. This is the rule for every side-by-side design in A19: 10, 11 and 15 plate, 1, 5, 8 and 9 reflow. A plate with no tag is empty — the fill and nothing in it, as in post 4. It is not filled with the publication name, a glyph or the word “Featured”, and A17 settled that. Divider Column rule widens the gutter to 48 and takes 24 from each cell. A19-8 takes its 24 from the lead alone because its columns are unequal; equal cells give equally. The rule runs the height of the taller cell, not of the shorter one, and stops at the last baseline rather than the section's padding.

The numbering ruleThis is still design 10. A19 runs 1 to 15: nothing was renumbered, no number was reused, and no gap was created or closed.
Item counts are a number pickerThe locked Count row is drawn greyed at 2 with its reason visible, which is what the rule's second half asks for and what this design already did. No design ever turns into another designAt one post the left cell is drawn at 636 and the right half is left as page. Two pictureless cells are two plates — honest and ugly — and the panel suggests 12 Picks without switching.
The Remove button never greys outA19's only repeating list is the Hand-picked post picker and remove is never disabled on it. JavaScript off: pixel-identical — nothing in this design runs.
