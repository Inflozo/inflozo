Slim

A label, a title, a date and an arrow on one 64 px line of surface. It is the smallest spotlight in the library — the design for a page that already has a hero and needs to point at one more piece without spending a screen on it.

A bar is a strip of surface on a background ground, and in some of the twelve packs those two tokens are two steps apart and in others they are nearly the same colour. That is what the Rule control is for ⚑, and it is the clearest case in A19 of a control that exists because of tokenisation rather than taste.

64 px tall, the title at 20, and the whole strip is the link. The label and the title sit together at the left with a 20 px gap; the date and the arrow are pushed to the right edge of the content width. There is no call to action label here — “Read the piece” beside an arrow is the same instruction twice, and the arrow is what a 64 px bar has room for.
The arrow is the third accent the floor allows this design and it is the only one it spends. ⚑ It is aria-hidden and decorative: #D96C3F on #FFFFFF is 3.4:1, which clears the 3:1 floor for a graphical object and would fail as text — so it is never the only thing carrying meaning, and the title beside it is the affordance. On hover it moves 4 px right at 160 ms and the title takes its 2 px accent underline; under reduced-motion the arrow does not move and the underline still appears.
The title is never truncated and never clamped. A 69-character title fits on one line at 1440 with 380 px to spare; a 140-character one wraps and the bar becomes two lines. A bar that grows is composed; a bar with an ellipsis in it has thrown away the publication's words, and A19 does not do that at any width.

Rule is a tokenisation control and it is honest about it. Rule 3 says a section must survive a pack change with only tokens changing; a 64 px strip whose only separation from the page is a surface fill is the one arrangement in A19 where that can fail — not because the design moves, but because in a pack where surface and background are one step apart it stops being visible. The control is the fix, it is named in words, and its default is Off because Paper does not need it. The hairlines are full-bleed and the content keeps the 72 px page margin. A rule inset to the content width would draw a 1,296 px line under a 1,440 px strip, which reads as a mistake. Density is 48 · 64 · 80 and the type does not change with it. Compact 48 is the floor: 20 px Georgia with 14 px of air above and below is the tightest a 44 px touch target can be honoured in, and the title never shrinks to fit a shorter bar.

Label Post tag falls back to the section's eyebrow when a post has no tag. ⚑ It is the only fallback between two fields anywhere in A19, and it exists because a bar with a hole at its left edge shifts the title 90 px and looks broken. If the eyebrow is empty too, the label is not drawn and the title starts at the margin, which is Label Off and is composed. Centred drops the detail rather than splitting the line. The label, title and arrow centre as one group; date and reading time have nowhere to go that is not a second alignment, so the setting simply does not draw them. The panel says so under the control. Detail is date or reading time, never both. A19's Meta ladder offers “Name, date and reading time” on designs with room; a bar has one slot at its right and the control names what goes in it. The author's name is never drawn here — the smallest spotlight spends its space on the title.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, drawn at Label Eyebrow. The title, sub, note and link label are in the union and this design reads none of them — a bar has no room for a head and no room for a labelled action. Post content is Ghost's and is never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking a post title, tag, author name or excerpt says “Edit in Ghost”. linkUrl is removed from this sidebar ⚑ — it was read by nothing in all fifteen designs, and a visible URL field nothing reads is a fake control; linkLabel labels the post's own link and stays. Every URL field that remains opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker. This design draws no image at any setting, so rule 10's Image focus has nothing to attach to ⚑. The trailing arrow is a glyph, not an icon slot ⚑ — it is the design's own mark at 16 px, so rule 11's Icon Picker does not reach it; a bar with a chosen icon in it is A2's announcement bar. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the only authored string here is the eyebrow, and the label falls back to the section's eyebrow when a post has no tag. The one authored list in A19 is Hand-picked, which takes the P0·3 item controls in the Data group and nowhere else. No Preview control existed in A19 to remove.

DATA · GHOST’S SURFACE, AND WHAT A19 REFUSESGuaranteed: title · url · published_at · primary_author.name · reading_time. Optional, every empty state drawn: feature_image + feature_image_alt · primary_tag · excerpt · profile_image. featured is the query and is drawn by nothing; visibility read, never drawn (A32's). The leanest read in the category: title, url, primary_tag at Label Post tag, and published_at or reading_time at Detail. No Tag row — Label governs the tag and has done since the design was drawn. Member visibility is offered nowhere in A19 ⚑ — every action here is a link to a post, and a spotlight that hides itself from a logged-out reader is A32's paywall wearing this design's clothes. Ghost cannot rank featured posts, which is what Source: Hand-picked now answers.
WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Density is the strip’s height, and Vertical spacing is the space around it — which is new in this pass. ⚑ Every other design pads a section around its content; here the bar's height is the section, and two controls with named values that both mean “how tall” would be a mistake to explain in a sidebar. The words are the same three so the ladder still reads as the library's. No Ratio, no Title size, no Excerpt, no Meta. A bar reads none of them: there is no picture, the title is 20 at every setting, an excerpt does not fit on a line, and Detail is the meta ladder cut down to one slot. Four controls the rest of the category shares are absent, which is what makes this the smallest control set in A19 by content and not by trimming. A bar is the most likely design in the category to be used twice on one page, and Hand-picked is now the fix — a featured piece near the top and another near the foot. Nothing prevents it, both instances query independently, and if the query and the order match they will draw the same post, which the panel does not warn about ⚑ and is a finding for the architect.
