Overlap

A4·18's shape, carried: a full-bleed image band with a quote card pulled up over its foot. It is the only design in A8 whose geometry depends on the section after it — the card's overhang sits on the next section's ground — and the only one that puts text over a photograph, which is what makes it the place the scrim rule gets stated.

Two settlements. The scrim exists only where text is over the image and nowhere else — no scrim under the card, which is opaque, and none at all at the two head values that keep text off the band. And the overhang is a stated contract, not a visual trick: the section reserves the overhang's height below itself, so the next section starts where it would have anyway and no page needs to know this design is above it.

THE NEXT SECTION STARTS HERE · the 72 px above this line is reserved by this section, not borrowed

The card is the one place in A8 that carries a shadow — the md warm shadow, 0 4px 16px rgba(28,27,26,.08) — and it is allowed here for a reason no other design has: the card is in front of something. A8-0 refuses the shadow on a quote card sitting on a page ground, where it is texture; over the foot of a photograph it is the only thing that says which plane is nearer.
The overhang is 72 px and the section reserves it. The band is 420 px, the card is pulled up 72, and the section's own bottom padding is 72 plus its normal Comfortable step — so the page below is unchanged whether this design is used or not, and a site that follows it with 9 Contrast Band does not get a card floating over a dark band. ⚑ The reservation rule and allowing the md shadow here are mine; the shape is A4·18's.

HEAD ABOVE THE BAND · no text over the image · no scrim at all · the photograph is untouched
The rule, in one sentence: a scrim is drawn only under text, only as far as the text reaches, and only from the contrast token. Here that is #232019 at 55% fading to 0 over the top 200 px — not black, not a full-height overlay and not a uniform 40% wash, both of which flatten a warm photograph into a grey one. Nothing is drawn behind the card, at any value: the card is opaque surface, so the image behind it is not visible and a scrim there would darken the bottom of a photograph for no reader. This is the mistake the rule exists to prevent — a section with a card over an image and a scrim under the whole thing is paying for legibility it already has. The carried colour is what the head text takes over the band — #FBF9F5 for the title, #EDE7DA for the eyebrow — which is 9 Contrast Band's pair reused rather than white invented for this design. Contrast is measured against the darkest 20% of the scrimmed area, and a photograph that cannot hold the title there is the site's crop to fix; the editor says so instead of deepening the scrim until anything passes. ⚑ The 55%-to-0 gradient, the 200 px reach and the measuring rule are mine.

NO SCRIM AT THIS HEAD VALUE · THE PHOTOGRAPH IS UNTOUCHED

In dark the card keeps its border and loses its shadow — a warm shadow on a near-black ground is a grey smudge, and the plane step from #171511 to #211D17 plus the #332E27 hairline is what says the card is in front. This is the one place the md shadow appears in A8 and the one place it is dropped in dark, and both halves of that are stated because the shadow is doing a job here rather than decorating.
Drawn at Head above the band, where there is no scrim at all, which is the value a site with a busy photograph should use: the head sits on the page ground at full contrast, and the image is left as it was shot. Quote and name 15.1:1, role 6.4:1. The image's own darkness is not adjusted in dark mode — the brief's “image scrims adjust” means the scrim, not the photograph, and dimming a reader's portrait because the page is dark would be the theme editing the picture.

1 · One <h2>, over the band or above it depending on the value, with aria-labelledby pointing at it; at head None the section has no accessible name. The band is an <img>, not a CSS background — it is content with imageAlt behind it, like 8 Portrait's photograph and unlike anything else in A8 — and the scrim is a sibling <div>, aria-hidden, never a filter on the image itself. 2 · The scrim is presentational and the contrast it buys is real: title at 12.4:1 and eyebrow at 10.1:1 against the darkest 20% of the scrimmed area, measured rather than assumed. Where a site's photograph is pale at the top the editor says the crop needs changing; the theme does not deepen the scrim to compensate, because a scrim heavy enough to rescue any photograph is a black band with a picture behind it. 3 · <figure> → <blockquote><p> + <figcaption>, no <ul>, no <h3>, no cite, no review markup, avatar aria-hidden. The card is not a link and does not lift, scale or move — it is already the nearest plane, and there is nowhere for a hover to take it. No motion at any value: no parallax on the band, no reveal as the card enters, no scroll-linked anything. A section that overlaps its neighbour is the most tempting place in this library to attach a scroll effect, and it has none. The optional link, where authored, is the only focusable element and takes A6's ring. Forced colours: the scrim disappears with the gradient, so at head Over the band the head moves above the band rather than sitting on an image with no scrim behind it — the one place in A8 where forced colours changes an arrangement, and it is stated here and in the spec. The card keeps its border and reads as the nearer plane.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. The quote, name, role and organisation edit inline on the card too, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater — and the quote edits with the marks toolbar suppressed: A8 puts no bold, italic or link inside somebody else’s sentence. Nothing here is Ghost-owned, so nothing says “Edit in Ghost”. The link opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker and takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Quotes are the P0·3 item list: Add quote arrives with content, Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. The band image carries Image focus — Centre · Top · Bottom in its Image Picker popover, beside its alt text.
