Portrait

The author as a photograph rather than an avatar: a 280 px portrait at 4:5 beside four lines of bio, the name at 28 px, the tags above and the share row under the words.

It is the only place in the library where a person is drawn at picture scale in a footer ⚑, and it is the design for a masthead of five writers rather than a site with a hundred contributors. A profile photograph that is a cropped group shot will look like one at this size, which the panel says out loud.

Bio lines defaults to Four here and to Two everywhere else ⚑: a 350 px portrait beside two lines of prose is a photograph with a caption. Four lines at 17 px is 116 px of text against 350 of picture, which is still unbalanced and is the honest shape of the thing.

An author with no photograph takes the one-letter plate at the portrait crop ⚑ — hover surface with the author's one letter in the heading font at a third of the width — never a stretched avatar and never a stock silhouette. It is A19's Plate at a size A19 never drew it, and it is the reason the design survives a masthead where two of five writers have no picture.
Two authors becomes two half-width portraits side by side ⚑, each with its own name and a three-line bio, and the layout stops being a split: it is a two-up. Three or more draws the primary author alone ⚑ — the owner's ruling of 30 August — {{#primary_author}} giving the portrait, the name, the bio and the archive link, with the co-authors named in one muted line under the name: "with Daniel Reith and others", no number. POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Bylines carry no number“Rosa Menendez and three others” is now “Rosa Menendez and others”. A template cannot subtract the drawn names from the author count, so the number could only ever have been hard-coded or wrong. The row, the avatars and the type are unchanged.
“More from” uses the full nameThe archive link reads “More from Rosa Menendez”, not “More from Rosa”. Ghost gives a theme one display name and no separate first name on the versions we support, so a first name was never available to interpolate. The default of authorLinkLabel changes with it.
Avatars with no photographThe avatar fallback draws one letter — “Rosa Menendez” shows R. Every person here comes from Ghost, which cannot produce two initials from a display name, so two initials were never available. The circle, its sizes and its colours are unchanged.
Item counts are a number pickerShare links is a stepper, 2 to 4, default 4 — new in this pass, where the row drew Two · Three · Four — drawing the first N of the site’s enabled destinations in the site’s own order. The + is greyed at four with the reason visible: a fifth link takes the row onto a second line. Superseded 1 September 2026: the stepper is cut and sharing opens Ghost’s own share panel.
No design ever turns into another design“Three or more hands off to 1 Author Bio” is deleted. Ruled by the owner on 30 August: at three or more authors the design draws the primary author alone — {{#primary_author}} gives the portrait, name, bio and archive link with no loop and no arithmetic — and the co-authors are one muted line under the name, “with Daniel Reith and others”, no number. The design renders as itself at every author count. The cost, stated: at three or more, only one writer gets a photograph and a bio, and the designs that credit everyone equally are 1 Author Bio and 14 Ledger.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject in this design — it contains no subscribe and no sign-in form, so there is nothing for a designed notice to replace. The share links are real <a href> URLs and work unscripted; only the copy-link button is hidden.
Member buttons are conditionalNo subject at rest — this design draws no member action. At Blocks Everything the inherited subscribe bar carries the note, and the note is 12 Subscribe’s.
POST FOOTERS PATCH · PASS TWO · 1 SEPTEMBER 2026 · INCLUDING THE NATIVE-SHARE RULING · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Sharing is Ghost’s own share panel, everywhereOne trigger, and Ghost’s panel owns the destinations. The drawn share block is now a label and a single Share control; no platform links, no brand glyphs, no per-section or site-wide destination list. Where the app reports an older Ghost, that panel does not exist — the same link opens the sign-in box — so the trigger is replaced by Copy link and Email, two links in the row the destinations used to fill, and the editor panel greys its Share destinations row with the reason beside it. The version comes from a capability flag the app sets when the site is connected ⚑, because a theme cannot ask Ghost its version. The site setting is reduced to two switches, Copy link and Email. This supersedes the earlier ruling of the same day that we would not use Ghost’s panel at all.
A control switched off by another is greyed, with the reason beside itThe Share destinations row is greyed with the reason beside it — Ghost’s panel owns the destinations — and the old 2-to-4 stepper is cut outright rather than greyed: a control with nothing to do is not a disabled control. On an older Ghost the row stays greyed and says which. Image focus is not drawn at all and the panel says why — the portrait is Ghost’s profile image, not an authored image field.
Avatars with no photograph show initials, and the two forms are not interchangeableOne letter, and it does not change — including the plate at the portrait crop. “Rosa Menendez” draws R, because the portrait is Ghost’s and Ghost gives a theme one display name it cannot promise is splittable. Two initials belong to a list the user types, and this design has none. The crop, the ratio and the plate’s colours are unchanged.
A count that picks between drawn layouts is a named set, not a number pickerThe 2-to-4 stepper is cut, so the rule has one fewer subject here — nothing in this panel counts destinations any more. Blocks stays a named set: five values, each an arrangement that has a frame.
A design may declare the width below which its script runsNo width is declared — share was never width-dependent — but the unscripted state changes: the trigger opens Ghost’s panel, which needs JavaScript, so unscripted only Email renders, a mailto: link, and Copy link is hidden with it. The collapse at 767 stays a media query.
Superseded elsewhere on this frameEvery older mention on this frame of platform destinations, a site-wide ordered list, a 2-to-4 stepper or per-platform brand glyphs is superseded by the row at the top of this panel. The drawn share block, the panel’s destinations row and the no-JavaScript quote are already changed; the surrounding prose from earlier passes is left in place rather than half-rewritten, so a reader can see what the ruling replaced.
