A26 POST FOOTERS · DESIGN 11 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · SEVEN CONTROLS + THE UNIVERSAL TRIO

Portrait

The author as a photograph rather than an avatar: a 280 px portrait at 4:5 beside four lines of bio, the name at 28 px, the tags above and the share row under the words.

It is the only place in the library where a person is drawn at picture scale in a footer ⚑, and it is the design for a masthead of five writers rather than a site with a hundred contributors. A profile photograph that is a cropped group shot will look like one at this size, which the panel says out loud.

The portrait sets the height and the words fill it ⚑ — 280 × 350 at 4:5, with the tags, name, bio and share row taking the 968 that remains on a 48 gutter. The words are top-aligned and the portrait is top-aligned; neither is centred against the other, because a bio one line shorter should not move a photograph.
Bio lines defaults to Four here and to Two everywhere else ⚑: a 350 px portrait beside two lines of prose is a photograph with a caption. Four lines at 17 px is 116 px of text against 350 of picture, which is still unbalanced and is the honest shape of the thing.
The portrait is profile_image at its natural crop, not a face-detected one ⚑ — Ghost stores one square-ish upload and the theme is cropping it to 4:5, which is a finding: a portrait control that crops from the top would serve most photographs better than one that crops from the centre.

One site-wide ordered list, not a section control ⚑ — X · Facebook · LinkedIn · Bluesky · Mastodon · Threads · WhatsApp · Reddit · Email · Copy link. The owner orders and enables them once with P0·3’s drag-reorder and per-destination enable in the setting, never here; this row is read-only and links to it. EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESInline on canvas with P0·1: authorLinkLabel, shareLabel, copiedLabel. Ghost-owned and never inline-editable: post titles, tag names, author names, bios and social handles — clicking one selects the section and the sidebar says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. The socials row draws under the bio, in the words column ⚑, at 38-in-44, glyphs from P0·2. The portrait carries alt text naming the author — at this size it is content — and the alt is Ghost’s, not authored here ⚑. P0·3’s item controls land nowhere: A26 authors no array — the tags, the authors and the two neighbours are Ghost’s, and a Ghost-sourced list never shows an Add ⚑. P0·4 and Member Visibility land on 12 Subscribe alone in this category. P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere — the footer reads the post’s own relations, not a query ⚑. No Preview control existed to remove; P0·6’s state switcher is how the absent blocks and the confirmations are seen while editing. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: every authored label carries a default, and the strings nobody authors are theme translation-catalog strings.
