Cards

1 Row's division with a card under each stat — A8·2's grid and card, cited whole, holding a number instead of a quotation. For a page whose ground is already carrying an image or a band, where three numbers sitting directly on it read as floating type.

The stat inside is unchanged — same value, same 10 px gap, same 15 px label — so switching between this design and 1 Row adds and removes a container and moves nothing inside it. What the card brings with it is the one thing A10 otherwise refuses: equal heights. Two cards side by side with different fills at different heights is a defect a reader can see, which is not true of two invisible cells.

Card padding is a control here and nowhere else in A10: Compact 24 · Comfortable 32 · Spacious 40, stepping to 24 · 28 · 32 at 834 and 20 · 24 · 28 at 390. ACCESSIBILITY · A CONTAINER THAT ANNOUNCES NOTHING 1 Row's structure with a <div> around each stat: one <ul> of <li>, each holding a card that holds one <p>. The card takes no role, no label, no <article> and no heading — it is a plane, and a plane with a role is a region a reader has to leave. The cards are not links and nothing inside them is focusable. The equal-height stretch is invisible to assistive technology, which reads three paragraphs of the same length whatever the boxes do. The empty space at the foot of a short card is not announced and carries no text, not even a hidden one. In forced colours the fill is dropped and the hairline is kept, so Surface and Ground become the same drawing. Stated, not corrected: the control still changes the theme's output, and a user in forced colours sees one of the two. Cited from A9·4. Contrast: value 15.4:1 on surface light / 14.7:1 dark, label 5.4:1 / 5.8:1, hairline 1.3:1 light and 1.5:1 dark against the card. The card-to-ground step is 1.05:1 in light and 1.2:1 in dark, which is why the hairline carries the edge in both modes rather than the fill. One tab stop at most — the link. Print: cards keep their hairlines, the fill is not printed.

STATS · the item list · outside the design’s listStats is the P0·3 item list: Add stat arrives with content — value 100, label “What this number counts” — Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. Per item: value ≤ 12 · prefix ≤ 3 · suffix ≤ 6 · label ≤ 60. note, prev and share held. Value source: Authored · Member count · Paid member count. At Member count the value renders Ghost’s {​{total_members}} string verbatim — “1,200+” in, “1,200+” out, no maths and no formatter, which is the category’s own no-formatter rule — prefix and suffix are disabled and the label stays authored. {​{total_paid_members}} is offered only when the site has paid members enabled. Eight is the ceiling of stats[], raised from six in this pass: “Eight numbers is as many as a reader holds in one look. A ninth is a table, and a table is a different section.” 3 Grid is the only design that draws eight; everywhere else seven or eight authored names it. Seven or eight authored → 3 Grid, which has no cards, and the panel says so before the frame changes.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesValue, prefix, suffix and label edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The value raises no marks toolbar — it is plain text, so bold, italic and a link inside a figure are withheld rather than offered and refused. Eyebrow, title, sub, source and the link label take the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. A value bound to a member count is not inline-editable: clicking it says “Edit in Ghost”, because the figure is the site’s member list and not a word anyone typed here. sourceUrl and linkUrl open the Link Picker; the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon. A card is not editable as a card — no per-card colour, radius or shadow field, only the section’s Cards value.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registrycount-up, from the fixed registry, and only at Count up On. Its own degradation is the argument: “The final value renders as static text — it is already in the HTML before JS ever runs.” JS off, reduced motion and assistive technology all get the figure rather than the animation, and the module does not re-run on the way back up the page. Edit-safe: it does not run while the section is edited, so every frame here is still a resting state. At Count up Off — the default — this design declares no module at all: the cards are a stretched grid row, Cards is a fill token, Card padding is a value the stylesheet reads, and a card has no hover state at any value.
DATA · Ghost’s two member helpers · outside the design’s listTwo figures in A10 can come from Ghost, and both are public theme helpers: {​{total_members}} and {​{total_paid_members}}, each rendering a pre-rounded string. They are reached from the item’s Value source and not from a “Populate from…” panel — there is no list to populate. Everything else is authored in this section.
