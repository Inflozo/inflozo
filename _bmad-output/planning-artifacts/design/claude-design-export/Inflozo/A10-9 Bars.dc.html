Bars

A proportional bar under each value, on a track the full width of the row. The one design in A10 that draws anything other than type, and the only one with a precondition: a drawn stat without a share draws no track, and the stats must be parts of one whole. Four unrelated numbers with bars under them is a chart of nothing.

It is also where A10 stops. A sparkline, a dial, a donut, a pie, an axis, a legend or a second series are all refused — those are charts, with their own conventions and their own accessibility problem, and a section library is not a charting library. A bar is the one exception because its whole grammar is “this much of that”, which is a thing a label can say in words as well.

The row takes the full 1,296 and the bar takes the full row — the one design in A10 that uses the whole content width for a single element, because a bar's length is a measurement and a longer baseline reads more precisely. Label at the left, share and value right-aligned on one baseline, bar 14 px below them.
The track is border and the fill is text, and the track is not optional. A bar with no track is a line whose 11% cannot be judged against 100%, which is the whole reason to draw it. The fill is not the accent: four accent bars is a section in one colour, and A10 spends the accent on links only. Square ends at every pack radius — the one place in A10 where the radius token is deliberately not applied, because an 11% bar with 8 px of radius on both ends is a lozenge whose length stops being readable. ⚑ Mine: the full-width bar, the track's colour, the fill in text and the square ends.

Bar height: Thin 4 · Medium 8 · Thick 16, the same at every width — a bar is a measurement, not type, and scaling it with the viewport would change what 44% looks like on a phone. Thick 16 is capped there because 16 is the label's own cap height, and a bar taller than the text beside it reads as a block rather than a line. ⚑ Mine. Share figure Shown draws the percentage at 15 px muted, to the left of the value on the same baseline — muted because it is the same information the bar already gives, and the value is the figure the site wants read. Hidden removes the number and keeps the bar, for a set where the proportion is the point and the arithmetic is not. The share is a number 0–100 and the bar is that percentage of the track. The theme does not check that the set sums to 100. A set summing to 130 draws four honestly long bars and looks wrong, which is the site's error to see and correct; a theme that normalised the shares to 100 would be publishing figures nobody typed. A share above 100 is held to the track by CSS — max-width on the fill, not arithmetic — and the editor says so. ⚑ Mine.

A share of 3 draws a 39 px mark on a 1,296 track, and it is not given a minimum width. A floor of, say, 8 px would make 0.6% and 3% the same length, which is a chart lying about its smallest value. A share of 0 draws no fill at all — an empty track, which is the honest drawing of nothing. ⚑ Mine. A row with no label keeps its bar and its value, right-aligned as usual, and the left of the row is empty. Unlike 8 Ledger, the value does not move: the bar under it already occupies the row's full width, so there is no hole for the value to fall into. The precondition, stated in the panel: every drawn stat needs a share. If one of the four has none, that row draws its value and its label with no track at all and the panel reads “Elsewhere has no share.” The section stays this design and nothing switches; a share invented from the values' own sum is still refused, because it would be the theme publishing a figure. Two or fewer: the rows are drawn as authored — 1 Row advised, because two bars is a comparison and a comparison of two needs no bars. One: drawn here — 4 Single advised.

ACCESSIBILITY · A GRAPHIC THAT ANNOUNCES NOTHING, AND WHY THAT IS RIGHT The bar is two nested <span>s with aria-hidden="true" — not a <progress>, not role="meter", not role="img" with a label, and not an SVG. A meter announces a value that is already in the text beside it, and <progress> claims something is loading. The share is the drawing's only content and it is in the DOM as text at Share figure Shown — so the bar is decoration for information that is already written. ⚑ Mine. At Share figure Hidden the percentage leaves the page, and a screen-reader user gets the value and the label only. That is a real loss and the panel states it: “At Hidden the proportion is drawn and not written.” A visually hidden percentage was considered and refused — text that exists for one reader and not another is two versions of the section, and the site can turn the figure on. In forced colours the fill takes the system's text colour and the track its background, which keeps the two distinguishable; the fill also takes a 1 px border in forced colours, because a fill and a track that both resolve to the same system colour would erase the bar. That border is drawn nowhere else. ⚑ Mine. Contrast: fill against track 12.1:1 light / 12.6:1 dark, value 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, label and share 5.6:1 / 6.0:1. No animation on the bar: a fill that grows from zero is a proportion nobody can read while it moves, and it stays refused at Count up On, where the figure counts and the bar does not. One tab stop at most. Print keeps the bars as an outlined track with a solid fill, so a printer that drops backgrounds still shows the proportion.

STATS · the item list · outside the design’s listStats is the P0·3 item list: Add stat arrives with content — value 100, label “What this number counts” — Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. Per item: value ≤ 12 · prefix ≤ 3 · suffix ≤ 6 · label ≤ 60, and share 0–100, required on every drawn stat; note and prev held. Value source: Authored · Member count · Paid member count. At Member count the value renders Ghost’s {​{total_members}} string verbatim — “1,200+” in, “1,200+” out, no maths and no formatter, which is the category’s own no-formatter rule — prefix and suffix are disabled and the label stays authored. {​{total_paid_members}} is offered only when the site has paid members enabled. A bound member count still needs a share — the proportion is authored and the figure is Ghost’s, which is the one place in A10 where one stat holds both. Eight is the ceiling of stats[], raised from six in this pass: “Eight numbers is as many as a reader holds in one look. A ninth is a table, and a table is a different section.” 3 Grid is the only design that draws eight; everywhere else seven or eight authored names it. Five to eight authored: this design draws its own count and 3 Grid is advised, and the bars are not drawn there, because 3 Grid does not read share.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesValue, prefix, suffix and label edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The value raises no marks toolbar — it is plain text, so bold, italic and a link inside a figure are withheld rather than offered and refused. Eyebrow, title, sub, source and the link label take the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. A value bound to a member count is not inline-editable: clicking it says “Edit in Ghost”, because the figure is the site’s member list and not a word anyone typed here. share is a number field in the item panel and is not editable on canvas — dragging a bar to set a proportion is a chart editor, and this is a section. sourceUrl and linkUrl open the Link Picker; the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registrycount-up, from the fixed registry, and only at Count up On. Its own degradation is the argument: “The final value renders as static text — it is already in the HTML before JS ever runs.” JS off, reduced motion and assistive technology all get the figure rather than the animation, and the module does not re-run on the way back up the page. Edit-safe: it does not run while the section is edited, so every frame here is still a resting state. At Count up Off — the default — this design declares no module at all: the fill’s width is an inline percentage on a <span>, server-rendered from share. count-up animates the figure and never the bar: a fill that grows into place is the refusal §0 made about a proportion nobody can read while it moves, and that half of the refusal stands.
DATA · Ghost’s two member helpers · outside the design’s listTwo figures in A10 can come from Ghost, and both are public theme helpers: {​{total_members}} and {​{total_paid_members}}, each rendering a pre-rounded string. They are reached from the item’s Value source and not from a “Populate from…” panel — there is no list to populate. Everything else is authored in this section. Refused on record: a post count from a tag (a number nobody wrote and nobody checks), any admin-API figure — opens, revenue, growth — a live figure from a third party, and the four ornaments: sparkline, dial, donut, pie. 9 Bars’ single proportional bar is as far as A10 goes.
