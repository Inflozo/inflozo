14 · Image Band

A full-bleed band carrying an authored photograph, a warm scrim, the head in white at the top and six portraits along the foot. The one design in A21 whose ground is a picture.

The image is the section's own upload — a newsroom, an office, a city — and never an author's cover, because picking one writer's photograph to sit behind twelve names is a decision no section should make.

ZERO WRITERS · THE BAND, THE HEAD AND THE NOTICE ⚑ · AT NO IMAGE THE DESIGN HANDS OFF TO 5 CONTRAST BAND ⚑

With no image the design hands off to 5 Contrast Band ⚑ — drawn in place, flagged in the editor, invisible on the site. That is A29·5's hand-off rule, reused: the user's chosen design is never silently changed ⚑, and the band is the nearest arrangement that survives without a picture. It is the only hand-off in A21. At twelve the band grows rather than cropping its foot row ⚑ — the head stays at the top, the portraits stay at the foot, and the space between them takes the difference.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe image is a section field, not a Ghost field ⚑ — an upload the user makes here, sitting beside the head fields in the sidebar. A21 is the first category in the run whose picture is authored rather than queried, and it is flagged for that reason.Height is a minimum and the control says so. Twelve writers at six across is two rows and a 542 px band; the alternative — a fixed height with the second row cropped — is the failure this design is most likely to ship with, so the value is named as a floor rather than a size.Scrim: Light disables itself against a light photograph and shows the ratio it measured on the bottom third of the crop, which is where the names sit. The check is A29·5's, unchanged, and it runs on upload rather than on render.Cut: a focal point ⚑ (Ghost stores none — A29 finding 3, and here the image is the section's own upload, so the gap is the product's), a tint value, a parallax ⚑, a per-portrait ring, and an action.
