1 · Split

The head and the publication’s own details standing in a 560 column at the left, the form in a 640 column at the right, on a 96 px gutter. The category default and the arrangement five other designs resolve to.

It is the design for a contact page that has both things to say and something to ask: an address, a phone number and reply hours on one side, and the form on the other. Nothing is raised, boxed or photographed.

WHAT THE SPLIT DOES THAT THE OTHERS DO NOT
Both columns are left-aligned, always ⚑ — there is no alignment control, because a centred head over a left-aligned form is two alignments in one section.The details column keeps its width when the form is in any state ⚑, including sent: the left column is not part of the form and does not move when the form does.Socials sit under the details, not under the form ⚑ — they are another way to reach the publication, which is what that column is.

The state tiles draw the form at Message: Short so all seven fit on one row of the canvas; the field set is the design’s own three ⚑. Invalid puts its message under the field it belongs to, never in one banner at the top ⚑ — a reader who mistyped an address should not have to hunt for which line is wrong. Sent shrinks the section and A16 lets it ⚑: a three-field form is 340 px and its confirmation is 96, and reserving 244 px of empty space on every page that never sends costs more than the reflow does. Failed keeps everything typed ⚑.

THE DELIVERY GROUP · IDENTICAL IN ALL TEN FORM DESIGNS · NOT COUNTED
Where it goes
An email addressA form service
Ghost cannot receive a form ⚑ — its theme layer has no handler. At An email address the script composes a mailto: URL out of the fields and opens the reader’s mail app ⚑; with no script the fields stay and a visible “Email us at hello@…” line sits under them ⚑. Never a <form action="mailto:…" method="post"> — browsers answer that with a blank draft or with nothing, and a form that looks posted and was not is the worst state A16 could ship ⚑. At A form service the native POST stands and the service’s own page answers.
The destination
hello@orbitweekly.com
An Inflozo project setting, promotable to a Ghost Admin custom setting (text) ⚑, so the owner can change the address without a redeploy. Validated per mode — an email address at An email address, an https URL at A form service. Until it is set the section is editor-only and the page cannot publish ⚑: the old “falls back to the site’s own address” is withdrawn — Ghost exposes no site address to a theme ⚑.
After sending
The service’s own page▾
Show the message in place · The service’s own page. Disabled at An email address ⚑ — the mail app is the response, and there is nothing for the section to show.
A consent line
OffA line of textA checkbox
Under the button. A line of text is the default ⚑ — a checkbox that everyone ticks is a worse record than a sentence that everyone reads. A2·13 Consent owns cookies; this row is about the message.
Prefill for members
OnOff
Name and email pre-filled from @member.name and @member.email when a signed-in member reads the page — server-side, in the template, with no script ⚑. A signed-out reader sees the empty form at either value. On by default ⚑ — a member the site already knows should not be asked to type their own address.
Spam · a hidden honeypot field, plus your service’s own guard. No captcha at any value ⚑ — a captcha is a third-party script and a contact form is not worth one. This is a caption under the group, not a fifth row ⚑: it was read-only, and a read-only row that looks like a control is a control that lies. The form-state copy is edited in place, not here ⚑ — sentHeading, sentText, invalidText and failedText are written on canvas through the P0·6 editor state switcher (Empty · Invalid · Sent · Failed). There is no Preview control in this panel ⚑, and there never was one to remove — previewing belongs to the editor.
ARCHITECT: REGISTRY ADDITION (contact-form)The ten form designs declared member-form and it is the wrong module ⚑ — it posts to Ghost’s members endpoint, which is not where a message goes. A contact-form module is requested and no other name is coined. The no-JS state is designed anyway: the native POST at A form service, the visible mailto: line at An email address.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesEvery visible text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the four contact-row labels and their values, the field labels, the placeholders, the button label and the consent line. The field labels and the placeholders are edited by clicking them on the canvas ⚑ — this panel holds no label boxes. Ghost-owned values are never inline-editable: at Social accounts: From Ghost a row’s URL is read-only and clicking it says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker. Buttons accept an optional icon before or after the label from the P0·2 Icon Picker with its size / colour-role popover, off by default ⚑. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: the five field labels, the five placeholders, the button label, the consent line and the four form-state strings are authored fields with defaults, and “Sending…”, “Send another message”, the failed banner’s first line, “Email us at {address}” and the honeypot’s hidden label are theme translation-catalog strings.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registrymember-form was declared here, flagged — and this pass says plainly that it is wrong: it posts to Ghost’s members endpoint. ARCHITECT: registry addition (contact-form), edit-safe, and no module name is coined beyond that request. The no-JS state is designed either way ⚑: at A form service the <form action method="post"> posts natively and the service answers; at An email address the fields stay, the “Email us at …” line under them is a real mailto: link, and the submit button is not rendered ⚑ — a button that cannot post is worse than no button. Empty, focus and the browser’s own required-field messages need no script; submitting, the in-place sent state and the failed banner are the script path.
DATA · Ghost’s surface, and what A16 cannot readNothing is read from Ghost’s content API ⚑ — no Show ladder, no filter, and the P0·5 “Populate from…” panel is not offered anywhere in A16 ⚑, because no design reads a Ghost list. The one Ghost value is @site.title. The destination is a project setting, not section content — see the delivery group.

At Logged out the section is hidden from signed-in members and Prefill for members never fires.
