No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the count’s three words are authored fields with the old literals as their defaults, and aria-label="Comments" plus the <noscript> line are translation-catalog strings. WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESReconciled. Padding retired into Vertical spacing; Inset stays, because a plane’s internal padding is not the space around the section. Background role is deliberately unlocked, with its reason drawn. Count words become fields, Match the page resolves to auto on a dual-mode site, the block group is recut as the Data group, and a <noscript> line sits under the head — which matters most here, where no-JS otherwise leaves a plane carrying 96 px of inset and nothing else. Five controls of its own.Inset is not Padding. Padding is the section's space on the page; Inset is the plane's own. Two controls with named values rather than one control that means different things at different widths.Plane edge Soft is disabled in dark and shows its reason — the shadow token is none in every pack's dark mode, so the control would silently do nothing. Named values only, and a value that cannot work is disabled with its reason, never allowed to fail quietly.The plane holds at 390 ⚑, unlike A25·3 Sheet, which drops below 767. A28's plane carries 20 px of inset and a full-width block; A25's carried a 350 px measure inside a 350 px screen, which was the reason it had to go.
