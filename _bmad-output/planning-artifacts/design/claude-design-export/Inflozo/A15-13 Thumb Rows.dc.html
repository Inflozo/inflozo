13 · Thumb Rows

One film a row: a 320 poster at the left, the title, the description and the meta line at the right, a hairline between rows. The set is read rather than scanned.

It is the section for three or four films that each need explaining — a documentary strand, a set of lectures — where a grid cell would leave the description with nowhere to go.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND WHY
There is nowhere to upload to, so the Upload source is deleted. Ghost’s public interface has no media library a theme can read, Inflozo is not permitted to put files on a customer’s Ghost, and Inflozo’s own asset system handles images only. Every film in this design is a pasted URL ⚑ — a provider embed, or a media file already hosted on the customer’s own Ghost, which stays first-party and keeps its native <video controls playsinline preload="none" poster>. The ambient loop goes with it. The Playback row — Click to play · Ambient loop — is deleted from the panel: the loop had no source of files. Nothing autoplays anywhere in A15 again, and the reduced-motion registry addition the loop needed is withdrawn — the category is back to four findings. Nothing reads a duration off a file. duration ≤ 8 is typed by the author again. Patched 1 Sep 2026: nothing is fetched from a provider either — the title, the description and the poster are required fields the customer fills in. No poster, no title and no duration each close up exactly as drawn. Embeds are unaffected. YouTube, Vimeo and the audio providers keep this design, its facade, its click-to-load notice and their refusal to autoplay. No layout, measure, type size, colour or spacing changed on this frame ⚑, and the design’s number is unchanged.
DESIGN PATCH PASS TWO · 1 SEPTEMBER 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND WHY
Nothing is ever fetched from a provider. Not by our server, not by the editor’s browser, at any point. So the film’s title, description and poster are required fields the customer fills in ⚑, and the panel asks for them in words. The duration stays typed. Only the provider’s name is derived, and it is read from the address itself. Editor-side seeding and “Use provider thumbnail” are deleted. Pasting a recognised URL fetches the oEmbed title once, and the duration where the provider supplies one, and seeds both fields; “Use provider thumbnail” downloads the image and re-hosts it as the poster. Both affordances are gone from this panel, and settlement 3’s 24 August narrowing — “never fetched by the reader” — is withdrawn: the rule reads as first written, never the provider’s own thumbnail. No affordance on this panel implies a lookup. Checked and confirmed: no spinner, no “fetch details” button, no provider-status row ⚑. Add is still one paste field, and what follows it is a form the customer types. The library rules, by name. A control switched off by another is greyed with the reason beside it — unchanged here: where this design locks or disables a row, the reason stays a short sentence in the row, never a tooltip, and nothing is hidden; the two controls this project can never offer, Playback and the deleted lookup, are not drawn and the panel says why. The Remove button never greys out — ✕ is never disabled; Add stops at 24 with the reason shown. A count that picks between drawn layouts is a named set — nothing converted: items 1–24 stays a number, and every row that picks an arrangement was already named. A design may declare the width below which its script runs — this design declares none ⚑, its behaviour runs at every width, so its one no-JavaScript line is complete. Avatars with no photograph — no subject: no person is drawn. The two Ghost server findings of 31 August land nowhere here. The feature-image caption differs between Ghost 5 and Ghost 6, but every line this design draws is an authored section field rendered by our own template — A15 reads nothing from Ghost but @site.title. A comment count renders nothing without JavaScript, and this design draws no count, no comment word and no widget — A28 Comments owns both. Nothing else changed. No layout, measure, type size, colour or spacing moved on this frame ⚑, no control gained or lost a value, and the design’s number is unchanged. Settled by the owner, 1 Sep 2026: required means the panel marks the empty field and says what is missing — and the section still publishes ⚑.
