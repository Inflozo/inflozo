THE ONE AUTHORED LIST IN A22
reasons[] is A6·11’s field, carried verbatim ⚑ — 1 to 3 lines, ≤ 40 characters each, text only. Its repeater sits below the six controls: one row per line with a drag handle and a remove action, Add reason at the end, disabled at three.Add never produces a blank row — a new reason arrives carrying a fact and lands last, A6·11’s rule ⚑.One reason is a supported state, keeping its number and its hairline; removing the last one leaves the column empty and the design becomes 3 Split, which the panel names ⚑.

The reasons do not change with the state ⚑ — they are the argument, and an argument that disappears the moment it works reads as a trick. They stay through done and through the signed-in subscribed line, where they become the reasons the reader was right ⚑.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored text edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: the eyebrow, the heading, the blurb, the button label, the note and each reason line (≤ 40 characters, text and marks only). The note is the consent line and now takes the link mark ⚑ — “…you agree to our Privacy Policy” links through the Ghost-aware Link Picker, which is what the field was missing. The five state strings are edited in the state that shows them through the P0·6 editor state switcher — Empty · Invalid · Done · Subscribed · Members off — inline, in place, never a sidebar preview row ⚑. The subscribe button takes an optional icon before or after its label from the P0·2 Icon Picker, with its size and colour-role popover. The endpoint, the Portal preferences target and the member count are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. Translation-catalog strings ⚑ — “Subscribing…”, “Use a different address” and the visually-hidden “Email address” label: theme catalogue, not fields.
BEHAVIOUR · MEMBER-FORM · GHOST’S FORM CONTRACTmember-form, edit-safe. The form speaks Ghost’s own contract ⚑ new in this pass: data-members-form="subscribe" on the <form>, data-members-email on the field and data-members-name when Ask for a name is on. Ghost’s members script drives loading, success and error, and member-form maps those three onto the designed seven. The native POST to /members/api/send-magic-link/ stays as the no-JS floor.
