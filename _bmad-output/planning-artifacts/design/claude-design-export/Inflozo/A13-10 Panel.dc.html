Panel

The whole sequence inside one surface panel with a hairline and the pack radius, the steps as hairline rows inside it. The only design in A13 whose containment is the section's own — 4 Cards draws a plane around each step, which is the item's geometry; this draws one around all of them.

It is the third member of the family 1 Three Up and 6 Contrast Band make, and the one that changes containment as well as ground — which is why it reads as a different design rather than a third colour of the first. It is one of three designs that read icon, here at the row's left rather than inline with the title, because a row has room a card does not.

One panel at the content width: surface fill, a 1 px border hairline, the pack radius, 48 px of padding, and no shadow. Inside it the steps are hairline rows 24 px above and below the rule — the panel's own inner rhythm, not the section's, which is why the panel has a padding control of its own. The icon box is the same 44 px on the pack's hover surface that 4 Cards uses, at the row's left with a 20 px gap; the numeral is Large 40 at the row's far right.
The numeral is at the right and the icon at the left, which is the reverse of the reading order — a reader hears icon, title, body and never the figure at all, and a viewer sees the figure last. That is deliberate: in a panel the figures are a column of markers down the right edge that says how far through the list a row is, and putting them first would make the panel a ledger. 14 Index is the ledger, and it puts them first. ⚑ Mine. Step 04 has no icon and draws the numeral plate in the box at 20/600, so its figure appears twice — and unlike 4 Cards the right-hand numeral is kept, because here the right-hand column of figures is the design and dropping one would break the column.

DIVIDER NONE · THE ROWS GO 32 APART AND THE PANEL'S EDGE IS THE ONLY LINE LEFT
Steps: Stacked rows · Two columns. Stacked is one column of full-width rows, the default, and the one that reads as a sequence. Two columns fills down and then across — 1, 2, 3 in the left column and 4, 5, 6 in the right — never left-to-right, because reading a numbered list in Z order puts 01 beside 04 and asks the reader to work out which way it goes. ⚑ Mine, and it is 14 Index's column rule carried. Divider: Hairlines · None. At Hairlines the rows are 24 apart with a line in the centre of the gap, never above the first row and never below the last, and at Two columns a vertical hairline runs the panel's inner height in the middle gutter. At None the rows go 32 apart and the panel's own edge is the only line in the design. Panel padding: Compact 32 · Comfortable 48 · Spacious 64, independent of the section's Padding at every value. The panel is surface and there is no Ground value — a panel the colour of the page with only a hairline is a border around the section, which is a different and worse design. ⚑ Mine, and it is the one place A13 offers a fill with no ground alternative. Two to six steps at Stacked, four to six at Two columns. Six divides; five at Two columns is 3 + 2 with the left column taller, which is stated rather than corrected. Seven or eight → 14 Index, which is this design without the panel and with a tighter row.

This is the one design in A13 that gains from dark mode rather than merely surviving it — a lifted panel on a deep ground reads more clearly than a white one on cream, and the panel's edge stops depending on a 1.3:1 hairline. EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Per step, title and body edit inline in the row. icon is a P0·2 icon slot: the Icon Picker on click, size Small 20 · Medium 24 and colour role Muted · Text in its popover, and an empty slot renders the numeral plate in the 44 px box — the right-hand figure is kept, unlike 4 Cards. The contained 1:1 upload alternative is dropped; accent stays refused on a sequence mark. At Source From posts the step title and body are Ghost-owned: the toolbar is replaced by the plain-text lock pill and clicking says “Edit in Ghost”. The numeral is never editable — it is a rendering of position, so there is nothing to click and nothing to type.
