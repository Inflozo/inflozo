Rail

One line of step cards wider than the window, scrolled by the reader. A11·9's rail and A12·13's, carried over, with one change that settles A12's third finding: this rail snaps.

A12·13 refused scroll-snap only to stay identical to the logo rail it inherited, and flagged the refusal as unforced. A13's cards are equal, the steps are ordered, and a step is exactly the unit a reader wants to land on — so the registry's snap strip is not just buildable here, it is the right drawing. The consequence is that the JavaScript-off drawing and the drawn drawing are the same drawing minus two buttons, which is the closest A13 comes to a design that does not need its module at all.

Module: carousel. JS off, quoted from the registry: “The slide track is a native horizontally-scrollable scroll-snap strip — fully usable, only dots and arrow buttons are hidden.” A13·8 matches that branch exactly, which A11·9 and A12·13 did not: the snap is drawn, the dots do not exist to hide, and the only thing JavaScript adds is the two arrows and the two fades. A12's finding 3 is settled here in the registry's favour. Edit-safe. The behaviour does not run while the section is being edited, and the scroll position is never persisted or restored — every frame in this file is a resting state at the start of the track. Reduced motion keeps the scroll and the snap and drops the smooth animation, which needs no script either. The precondition is measured, not counted. If the cards are not wider than the content width, the section draws 12 Media Top — same pictures, same crop, same numerals, in a grid — with no arrows, no fades and no scroll container. At Standard 380 that is three steps or fewer; at Narrow 306, four or fewer. No module has a branch for a measurement, so with JavaScript off the section stays a rail whose track happens not to overflow rather than becoming a grid: a grid is a cell layout, and nothing without script can decide to build one. ⚑ A12·13's finding 4, carried, and it is why the count class is variable. Refused: autoplay, dots, a counter, a per-step pin, a loop, and a “drag to explore” hint. Nothing in A13 auto-advances. A process that advances itself is telling the reader how fast to read a sequence, and the one thing a numbered list is for is letting them set that pace.

No design ever turns into another designThe two-way hand-off is deleted — four sentences, and this design is the rule own example. It never becomes 12 Media Top and 12 Media Top never becomes it. When the track already fits, the module hides the two arrows and the two fades and the track sits on the page margin with nothing to scroll; the panel advises 12 Media Top for a section whose steps always fit. The measurement is the module own, made in the browser, and with JavaScript off there are no arrows to hide. This closes the category old finding that the precondition is a measurement no module makes — it is not a hand-off any more, so nothing needs to make it.
Item counts are a number pickerThe steps are a repeater and always were — Add step at the foot, drag handles, Remove on every row, and a stepper capped at this design own ceiling in bound mode — so there is no row of fixed count buttons to replace. Count per row counts planes across the page rather than items and stays a row of named values, the same reading the testimonials category was ruled on. Two to eight, all drawn — a track has no row to break — and Add is disabled at eight with its number. Head Centred is greyed at Arrows Above the rail with the reason visible.
The Remove button never greys outRemove is visible and clickable at every count and is never dimmed and never hidden. At two steps clicking it says a process needs at least two steps and the row stays, so the one-step state is not reachable from the button. Removing until the cards fit hides the arrows and the fades and leaves the rail itself.
Member buttons are conditionalA13 draws no button anywhere. Its one destination is the section link, and where that link points at a member action it now carries the ask: it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens. Scope-tagged apart from Member visibility, which hides the section server-side.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject for the notice — this design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for a notice to replace, and no line on this frame ever promised a subscription without script. JavaScript off: the native scroll-snap strip is fully usable; only the two arrows and the two fades are absent. There are no dots to hide. The phone drawing and the no-script drawing are the same drawing.
Ghost templates cannot count, add, or rememberThe numeral is a stylesheet counter, not arithmetic: the ordered list carries a CSS counter and each item renders counter(step, decimal-leading-zero), which is where 01 to 08 comes from — no padding helper, no authored field, no template maths. The plate figure at 0.4 of the box shorter edge is 40cqmin on a size container, so nothing measures a box either. Nothing counts across its own loop: no step 3 of 6, no total, no and 2 more.
