Open List

Every answer drawn: the question above its answer, rows between hairlines, one column on 780. No button, no marker, no region, nothing to open — and, unless a link is authored or an answer contains one, nothing focusable in the whole section. It is the plainest thing A9 can put on a page and the answer to the question the ten accordions raise.

That is the reason it exists rather than being a value of 1 Accordion. Every accordion in A9 hides its answers from a browser's find and from a reader who scans, and every accordion's panel names this design for a site that would rather not. Six answers on a page is longer than six questions on a page; a site with short answers and a page to spare should be reading this frame and not the other one.

Between is A9-0's row: a border-top on each item with the row padding above and below, and nothing under the last one. Above and below adds the two outer hairlines as borders on the <ul>, which makes the list read as one block on the page — A8·12's values and their reasons, carried over. At Rules None the rows sit 40 px apart whatever the padding control says. The row padding is what a hairline needs on each side of it; with no hairline there is nothing to be padded, and 20 px of nothing between an answer and the next question does not end the answer. 40 is one value, not a scale: it is the question-to-answer gap doubled and a half, and it is the same at every question size. Rules None is the value a page with a coloured ground wants, and the value a page of six long answers should not use: without the hairlines, six 90-word answers is one column of text with bold lines in it. The panel says that in one sentence rather than disabling the value. ⚑ The 40 px, the reason and the panel's advice are mine.

Focus lands on the heading, which carries tabindex="-1" for exactly this. In the accordions focus goes to a button that was going to be pressed; here there is no button, and a reader arriving from somebody else's link should be reading at the question rather than at the top of the page. It is the only tabindex in A9 and it adds no tab stop. ⚑ Mine. No highlight, no tint, no flash — the same refusal as everywhere else in the category. The ring above is a focus ring, not an arrival state, and it appears only for a reader whose browser draws one. This is the one design where the printed page and the screen are the same drawing. Every other design in A9 prints something it does not display, or displays something it does not print.

On a phone this design is the whole FAQ as one scroll, which is either its best or its worst property depending on the answers: six short answers is a page a reader thumbs through, and six long ones is a page they leave. The panel says which and names 1 Accordion for a site whose answers run long. Padding 64 / 20, question Large 18, answer 16 / 1.6, row padding 16, the 40 px value unchanged at Rules None.

ACCESSIBILITY · THE DESIGN WITH THE LEAST IN IT One <h2>, a <ul> of <li>, an <h3> and its prose in each. No <button>, no <summary>, no role="region" — a region nobody can toggle is a landmark for nothing, and six of them would be six landmarks in a list. The heading rotor gives a reader the same six questions the accordions give them, and here the answers are underneath. Nothing is focusable unless the content puts something there: the optional section link, and links inside answers. With none of those authored the section has no tab stop at all, which is true of exactly two designs in A9 — this and 15 Ledger. No hover state anywhere. There is nothing to press, so a plane under the cursor would be a lie about what pressing does. Nothing here is hidden from find-in-page, from a reader scanning, or from a printer, and no answer requires an action to read. That is the whole argument for the design and the reason ten panels name it. Contrast: question 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark, answer the same, list markers 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, inline code 15.4:1 / 14.6:1 on its chip, sub and note 5.6:1 / 6.8:1, link underlines 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. In forced colours this design is unchanged apart from the system's own colours, because there is no plane, band, marker or ring in it.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Questions and answers edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The answer raises the marks toolbar on selection with bold · italic · link · inline code and no underline — an underline inside prose is a link that is not one. Recorded as a PRD delta. Nothing in A9 is Ghost-owned, so nothing here says “Edit in Ghost” — every word on the page is the site’s own. The link opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker and takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Questions are the P0·3 item list: Add question arrives with content, Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only. Open rows is not offered: there is nothing to open.
BEHAVIOUR · from the fixed registryNo module. Nothing to declare: no button, no summary, no region, no hover, no motion, and the anchor is an id on a heading. JS off: identical — print, screen and no-JS are one drawing. ARCHITECT: registry addition — deep-linking (open the row at the fragment, scroll it to 96 px, move focus, animate nothing) is script no registry module covers. The no-JS state is drawn and no module name is coined.
DATA · nothing from Ghost · outside the design’s listNothing here comes from Ghost. There is no FAQ, question or answer resource, so every question, answer and group label is authored in this section: no “Populate from…” panel is offered on any A9 design and there is no missing-data branch. Rows pulled from posts under an internal tag were considered and refused — a post has its own URL, and a page of tagged posts is a documentation index. The only failure available is an empty repeater, and then the section does not render. Refused on record, at every value: no Expand all or Collapse all; no question count in the resting state (12 Filter’s while-typing count excepted); no “Was this helpful?”, votes or thumbs; no icon per question; no chat launcher, ask-AI or search-the-site field; no accordion inside an accordion; no popular / new / important flag; and no FAQPage schema — a section cannot know what the page is.
