Tag names and counts are Ghost’s and are never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. No all-topics link is drawn here, so no URL field appears. A20 has no authored image field ⚑ — the pictures are Ghost’s feature_image, so rule 10’s Image focus has nothing to attach to and the crop stays centred; a focus control here would be a fake one.

The scrim is the one thing in A20 that is re-tuned rather than re-tokenised ⚑: it moves from a warm rgba(35,32,25,…) wash to rgba(23,21,17,…) at the same two stops, so a photograph sits against the dark page rather than glowing off it. The name stays white in both modes — a tile is a picture, and white on a scrim is white on a scrim.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A few topics as full-picture tiles with the name and count set over a warm scrim.2 · Structural descriptor. grid-of-N · none · page · few · background · names set over their own pictures
Media background and count class few, both unique in A20. 2 Tiles is the other picture grid and puts its media on top with a many set.3 · Archetype. grid-of-N.
