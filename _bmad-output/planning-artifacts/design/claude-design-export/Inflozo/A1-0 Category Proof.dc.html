What holds it. The wordmark is capped at 280 px and never wraps to a second line at this height — it truncates at the word. Nav items past the fourth fold into More, in Ghost’s order, and the item that would break the row is ellipsised at 150 px rather than shrinking the type below 15 px. What breaks, honestly. Nine items with three long labels leaves More holding five of nine — a menu doing the nav’s job. The escape hatch is a design change, not a control: A1·7 Mega Bar or A1·6 Drawer-First. The sidebar advises so at the point the fold exceeds four items — advice, never a switch (Part A·A8); it does not silently degrade.

Open decisions for you, before A2
1 · Dropdown depth — settled: two levels. Children and grandchildren are authored in Inflozo, since Ghost’s navigation is flat. A grandchild group indents under its child against a hairline; never a second flyout panel, never a third level. Drawn in A1·7 Mega Bar and in the A1·2 drawer. 2 · Sticky as control vs design — settled: control. I made sticky a control on every design rather than splitting “Rail” and “Sticky Rail” into two of the 16. That buys four more arrangements; if you would rather spend slots on behaviour, say so now and the roster changes. 3 · Where unused fields go. Drawer foot, always. It keeps content but makes some drawers long. The alternative — hiding it with a “not shown in this design” note in the sidebar — is quieter but loses it on export. 4 · A2 sits above all of this. Every A1 design must tolerate an announcement bar above it, including Overlay, where the bar is opaque and the header is not, and Utility + Nav, where it makes three stacked bands. Drawn in A2, not here. 5 · No design turns into another design. Settled against A1 by the patch pass (Part A·A8), ruled to apply in all 33 categories. A1·4 keeps its own arrangement over the page ground with the scrim off and the normal lockup tokens; A1·8 hides the utility strip when there is no secondary nav to put in it. A1·11 collapses to a Menu button — the same 240 column slides in from the left over a scrim below 1200, full width at 390, and renders as a stacked list under the logo without JavaScript. Ruled by the owner in this pass. 6 · Contrast must be defined per mode in every pack. A1·10 needs a near-ink band in light mode and a pale warm one in dark. This is the only addition A1 makes to the pack schema — settle it before the 12 packs are built.
