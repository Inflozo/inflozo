What holds it. The wordmark is capped at 280 px and never wraps to a second line at this height — it truncates at the word. Nav items past the fourth fold into More, in Ghost’s order, and the item that would break the row is ellipsised at 150 px rather than shrinking the type below 15 px. The action cluster never compresses: it is the last thing to yield, and it does not yield at 1440.

What breaks, honestly. Nine items with three long labels leaves More holding five of nine — a menu doing the nav’s job. The escape hatch is a design change, not a control: A1·7 Mega Bar or A1·6 Drawer-First. The sidebar says so at the point the fold exceeds four items, and offers the switch; it does not silently degrade.

The three limits every A1 design inherits from this frame
WORDMARKCapped at 280 px desktop / 150 px mobile. Truncates at the word on one line desktop; wraps to two lines mobile and the bar grows.
NAVType never drops below 15 px and gaps never below 16 px. Overflow past the design’s cap (4 on Rail) folds to More in Ghost’s order. A single label over 20 characters ellipsises at 150 px.
ACTIONSNever compress, never truncate. Order of yielding under pressure: search icon → ghost Sign in → primary CTA to drawer. Tap targets 44 px throughout.

Open decisions for you, before A2
1 · Dropdown depth — settled: two levels. Children and grandchildren are authored in Inflozo, since Ghost’s navigation is flat. A grandchild group indents under its child against a hairline; never a second flyout panel, never a third level. Drawn in A1·7 Mega Bar and in the A1·2 drawer. 2 · Sticky as control vs design — settled: control. I made sticky a control on every design rather than splitting “Rail” and “Sticky Rail” into two of the 16. That buys four more arrangements; if you would rather spend slots on behaviour, say so now and the roster changes. 3 · Where unused fields go. Drawer foot, always. It keeps content but makes some drawers long. The alternative — hiding it with a “not shown in this design” note in the sidebar — is quieter but loses it on export. 4 · A2 sits above all of this. Every A1 design must tolerate an announcement bar above it, including Overlay, where the bar is opaque and the header is not, and Utility + Nav, where it makes three stacked bands. Drawn in A2, not here. 5 · Designs may hand off to other designs. New, from 9–12: A1·11 becomes A1·1 below 1200 rather than inventing a narrow-rail variant, and A1·4 and A1·8 fall back to A1·1 when their preconditions are absent. That makes handoff a category rule. It needs your confirmation before A2 relies on it. 6 · Contrast must be defined per mode in every pack. A1·10 needs a near-ink band in light mode and a pale warm one in dark. This is the only addition A1 makes to the pack schema — settle it before the 12 packs are built.
