834 is a simple narrowing, and it is drawn only as the measurement reference for the pack — nothing leaves the bar and nothing re-arranges. Bar height holds at 76 — height is the Height control’s value, not a function of width, and it is identical at 1440 and 834. What changes: wordmark 19 → 18, mark 26 → 24, padding 72 → 40, nav gaps 28 → 20. Both actions hold, and the 4-item cap holds. The single width change in this design happens at 767: the nav and “Sign in” move into the drawer, “Subscribe” stays in the bar.
