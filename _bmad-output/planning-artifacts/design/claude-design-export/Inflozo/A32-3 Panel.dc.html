WHAT THE FOUR GATES SETTLE FOR THE WHOLE CATEGORY
Four gates, one arrangement. Free-signup (a members post, a signed-out reader) · paid (a paid post, a signed-out reader) · upgrade (a paid post, a signed-in free member) · specific tier (a post sold to one named tier) ⚑. The design never changes between them — only the eyebrow, the heading, the sentence and the button label do, and all four are fields.The upgrade gate drops the sign-in link ⚑ — a signed-in reader has nothing to sign in to — greets them by name, and its action goes to #/portal/account/plans rather than #/portal/signup. It is the one gate that must not offer an email field: Ghost already has the address.A reader with access never sees the section ⚑, so there is no unlocked, no “thanks for reading” and no dismissed state. The free gate is the only one whose action can be an email field, because free signup is the only action a theme can complete itself ⚑.

The upgrade gate drops the included row ⚑, and at that point the plane is 1,296 px wide holding 560 px of copy. The panel names 1 Fade as the better design for a site that mostly meets signed-in free members ⚑ — that is the hand-off rule, used deliberately.

WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE THREE STATES EVERY A32 DESIGN OWES
In the editor the gate draws at its resting state ⚑ — the paid gate, the fade at its control value, no hover and no ring. Nothing in A32 has a behaviour to preview, so the edit-time frame and the published frame are the same picture. With JavaScript off the section is identical ⚑ — pixel for pixel in ten of the twelve. The gate is server-rendered HTML, the fade is a gradient, the suppression rules are :last-child selectors. Only 11 Sticky Bar’s close button and 12 Meter’s label depend on a module, and both have a quoted degradation. A reader who has access never sees this section ⚑ — Ghost sends the whole post and A32 does not render. There is no “unlocked” state to design, and no design may draw one.

The ring is drawn against the plane’s surface, where the accent measures 4.4:1 on #FFFFFF ⚑. The mono ordinals in the included row are aria-hidden and are not targets.
