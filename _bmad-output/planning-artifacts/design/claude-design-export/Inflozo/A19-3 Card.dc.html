Card

One featured post on a surface plane, inset 48 px from the content width, its picture held inside the card's padding at the left and the text at the right. The section is the card, which is what separates this from 1 Split — there the post sits on the page, here it sits on something.

The reason to reach for it is a page that has no other boundary. Between two text sections a split reads as a paragraph with a photograph; a card reads as an object. It is also the one design in A19 where A17's tag plate still works at full size — the picture is 480 px inside a container rather than half a page, so a plate at that box is a card element rather than a wall. A19-0 settlement 3 calls that answer Plate.

The 48 is what makes the page visible on both sides of it, which is the whole reason to draw a card at all.
The picture is inside the padding, not bled to the card's edge. That is a decision and it costs 80 px of picture: a bled image would make the card a post card at section scale, and A17·4 already owns that object. Holding the picture inside the padding keeps the card reading as a container with a post in it rather than as one enormous post card. A17·17 Panel offered Head Inside · Outside as a control; A19 does not, because a section title inside a card that contains one post is three levels of framing. Outside is the only value and it is not a setting. Two “Featured” labels, one in the head and one on the card, is the failure this rule prevents. The heading level moves with it. Head authored → the head title is the <h2> and the post's title is an <h3>; no head → the post's title is the <h2>. Category rule, drawn both ways in this file. WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES Vertical spacing is the space around the card, and the old Padding row was that under another name. The card's own 40 px of internal padding is fixed. A19 has no equivalent of A18's Row density, and this is the design where the absence is most visible — one post has one spacing, and the card's padding is a property of the card the way its radius is. The panel names Outline as the stronger dark value, and does not switch to it. A18 recorded that its two plane designs are measurably weaker in dark because Paper's surface step is modest and the shadow is dropped; the same is true here and the honest fix is a pack-level dark elevation, which is a finding rather than a control. Until it exists the panel advises and the user decides. Cut: a hover lift. A17·4's card rises 2 px under the cursor and this one does not — a 1,200 × 400 plane that moves is a page that moves. The hover state is the title's accent underline, as everywhere else in the category. Cut: a Media side control, for the reason drawn above. Cut: a card width control — 48 px of page on each side is what makes the card a card, and every other value of it is either a band or a box.

1 · Descriptor. The only card containment in A19 apart from 7 Overlap, which differs on ground, count-adjacent slots and media placement. 3 · Archetype. split. One departure: the ladder narrows a split's columns before stacking them, and this stacks at 834 without narrowing, because the card's padding has already taken 80 px out of the content width. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 card 1,200 at a 48 inset, padding 40; image 480 × 320, gap 48, text 592; title 34. 1080 card 936 at a 36 inset, image 400, text 460. 834 inset 0, card 754, padding 32, picture above the text at 690 wide, title 30. ≤ 767 card at the content width, padding 20, title 26, excerpt clamped to one line, reading time dropped. Six of its own, plus the universal trio and the Data group outside the list. Quick: Card, Ratio, Title size, Excerpt.
7 · Data. As 1 Split: featured:true, limit: 1, Count the stepper, locked at 1 and drawn disabled. 0 → the section does not render and the card goes with it; an empty card is a hole with a border around it. 0 in the editor → the card's outline at its set ratio plus the standard line. 1 → the design's designed case. This design is the strongest in A19 at exactly one post and the panel says so, because a container is a claim that what is inside it is complete. 8 · Empty state. No feature image → Plate: A17's tag plate at the image box, #F4F0E8 light and #2A251E dark, the primary tag centred at 13 px uppercase, aria-hidden, capped at 320 px tall so Portrait and Square fall back to Landscape's box. Untagged and no picture → the plate is drawn empty, never with the date or the author's name. No excerpt → the card is shorter and the picture sets its height. No tag and a head authored → no eyebrow line at all. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. The card is a <div> and the anchor is inside it, wrapping picture and text; the card's own padding is not part of the link target, so the ring sits inside the card at a 4 px offset and does not cross its edge. Head authored → head title <h2>, post <h3>; no head → post <h2>. Plate aria-hidden and its tag repeated in the eyebrow, so nothing is announced twice and nothing is lost. Contrast on surface 16.1:1 title, 5.8:1 muted light; 14.9:1 and 6.4:1 dark. Outline in dark measures the same as Surface, which is the argument for it. Reconciled. Padding retired into Vertical spacing (it was already “the space around the card”); Tag: Show · Hide added, and at Hide with no picture the plate is drawn empty rather than dropped, because the card's geometry needs the box; linkUrl removed; the Data group carries Hand-picked (one post) and Count as a stepper locked at 1; the excerpt binds excerpt.
