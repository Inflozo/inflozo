Big Type

Three titles at 44, one under another, with a hairline between them — no pictures, no excerpts, and a byline in 14 px under each.

It is the loudest design in the category and it says the least: the title is the only thing a related post has that is reliably good, so this design prints it at the size a headline deserves and stops. It is also where 8 Overlay lands when a set turns out to have no photographs.

44 px, line height 1.08, tracking −0.025em, on a 1,180 measure ⚑ — a 69-character title takes two lines and the design expects it. The hairline is 28 px from the line above and 28 from the one below, so the rhythm is the same whether a title is one line or three.
This is the only design in A27 whose vertical size is set by its content rather than by a ratio ⚑: three short titles are 380 px of section and three long ones are 620. Nothing clamps and nothing truncates.
⚑ Mine: the 1,180 measure, the 28 px hairline inset, and holding the byline at 14 rather than 13 — one step up, because under a 44 px line a 13 px byline reads as a footnote.

Post content is Ghost's and is never inline-editable ⚑ — clicking a post title, tag name, author name or excerpt says "Edit in Ghost". Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker. A27 authors no image ⚑, so rule 10's Image focus has nothing to attach to: the crop is named by the Ratio ladder and the focal point belongs to the post, in Ghost. No button anywhere in A27 ⚑, so rule 11's optional icon has nowhere to sit — the archive link is text with an accent underline. There is no Preview control and there never was one to remove.Member Visibility lands nowhere in A27 ⚑ — and that is a judgement, not an omission: nothing here is a CTA. Large 44 → 34 → 30 and Display 56 → 42 → 30 ⚑ — the two values converge on a phone, so a site that set Display sees the same 350 px column as one that set Large. It is the honest end of a display ladder at 350 px: 56 px type is four words a line.
