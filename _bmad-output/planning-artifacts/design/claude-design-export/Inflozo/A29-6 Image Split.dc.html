PASS FIVE PATCH · 3 SEPTEMBER 2026 · IMAGE FOCUS IS ONE SHARED CONTROL, DEFINED IN P0·9
What changed on this frameThe panel's Image focus row now draws both axes — one label, two segmented rows, both resting at Centre — and its note points at P0·9 instead of listing the values. The panel's prose note, the Reconciled line and the drawn spec's Controls entry lost their copies with it. No control was added or removed and the count stays six; focus sits in the uncounted Data group. No measure, ratio, type step, colour or spacing step changed.
The rule that required itOne control name means one set of values. Image focus was never a shared control: two dozen category specs each wrote out their own copy of its values, which is that rule at its worst in the library. It is now defined once, in P0·9, carrying both axes — up and down, and side to side — and a category supplies only the slot it applies to. The side-to-side axis is the owner's ruling of 3 September 2026 and A29 did not have it.
On this designThree named shapes, and the crop is hardest at Square: up and down chooses the head or the feet of a landscape file, and side to side chooses which of two people stays in shot. Neither axis is narrowed. Image side is still visual only, and the DOM stays name-then-image at both values.
Ghost never sees thisFocus is a hint the compiler resolves into the crop the theme ships with: the project stores the choice, the compiler resolves it, and nothing about it reaches Ghost or comes back — which is why it is a control and not a data binding. It declares no behaviour module, so this design's no-JavaScript line is unchanged.
OPEN FOR THE OWNEROPEN FOR THE OWNER — where the control is drawn. What I would have needed to know: whether P0·9's "one label, two segmented rows, under the image slot" means the section panel, or whether A29's Image Picker popover satisfies it. Why it is open: A29 settled on 25 August 2026 that focus lives in the picker's popover and is never a hidden field; P0·9 describes the shape of the control but not which surface hosts it, and A16 raised the same question independently. What I did instead: kept A29's popover home as found, drew both axes in it, and cited P0·9 as the definition.
The numbering ruleThis is still design 6 · Image Split. A29 runs 1 to 14: nothing was renumbered, no number was reused, and no gap was created or closed.
No design total is written anywhereChecked, and nothing was added. No copy on this frame quotes a library size, in any form.
