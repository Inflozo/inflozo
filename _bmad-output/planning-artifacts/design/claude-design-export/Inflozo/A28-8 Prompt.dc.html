8 · Prompt

For a reader who is not signed in, the section is the publication’s own invitation: a heading, the count of what they are missing, and a sign-up field that posts straight to Ghost’s members endpoint. Signed-in members get the block and no prompt at all.

It buys that with the one real cost in the category: by default a signed-out reader does not see the discussion ⚑. Ghost’s block draws its own sign-up prompt and it cannot be switched off, so a section that shows both shows two calls to action stacked. The control that allows it is in the panel, and its hint says what it does.

No control anywhere in A28 claims to style inside Ghost’s component ⚑ — mode and saturation pass through, and everything else in this panel is the chrome around the block. EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery visible authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: headingText, promptHeading, promptBody, promptButton, signinLabel, upgradeHeading, upgradeButton, rulesText, closedNotice. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the comment count, and every name, avatar and word inside the block, say “Edit in Ghost” or show P0·1’s lock pill. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the count’s three words are authored fields with the old literals as their defaults, and aria-label="Comments" plus the <noscript> line are translation-catalog strings. There is no Preview control and there never was one to remove.Member Visibility is not offered here ⚑ — and that is rule 9’s own exception: this design already carries a richer member-state model. Signed-out readers, Free members and the Ghost commenting setting decide between three arrangements, and a fourth Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid row on top of them would be two systems answering one question. P0·4 opens twice here, on both buttons. Rule 10’s Image focus has nothing to attach to.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESReconciled. Padding retired into Vertical spacing, and Top divider arrives as a row this design never had. The free-member-under-paid-commenting state is designed ⚑ — Free members joins the panel, upgradeHeading and upgradeButton join the fields, the button takes Portal’s upgrade action through P0·4, and the email field is never drawn in that state. ARCHITECT: verify stays on the frame. Count words become fields, Match the page resolves to auto on a dual-mode site, the block group is recut as the Data group. Six controls of its own — the most in A28.This design decides what a signed-out reader sees, and that is a publishing decision rather than a styling one. The default withholds the thread and offers membership; the alternative shows the thread and asks twice. Both are drawn on the states frame so the choice is made with the picture in front of you.The prompt is never drawn to a member ⚑, at any setting. There is no control for it, because a sign-up form shown to a subscriber is a bug wearing a toggle.Accent appears once: the button at Form Email field, or the link at Link only. Never both, and never anywhere else in the section — A28’s accent rule, which is the design system’s.
