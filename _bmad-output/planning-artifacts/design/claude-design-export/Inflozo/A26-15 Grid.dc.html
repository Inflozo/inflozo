Grid

Three labelled columns across the content width — written by, filed under, read next — the avatar at the top of the first, the share row spanning under all three behind a hairline.

It is the site footer’s shape applied to a post: A3·2’s columns, at a third of the height, with the post’s own facts in them. Where 2 Rows names its blocks in a column of labels, this one names them across the page, which is the difference between a list and a directory.

Three 400 px columns on 48 gutters with a hairline in each ⚑ — the same construction as A3·2’s site footer, at a quarter of its content. The columns are equal and are not weighted: a bio, three tags and two titles are three different amounts of content and giving them three different widths would make the labels stop lining up, which is the whole design.
The avatar sits above the name rather than beside it ⚑ — that is what makes the media placement top and what separates this design from 1 Author Bio at the tuple. It also makes the three column heads line up: label, then content, in every column.

One site-wide ordered list, not a section control ⚑ — X · Facebook · LinkedIn · Bluesky · Mastodon · Threads · WhatsApp · Reddit · Email · Copy link. The owner orders and enables them once with P0·3’s drag-reorder and per-destination enable in the setting, never here; this row is read-only and links to it. Share links draws the first N of what is enabled, in the owner’s order, so a site that shares to LinkedIn now can — A24·14’s stated cost, paid off. Glyphs are fixed per-platform brand icons from P0·2’s Social / Brands group and are not choosable per section ⚑.

EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESInline on canvas with P0·1: the three column labels, tagsLabel, shareLabel, copiedLabel, authorLinkLabel. Ghost-owned and never inline-editable: post titles, tag names, author names, bios and social handles — clicking one selects the section and the sidebar says “Edit in Ghost” ⚑. The socials row draws under the bio in the first column ⚑ at 38-in-44, glyphs from P0·2, and wraps inside its column rather than widening it. P0·3’s item controls land nowhere: A26 authors no array — the tags, the authors and the two neighbours are Ghost’s, and a Ghost-sourced list never shows an Add ⚑. P0·4 and Member Visibility land on 12 Subscribe alone in this category. P0·5’s “Populate from…” panel lands nowhere — the footer reads the post’s own relations, not a query ⚑. No Preview control existed to remove; P0·6’s state switcher is how the absent blocks and the confirmations are seen while editing. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships ⚑: every authored label carries a default, and the strings nobody authors are theme translation-catalog strings.
DATA · THE POST’S OWN RELATIONS, AND NOTHING QUERIEDtags[], authors[] with the socials on Ghost ≥ 6.36, prev_post/next_post, post.url, post.title. {{#prev_post}}/{{#next_post}} are post-context only ⚑ — on a page the pair is absent, which is the category’s absent-block rule and not a new one. Tags 0 at Columns Three → two columns, widened, no reserved third; destinations 0 → the column is absent and the grid is two. There is no way to put a block in a different column ⚑, and no Add anywhere — all three columns are Ghost’s content. The destinations are the site setting’s.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThere is no four-column value ⚑ — A26 has four blocks and the fourth is the share row, which spans rather than sits in a column. A subscribe form in a 300 px column is a field with no room for an address, and that settles it.The column order is fixed: written by, filed under, read next ⚑ — the category’s order with share lifted out of it, and it is the one place in A26 where the fixed order is expressed left to right rather than top to bottom.Cut: column weights, a per-column alignment, a fourth column, a heading over the grid ⚑ — "About the author" above a column labelled "Written by" is the same words twice.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. Three labelled columns across the content width — written by, filed under, read next — the avatar above the name in the first, the share row spanning beneath behind a hairline. A3·2’s site footer, at a post’s scale.2 · Structural descriptor. grid-of-N · none · page · few · top · three closing columns
Archetype grid-of-N: the only one in A26. Media top is the avatar above the name — the slot that separates this design from 1 Author Bio’s left. Count few: two or three columns.3 · Archetype. grid-of-N. No departures — three to two to one is the archetype’s own ladder, and the only addition is that the width and the Columns control resolve to the same two-column arrangement ⚑.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 three 400 px columns on 48 gutters with a 1 px rule in each, labels 11 px uppercase, avatar 56 above a 19 px name, bio clamped at three lines, destinations 17 px with next above previous, share row spanning under a hairline. 834 two columns, tags under the bio ⚑, gutters 40. ≤ 767 one column, labels kept, gutter rules become horizontal hairlines ⚑.5 · Content fields. Read: tags[], authors[], prev_post, next_post, post.url, post.title. Authored: shareLabel, copiedLabel, authorLinkLabel, tagsLabel. The three column labels are theme strings ⚑, as 2 Rows established. Unread: subscribeHeading and subscribeLine except at Blocks Everything, archiveLabel, post.updated_at.6 · Controls, in sidebar order. Columns Two · Three. Column rule Hairline · None. Blocks (five values). Avatar Compact 48 · Comfortable 56 · Spacious 72. Bio lines One · Three · Full — a different ladder from the category’s ⚑. Share links Two · Three · Four. Six. Cut: column weights, a fourth column, per-column alignment, a heading.
7 · Data. Tags: 0 at Columns Three → two columns, widened, no reserved third ⚑; 0 at Two → nothing under the bio; many → the list runs down its column and the column grows. Authors: 2 → two stacked in the first column at 48 px avatars with a hairline ⚑; 3+ → overlapped avatars and a names line. Destinations: 1 → one in the column; 0 → the column is absent and the grid is two ⚑. A grid of one column is 1 Author Bio and the panel says so ⚑.8 · Empty state. An empty column is never drawn and never reserved ⚑ — the remaining columns widen to fill the content width. With every block empty the section renders nothing. Editor: three dashed columns, each labelled with what Ghost supplies.9 · Behaviour module. share, and member-form at Blocks Everything. No-JS, quoted: share — "Share links are real <a href="https://…"> URLs and work normally; only the copy-link button is hidden." member-form — "The <form> posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint; Ghost’s own server response replaces the designed sent state." Edit-safe.10 · Accessibility. <footer> inside the <article>. Each column is a <section aria-label> taking its label, and the labels are <span> elements rather than headings ⚑ — A24 owns the page’s heading levels and a footer contributes none. DOM order matches visual order at every width. The destinations column is <nav> with rel attributes. Measured light: labels 5.4:1, name 13.1:1, bio 5.4:1, titles 13.1:1. Print: the columns print as three stacked blocks ⚑, share omitted.Repeating items. Three, all Ghost’s. Columns writes one value onto the section; there is no per-column control, no column reordering and no way to put a block in a different column.Reconciled. Author socials joins the list (in the first column, under the bio, wrapping inside it) and the three column labels become authored fields with defaults ⚑, editable inline while staying <span> elements used as aria-label. The destinations become one site-wide ordered setting with fixed brand glyphs. Seven controls plus the universal trio, Line spanning all three columns.Flagged ⚑ — equal columns rather than weighted; the avatar above the name as the tuple’s media slot; next above previous; the tags moving under the bio at Columns Two and at 834; no reserved column for absent content; two authors stacked at 48; the gutter rules becoming horizontal; Bio lines carrying a different ladder here; the labels as theme strings; the columns printing stacked.
