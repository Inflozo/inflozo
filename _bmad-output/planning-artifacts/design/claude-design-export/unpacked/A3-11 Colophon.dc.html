Colophon

A short paragraph about the publication, set as reading text, with the credits beneath it as labelled pairs. The footer as the back page of a magazine: who makes this, where, since when, set in what. It is the only design in A3 whose main content is prose, and the only one where the body font does the display work.

Prose is the one place in A3 where links sit inside a sentence: they take a 30% underline at rest and a full one on hover, and they stay text colour rather than muted, because a muted word inside a dark paragraph reads as a typo. 1 · The colophon is a plain <p> inside the footer, with no heading above it — it introduces itself in its first four words, and a “About this publication” heading would say less than the sentence does. Inline links are ordinary links; two per paragraph maximum. 2 · Credits are a <dl> in a section labelled by its visible <h2> “Masthead”, so each label names its value. The hairlines are borders on the rows, not <hr>s. 3 · Values are plain text unless authored as links. The ISSN is not linked anywhere by default — flagged as mine, and it is a place a site might reasonably want a link the design does not provide. Reading text in a footer · at 17 px on a 600 px measure the paragraph is genuine reading text, so it obeys the body rules rather than the meta rules: text-wrap: pretty, no justification, no hyphenation, and it reflows rather than clipping at any zoom. There is no character cap on it — the ugly test for this design is a 600-word paragraph, and the answer is that it renders in full and the footer gets tall, which is the site's choice to make. Contrast · prose 12.1:1 light; in dark it is #E8E2D7 at 10.9:1, stepped back deliberately from 11.8:1 to reduce glare, still far above the floor. Credit labels 5.4:1 / 6.4:1, values 12.1:1 / 11.8:1.
