Slim

A band 92 to 148 px tall with no head, no note and no link: three to six marks and nothing else. What sits directly under a hero or directly above a footer, where the page has already said what the logos are for and a second title would say it again.

It carries its own padding scale, which does not step with width — A7·8's and A10·13's, the one width-independent spacing scale in the category — and it keeps ten of the fourteen fields undrawn. Switching to 1 Row draws all of them.

At Alignment Spread the logos sit at their natural widths with the space distributed between them, the first on the left page margin and the last on the right. That makes this the third design in A11 with no cell grid, after 8 Marquee and 9 Rail — and the only one of the three where the absence is visible, because a spread row's gaps are unequal by construction: Meridian Press to longform is not the same distance as Cartograph to Pinhole. That is the design, not a defect: equal gaps with unequal artwork is what a cell grid is for, and 1 Row is the cell grid.
Band height is the padding twice plus the box: 92 at Small/Compact, 124 here, 148 at Medium/Spacious with two rules. The rule is a border hairline at the full content width, never inset, never the accent, and it sits inside the page margins rather than running edge to edge — an edge-to-edge rule is a section divider and this is a section. ⚑ Mine: the three band heights, the unequal gaps at Spread, and the rule keeping the margins.

Spread is right for five and six and wrong for three: three marks distributed across 1,296 puts 400 px of ground between them and reads as three separate things. Left and Centred take a fixed 64 px gap — 9 Rail's Comfortable value — and the panel advises Centred under four. The control does not switch itself. ⚑ Mine. Rule has three values and Above is the default, because this band's usual job is to close off whatever is above it. Both is the value for a band between two sections that both need separating; None is for a band inside a page that is already ruled. The rule takes border and never the accent, at 1.4:1 light and 1.6:1 dark, decorative. Logo size Large 48 is not offered, and it is the fourth design in A11 to refuse it. A 48 px box inside a 32 px padding is a 112 px band, which is no longer slim — and the design's whole contract is a short band. This is also the only design in A11 with no head at any value, so there is nothing else in the band to set the box against.

This is the only design in the category that refuses a second row outright rather than reflowing it. At Alignment Left with six marks the row ends 214 px short of the right margin, which is what Left means — the row is a row of marks, not a row that fills the page. Spread would close that gap and unequalise the others; the site chooses which it minds.
The padding does not step with width: 32 · 44 · 56 at 1440, 834 and 390 alike. A band chosen for being short has nothing to give back on a phone, and a scale that halved would make Compact a 20 px inset around 24 px of artwork. Treatment Muted at 74% in dark is the quietest a wall gets in this category, and with no URLs authored this frame contains no accent pixel and nothing focusable at all. ⚑ Mine: the three band heights, and the padding not stepping.

ACCESSIBILITY · A SECTION WITH NO NAME AND USUALLY NOTHING TO OPERATE No <h2>, no aria-label, no aria-labelledby, no landmark name. The section is a <section> with a <ul> in it, so it is not announced as a region at all — which is correct for a band with no title, and is A9·13's and A10·13's position taken a third time. Generating “Logo wall” would put a name in the landmark list that the site never wrote. The alt text is doing all the work in this design. With no title, no note and no link, the five brand names are the section's entire content for a screen reader — which is the strongest case in the category for alt being required and never empty. The rule is a border on the section's inner container, never an <hr>. An <hr> is a thematic break and this rule is a margin — it says where the band starts, not that a subject changed. In forced colours it survives as a system border. With no URLs authored the section has no tab stop, no state, no behaviour and no accent pixel — the emptiest section in A11 by every measure. With URLs it has one tab stop per linked logo and the hover rule is A11-0's, unchanged. Contrast: the rule 1.4:1 light / 1.6:1 dark, decorative. Nothing else in the section is text, so there is no other ratio to report — the one design in A11 with no type in it at all. A logo takes no ratio; at Muted the editor's 3:1 advice applies. Reduced motion changes nothing. At 400% the band is two across and about 190 px tall, which is the stated cost rather than a defect. Print draws the band and its rules as they are.
