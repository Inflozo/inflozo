Two focal points, ranked and kept apart: the play control sits on the poster's own centre line — 250 px down, which is the optical centre of the picture above the text rather than of the whole frame — and the set stays in the lower third where 4 Full Bleed put it. The play control is a labelled button, never a bare glyph: a triangle in a circle over a photograph is a convention, not a name, and this one has to say what it plays and how long it lasts. Its ground is the pack's lightest surface with the md shadow, because it is the one element in the category that must be findable against a picture nobody has seen. The poster is a button and the button opens a dialog: an 88% contrast wash, the player at 16:9 on the widest measure that fits with 40 px to spare, a 44 px labelled close at the top right, and the video's own title and duration beneath. A15 Video and Embeds owns the player itself — its controls, its provider handling and its aspect logic — and A4 owns only the poster, the button and this shell. Nothing plays until the click: no autoplay, no muted background loop, no preloading of the provider's iframe, which is also why the hero is not 300 kB of third-party script on first paint.

No videoUrl at all · the design is not offered in the picker, and if the field is cleared on a live section the editor says which field it needs and shows 4 Full Bleed in its place until one is supplied. 1 · The poster is decorative — alt="" — as every picture with words over it is in this category. It is not wrapped in the play button: the button is the button, so the target is 46 px of labelled control rather than 810 px of photograph, and a reader dragging to select the headline does not launch a film. 2 · The play control is a real <button> — it does something rather than going somewhere — named by its visible words plus its duration: “Watch the film, 4 minutes 12 seconds”, with the mono figure given a full reading in an aria-label so nobody hears “four colon twelve”. The triangle is aria-hidden CSS. Flagged as mine: the spoken duration. 3 · Headline is the page's h1 and plain text, as 4 Full Bleed; contrast is measured against the scrimmed poster and carries the same honest limit — a floor against the darkest reasonable still, not a guarantee. The dialog · a real <dialog> with aria-modal, labelled by the video's title. Focus moves to the close button on open, is trapped inside while open, and returns to the play control on close. Escape closes it. The page behind does not scroll. Nothing plays before the click and nothing keeps playing after the close — closing stops playback rather than muting it. Reduced motion · the dialog appears without a transition and the play control's hover is a colour change only. The video itself is the user's choice to start, which is why an autoplaying hero would fail this rule and a click-to-play one does not. Contrast · play label on the lightest surface 12.6:1, its duration 5.1:1, headline over the scrim measured as 4 Full Bleed, close button's outline on the 88% wash 8.9:1. The control's own ground does not depend on the poster at all, which is the point of giving it a fill.
