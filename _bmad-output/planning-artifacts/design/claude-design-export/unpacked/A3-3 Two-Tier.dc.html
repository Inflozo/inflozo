A3 FOOTERS · DESIGN 3 OF 16 · PAPER PACK

Two-Tier

The links sit on one ground and the legal bar on another: a fat tier of columns above, then a full-bleed bar carrying the lockup, the legal line and the social row. The lockup lives in the bar, not in the tier — that is the whole difference from A3·2, and it is what lets the bar be reused as the site's constant while the tier above it changes. The bar is A3·1 Minimal Line, unchanged.

Background tier, contrast bar. On the contrast bar the mark inverts — accent square becomes the pack's lightest surface carrying the contrast colour as its letter — because an accent square on near-ink is the one place in Paper where the mark loses its edge. Carried text runs at 78% for the legal line, full strength for the wordmark. This is the pairing to use when a page ends in a wide image: the dark bar closes the page.

Contrast tier, background bar. In dark the tier's surface is #211D17 against a #171511 bar — a lift of about 4% in luminance. It reads, but it is the smallest ground change in the pack, so the hairline between the tiers is load-bearing in dark and decorative in light. Where a pack's dark surface and background are closer still, the hairline is what separates them; that is why Top edge: None is not offered on this design.

Content fields Same list as A3·2 minus tagline, which has nowhere to sit in either tier: wordmark · mark · linkColumns[] (2–7) · social[] · copyright · legalLinks[] · showAttribution. The tagline is kept and returns on switching to A3·2 — the case rule 3 of the brief exists for.
Controls · data Padding: Compact 40 · Comfortable 56 · Spacious 80 (tier; the bar is fixed at 24 and does not follow). Ground pair: Surface tier · background bar / Background tier · contrast bar / Contrast tier · background bar. Columns: Auto · Two · Three · Four. Bar contents: Lockup, social, legal · Lockup and legal · Legal only. Social: Glyphs · Labels · Off. Ghost attribution: Show · Hide.
Data: as A3·2. No Top edge control — the pair always differ, so the ground change is the divider and the hairline is fixed on.
Responsive · empty · a11y · flagged Tier follows A3·2's column rule at every width, including the five-column accordion threshold. Bar follows A3·1: one row to 768, three centred rows at ≤ 767. Both keep their grounds at every width.
Empty: no columns → the tier is removed and what remains is A3·1 Minimal Line on the bar's ground; the sidebar says so rather than drawing an empty tier. Bar contents: Legal only → the lockup leaves and the legal line spans left.
a11y: one contentinfo around both tiers; no CSS re-ordering at any width; per-pairing contrast measured in the accessibility frame.
Flagged as mine: the three ground pairings and the refusal of a matched pair, the bar's fixed 24 px padding, the mark's inversion on contrast, and the 72% / 78% carried-text values.
