Centre Nav

Logo left, actions right, nav centred in the space between them. The nav is the thing the eye lands on, and the wordmark and actions become the frame around it. Where A1·2 centres the wordmark and splits the nav, this keeps the nav whole and centres that instead — the same parts, weighted the other way.

The nav is centred on the page, not on the space left over: a three-column grid with equal outer columns holds it in place even though the wordmark and the action cluster are different widths. That is the “True centre” setting, and it is the default because a nav that shifts as the CTA label changes looks like a bug.

ACCESSIBILITY · visual centre, source order unchanged
1Placed by grid. The three-column grid places the nav visually; the DOM stays logo → nav → actions, so focus moves left to right across the bar in both the centred and anchored arrangements.
2The re-anchor is silent. It is a layout change with no change to content or order, so nothing is announced and no focus is lost when the viewport crosses the threshold mid-session.
3One landmark, one label. header, then nav aria-label="Primary"; the active item carries aria-current="page" alongside its underline. Skip link first in focus order.
4Nav spacing has a floor. “Tight” is 20 px between items, which keeps 15 px labels from reading as one string and leaves room for the focus ring. “Airy” is 40 px.
5Icon-only search. 38 px visually, 44 px hit area, accessible name “Search”. Same component as A1·1.

Content fieldsShared A1 list, no additions. 4 nav items shown, items 5+ fold to More, children two deep.
DataIdentical to Rail. Empty nav → the centre column is removed and the grid becomes logo left, actions right; the bar keeps its height.
Responsive≥ 1025 three-column grid, nav truly centred. ≤ 1024 the grid becomes a row and the nav anchors beside the logo; nothing is dropped or folded at that point. 1023–768 narrowing only. ≤ 767 nav and Sign in to the drawer, Subscribe stays with its short label.
Empty stateNo CTA → the right column holds search and Sign in; the nav stays centred, since the grid’s outer columns are equal regardless of their contents.
FlaggedThe 1024 re-anchor threshold and the 20/30/40 px spacing values are mine. So is the choice to make True centre the default over Optical — optical centring shifts the nav as the CTA label changes, which reads as instability.
