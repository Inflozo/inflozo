↑ Ground: Contrast · the hairline goes, the button inverts, the ghost action takes the carried colour at 72%. Only two grounds here: a toast on accent at 380 × 120 px is a poster, not an announcement.
Appears · three values, and why there is no fourth Immediately — with the page, like every other A2 design. After 2 s — the default; long enough that the page has painted and the reader has started reading, short enough to still be about this page. After 25% scroll — arrives when the reader has committed to the article. No “on exit intent”: it needs mouse tracking, does not exist on touch, and reads as a trap. Flagged as a deliberate omission rather than an oversight.
Position · and what it must not cover Bottom right (default) · Bottom left · Bottom centre. Whichever is chosen, the card sits above the mobile safe-area inset and never over a fixed element the site already owns — if the theme has a bottom-anchored player or a consent bar (A2·13), the toast stacks 12 px above it rather than on top of it. Flagged: the stacking order between Toast and Consent is mine — consent first, always, because it gates everything else.

1 · <aside aria-label="Announcement"> at the end of the body — the one A2 design that is not before the header, because it is not part of the top of the page and putting it first would make it the first thing every keyboard user meets. It carries aria-live="polite", which the load-present bars do not: this card arrives after the page has settled, so it must be announced when it does, once. 2 · The title is a <p> at 14 px / 600, not a heading — the category floor holds even in a card that looks like it wants an h3. 3 · Focus is not moved into the card. It is not a dialog: it does not trap focus, it does not block the page, and the reader may ignore it entirely. Focus order is title link if any, primary, ghost, close — reached in normal document order at the end of the page. 4 · Close is a labelled button, 32 px in a 44 px hit area, 44 px at ≤ 767. Escape dismisses the card from anywhere on the page, the only A2 design where that is true, because it is the only one that behaves like something that arrived. Dismissal returns focus to where it was, never to the header. Contrast and placement · title 13.2:1, body 5.4:1, ghost action 5.4:1 with an underline on hover and focus. The card never covers a fixed element the site already owns: it stacks 12 px above a consent bar or a player, and sits above the mobile safe-area inset.
