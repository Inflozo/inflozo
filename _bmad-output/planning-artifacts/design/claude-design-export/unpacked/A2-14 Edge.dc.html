A2 ANNOUNCEMENT BARS · DESIGN 14 OF 15 · PAPER PACK

Edge

A 4 px accent rule across the top of the page with a small chip at its right end. Pressed, it opens into a full bar; pressed again, it closes back to the rule. It costs 4 px of furniture instead of 44, which makes it the design for a site that wants a standing announcement without giving up the top of every page — and the one whose whole difficulty is being noticed at all.

Open, the bar is A2·1 Rule on contrast with the accent rule still above it — 48 px in total, and the page moves down by the 44 px difference over 160 ms. The chip becomes the word “Close” with the caret flipped, in the carried colour at 72%, and it keeps its position so the thing you pressed has not moved. The × beside it is not the same control: the chip collapses back to the rule, the × dismisses the bar for the memory period and takes the rule with it.

1 · Skip link first, ahead of the bar, as everywhere. 2 · <aside aria-label="Announcement"> before <header>. The 4 px rule is decorative and aria-hidden — it carries no information a reader could act on. 3 · The chip is the design: <button aria-expanded="false"> with the sentence's own text as its accessible name, so a screen reader hears the announcement without opening anything — “Issue 47 is out — the slow return of the personal homepage. Show announcement.” The visible label stays the authored short one. Chip: None is disabled because a hover-only 4 px strip fails 2.1.1. 4 · Opening does not move focus; the sentence is inside the same aside, so focus reaches it next in order. The chip toggles aria-expanded; Escape collapses the bar when focus is inside it. The × is a separate labelled button — “Dismiss announcement” — and its effect is different: collapse keeps the rule, dismiss removes both for the memory period. Two controls 24 px apart that do different things is the risk this design carries, and it is why the collapse control is a word rather than a second glyph. Motion and contrast · opening is a 160 ms height change and the page moves down 44 px with it; under reduced-motion the change is instant, and the page still moves — a state change is not motion. Chip label on accent 4.9:1 at 11.5 px / 600; the rule is decoration, so its contrast is not load-bearing.
