A caption is the one piece of content in the category that belongs to the picture rather than to the page, which is why it is the only text below the band and why it never becomes a credit line, a link or a source. Where a site needs a photographer's credit it goes in the caption's own words — the theme does not add “Photo:”.

1 · Eyebrow and headline as 1 Centred: a <p> above the page's single <h1>, in an unlabelled section. 2 · Alt is asked for, as 3 Split: nothing sits over this picture, so it is not decoration by construction. Explicit dimensions, eager and high priority, and the 2.4:1 box reserved — this is the design where reservation matters most, because 600 px of unreserved height would move the whole page when the file lands. 3 · The caption is a real <figcaption> and the band and caption together are a <figure> — so the association survives when the caption is 80 characters and sits under a full-bleed band whose left edge is 72 px away. Where the caption is off, the figure is still a figure with no caption rather than a bare <img>. Flagged as mine — the figure is the reason a caption in this category never becomes a link or a credit field. Contrast · headline 12.1:1 / 11.8:1 · sub and caption 5.4:1 / 6.4:1 · buttons as 1 Centred. No text sits on the picture at any width, so no contrast in this design depends on the user's file — which is why it is the design the editor offers when a photograph defeats 4 Full Bleed's scrim. Forced colours and zoom · the band keeps its box, the caption keeps its size, and at 200% text zoom the set above grows while the band's height stays a function of its width. Nothing overlaps, so nothing can collide.
