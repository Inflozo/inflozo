ACCESSIBILITY · icons need names, settings need state
1Every icon has a text name. “Search”, “RSS feed”, “Dark mode” — as the accessible name, and as a tooltip on hover and focus after 500 ms. The glyphs are decorative and hidden from the accessibility tree.
2The toggle announces its state. A button with aria-pressed, name “Dark mode”, kept in sync with the drawer’s switch row. Its on state is a fill, not a colour change, so it survives forced-colours mode.
3The member menu is a menu. aria-expanded on the pill, Escape closes and returns focus, arrow keys move between rows. The member block at the top is static text, not a focus stop.
4Bare icons still need contrast. With no border or fill, the glyph carries the whole control: muted on background must clear 4.5:1, and a pack that cannot manage it renders the Outlined style instead.
5Initials are a fallback, not an identity. The avatar’s accessible name is the member’s full name; the initials are decorative. A member with no name falls back to their email’s local part, and the pill keeps its 44 px target either way.

Empty stateNo social links and no dark mode → the cluster is search alone, and the hairline separator is dropped rather than left dividing one item from the member pill. No member picture → initials on accent.
FlaggedThe 2-social cap, the 500 ms tooltip delay, the mono “RSS” wordmark in place of a drawn glyph, keeping the avatar in the mobile bar, and the fallback from Bare to Outlined on a failing pack are mine.
