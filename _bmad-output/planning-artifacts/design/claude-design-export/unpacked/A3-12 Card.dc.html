A3 FOOTERS · DESIGN 12 OF 16 · PAPER PACK

Card

The footer as a panel set inside the page rather than a band across the bottom of it: a surface card on the pack's radius, inset from all three edges, with the page ground visible around it. The one design in A3 that does not touch the edges of the window, and the reason it exists is a page whose content is already carded — an archive grid, a pricing table — where a full-bleed footer reads as a different website.

40 px of page ground on the left, right and bottom; the card carries the pack's radius, one hairline and the md shadow — the same three values as every panel in the library, from A1's dropdown to A2·11's toast. Because the card supplies its own edge there is no hairline above the footer: the gap is the separator. Content inside sits on the card's own 48 px padding, which is why the columns do not line up with the page's 72 px text margin, and that misalignment is the point — a card is not the page.

INSET · the three named values · cropped to the bottom-left corner
Snug 16 — the card is nearly a band; use it when the page above is dense
Comfortable 40 — the default
Wide 72 — the card lines up with the page's own text margin

The inset is equal on left, right and bottom and always zero at the top, because the gap above the card is set by the page's last section, not by the footer. At Wide the card's left edge meets the 72 px text margin and the composition locks; at Snug it reads as a raised band. Radius stays the pack's token at every inset — the card never becomes a pill.

In dark the card is the pack's raised surface one step above the page and the shadow is dropped entirely: a warm shadow on a deep ground is invisible, and keeping it costs a paint for nothing. The hairline does the separating instead, which is the same substitution A3·3 documents between its tiers. This is the only design in A3 where a depth value is removed rather than re-tuned in dark.
