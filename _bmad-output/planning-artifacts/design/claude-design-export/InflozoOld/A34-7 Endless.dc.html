Endless

The next page arrives before the reader asks for it, and a small pill pinned to the bottom of the viewport says how far in they are. Nothing is drawn in the flow of the page at rest — the section's only visible element floats over the feed, which is why its ground is transparent.

It is the least honest pager in A34 and the category draws it anyway, with the costs written on the frame. The URL never changes, the footer moves away as you read, and a reader who leaves cannot come back to where they were. Two decisions answer that: it stops — by default after four pages, handing the archive to 6 Load More's button — and a real control is always in the markup for anyone not driving with a mouse wheel.

The pill is 44 px tall and sits 24 above the viewport's bottom edge, centred. It is 4 Pill's shape at 4 Pill's tokens — surface, hairline, md shadow, radius at half the height — borrowed rather than redesigned, and it is 12 px shorter because it holds one line of meta rather than a row of targets. Its ground is the feed, which is what transparent means in the tuple: the section contributes no ground of its own and the pill floats over whatever the reader has scrolled to.
“24 of 132 posts” is the same count line 6 Load More draws, in the same 13 px muted, and it is the only claim this design can honestly make: how much you have seen, never where you are. There is no page number in this design at any setting, because after two automatic loads there is no page the reader chose to be on.
Back to top is inside the pill and it is not decoration. ⚑ A feed that grows as you read puts its footer further away with every screen; the pill is where the reader already is, so the way out belongs there. It is an <a href="#top">, a real fragment link, working with the module absent.

The stop is the design's whole argument. Infinite scroll that never ends makes the footer unreachable, and a site's footer holds its archive links, its contact page and its licence. Four pages is 48 posts, which is more than anyone browses in one sitting, and after it the reader gets a control and the page gets an end. Stop after is a number now — two to eight pages, four by default — and the value that switched the stop off has gone with the row of fixed buttons it lived in. The hidden button is the difference between this design and a broken one. A sentinel that only responds to scrolling cannot be operated by a keyboard, a switch or a screen reader's virtual cursor; a focusable control in the flow can, and it is the same button 6 Load More draws, in the same three styles, at the same size. Focus does not move on an automatic load, and that is a departure worth stating. A18·15 moves focus to the first new row because the reader pressed a button and expects to be taken somewhere. Here nothing was pressed. The live region reports; the caret stays.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES The warning is three sentences and it is at the top of the panel, above the controls. ⚑ Not a tooltip, not a link to documentation, not a conditional error. A user choosing this design should read what it costs before they configure it, and the three costs are the ones a publisher actually feels: no shareable position, a receding footer, and crawling that happens through the hidden links rather than the scroll. A34 does not refuse infinite-scroll, and A17 and A18 both did. ⚑ That is a deliberate difference and it is not a reversal: A17 and A18 refused it inside a feed section, where the section owns a query with a Show value — auto-loading past Show makes the control a lie. A34 owns no query; it attaches to a route whose pagination is Ghost's, so the mechanism has something real to walk. The registry lists the module and the category that attaches beneath feeds is the right place for it. Stop after is the control that makes the module acceptable, and in this pass it became a number. It was three fixed buttons — 2 pages, 4 pages, Never — and an item count is a number picker with bounds the design can justify: 2 to 8, default 4. The cap is drawn with its reason: past eight automatic pages a reader is further from the footer than any button can bring them back. Never is gone rather than hidden — it was not a number, and a stop that never fires is the failure this design exists to avoid. The default is still 4 and still a design decision, not a shrug. Four controls. Cut: a scroll-threshold slider — Trigger's two named values are the only two anyone needs, and “load at 800 px” is not a named value — and a “resume where I left off” toggle, which needs storage the theme does not have.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. An invisible sentinel that fetches the next page as the reader approaches the end of the feed, a 44 px surface pill pinned to the bottom of the viewport carrying the count and a Back to top link, a focus-revealed button in the flow, and a hard stop after four pages, past which the reader is given a button in the flow and the pill retires. The only A34 design with nothing in the page's flow at rest. 2 · Structural descriptor. sticky · pill · transparent · none · none · auto-load with a pinned status
The category's only sticky, only transparent ground and only none count — three closed slots that no other design touches. The count is none because the reader presses nothing in the intended path; the focus-revealed button is a stated accessibility route, not the design's unit ⚑. Containment is pill and shares 4 Pill's geometry exactly. 3 · Archetype. sticky. One departure: at the stop the design leaves its archetype entirely and becomes a feed — the pill unpins, the sentinel is retired, and a button takes the flow. Stated as a hand-off rather than a state, which is A1·11's mechanism. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 pill contents-width, 44 tall, radius 22, centred, 24 above the viewport's foot; nothing in the flow; at the stop a 45 px contents-width button with the count beneath. 1080 and 834 identical — a pinned object does not scale. ≤ 767 pill takes the measure, 16 from the bottom plus env(safe-area-inset-bottom) ⚑, count shortens to “24 of 132”, Back to top becomes “Top”, and at the stop the button is full-width at 48 with a 32 px gap above it rather than 48 ⚑. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”); moreLabel (default “Load older posts” here, against 6 Load More's “Load more posts” — the same field with a different default, because at the stop the reader has already been given four pages ⚑); endLabel (default “That's all 132 posts.”); newerLabel and olderLabel read by the hidden numbered links only. Read-only: the six pagination values. Nothing in the union is unread by this design — it is the only one that spends all six authored fields, four of them invisibly. 6 · Controls. Trigger One screen early · At the last row. Status Pinned pill · Inline line · Off. Stop after a stepper, 2–8 pages, default 4 ⚑ — a count is a number rather than a row of fixed buttons, and Never was deleted with the row. Back to top Off · In the pill — disabled with its reason at Status Off. Four, and all four block fields inert ⚑. Cut: a threshold slider, a resume toggle.
7 · Data. First page server-rendered; the sentinel fetches /page/N/ against the route's own query and appends. Designed for archives of 48 posts and up — below four pages the stop never fires and the design is 6 Load More with extra machinery, which the panel says. 0 posts → the feed draws its own empty state; no pill, no sentinel and no fetch. 1 page → nothing renders, not even the pill: a status pill over a complete feed is furniture reporting itself. 2 pages → one automatic load and then the end; the pill shows “24 of 19”-style arithmetic never occurs because the count is clamped to the total. Many → four automatic pages, then the button, then Ghost's own pages behind it. 8 · Empty state. An empty feed → the feed draws its own designed “nothing here yet” state and the pager renders nothing at all ⚑ — no pill, no sentinel, no fetch and no revealed button. A pager under a feed with nothing in it offers a route through nothing, which is the one-page rule arriving by the same reasoning. The copy belongs to the feed, not to A34, so the category gains no thirteenth catalog string; in the editor the pager's outline is greyed with its own cause — “This feed has no posts, so there is nothing to page through.” — a different sentence from the one-page notice because it has a different fix. One page → nothing at all. Archive exhausted → the pill unpins and is replaced in the flow by endLabel, so the reader gets an ending rather than a pill that stops changing. A failed fetch shows the stop's button with “Those posts didn't load. Try again.”, as 6 Load More does. In the editor the module does not run at all: the resting frame is the feed with the pill drawn at its server-known count, and the sentinel is inert — which is why the primary frame is the resting state and not a mid-load one. 9 · Behaviour module. infinite-scroll. No-JS degradation, quoted from the registry: “Same — numbered pagination links render (FR-G4, explicitly).” Those links are in the markup on every render, visually hidden, exactly as in 6 Load More; the pill, the sentinel and the revealed button are all absent in that branch. Edit-safe: the module does not run while editing. Reduced motion: no smooth scroll on Back to top, no fade on the pill, static loading dots. A34 declares two modules and A17 and A18 refused this one — the difference is that those sections own a Show value and this one owns no query ⚑. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> holding the hidden <ol> of page links, a real <button> visually hidden until focused — the skip-link pattern, so a keyboard reader tabbing past the last post finds a control ⚑ — and the pill. A visually-hidden aria-live="polite" region announces each load and the stop: “12 more posts loaded. Showing 24 of 132.” and “36 of 132. Four pages loaded — use the button to continue.” Focus never moves on an automatic load — A18·15's rule departed from deliberately, because nothing was pressed — and does move to the first new post after the button is pressed, where the rule applies. The pill is not the live region and is aria-hidden, since the region says the same thing; Back to top inside it is a real focusable link with a 32 px inner target on a 44 px pill. aria-busy on the feed while fetching. Print: nothing prints — the only A34 design with no printed form at all, since it has no position to state. Flagged ⚑ — infinite-scroll declared where A17 and A18 refused it, and why; the four-page stop and its hand-off; the focus-revealed button; no focus move on auto-load; the standing three-line warning; all four block fields inert; the pill's two-step dark lift and its derived #2C2721 / #3D372F outside the seven roles; the safe-area inset; the 32 px gap at the stop; moreLabel carrying a different default from 6 Load More's.
