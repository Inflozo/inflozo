MEMBER ASK · PART A · A9 · ACTION · BUTTON OR TEXT LINK It does not render when the connected site cannot honour it — signup closed, members disabled, or no payment provider for a paid ask. The band closes up: no reserved gap, no empty slot. It opens Ghost’s Portal, so with JavaScript off nothing happens. Nothing in this bar is subscribable without script. It renders when members are on and signup is open; a paid ask needs a payment provider connected and a live paid tier as well; a sign-in link needs members on and nothing more. Withdrawn, headline and body hold the band, the close keeps the top-right corner, and the 900 px re-arrangement is unaffected — it is a media query, not a consequence of the action.
