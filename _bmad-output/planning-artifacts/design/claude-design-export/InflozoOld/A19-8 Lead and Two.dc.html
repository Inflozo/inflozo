Lead and Two

One spotlight on 848 with its picture at the leading edge, and two text-only followers stacked in a 424 px column beside it. This is A19's answer to “one or three”: three posts, one of them clearly the piece, and the hierarchy carried entirely by size.

The followers have no pictures at any setting, and that is the structural claim — a small photograph beside a large one asks the reader to compare them, and the section exists to say one of these is bigger than the others. Count sets the number and there is no Add anywhere: at Count 3 the section draws a lead and two, at Count 2 a lead and one, at 1 the lead alone.
