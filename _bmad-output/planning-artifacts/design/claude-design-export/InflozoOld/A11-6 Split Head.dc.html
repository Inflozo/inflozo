Split Head

Head, sub, note and link in a 416 column at one side; the wall in the 824 at the other. A8·6's split and A10·6's division, carried forward verbatim — 416 · 56 · 824 — holding artwork instead of quotes or numbers.

It is one of the two designs in A11 that offer Title size Large 40, because a title with a column of its own can carry one, and the only design where the head has somewhere to put a long sub without pushing the wall down the page. With no title authored the head column draws empty and the section takes no accessible name: the design is a head beside a wall, and it still renders as one.

ACCESSIBILITY · TWO COLUMNS, ONE READING ORDER Head column changes DOM order, and no order property is used anywhere in this design. At Right the twelve names are announced before the title, which is the reading a sighted reader gets too. Below 1080 the head is always first at both values, so the phone and the screen reader agree. The note and the link move in the DOM at 1080 as well as on the frame — out of the head column and under the list. That is a real reorder rather than a repositioning, and it is stated because it changes what follows the heading: above 1080 the sequence is title, sub, note, link, wall; below it, title, sub, wall, note, link. The two columns are a flex row, not a grid with named areas, and neither is a landmark: one <section aria-labelledby>, one <h2>, one <ul>. With no title authored the head column draws whatever head fields exist — and nothing at all when none is, and the 824 block does not move. The section then takes no accessible name, which is the same outcome as Head None elsewhere in A11. Tab stops: one per linked logo, plus the head column's link. At Head column Right the logos are reached before the link, which is the drawn order. Focus is A6's ring on the logo's box and on the link's text. Contrast: title 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark, sub and note 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, link underline 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. A logo takes no ratio, and at Muted the plate takes the treatment's opacity with the rest of the wall. At 400% the head is above the wall and the wall is two across. Reduced motion changes nothing. Print keeps the two columns at 1440 and stacks them at narrower page widths, with the foot inside the head block wherever the head is above the wall.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. alt is edited in the item row rather than on canvas, being the one required string this category never draws. Nothing here is Ghost-owned, so nothing answers “Edit in Ghost” — every word is authored in this section and every mark is a site upload. The head column’s four fields edit in place at either side, and the note and link stay editable in the head’s foot at every width.
