Counter

Where you are, set at 52 px in the pack's heading font, with a step in each direction beside it. Every other design in A34 puts position in a 13 px line under something else; this one makes it the largest thing in the section and lets the two arrows be furniture.

It is the answer to settlement 1 taken to its end: if numbered pages are the honest ones, this is the honest one drawn at full volume. The trade is the same as 2 Prev and Next's — a reader moves one page at a time — and it buys a page ending that reads as deliberate rather than administrative.

Three tones in one numeral group, and they are the whole design. The current page is text, the total is text-muted, the slash is border ⚑ — the same three-step read A19·13 used for its quote mark, and the reason the pair reads as “two of eleven” rather than as a fraction. The slash is aria-hidden and the accessible name is spoken, not punctuated.
The arrows are A1·14's icon button at 38 px, outlined, unchanged, and they sit 32 from the numerals — far enough that they are not part of the display moment and close enough to belong to it. There is no Arrows Off value ⚑: a pager a reader cannot page with is a caption, and the control would let a user build one by accident.
The hairline is above and the top gap is 40 rather than 24 — the only design in A34 that changes it. A 52 px numeral 24 px under a rule is a heading with a line over it; at 40 it is an object in its own space. Padded numerals are gone, and the line beneath the counter with them. A theme cannot pad a number to two digits and it cannot work out which posts page 2 holds, so “02 / 11” and “Posts 13–24 of 132” both leave in this pass. What is left is the fact the design was always about — this page, of that many — and the tabular numerals hold the group's width as the archive walks past 9 without any padding.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A design may mark a block field inert, and Position line is inert here. ⚑ The field is drawn greyed at Off with its reason, rather than removed, because it exists in all ten designs and a user switching designs needs to see where their setting went. Two of its three values are gone in this pass: Post range everywhere in the category, because a range of posts needs a page number multiplied by a page size and a template cannot multiply; and Page count here, because the counter already is the page count, at 52 px. So the design states its position once and says nothing twice, and the frames lose the 14 px line they carried under the numerals. Arrows has no Off value and that is a deliberate hole in a closed set. Every other A34 control that could remove a link offers the removal, because in those designs something else still navigates. Here nothing else does. A control that lets a user build a broken section is a defect, not a freedom — the same reasoning A17·16 used when it refused an auto-load value. Counter size Large and Arrows Outside is the combination the panel warns about. A 72 px numeral with two 38 px boxes 32 px away reads as a headline with two stray glyphs; the panel suggests Arrows Below and does not enforce it, which is A34's standard posture — recommend, name the alternative, never switch for the user. Five controls. Cut: a “Show total posts” toggle, which is the block's Position line, and an accent option for the counter — the display moment is a size, and colouring it would make the pager the loudest thing on the page.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The current page and the total set as one 52 px group in the pack's heading font, three tones deep, with a 38 px icon button either side and nothing beneath it. A34's only display moment and its only design whose subject is position rather than navigation. 2 · Structural descriptor. stack · none · page · few · none · position at display size
The category's only stack. It shares ground, containment, count and media with 2 Prev and Next and separates on archetype, which is the true difference: 2 is a row of two links with a caption between them, this is a centred column whose largest element is not a link at all. 3 · Archetype. stack. No departure — it narrows and re-sizes, and nothing reorders at any width. The one width-driven change is that Arrows Outside resolves to Arrows Below below 767, which is a value resolving, not a re-order. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 counter 52, arrows 38 at a 32 gap either side, hairline above with 40 beneath it, position line 14 under the counter group, 96 below. 1080 counter 48. 834 counter 44, arrow gap 24, page margin 40, padding 80. ≤ 767 Arrows Outside resolves to Below ⚑, the pair becomes two half-measure 44 px buttons with a 12 gap, counter 34 — or 26 at Numerals Worded ⚑ — position line directly under the counter, padding 64. Counter size Large at ≤ 767 resolves to 44, not 72: 72 px on a 350 measure is four glyphs and no room. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”). newerLabel and olderLabel authored, never drawn — Labels is locked to Glyphs only, and both become the arrows' visually-hidden text. Read-only: the six pagination values; .page and .pages are the composition. Also read: the site title, in print only, beside the range ⚑ — the only A34 design that reads anything outside the pagination object. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. 6 · Controls. Counter size Small 34 · Medium 52 · Large 72. Numerals Plain · Worded — Padded deleted in the patch pass, a template being unable to pad a number. Arrows Outside the counter · Below the counter — no Off ⚑. Arrow style Bare · Outlined · Filled, A1·14's three unchanged, Filled in contrast and never accent. Rule None · Hairline above. Five, then the block's four fields and its two doors — Labels locked, Position line fixed Off, At the ends defaulting to Dimmed.
7 · Data. pagination.page and .pages for the counter, .prev and .next for the arrows, .limit and .total for the range. 0 posts → the feed draws its own empty state and no counter renders. 1 page → does not render — and this is the design where that rule bites hardest, because “1 / 1” at 52 px is a striking way to say nothing. 2 pages → “01 / 02”, which needs no adjustment and is the design's second-best case. Many → the numerals grow by a glyph at 100 pages and the group re-centres; the tabular figures hold the group's width, which is what the padding was for. 8 · Empty state. An empty feed → the feed draws its own designed “nothing here yet” state and the pager renders nothing at all ⚑ — no counter, no arrows and no rule — the display moment has no position to state. A pager under a feed with nothing in it offers a route through nothing, which is the one-page rule arriving by the same reasoning. The copy belongs to the feed, not to A34, so the category gains no thirteenth catalog string; in the editor the pager's outline is greyed with its own cause — “This feed has no posts, so there is nothing to page through.” — a different sentence from the one-page notice because it has a different fix. One page → nothing renders on the site; the editor draws the counter greyed at “1 / 1” with the cause beneath it, which is the clearest editor-only state in the category because the reason is legible in the design itself. At the ends Dimmed is the default: the spent arrow keeps its box and both its glyph and its border go to the muted-border step — the only place A34 dims two tokens at once ⚑, because an outlined box with a dim glyph inside a live-looking border reads as broken rather than spent. At Hidden the counter shifts 70 px off centre and the panel says so. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. count-up was the obvious module and is refused ⚑: it is in the registry and its no-JS branch is exactly what this design already renders, but a page number that counts up from zero on arrival is a number pretending to be an achievement. Named here so the refusal is recorded rather than looking like an oversight. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination">, no heading, no list — two links. The counter is a <p> whose text reads “Page 2 of 11” to a screen reader whatever Numerals is set to ⚑: the visible “2 / 11” is composed of spans with the slash aria-hidden and a visually-hidden “of” between them, so nobody hears “zero two slash eleven”. Arrows are <a rel="prev"> / rel="next" with visually-hidden names, 38 px boxes on 44 px targets. Focus is A6's ring at 4 px on the box; hover is A17's 2 px accent underline on the glyph, since there is no label to underline. Current page 13.6:1 light and 14.2:1 dark. Print: the counter prints, worded, at 22 pt with the range and the site title beneath — the one A34 design whose printed form is itself. Flagged ⚑ — the three-tone numeral group with the slash in border; 40 below the rule rather than 24; Arrows with no Off value; Filled in contrast; Worded's pack-dependent width and its own type step at 390; Large resolving to 44 below 767; Position line narrowed to one value; two tokens dimmed at once; the spoken counter differing from the drawn one; the site title read in print; count-up refused.
