Index

Four to eight steps as hairline rows with the numeral, the label, the title and the body in aligned columns. The design four of the other fourteen name when a site adds a seventh step — A13's equivalent of A12's 1 Grid, and the answer A13-0's settlement 1 gives to “what happens at eight”.

The archetype names the shape rather than the markup: rows share a baseline grid so the four columns line up down the section, and a row is four aligned fields, which is the table archetype's geometry. It is still an <ol> and not a <table> — there are no column headers and nothing in the body column relates to anything but its own row.

The Remove button never greys outRemove is visible and clickable at every count and is never dimmed and never hidden. At two steps clicking it says a process needs at least two steps and the row stays, so the one-step state is not reachable from the button. The rows close up and everything after re-numbers. Nothing is dimmed at four, at three or at two.
Member buttons are conditionalA13 draws no button anywhere. Its one destination is the section link, and where that link points at a member action it now carries the ask: it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens. Scope-tagged apart from Member visibility, which hides the section server-side.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject for the notice — this design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for a notice to replace, and no line on this frame ever promised a subscription without script.
