DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Item counts are a number pickerThe Tags block’s Show is a number picker, 1–14, where it drew Four · Six · Eight · Twelve · All. Ghost returns at most 100 tags: “All” tripped Ghost’s own warning and silently returned 100 anyway, so the cap and the truncation are stated in the panel instead of promised away. This design’s own maximum is 14 — display type past fourteen lines is several screens of scrolling — drawn greyed with the reason visible.
The Remove button never greys outThe hand-picked list’s ✕ stays visible and clickable at one reference and explains why it cannot go lower — “a hand-picked list needs at least one topic”. Never dimmed, never hidden.
No design ever turns into another designNo subject in this design. It draws itself at every count — one topic is drawn as one topic — and nothing in it switches to a different design. The panel may advise at a count; advice is never a switch.
Member buttons · the no-JavaScript noticeNo subject. This design carries no subscribe form, no sign-in form and no paid-tier button — the all-topics link is navigation, not a call to action — so there is nothing to make conditional and nothing for a notice to replace.
NumberingUnchanged. This category is 1–15 and this design is still 8 Big Type. No gap was created or closed, no number reused.
OWNER'S RULING · 29 AUGUST 2026
The all-topics link gains the same on/off switch the other designs have, On by default. It was the one open question left on this design by the reconciliation pass — the link was drawn with no control — and the owner closed it on 29 August 2026. Nothing drawn changes: On is the default, the label and URL are the same fields, and the link still drops at 390. This design now owns five controls.
