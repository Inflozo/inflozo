A8 TESTIMONIALS · DESIGN 13 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · HOW FAR ONE VOICE MAY LEAD

Highlight

One quotation at Display 40 in an 848 px card, with two more at Small 17 stacked in 416 px cards beside it. Everything is 2 Three Up's card at two different scales, and the only question this design has to answer is a ratio: how much larger the lead can be before the other two stop being read at all.

The answer here is two steps of the ladder and no more — Display 40 against Small 17, or Feature 27 against Small 17 for a site that wants the three closer together. A third step was drawn and refused: at Display 40 against a hypothetical 13 px, the small cards read as a caption to the big one, and a section that quietly demotes two of its three readers is worse than a section with one.

REFUSED · a third step, and the muted colour that usually comes with it · not offered at any value

The refused frame is the design this category could easily have shipped: a big quotation with two small grey ones beside it, which every reader correctly reads as one testimonial and two decorations. The small quotations are never muted in colour at any value — they are text at 17 px, the same colour as the lead, and the size is the only difference between them. A8-0's ladder has a step below Small for exactly nothing, which is why 17 is the floor here as everywhere.
Lead size Feature is the value for a site whose best quotation is long: at Display 40 a 155-character quotation fills the 848 card and leaves the pair looking crowded, and the sidebar's help line says “Display suits a quote under 90 characters”, the same advice 10 Big Quote gives. ⚑ The refusal, the never-muted rule and reusing the 90-character advice are mine.

DARK · desktop 1440 · padding Comfortable 96, head None, lead Right, lead size Display 40, cards Surface

Drawn at head None and lead Right, which is the setting where the section is three cards and nothing else — no title, no eyebrow, no foot — and therefore the setting that shows the ratio doing all the work. In dark all three cards lift to surface #211D17: the lead does not get a further step up. A card that was lighter as well as larger would be two signals for one hierarchy, and the plane step is already carrying the difference between card and page. Quote and name 15.1:1, role 6.4:1. The 40 px quotation on a dark card is the largest type in dark mode anywhere in A8, and it takes the same weight and tracking as in light.
