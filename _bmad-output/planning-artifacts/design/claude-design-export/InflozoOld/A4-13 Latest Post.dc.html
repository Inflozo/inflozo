The post is members-only. A third item joins the meta row at 13 px in text rather than muted — no lock glyph, no badge, no accent. This is a stated departure from A3·9, which shows members-only posts in the footer with no marker at all because “the footer is not the place to sell”. The hero is the place a reader arrives, and sending them from the front door into a paywall unannounced is worse than telling them. Flagged as mine, and flagged as a departure.
Which posts. Latest published · Latest in a tag · Featured only. Drafts, scheduled posts and pages are excluded, as A3·9. A tag or a featured set with nothing in it falls through to the no-post rule above rather than to the latest post — a site that asked for one tag and got another one's post has been lied to. Routes. Offered on home and custom routes only. A latest-post card on an About page is a section that has wandered in from the home page, and the picker says so rather than hiding the design.

1 · The authored headline is the page's <h1>, not the post's title. That is the structural expression of the A19 boundary: the page is about what the publication is, and the post is a link inside it. 2 · The post's title is an <h2> inside the card's link, so the page's outline reads “the hero's headline, then one post”. The card is not a <nav> and not a feed — it is one piece of content, and A3·9 settled that marking content as navigation makes the landmark list useless. 3 · The post's picture is decorative — alt="" — because the title beneath it is the link's name and Ghost's own alt text for a feature image is usually empty anyway. Explicit dimensions, loading="eager" and high priority, as every hero picture in A4. 4 · Tag is plain text, never a nested link inside the card's link. Date is <time datetime="2026-08-18"> with its long form visible. The members-only marker, where it appears, is text in the same row and is read as part of the meta rather than as a status. Live data · the card changes on its own every time the site publishes, so nothing in the design may depend on the length of what is in it: two-line title cap with the full title in the DOM, a fixed 3:2 box, and a meta row that never wraps to a third line. The card's height is set by its own geometry rather than by its content. Contrast · authored headline 12.1:1 / 11.8:1 · post title 12.1:1 / 11.8:1 · meta 5.4:1 / 6.4:1 · members-only marker 12.1:1 · focus ring on the card 4.85:1, above the 3:1 non-text floor. Hover motion is a colour change only, so reduced motion removes nothing but the darkening.
