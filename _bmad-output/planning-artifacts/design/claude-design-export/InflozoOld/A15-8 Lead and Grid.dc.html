8 · Lead and Grid

The first film at the full content box with its title beside its meta line, and the rest of the set in a row of three beneath it. One film is the point; the others are what else there is.

It is the section for a new release with a back catalogue under it — this week’s film, then the season — and the only design in A15 where two sizes of frame sit in one arrangement.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND WHY
There is nowhere to upload to, so the Upload source is deleted. Ghost’s public interface has no media library a theme can read, Inflozo is not permitted to put files on a customer’s Ghost, and Inflozo’s own asset system handles images only. Every film in this design is a pasted URL ⚑ — a provider embed, or a media file already hosted on the customer’s own Ghost, which stays first-party and keeps its native <video controls playsinline preload="none" poster>. The ambient loop goes with it. The Playback row — Click to play · Ambient loop — is deleted from the panel: the loop had no source of files. Nothing autoplays anywhere in A15 again, and the reduced-motion registry addition the loop needed is withdrawn — the category is back to four findings. Nothing reads a duration off a file. duration ≤ 8 is typed by the author again, seeded from the provider on paste where the provider supplies one. No poster, no title and no duration each close up exactly as drawn. Embeds are unaffected. YouTube, Vimeo and the audio providers keep this design, its facade, its click-to-load notice and their refusal to autoplay. No layout, measure, type size, colour or spacing changed on this frame ⚑, and the design’s number is unchanged. This design’s own delta. Gap is renamed Tight · Normal · Loose at the same 8 · 24 · 40 px; the values did not move.

THE COUNTS THIS DESIGN IS WRITTEN FOR ⚑
Designed for 4–10 ⚑ — a lead and one to three rows of three. At 1 the lead is drawn alone and no empty row is reserved ⚑; the section is then 1 Player at Width: Wide, and the panel says so.At 2 and 3 the row is short and left-aligned ⚑ — never centred, never stretched to fill the box.Above 10 the rows continue in threes ⚑ and the lead is never repeated. There is no “load more” here — load-more is A17 and A18’s module and the list is authored, not paged.

The lead and the row share every state ⚑ — one component at two sizes, and the notice, the plate and the loaded player look the same in both. Playing a film in the row does not promote it to the lead ⚑: the arrangement is the author’s order, not the reader’s history. The box is reserved before anything loads ⚑ — every frame is an aspect-ratio box and the poster, the notice and the player all sit inside it. Nothing moves when a film is played. Settlement 2.

The Playback row is deleted in this pass ⚑ — Click to play · Ambient loop went with the upload source, because the ambient loop had no source of files. An embed is never asked to autoplay, nothing autoplays anywhere in A15, and the registry addition that would have gated a muted loop on prefers-reduced-motion is withdrawn with it.Editor-side seeding, new in this pass ⚑: pasting a recognised URL fetches the oEmbed title once, in the editor — and the duration where the provider supplies one, and seeds both fields; “Use provider thumbnail” downloads the image and re-hosts it as the poster ⚑. The reader’s browser still never touches the provider — settlement 3 protects the reader, not the editor. Nothing reads a duration off a file ⚑: that reading is deleted with the upload branch, and where the provider supplies no duration the author types it, which is what the field was always for. Both fields stay editable and an author who types over them wins.Recognised providers, named ⚑ — video: YouTube · Vimeo. Audio, for 12 Embed Card: Soundcloud · Spotify · Apple Podcasts · Bandcamp · Mixcloud. An unrecognised URL takes 12 Embed Card’s link-out fallback everywhere ⚑: the frame is drawn, the label reads the domain, and the frame is a link rather than an iframe — nothing goes in an iframe the theme cannot name.Nothing is read from Ghost’s content API ⚑ — no Show ladder and no From posts branch: the count is the length of the authored list. Member Visibility is not offered in A15 ⚑ — no design carries an auth or subscribe action, and the two action rows are play triggers for content already on the page; a members-only film is A32 Paywall’s ground.
