Slim

Three or four rows in a tight band, no head, its own 32 · 44 · 56 padding scale — A7·8's, reused — and a 300-character ceiling on the answer that no other design imposes. The design for the four questions that follow a pricing table or a sign-up form, where a full FAQ section would be a second page.

It draws three fields and keeps ten. No eyebrow, no title, no sub, no note, no link — the page above it has already said what this is — which makes it the design that loses nothing when a site switches away and the design that says least about itself. It is also the only design in A9 that refuses lists and code inside an answer, and it says so at the field rather than dropping the markup silently.

ACCESSIBILITY · A SECTION WITH NO NAME No <h2> and no aria-labelledby — the design draws no title at any value, so the section takes no accessible name rather than being labelled by invented text. It is the only design in A9 with no <h2>, and the questions are still <h3>: a heading level is skipped, which is a lint warning and the honest structure, because the level above it belongs to the page's own section rather than to this band. Otherwise it is 1 Accordion exactly: a <ul>, <h3> + full-width <summary>, the answer the <details>’s own content — no role="region" and no hidden attribute, one tab stop per row, Enter and Space, no arrow keys, 160 ms on the height and the marker, instant under reduced motion. The panel at Ground Surface is a <div>, not a landmark or a group — no role, no label. It is a plane, and a plane with a role is a region a reader has to leave. 44 px targets at the tightest padding in the category: 13 + 24 + 13 = 50 on a phone, 14 + 24 + 14 = 52 above it. The row's height was checked against the floor before the padding was chosen rather than after. Contrast: question and answer 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark on background, 15.4:1 / 14.7:1 inside the panel, marker 5.6:1 / 6.2:1 resting, ring 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. No accent pixel unless an answer contains a link, and the answers here contain none. Print: every row open, panel kept, no marker.
