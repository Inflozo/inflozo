Grouped

Group labels with their own rows beneath, stacked down one column. The design a site reaches for when twelve questions are three subjects rather than one list, and the first of three designs that read groups[] — the other two, 7 Index and 11 Tabs, do something else with the same field.

Two things had to be settled to draw it. The group label takes the eyebrow's type, so this design keeps the section eyebrow and never draws it — two ranks of 13 px uppercase in one section is one of them competing. And the questions become <h4> under the label's <h3>, which contradicts A9-0's “every question is an <h3>” and is logged as an amendment rather than quietly applied.

The group label is 13 px, 600, uppercase, tracked .08em, in text — the eyebrow's type at the eyebrow's size, one weight up and in full-strength colour, which is what separates a label with rows under it from a kicker over a title. Label to first row 20 px; group to group 48 px, the same 48 the head takes above the list, because a new subject is the same size of break as the start of the list. Every group's rows keep the hairline on each row and none under the last, so the 48 px gap is the only thing dividing two groups.
The section eyebrow is kept and never drawn here. A site switching from 1 Accordion keeps “Questions” in the field and does not see it, and the panel says so at the head control. ⚑ Mine: the label's type, both gaps, and suppressing the eyebrow rather than restyling the label.

Beside the rows puts the label in a 200 px column at the left, on A9·5's 56 px gutter, with the rows still 780 wide — 200 + 56 + 780 = 1,036, centred in the 1,296 width. The row's width is identical at both values, which is what makes the control a change of label position rather than a change of the list. The label is top-aligned with its first row's text, not with the row's hairline: 10 px of the row's own padding sits above it, so the label reads as belonging to the row rather than sitting on the rule. At ≤ 767 Beside draws Above and the control says “Above at this width”. A 200 px label column on a 350 px screen leaves 90 px for a question. A group of one is drawn as a group of one. It is not merged into its neighbour and its label is not dropped: the site wrote the label, and a lone question under it is the site's structure rather than an error. ⚑ Mine.

TWO UNGROUPED QUESTIONS AT THE FOOT · NO LABEL INVENTED FOR THEM
Questions with no group are drawn as a last set with no label, after every labelled group, in authored order. No label is invented for them — not “Other”, not “More”, not “General”. The theme does not name a group the site declined to name, and the 48 px gap above the set is the only thing that says it is separate. A group with no questions is not drawn at all, label included: an empty subject is a heading with nothing under it. A deep link into a group opens its row and nothing else. Every group is drawn at all times in this design, so there is no container to reveal — the difference from 11 Tabs, where the tab has to be selected first, and the reason this design is the plainest place to send somebody a link. With no groups authored the labels are simply not drawn and every question falls into one unlabelled set — the design renders, and no label is invented for it. The panel advises 1 Accordion, which is that same list without the grouping machinery.

ACCESSIBILITY · AND THE HEADING LEVEL THIS DESIGN CHANGED The group label is an <h3> and the questions under it are <h4>, each inside a full-width <summary>. A9-0 said every question in the category is an <h3>; this design and 7 Index make it <h4>, because a label with rows under it is a level and skipping one to keep the questions at <h3> would put a heading inside nothing. 11 Tabs reads the same field and keeps <h3>, because there its tab is the group label and no heading is drawn inside the panel. Logged as an amendment in A9-0's consistency pass. One <ul> per group, not one for the section. Three groups is three lists, each announced with its own count, which is the true shape: the rows in a group are neighbours and the rows across a gap are not. The ungrouped set is a fourth list with no heading above it. The rotor reads three labels and eight questions in authored order, which is the argument for the label being a heading at all rather than a styled <p>: a reader can jump between subjects without opening anything. Tab order is the rows only — labels are not focusable, not links and not buttons; a group cannot be collapsed as a whole, because that is an accordion inside an accordion and A9-0 refuses it. Contrast: group label 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark, question and answer the same, marker 5.6:1 / 6.2:1 resting, link underline 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. Print: every row open, labels kept, group gaps kept.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Questions and answers edit inline on canvas, selected where they are drawn rather than only in the repeater. The answer raises the marks toolbar on selection with bold · italic · link · inline code and no underline — an underline inside prose is a link that is not one. Recorded as a PRD delta. Group labels edit inline too — they live in a second repeater, so renaming one on canvas renames the group for every question in it, and the confirm says so. Nothing in A9 is Ghost-owned, so nothing here says “Edit in Ghost” — every word on the page is the site’s own. The link opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker and takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Questions are the P0·3 item list: Add question arrives with content, Remove is never disabled, drag to reorder, per-item content only.
