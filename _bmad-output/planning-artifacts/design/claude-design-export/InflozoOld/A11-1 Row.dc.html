Row

Three to six logos in one row of equal cells across the 1,296 content width, each contained in a fixed-height box, all of them on one centre line. The design a site gets when it types “logos” into the picker, the one A11-0's logo component was drawn for, and the design eleven others name as advice.

All four of §8's settlements are visible on the primary frame at once: the per-row count, the three aspect classes normalising three shapes to one weight, the greyscale default, and a wall where two of the five logos are links and three are not. Everything else in A11 is a rearrangement of this row, and every rule it establishes is cited by the other fourteen rather than restated.

Two logos of the five are links on the primary frame and three are not, which is the ordinary case. A logo with a url is an <a> around the <img>; the other three are bare images with no cursor, no plane and no target. The resting state gives no sign of which is which — a stated cost, and the alternative is decorating nine logos to explain three. ⚑ Mine. The hover reveals the artwork rather than dressing the cell. At Greyscale and Muted there is something to resolve; at Full colour the hover surface is the whole of it. One rule, three treatments, and no design in A11 does it differently. Muted is the one enabled value in the library the theme cannot prove. §7.4 wants a failing value disabled with its ratio; a logo is an uploaded file and has no ratio the theme can read. The editor carries the advice — 3:1 against the ground, non-text — and the value stays available. ⚑ A11-0's settlement 3.

ACCESSIBILITY · A WALL OF NAMES A READER CANNOT SEE <section aria-labelledby> named by the title, an <h2>; the wall a <ul> of five <li>; each logo an <img> carrying its alt. A linked logo is an <a> around the image and takes its accessible name from the same alt, so “Meridian Press, link” is the whole announcement — no aria-label, no “opens in a new tab”, no visually hidden “visit”. Alt text is the content of this section, not a courtesy. Five images with alt="" is a section that announces a title and then nothing at all, so alt is required and cannot be saved empty, and it is the brand's name as the brand writes it — “Meridian Press”, not “Meridian Press logo”, because the word logo is what the image already is. ⚑ Flagged: the requirement, the 60-character limit and the refusal of the word “logo”. A dark file is delivered by <picture> with a prefers-color-scheme: dark source, one <img> and one alt underneath it, so the announcement is identical in both modes. In forced colours the plate survives as a system-bordered box and every logo keeps whatever contrast its file has — the theme adds nothing, because a filter on somebody's mark is a change to their mark. Tab stops: one per linked logo, plus the section link. Two and one on the primary frame; none at all with no URLs authored. The focus ring is on the cell box, not the artwork, so a 26 px Square logo and a 36 px wordmark take the same ring. No behaviour, no state, no live region, no roving index. Contrast: title 15.8:1 light / 15.1:1 dark, sub and note 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, link text 15.8:1 / 15.1:1 with the underline at 3.3:1 / 6.3:1, focus ring 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. A logo takes no ratio — it is an uploaded file, the theme cannot measure it, and the editor's advice is 3:1 non-text against the ground. Muted at 64% is where that advice matters and is never disabled. Reduced motion changes nothing, because nothing moves. The only transitions are the 160 ms hover on a linked cell and the link underline's thickening, both instant under reduced motion and neither changing what anything is. At 400% zoom the wall is two across and the note reflows; nothing overlaps and nothing scrolls sideways.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. alt is edited in the item row rather than on canvas, being the one required string this category never draws. Nothing here is Ghost-owned, so nothing answers “Edit in Ghost” — every word is authored in this section and every mark is a site upload.
