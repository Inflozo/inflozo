WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESReconciled. Padding retired into Vertical spacing, Divider renamed Bottom rule, and Eyebrow: Show · Hide arrives — the eyebrow defaulted by route, so clearing the field could never turn it off ⚑. The zero count string leaves the template and becomes the archive.count_empty catalog string. The Content group names all five authored strings, backUrl opens the Link Picker, and the Archive source group is recut as the Data group with its Image row drawn disabled. Six controls of its own.Four of the six controls are the category's, not this design's. Padding, Name size, Description and Post count recur in eleven of the fourteen. That repetition is deliberate: a user switching designs keeps the settings that still mean something.There is no Alignment control here. Centred is what the design is; a Left value would make it 2 Split Head without a right column, and two designs that differ by one control value are one design ⚑.The Archive source group is identical in all fourteen and is not counted toward the brief's four to seven. It is where the name, the description and the image come from, and what happens at zero posts — the four questions §8 asks, answered once for the category.
