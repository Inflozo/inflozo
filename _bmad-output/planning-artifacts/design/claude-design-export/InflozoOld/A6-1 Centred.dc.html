Centred

The ask centred on the page's own ground: eyebrow, title, sub, one action, a note. It is the floor the other fourteen depart from — it sets A6's title ladder, its action scale, the accent budget, the two things a secondary action is allowed to be, and the rule for a banner sitting immediately above the footer, which is where most of them will sit.

A6's title ladder is A5's head ladder, unchanged — Medium 34 · Large 40 · Display 48 — because a banner is a section head with an action under it, and a banner that outshouted the page's hero would be arguing with it. The one departure is 9 Big Type, which takes A4's Display 76 and says so. The action is A4's hero-scale button — accent fill, 15 px/600, padding 13×22, 46 px tall — one step above the mid-page button A5 uses, on the argument that this section exists for its action. The accent is spent exactly once per banner. The secondary is A1·1's ghost action at the primary's size, 24 px away, muted until hovered. Under it, the note at 13 px.
Above the footer: the banner keeps its own bottom padding and the footer supplies its top hairline, which is the edge between them. The theme drops the footer's top padding one step when the section above it is an A6 banner, so 96 + 96 does not become 192 px of nothing. Flagged as mine, and settled once in A6-0 for all fifteen.

The accent budget falls as the ground gets louder. On background and surface the accent is spent once, on the button. On contrast it is spent nothing: an accent fill carrying the band's colour is 3.2:1 in Paper, so the value is disabled with its ratio shown, and stays disabled in packs whose accent would pass — a control whose availability varied by pack would make one design twelve. A4·9 and A5·10 reached the same answer; this design carries it rather than re-deciding it. 5 Contrast Band is the design for a band that wants edges, inner padding and a lift; this control is for the site that wants one banner inverted and nothing else.

A secondary action is allowed to be two things and nothing else: a ghost action in muted text, or a text link with an arrow. It is never a second filled button and never an outlined twin of the primary — two buttons of equal weight is a page asking a reader to choose rather than to act. Settled in A6-0 for all fifteen. The primary's hover is a 6% darkening of the fill, the pressed state 10%; nothing scales, lifts or gains a shadow anywhere in A6, which is A5's rule carried. 160 ms ease-out, one transition per state change.

1 · The eyebrow is a <p> above the heading, not part of it and not a heading itself — A5's rule, carried. It is not aria-hidden either: “Subscribe” tells a reader what the section is for. 2 · <section aria-labelledby> named by the title, which is an <h2>. With no title the section is not a labelled region and the banner is the action plus its note — a banner with no title is allowed, and 10 Slim is the design for it. 3 · Actions are <a> elements, never buttons: they navigate. The visible label is the whole accessible name — no aria-label lengthening it, which means “Start reading” has to be written to stand alone, and the editor says so where a label reads “Click here” or “Learn more”. Two tab stops, in DOM order, primary first. 4 · The note is a <p> after the actions in the DOM, and not aria-describedby the primary: “No card needed” is a fact about the offer, not a description of the link. Where a site puts terms in it, that is a fact about the offer too. Contrast · title 12.1:1 light / 12.6:1 dark · sub and note 5.4:1 / 6.1:1 · button label on accent 4.6:1 / 8.1:1 · ghost action resting 5.4:1, hover 12.1:1 · focus ring 3.1:1 against background. On the band the ring is the carried colour rather than the accent, 12.8:1 — A5·10's rule. Forced colours keep both actions: the primary is a real link with a border, the ghost keeps its underline on hover, and nothing in the design depends on the fill.

MEMBER ASK AND NO JAVASCRIPT · PATCH PASS · 29 AUG 2026 The ask is conditional. Where the primary is a sign-up ask or a paid-tier ask it does not render when the connected site cannot support it — self-signup switched off in Ghost, or no payment provider connected — and the sidebar names the setting rather than shipping a button that fails. An ordinary link is unaffected. Ruled by the owner, 29 August 2026: the button keeps its place and its scale, and the site chooses what it says and where it goes — a fallback label and link (fallbackLabel ≤ 24 · fallbackUrl, in the panel beside the ask), so a reader meets a working button rather than a gap. Left empty, this design’s own rule applies: no primary label or URL, no section. Where the ask opens Ghost's own sign-up pop-up: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. The link is a real href to a Portal address, so the page stays where it is and no window opens. No JavaScript: this design draws no form, so there is nothing to replace — every word and both actions are rendered by the server, and the banner is the banner.
