Nothing about the plate’s size, shape, colour role or ratio changed, and lists a user types themselves keep two initials — A21 has none of those.
Item counts are a number pickerThe Data group’s How many is a number picker, 1–24, default 6 — it drew Three · Six · Twelve · All. “All” is retired: it promised every writer, and Ghost’s own get helper stops at 100 ⚑ 29 August 2026 — beyond 100 it returns nothing at all — so 100 is the ceiling this maximum sits under; reaching past it needs the content API and a script, which nothing in A21 asks for. This design caps at 24, with the reason drawn beside the greyed numbers. Still a count and never a chosen set: the writers are Ghost’s, and the row disables at Hand-picked where the list is the count.
The Remove button never greys outThe hand-picked list’s ✕ stays visible and clickable at one reference, and clicking it explains why it cannot go lower — “a hand-picked list needs at least one writer”. Never dimmed, never hidden. This is the category’s only repeating list; every other value in A21 is Ghost’s and has no Add or Remove at all.
No design ever turns into another designNo subject — this design draws itself at every count and nothing in it switches to another design. The panel advises (6 Founder at one writer and 10 Directory above two dozen) and advice is never a switch: the placed design is the design that renders. What does not apply is hidden rather than substituted.
Author social links go through Ghost’s own helperNew in this pass ⚑ 29 August 2026, and it changes the build rather than the drawing. The row is drawn with Ghost’s own social_accounts helper, passed the author — never by gluing a prefix onto the stored handle, which breaks because Ghost keeps handles in several shapes (bare handle, @handle, whole URL, a server for Mastodon and Bluesky). The helper hands over the finished address, the platform’s own label and a platform key per connected account and skips any platform with nothing set, so the absent-handle rule is Ghost’s behaviour rather than the theme’s. Nine platforms — X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram — and the website is not one of them: it is the author’s own website field, drawn beside the row. The label is the accessible name, and the icon is picked from the platform key with a fallback, so a platform Ghost adds before this theme has an icon still renders. The old “which networks have a link helper” question is closed by Ghost’s documentation.
A theme’s templates cannot countNo subject — nothing in this design adds, subtracts or remembers. Counts are read whole from the platform, and no line here is assembled from a sum.
Member buttons are conditional, and Ghost’s sign-up pop-up needs JavaScriptNo subject — this design carries no action at all: no subscribe button, no paid tier, no Portal link, and therefore no Member visibility row.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject — this design contains no subscribe form and no sign-in form, so there is nothing for the notice to replace and no “works without JavaScript” promise to withdraw. A21 bears no member form anywhere.
A design may offer fewer choices on a shared control, and must say whyRe-checked on this panel. The colour swatch row is Base; there is no “Inherit” value anywhere in A21; no shared control is renamed and no value is added to one. Nothing here offers fewer choices than the shared control allows.
Slider labels · gap namesNo subject — this design draws no slider and no gap control. Its named-value rows each say what they affect, and every three-value row is Compact · Comfortable · Spacious.
The two free designs are the owner’s choiceNot shortlisted — the free pair is drawn from the plainest designs that do not depend on the customer’s photography, and this one is not among them. Five were shortlisted (3 Rows, 7 Panel, 10 Directory, 11 Rail, 15 Slim), and the owner ruled 3 Rows and 11 Rail on 29 August 2026.
