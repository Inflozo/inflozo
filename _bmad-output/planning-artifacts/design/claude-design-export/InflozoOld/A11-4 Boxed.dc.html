ACCESSIBILITY · A PANEL THAT IS NOT A REGION The panel is a presentational <div> around the <ul>: no role, no aria-label, no tabindex, not a <figure> and not a nested <section>. It draws a boundary for the eye and announces nothing, so the section is one region named by its <h2> with a flat list inside it — the same announcement as 1 Row and 3 Grid, which is what makes switching between the three safe. The head is outside the panel in the DOM as well as on the frame, so the heading precedes the list and nothing is nested inside a container the heading does not describe. Focus rings live inside the panel and never cross its edge. A linked logo's ring is 2 px outside its box, and the panel's padding is 32 at its tightest, so the ring has 28 px of clearance at the worst value. That clearance is why panel padding Compact is 32 and not 24. ⚑ Mine. Contrast: panel on ground 1.1:1 light / 1.6:1 dark, hairline 1.4:1 / 1.6:1, both decorative and neither carrying meaning — the panel is a boundary, not a state. Title 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, eyebrow and note 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, link underline 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. A logo takes no ratio. In forced colours the panel keeps a system border and loses its fill, which is the correct outcome: the boundary survives and the two-surface hierarchy does not, because it was never carrying information. Reduced motion changes nothing. At 400% the panel is two across with 20 px of padding and nothing scrolls sideways.

EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. alt is edited in the item row rather than on canvas, being the one required string this category never draws. Nothing here is Ghost-owned, so nothing answers “Edit in Ghost” — every word is authored in this section and every mark is a site upload.
