Cards

One logo per card, every card the same height. This is the design that answers §8.4's harder half: in the other fourteen a linked logo’s target is the artwork’s own box — about 4,800 px² for a wordmark, and less for a Square mark — and here the target is the card, 306 × 112 on a desktop and 135 × 88 on a phone. It clarifies the target, not the link: a linked card and an unlinked one still look identical at rest.

It is A11·1 Row's division with a container per logo instead of a container round all of them, and the one design in A11 where the whole cell is the <a> rather than a plate behind the artwork.

1 Row's divisions — 416, 306, 240, 196 on a 24 px gutter — with a card at each and a 24 px row gap rather than 40. The rows are closer here than anywhere else in A11 because the cards have edges: 40 px between two hairlines reads as a gap in a table, and 24 reads as a grid of cards. Card height is a control, not a computation — Compact 88 · Comfortable 112 · Spacious 136 — because one logo per card means there is nothing to equalise against. A10·2 equalised to its tallest card; this design does not need to.
The logo box is Medium 36 inside the card and Small 28 at Count Five and Six, and there is no Logo size control: a card is a fixed frame and the artwork's size inside it is the design's, not the site's. Card: surface, 1 px hairline, pack radius, no shadow at any value. ⚑ Mine: the 24 px row gap, the card height scale, and dropping the Logo size control.

ACCESSIBILITY · THE ONE DESIGN WHERE THE TARGET IS THE POINT A linked card is <li><a><img alt></a></li>, and the card's box is the <a> — not a <div> with a click handler, not a nested link, not an ::after overlay. The accessible name is the alt and nothing else: no “read more”, no “opens in a new tab”, no title attribute, so the announcement is “Meridian Press, link”. An unlinked card is not focusable and takes no tabindex, so a wall of eight cards where three are links has three tab stops and not eight. That is the correct behaviour and it is also why the resting states are identical — the keyboard order tells a keyboard user which cards are links, and nothing tells a sighted user until they hover. Stated as a cost in the panel. The card is 306 × 112 at its ordinary size and 135 × 88 at its smallest, both past the 44 px minimum in both axes at every value of Card height and Count. Compact 88 exists as a floor rather than a value the design would ever go under. Focus is A6's ring on the card box, 2 px accent 2 px outside, and the 24 px row gap means a ring never touches the card below. In forced colours the card keeps a system border at both Cards values and the hover fill is a system highlight. Contrast: card on ground 1.1:1 light / 1.6:1 dark, hairline 1.4:1 / 1.6:1, hover fill 1.2:1 / 1.4:1 — all decorative, and none of them carrying the link's meaning, which the <a> carries. Title 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, eyebrow and note 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, focus ring 3.3:1 / 6.3:1. A logo takes no ratio. Reduced motion removes the 160 ms fill transition and keeps the fill. At 400% the wall is two across, the cards keep their height, and nothing scrolls sideways.

LOGOS · the item list · outside the design’s listLogos is the P0·3 item list. Add logo arrives with content — the editor’s placeholder tile, a 3:1 wordmark reading “Add a logo” at this design’s box height, alt seeded “Publication name”, the file picker open; an item still holding the tile is not published and the counter reads “11 of 12 placed”. Remove is never disabled; drag to reorder; per-item content only. Per item: logo req · logoDark opt · alt req ≤ 60 · url opt. Image focus is P0·9’s shared control — both axes, and the values are not restated here; it sits in the Image Picker’s popover on both image fields and is disabled here with its reason shown — nothing in A11 is cropped, so there is no discarded edge for either axis to choose: the file is drawn whole inside its box. Ghost never sees it: focus is a hint the compiler resolves, not a data binding. Add logo stops at twenty-four: “Twenty-four marks is the most a wall holds.” A card is never drawn empty and the count never reserves one, so the placeholder tile sits inside a real card and that card is not published until a file is placed.
EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. alt is edited in the item row rather than on canvas, being the one required string this category never draws. Nothing here is Ghost-owned, so nothing answers “Edit in Ghost” — every word is authored in this section and every mark is a site upload.
