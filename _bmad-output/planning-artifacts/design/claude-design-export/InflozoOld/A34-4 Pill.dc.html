Pill

The same page links as 1 Numbers, gathered into a single raised pill on the page ground. A1·5 drew this shape for a floating header and it is reused here without a change — surface fill, hairline, md shadow, radius at half the height.

It is the friendliest pager in the category and the least serious. The lift makes the whole thing read as one control a reader can put their thumb on, which is exactly right under a gallery or a light feed and slightly wrong under investigative reporting. That judgement belongs to the person choosing, and the picker's description says it in one line.

PATCH PASS TWO · 1 SEPTEMBER 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN The empty state is written, and the case it was missing is an empty feed. A route with no posts still renders: the feed above draws its own “nothing here yet” state and this design draws nothing beneath it — the pill included, since a container with nothing in it is furniture. The editor names the cause in one line, and the drawn proof of both states — site and editor — is on A34-0 rather than repeated in ten frames. A control switched off by another is greyed, with the reason beside it — re-checked here and unchanged: Labels locked to Glyphs only is drawn greyed, showing its value, with the reason beside it: the words inside a pill make it 480 px wide and it stops being a pill. Posts per page: confirmed, not changed. The read-only door in this panel already states INFLOZO's own theme setting and links there, Ghost's admin having no posts-per-page field at all. Nothing in this frame pointed at Ghost Admin, so nothing was re-pointed.
PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN The Numbers control is deleted — four controls now, not five. A theme cannot loop over a range of numbers, so All pages cannot be built at any archive size. The seven-slot window survives because the route hands over every number in it, which also settles this design's width: the pill grows with the window to its 340 px ceiling and nothing can push it past. The internal horizontal scroll is gone — it existed only for All pages. Post range leaves the Pager block, so Position line reads Off · Page count. A range needs a multiplication no template can perform; only the two designs carrying a script can count. Posts per page is INFLOZO's setting, not Ghost's. Ghost's admin has no posts-per-page control, so the read-only row points at the theme's own settings.

The current page changes its marker here, and it is the only design in A34 that does. ⚑ Inside a pill the 2 px accent underline sits 4 px off the container's inner edge and reads as a defect in the shape, so the current page takes a filled inner pill in the pack's hover surface — #F4F0E8 in Paper — with the numeral at 600 in text. It is a token, not a new colour, and A1·14 established the filled icon-button variant that it borrows.
An accent fill was drawn first and rejected. An accent-filled numeral in a row of muted ones reads as the button you should press, and the current page is the one place a reader cannot go. So this design spends accent once, on the focus ring, and nowhere else — the fewest of any A34 design.
The interior boxes grow 38 → 40 and the gap drops 8 → 4. The pill's own 8 px inner padding replaces the gap the boxes needed on open ground, so the whole cluster is 8 px narrower than 1 Numbers' at the same page count. The 44 px target is kept by the boxes overlapping the pill's padding, not by making the pill taller.

THE TWO-PAGE FEED · 172 PX OF PILL · THE DESIGN'S BEST SMALL CASE AND THE ONE PLACE IT BEATS 3 BAR, WHICH HAS A BAND TO FILL AND THIS HAS NOTHING TO FILL
The pill is as wide as its contents and never wider. There is no minimum width and no fixed size: at two pages it is 172 px, at eleven it is 340, and at Contents Position and chevrons it is 232. This is why it is the one design that gets better as the archive gets smaller, and the picker's description for a short feed names it first. Both accent uses here are states, not decoration — the hover underline and the inset focus ring — and neither is present at rest. A pager that draws attention to itself takes attention from the feed it belongs to.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A block field can be locked as well as narrowed, and it is drawn locked rather than hidden — the third and last thing a design may do to the Pager block, after narrowing a value set (2 Prev and Next) and setting a default (3 Bar). Locked shows the value, greyed, with the reason beside it, exactly as A19·13's Show row does. The mechanism now has all three of its cases drawn. The radius is the pill's height halved and it is the one shape in A34 that ignores the pack's radius token. ⚑ A1·5 set this precedent for the floating header and A2·10 followed it for the announcement pill; A34 inherits rather than re-deciding. The inner current-page pill is half the box's height by the same rule, which is what keeps the two shapes concentric in every pack. There is no Sticky control. A pill that follows the reader down the page is a different design with a different module and a real cost to the footer; 7 Endless owns pinned things in A34 and says what they cost. Five controls, and no Rule or Separator. The pill is its own separation — a hairline above a raised object gives the eye two boundaries 48 px apart and neither wins.

The shadow is dropped in dark and the hairline is not. ⚑ Both warm shadows are rgba(28,27,26,·) — near-black — and on a #171511 ground they are invisible; drawing them anyway is a pack rule pretending to be a design. So Elevation Hairline and shadow renders as hairline alone in dark, and Elevation Shadow renders as a hairline too, which is the only case in A34 where a control's value resolves to a different value by mode rather than by width. Stated in the panel and the spec.
The current page's fill is #2C2721 — the dark equivalent of the hover-surface step, one above surface and below border ⚑ derived, as the packs' hover surfaces are not recorded in dark. It measures 1.3:1 against the pill, which is a fill doing what a fill should do: it is the numeral's 600 weight and 12.1:1 contrast that says which page you are on, and the fill only says where to look.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The windowed page links and two chevrons inside one contents-width surface pill with a hairline and a warm md shadow, centred on the page ground, position line beneath. The current page is a filled inner pill rather than an underline — the only A34 design that changes that marker. 2 · Structural descriptor. nav · pill · page · variable · none · one floating pill
The category's only pill containment and, with 7 Endless, one of only two contained designs in A34. Same archetype, ground and count as 1 Numbers; containment is the whole difference, which is what the slot is for. 3 · Archetype. nav. One departure. Below 767 the pill goes full-width rather than staying contents-width, and its numerals leave for the position line as 1 Numbers' do. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 pill 56 tall, radius 28, inner boxes 40 on a 4 gap, 8 px inner padding, centred; position line 14 below; 96 beneath. 1080 and 834 identical — the object does not scale with the measure. ≤ 767 pill full-width at 350, chevrons at the inner edges at 44, page count between them at 15/600, position line below removed, padding 64. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”). newerLabel and olderLabel are authored and never drawn — Labels is locked to Glyphs only here, so both survive as the chevrons' visually-hidden text ⚑. Read-only: the six pagination values. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. 6 · Controls. Contents Numbers and chevrons · Numbers only · Position and chevrons. Elevation Hairline · Shadow · Hairline and shadow. Size Compact 48 · Comfortable 56. Alignment Left · Centre. Four, then the block's four fields — Position line now Off · Page count and its two doors — Labels locked, At the ends defaulting to Dimmed. Cut: Sticky — 7 Endless owns pinned things.
7 · Data. The route's pagination object; destinations /page/N/. 0 posts → the feed draws its own empty state and no pill renders. 1 page → does not render, pill included. 2 pages → the design's best case at 172 px, and the one place it beats 3 Bar. Many → the pill grows with the window to a 340 px ceiling and stops; the window's seven slots bound it exactly as they bound 1 Numbers' row. There is no setting that can push it past that ceiling any more. 8 · Empty state. An empty feed → the feed draws its own designed “nothing here yet” state and the pager renders nothing at all ⚑ — the pill included — a container with nothing in it is furniture. A pager under a feed with nothing in it offers a route through nothing, which is the one-page rule arriving by the same reasoning. The copy belongs to the feed, not to A34, so the category gains no thirteenth catalog string; in the editor the pager's outline is greyed with its own cause — “This feed has no posts, so there is nothing to page through.” — a different sentence from the one-page notice because it has a different fix. One page → nothing renders; greyed outline plus cause in the editor. At the ends is Dimmed by default because a pill missing a chevron at one end is a lopsided shape — the glyph stays at the muted-border step as a <span>. At Hidden the pill contracts by 44 px and stays centred, which is the one case where the design's own geometry changes at an archive end; it is allowed because a contents-width object has no layout to disturb. Position line Off simply removes the line below; Contents Position and chevrons ignores it, since the position is inside the pill. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical, the internal scroll included. Edit-safe. A hover lift was drawn and cut: the pill is not a button, and a raised object that rises further on hover invites a press that does nothing. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> with an <ol>; no heading. The pill is a container, not a control — it takes no role, no tabindex and no label of its own. aria-current="page" on the filled numeral, and the fill is not the only signal: weight and contrast carry it for a reader who cannot see the 1.3:1 step ⚑. Chevrons keep visually-hidden text at all times, since Labels is locked to glyphs. Focus is A6's ring inset to −4 px and rounded to the inner box, A17·18's rule, because +4 lands outside the container. Targets 44 by overlap at both Sizes. Muted numerals 5.4:1, current page 12.1:1 in dark and 13.1:1 in light. Print: the pill prints as nothing and the position line prints. Flagged ⚑ — the radius as height/2 rather than the pack token, inherited from A1·5; the filled inner pill replacing the accent underline and the accent fill refused; the inner fill's derived dark value #2C2721; the shadow dropped in dark, the one value that resolves by mode; Labels locked to Glyphs only; the pill contracting at Hidden.
