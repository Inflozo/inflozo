Split Head

Head, prose and foot in a 416 column, the steps in the 824 beside it. A8·6's split, carried over, and one of two designs in A13 that read the section's own body — the other is 15 Sticky Rail.

This is the design for a process that needs explaining before it is listed. The left column carries as much prose as the site wants; the right carries the sequence at a measure short enough that five or six steps stack without the section becoming a page. It is also the only design besides 9 Big Numbers that offers Title Large 40, because a 416 column can hold one.

416 · 56 · 824 = 1,296, both columns top-aligned, and the first numeral sits level with the top of the title. The head column's own rhythm is 24 — eyebrow to title 10, title to prose 24, prose to note 24, note to link 24 — and the prose is 16/1.7 in text rather than muted, because it is reading matter and not a caption. In the right column each step is a 44 px numeral gutter and a 756 px text block, 32 px apart; the numeral is Medium 28 and sits level with the title's first line. The 80-character title at 03 is one line on 756.
The head is never sticky, and that refusal is the one control this design would have needed a module for. A head column that follows the reader down five steps is scroll-spy's neighbourhood, and A13 already ships that as 15 Sticky Rail — a whole design rather than a control on this one. ⚑ Mine, and it is the clearest case in A13 of a control being refused because a design already exists.

ACCESSIBILITY · TWO COLUMNS, ONE LIST, ONE TAB STOP Two <div>s, neither a landmark. The head column is not <header> and the step column is not <main> — a section inside a page is not a document. Both column sides and both foot placements are done in the source with no order property, so the announcement is eyebrow, title, prose, steps, note, link at every setting. The prose is body, up to 900 characters and three paragraphs, and it is <p>s rather than one block. It is not a <blockquote>, not an <aside>, and it takes no heading of its own — this design has exactly one heading, the <h2>, and it is required in practice: at head None there is no accessible name and the section hands off to 1 Three Up. Tab stops: one, the section link. Contrast: title, step title and prose 15.8:1 / 15.1:1, body, note and numeral 5.6:1 / 6.0:1, the foot's rule 1.3:1. At 400% zoom the columns are stacked, which is the ≤ 1080 drawing, and the numeral gutter has closed.

No design ever turns into another designTwo sentences deleted: seven or eight steps no longer hand this frame to 3 Rows, and a section with no title no longer becomes 1 Three Up. With no title there is no h2 and no accessible name — the head column draws its prose and its foot, the steps draw beside it, and the design stays itself.
Item counts are a number pickerThe steps are a repeater and always were — Add step at the foot, drag handles, Remove on every row, and a stepper capped at this design own ceiling in bound mode — so there is no row of fixed count buttons to replace. Count per row counts planes across the page rather than items and stays a row of named values, the same reading the testimonials category was ruled on. Two to six drawn at One or Two per row; five at Two is 2 plus 2 plus 1 and the panel names the counts that divide rather than locking the value.
The Remove button never greys outRemove is visible and clickable at every count and is never dimmed and never hidden. At two steps clicking it says a process needs at least two steps and the row stays, so the one-step state is not reachable from the button. The head column is unaffected, so a short list leaves the prose taller than the sequence it introduces — stated, not corrected.
Member buttons are conditionalA13 draws no button anywhere. Its one destination is the section link, and where that link points at a member action it now carries the ask: it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens. Scope-tagged apart from Member visibility, which hides the section server-side.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject for the notice — this design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for a notice to replace, and no line on this frame ever promised a subscription without script. JavaScript off: identical at every width. Both column sides and both foot placements are real DOM order with no order property.
Ghost templates cannot count, add, or rememberThe numeral is a stylesheet counter, not arithmetic: the ordered list carries a CSS counter and each item renders counter(step, decimal-leading-zero), which is where 01 to 08 comes from — no padding helper, no authored field, no template maths. The plate figure at 0.4 of the box shorter edge is 40cqmin on a size container, so nothing measures a box either. Nothing counts across its own loop: no step 3 of 6, no total, no and 2 more.
