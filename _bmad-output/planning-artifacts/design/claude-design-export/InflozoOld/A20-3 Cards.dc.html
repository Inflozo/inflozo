The whole set lifted onto one raised plane, with each topic in a hairline card carrying its description. The design for a site whose tag descriptions are written and worth reading — and the only design in A20 whose ground is a surface rather than the page.

A26·3 and A27·4 settled that a plane is a ground rather than a containment. That holds here: the section rests on surface, and the cards inside it are the items' own geometry ⚑.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Item counts are a number pickerThe Tags block’s Show is a number picker, 1–12, where it drew Four · Six · Eight · Twelve · All. Ghost returns at most 100 tags: “All” tripped Ghost’s own warning and silently returned 100 anyway, so the cap and the truncation are stated in the panel instead of promised away. This design’s own maximum is 12 — beyond twelve cards the plane is taller than a screen — drawn greyed with the reason visible.
The Remove button never greys outThe hand-picked list’s ✕ stays visible and clickable at one reference and explains why it cannot go lower — “a hand-picked list needs at least one topic”. Never dimmed, never hidden.
No design ever turns into another designNo subject in this design. It draws itself at every count — one topic is drawn as one topic — and nothing in it switches to a different design. The panel may advise at a count; advice is never a switch.
Member buttons · the no-JavaScript noticeNo subject. This design carries no subscribe form, no sign-in form and no paid-tier button — the all-topics link is navigation, not a call to action — so there is nothing to make conditional and nothing for a notice to replace.
NumberingUnchanged. This category is 1–15 and this design is still 3 Cards. No gap was created or closed, no number reused.
