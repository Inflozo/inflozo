Three Up

WHY THESE SEVEN, AND WHAT WAS CUTThe list is seven now: Tag and “View all” link joined the six. The Padding row is gone — it was the universal Vertical spacing under another name, and it is drawn outside the list with Background role and Top divider.The Data group became the Data group — P0·5 “Populate from…” configured for posts, with Hand-picked added to Source, Count as a stepper and, at This route’s posts, Pagination style and an empty state. Per row, Image ratio, Excerpt and Meta are the four that change what a reader sees. First cell, Tag and the “View all” link are the three that decide what else the section carries. That is the whole list: seven, and an eighth would have to displace one of these — the universal trio and the Data group sit outside it and are not part of the trade. Cut: Head alignment. A17's head is flush left in all eighteen designs, and a centred head over a left-aligned grid is a different design — 5 Lead + Grid and 6 Split Head are where the head moves. ⚑ Mine, and it departs from A12·1, which offered it. Cut: Show excerpt on the first post only. It is a per-item control wearing a section control's clothes, and A17-0 rules those out by construction. The design that wants it is 5 Lead + Grid, where the span carries the excerpt and the uniform cells do not. Cut: Card background. A plane per post is 4 Cards and a plane per section is 17 Panel; offering it here would make three designs out of one control. Cut: Gap. The 24 px gutter and the 48 px row gap are the grid, not a preference — A10's rule, inherited. Meta None is the one value that changes the card's shape rather than its content, and at Excerpt Off with Meta None this design is a grid of images and titles — which is 14 Dense at three across. Allowed, and the panel does not warn: a user who turns both off knows what they want.

THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS · THE PROSE COPY LIVES IN “A17 POST GRIDS - SPEC.MD”
1 · Descriptor. Equal cells, two to four across, each an image above a tag, a title, an optional excerpt and a meta row. The category's default and its most uniform arrangement. 2 · Structural descriptor. grid-of-N · none · page · many · top · uniform cell rhythm
Containment none because the section sits in nothing; the posts have no plane either. Ground page. This is the tuple six other designs also reach — see A17-0's honest note. 3 · Archetype. grid-of-N. Default collapse ladder applies with no departures. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 per-row count as set, 1,296 content width. 1080 Four → three. 834 Three and Four → two at 365; Two stays; excerpt caps at two lines. ≤ 767 one across, full-width image at its ratio. Departures from the ladder: two. The excerpt cap at 834, and the first cell’s span dropped at ≤ 767 with the excerpt kept. 5 · Content fields. From the section: eyebrow ≤ 26 opt · title ≤ 104 opt · sub ≤ 178 opt · note ≤ 120 opt · linkLabel ≤ 24 + linkUrl opt as a pair. From the Data group: all six. From each post: all ten — this is the one design that draws every optional field the category has. 6 · Controls. In sidebar order, after the Design picker: Per row Two · Three · Four. 7 · Data. Ghost's #get "posts" helper per the Data group. Designed for 6 and 9; correct at 2 to 12. 0: section does not render; in the editor, the outlined grid and the query line. 1: one cell at the count's width, at the left, not promoted. Fewer than the count: short last row keeps the cell width and sits at the left. More: the extras are not drawn; pagination is A34's, attached below. Order is Newest or Oldest first, nothing else. 8 · Empty state. Per field, on the post: no image → the tag plate at the same box and radius; no image anywhere in the set → the image row is not drawn and the design becomes a text grid; no tag → the title moves up and the eyebrow's space is not held; no excerpt → nothing drawn, the card is shorter; no author photograph → A12's one-letter initial at 24 px; no reading time → the separator goes with it. On the section: no eyebrow, sub, note or link → each simply absent. No placeholder is ever drawn for a post that exists. 9 · Behaviour module. None. core is assumed category-wide. No-JS degradation: the design is pixel-identical with JavaScript off — every card is a server-rendered <a>, the hover underline and focus ring are CSS, and nothing is revealed, counted or loaded. Edit-safe by having no behaviour to be unsafe with. 10 · Accessibility. <section aria-labelledby> → the <h2> title; at Head None the section has no accessible name and the panel says so. Posts are a <ul> of <li>; each title an <h3> inside the card's <a>, which takes its accessible name from that <h3>. Tag and meta are <p>s and are inside the link, so they are read after the title — one announcement per card, not four. Image alt from feature_image_alt, else alt=""; the tag plate is aria-hidden because its text repeats the eyebrow below it. Focus order is DOM order, left to right by row; focus ring is A6's, on the whole card box. Date is a <time datetime>. Contrast: muted-on-ground 4.9:1 light, 5.4:1 dark; accent underline 3.4:1 against the ground, which is a non-text indicator and passes at 3:1.
