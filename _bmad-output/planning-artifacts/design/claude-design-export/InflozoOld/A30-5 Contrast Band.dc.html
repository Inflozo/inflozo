WHAT THE BAND SETTLES ABOUT ACCENT
Accent is not available on this ground and the control says so ⚑ — the Button control’s Accent value is disabled with “4.0:1” beside it, rather than being silently allowed. That is §7·4 of this project’s rules, applied.The substitution is derived, not chosen: the button takes the band’s own carried colour, so it re-derives correctly in all twelve packs without a per-pack decision ⚑.One accent use survives — none. This is the only design in A30 with no accent on it at all ⚑, and the category’s only section where the primary action is a neutral.

Every state stays on the band ⚑. The sent and expired panels use the 7 % plate rather than a surface colour, because a light card on an inverted band is a hole in it. The field’s focus border is the carried colour, not the accent ⚑ — the same substitution the button makes, for the same measured reason.

Everything that changes a subscription
Ghost Portal🔒
Read-only. Plan changes, checkout, billing, email changes and newsletter preferences are Portal’s ⚑ — the theme draws the trigger and Portal draws the rest. This is the category’s boundary and no control moves it.
Member buttons are conditional ⚑ — every Sign up, Subscribe and paid-tier button is absent where the connected site cannot support it: self-signup switched off, members disabled, or no payment provider connected. Ghost seeds a $5 tier at install, so an unconnected site would otherwise ship a card whose checkout cannot complete. The rows stay in this panel, disabled, with the reason readable.Ghost’s own pop-up needs JavaScript ⚑ — every button that opens Portal, including every tier deep link and every hand-off on the account page: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. The theme cannot substitute anything for it, so the notice is the panel’s, not the page’s.Nothing identifying is printed ⚑, new in this pass — no member name, no member email, and no join date on any of the thirteen. Ghost gives a theme no join date and no list of a member’s newsletters, so both rows are gone: the newsletter row is a link out to Ghost’s own account screen, and the email row hands off the same way. Layout and actions are ours; anything that names the reader is Ghost’s account panel’s.
EDITING · THE P0 PRIMITIVESEvery authored string edits inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, its popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored: eyebrow, heading, blurb, ctaLabel, note, legal, benefits[] and every state string. Ghost-owned content is never inline-editable ⚑ — the member's name and email, the tier name, the price, the renewal date, the newsletter names and a tier's description and benefits all answer "Edit in Ghost" or show P0·1's lock pill. Every URL field opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, Portal actions first. No fixed English visitor-facing string ships: the rescued literals above are fields with defaults, and the badge vocabulary and sending word are catalog strings. There is no Preview control in this panel and there never was one to remove — states are P0·6's switcher, audiences are View as.benefits[] is the P0·3 authored list ⚑ — drag to reorder, per-row overflow with Duplicate and Remove, Add arrives with content rather than an empty shell, Remove is never disabled, and the range line reads 0–6 · 4 used. Add disables at six with its reason in a sentence. Per-item content only: a name and a detail edit inline, and no per-item layout control exists ⚑. Tiers are the other list and are Ghost’s — no Add, no Remove, no drag.The one icon slot is the primary button’s ⚑ — P0·2's button-icon rules: Before or After the label, always Small, always the label's colour, its Size and Colour rows hidden. The list marker is the second where a design draws ticks, and it is whole-list. On the band the icon’s Accent colour role is disabled at 4.0:1 ⚑ — P0·2's rule that a role failing AA on its ground is disabled with its ratio shown, met here for the first time.Member Visibility lands nowhere in A30 ⚑, and it is a judgement rather than an omission: the route is the member state. A signed-in member never sees a signup form — the Data group's "A signed-in member on this page" decides what they see instead — so a section-level Everyone / Logged out / Free / Paid row would hide a page from readers the route has already sorted. P0·4’s member-aware action editor never opens here for the same reason. Recorded in the Reconciliation notes.

In dark the band inverts to the light contrast token #EDE7DA carrying #171511 ⚑ — the band is always the opposite of the page, which is why it is a re-tune and not an inversion. The 72 / 20 / 8 derivations recompute from the new carried colour, and the button is again the carried colour: #171511 on #EDE7DA at 15.6:1.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. The whole membership page on one full-bleed inverted band carrying its own padding, with the form centred on a 480 measure and the included list under a 20 % hairline. The only design in A30 with no accent on it.2 · Structural descriptor. form · none · contrast · none · none · the page as an inverted band
Ground contrast is the whole distinction from 1 Centred and 4 Panel — same components, same measure, three grounds, three designs. Containment none: a full-width band is a ground ⚑.3 · Archetype. form. One departure: the section has no padding of its own at any width ⚑ — the band carries it.4 · Responsive rule. 1440 band full bleed, inner padding 96, column 480 centred, heading 40. 834 inner 80, column 440, heading 34. ≤ 767 inner 64, column = frame − 40, heading 28, field and button full width. Band width: Content box resolves to full bleed below 1,440 ⚑.5 · Content fields. As 1 Centred, and nothing more — the band adds no field ⚑. What changes is the derivation of every colour in it, and that is not a field.
6 · Controls. Band padding Compact · Comfortable · Spacious (kept — it is the band's own ladder ⚑) — Band width Full bleed · Content box — Alignment Centred · Left — Button Carried fill · Outline · Accent, disabled at 4.0:1 ⚑ — Included list — Fields — Sign-in link (new ⚑) — Eyebrow (new ⚑) — Note line (new ⚑) — List marker (new ⚑) — CTA icon (new ⚑). Eleven, plus the P0·3 list. Then the trio, all three rows locked ⚑ — Background role at Contrast, Vertical spacing resolving to 0, Top divider at None — and the Data group.7 · Data. As 1 Centred; tiers read and not drawn. The band renders identically for every member type and only its height changes ⚑.8 · Empty state. No benefits → hairline and list go, band shortens. The band is never hidden ⚑. Free and comped drop rows as in 1 Centred.9 · Behaviour module. member-form. Edit-safe: nothing posts while the section is edited and the states are drawn rather than reached. No-JS — corrected this pass (the no-JavaScript notice): “The <form> posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint”. It cannot. Tested against both live Ghost servers, Ghost’s signup endpoint refuses a plain form submission, so a scriptless field would take an address and lose it. P0·4’s notice replaces the form — no field, no button, the head and everything around it unchanged. Sending, sent, expired and invalid are untouched: they are Ghost’s own script and they work.10 · Accessibility notes. h1 then the form, as 1 Centred. Every contrast pair on the band was measured ⚑: carried on band 13.4:1, muted 72 % 7.1:1, the 8 % field plate’s placeholder 4.9:1, the carried-fill button 13.4:1, and the accent 4.0:1 — which is why it is disabled.
