One button under the feed that appends the next page in place, a count line saying how far in you are, and an optional hairline meter. The button is A17·16's — Outline, Solid or Text at 45 px on a 24 px radius — and A18·15 used it second. This is its third appearance and nothing about it has changed.

What A34 adds is the honest account of what it costs. The URL does not change, so a reader who presses twice, opens a post and comes back lands on the first twelve again; the footer moves further away with every press. Both are stated in the panel rather than in a release note, and neither is a reason not to use it — a feed people browse rather than search is exactly what this is for.

The meter is 320 px wide, 2 px tall, and not accent. ⚑ Its track is border and its fill is text-muted, because it is information rather than an action — the accent in this section belongs to the button at Solid and to the focus ring, and a third use would make the pager louder than the feed. It reports posts, not presses: 24 of 132 is 18%, and a reader can see at a glance that this archive is deep.
The count line is A17's editor count line's public sibling and reads “24 of 132 posts” — a count, not a percentage, and never “Page 2 of 11”, because after a press there is no single page the reader is on. That is the design's honest position: it can say how much you have seen and cannot say where you are, which is why the block's Position line is fixed Off here.
There is no Batch control. ⚑ A17·16 had one because that section owned its query; A34 attaches to a route and each press fetches exactly one Ghost page — posts_per_page, 12 here — so the batch is the site's setting and the panel states it as a read-only line.

NO JAVASCRIPT · QUOTED FROM THE REGISTRY: “GHOST'S NUMBERED /PAGE/2/ PAGINATION LINKS RENDER INSTEAD (FR-G4, EXPLICITLY).” · THE BUTTON, THE COUNT AND THE METER ARE ABSENT, NOT INERT — AND WHAT RENDERS IS 1 NUMBERS AT ITS DEFAULTS
The numbered links are in the markup on every render, whether or not they are drawn. That is what makes the no-JS branch automatic, what makes the archive crawlable, and what lets Page links Shown be a display decision rather than a build one. At Hidden they are .visually-hidden, not display:none ⚑, so a screen-reader user always has a page-by-page route through the archive even when the sighted route is a button. A finding, not a change: the button could be an <a href="/page/3/"> that the module intercepts, which would give the same no-JS result with one element instead of two. A17·16 and A18·15 are drawn with a real <button> and a component does not change in its third category, so this is recorded for the architect rather than acted on.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A panel may carry a standing warning about the design it is configuring, and this is the first one in the library. ⚑ It is not an error and it does not appear conditionally — it is a property of the mechanism, drawn in the pack's surface with a hairline rather than in a warning colour, because nothing is wrong. 7 Endless carries a stronger one for the same reason. A block field may be inert for a design, and it is drawn inert rather than removed. The fourth and last thing a design may do to the Pager block, after narrowing, defaulting and locking. Position line reads “Off · fixed by this design”, At the ends reads “Not applicable”, Labels says where the value still goes. A user switching from 1 Numbers can see exactly which of their settings this design cannot honour. There is no Batch control and the panel states the site's value instead — “12 posts a page, from INFLOZO's theme settings”. A34 does not own the query in any of its ten designs, and the one design that could plausibly have taken a batch size is the one where it would have desynchronised the button from the numbered links in the same markup. Four controls. Cut: auto-load on scroll — that is infinite-scroll and 7 Endless declares it — and a “remember my position” toggle, which would need storage the theme does not have and would be a lie in a private window.

The outlined button's border is text, not border, in both modes — A17·16's decision, inherited: a hairline-bordered button on a dark ground disappears, and an outline button whose outline is the same weight as a divider is not a button. In dark that means a 1 px #F2EDE4 ring at 14.9:1, which is a strong line and the right one: this is the only control in the section.
The meter's two tones invert to border over text-muted and hold their relationship — the fill is 3.0× the track's lightness in light and 2.7× here, close enough that no adjustment is needed. Solid's accent fill is checked separately: #E0805A with the derived on-accent label measures 5.4:1 in dark against 4.8:1 in light, so the darker mode is the safer one and neither needs a re-tune ⚑.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A17·16's centred button under the feed, appending one Ghost page per press, with a count line and a 320 px hairline meter beneath it and the route's numbered links present in the markup at all times. The only A34 design that grows the feed rather than replacing it. 2 · Structural descriptor. feed · none · page · one · none · one button appends a batch
The category's only feed archetype and, with 7 Endless, one of two designs whose count is not a set of page links. One unit: the button. 7 Endless has none, which is the difference the count slot records between pressing and scrolling. 3 · Archetype. feed. One departure, inherited: the control goes full-width below 767, A17·16's own rule. Otherwise the design does not respond at all above that width. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 button 45 tall on a 24 radius, contents-width, centred; count line 16 below; meter 320 × 2; 96 beneath. 1080 and 834 identical but for the page margin; padding 80 at 834. ≤ 767 button full-width at 350 and 48 tall, meter widens to the measure, Alignment ignored, jump-row boxes 26 → 32 wrapping, padding 64. No element leaves at any width, which makes this and 5 Counter the only A34 designs that drop nothing. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”); moreLabel (text, optional, 32 chars, default “Load more posts” — the union's only field this design alone needs, and the reason it is in the union at all); endLabel (text, optional, 48 chars, default “That's all {total} posts.” with the total substituted); newerLabel and olderLabel read by the hidden numbered links only. Read-only: the six pagination values; .limit is the batch and .total drives both the count and the meter. 6 · Controls. Button style Outline · Solid · Text. Progress Off · Count line · Count and meter. Page links Hidden · Shown below. Alignment Left · Centre. Four, then the block's four fields and its two doors — Position line fixed Off, At the ends not applicable, Labels routed to the hidden links. No Batch ⚑ and cut: auto-load on scroll and a position-memory toggle.
7 · Data. The first page is server-rendered by the route; each press fetches the next /page/N/ against the same query and appends its items to the feed above. Designed for archives of 24–144 posts across two to eleven presses. 0 posts → the feed draws its own empty state; no button, no count, no meter, and no hidden links. 1 page → no button, and at Progress Count line the count alone renders reading “12 of 12 posts”; at Off nothing renders. Many → the button walks the whole archive, unlike A18·15's, which stopped at Show — because A34 has no Show ⚑, and a pager that stops halfway through a route is a dead end. The batch is always Ghost's posts_per_page, so the button and the numbered links in the same markup can never disagree. 8 · Empty state. An empty feed → the feed draws its own designed “nothing here yet” state and the pager renders nothing at all ⚑ — no button, no count line, no meter and no hidden numbered links — there is no page to link to. A pager under a feed with nothing in it offers a route through nothing, which is the one-page rule arriving by the same reasoning. The copy belongs to the feed, not to A34, so the category gains no thirteenth catalog string; in the editor the pager's outline is greyed with its own cause — “This feed has no posts, so there is nothing to page through.” — a different sentence from the one-page notice because it has a different fix. Nothing left to load → the button is replaced by endLabel in the button's own position at the button's own height, so the page does not jump — A17·16's rule verbatim — and the meter fills and stays. It does not grey out and remain. A failed fetch returns the button to rest and adds one line: “Those posts didn't load. Try again.” ⚑ — A34's own addition, since neither A17 nor A18 drew a failure and a network is a thing that fails. In the editor the module does not run: the resting button, the count at its server value and the meter at its first-page fraction. 9 · Behaviour module. load-more. No-JS degradation, quoted from the registry: “Ghost's numbered /page/2/ pagination links render instead (FR-G4, explicitly).” Those links are in the markup on every render — .visually-hidden at Page links Hidden, never display:none ⚑ — so the branch is automatic and the archive stays crawlable. The button, the count and the meter are absent in that branch, not inert. Edit-safe: the module does not run while editing. Reduced motion: the loading dots hold still and the label carries the state; the meter's width changes without a transition. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination"> holding a real <button> plus the hidden <ol> of page links. A visually-hidden aria-live="polite" region announces the count and not the titles — “12 more posts loaded. Showing 24 of 132.” — and focus moves to the first new post's link, A18·15's rule unchanged. aria-busy on the feed while loading; the button keeps its width and its accessible name changes to “Loading”. The meter is role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow in posts, or aria-hidden when the count line beside it says the same thing — it is the latter, because it does ⚑. Button 45 px on a 44 px minimum; the jump row's 26 px boxes are the one sub-44 target in A34 and are flagged as a secondary route. Outline border 13.1:1 light, 14.9:1 dark. Print: button, count and meter absent; nothing else to keep. Flagged ⚑ — the meter in text-muted rather than accent, and aria-hidden beside its own count; no Batch control; the button walking the whole archive where A18·15 stopped at Show; the numbered links always in the markup and only visually hidden; the standing warning in the panel; three block fields inert; the failure line as A34's addition; the jump row raised to the category's 38/44 box, so A34 has no sub-44 target left; the anchor-instead-of-button finding, recorded and not acted on.
