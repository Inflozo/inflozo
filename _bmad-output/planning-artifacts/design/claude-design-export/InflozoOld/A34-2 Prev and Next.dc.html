Two links pushed to the ends of the 1,296 measure with the position line centred between them, under a hairline. It spends the width 1 Numbers refuses to spend, and it can only afford to because there are two things in the row instead of nine.

A reader still knows where they are — the position line is doing that work — but they cannot get to page 7 from here, and on an eleven-page archive that is a real cost. It is the right pager for a feed of two or three pages and the wrong one for a decade of writing; the panel says so in those words.

The links sit on the content edges, not the viewport's — they start and end exactly where the feed's first and last columns do, which is the whole reason this design reads as belonging to the feed rather than floating under it. The label is the heading font at 20/700, the one place in A34 where a pager borrows the pack's heading face: at 15 px in the body font two words 1,100 px apart are invisible, and the row needs enough weight to be seen as a pair.
The chevron is 26 px and sits outside the label, so both arrows are at the extreme edges and the eye reads outward. At Chevrons Beside the label they move inboard and the labels take the edges instead ⚑ — a real difference at this width and nothing at 390, where the row has stacked.
The sublabel is the destination's page number, not its date. ⚑ A date range would be better and Ghost cannot give it without a lookahead query for the next page's posts; that is named as a finding for the architect rather than drawn as a control that might not resolve.

Hidden reserves the slot rather than collapsing the row. ⚑ If the empty side collapsed, the position line would drift off centre and the surviving link would slide inward — two things moving because one thing is absent. The reserved slot is the same rule 1 Numbers uses and the reason both designs look still at the ends of an archive. Dimmed here earns a sublabel the other designs do not have. “No older posts” at the muted-border step explains the dim in three words. It is a <span>, so there is nothing to click and nothing inert. WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A block field can be constrained by a design, and after this pass this design locks one. ⚑ Position line has two values left in the category — Off and Page count — and this design disables Off, so Page count is the only value it will render. The disabled value is drawn greyed with its reason rather than removed, because the field exists in all ten designs and a user switching designs needs to see where their setting went. The rule is unchanged: a design may narrow, set, lock, ignore or interpret a block field, and may never add a value to one. Post range was not narrowed away here — it left the block entirely, in every design, because no template can work out which posts a page holds. There is no Alignment control, because the alignment is the design. Both links are at the content edges at every setting; the moment they are centred this is 1 Numbers with the numerals switched off, which is a setting 1 Numbers already has. Four controls, and the sixth was cut for being a different design. A “Show page numbers between the links” toggle was drawn, worked, and was removed: it made 1 Numbers reachable from inside 2, and two designs that can become each other are one design with a control. The panel recommends and does not enforce. On an archive over six pages it reads “11 pages. A reader can only move one page at a time from here — 1 Numbers reaches any page in one click.” That line is advice with a named alternative, which is A19's pattern for a design used outside its range.

Nothing is re-tuned here and one thing was checked. Georgia at 20/700 in text on background measures 13.4:1 and holds its stems at this size — A19·13 checked the same face at 34 regular and found it sound, and 20 bold is the easier case. The sublabel at 13 in muted measures 5.7:1, above AA and deliberately the quietest thing in the frame.
This design spends no accent at rest, which makes it the only pager in A34 with none: the current page has no marker because there are no page numbers to mark. Accent appears on hover and on focus and nowhere else, in both modes.
THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. Two directional links at the ends of the content measure, each with the destination's page number beneath it, the position line centred between them, a hairline above. No page links, and the only A34 design that reaches the content edges. 2 · Structural descriptor. nav · none · page · few · none · facing links at the margins
Separates from 1 Numbers on count — two units against a windowed set — and from 9 Cards on archetype, which is the real difference: 9 puts the same two destinations in two contained cards and this one leaves them bare on the page. 3 · Archetype. nav. One departure: below 767 the two links stack into full-width rows rather than compressing, the position line moves above them, and the reserved empty slot is abandoned — at the ends of the archive the absent row is removed. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 two links on the 1,296 edges, label 20/700 heading font, sublabel 13, chevron 26 outside the label, position line centred at 13, hairline above, 96 beneath. 1080 unchanged. 834 chevron 24, page margin 40, padding 80, arrangement identical. ≤ 767 two stacked 60 px rows separated by a hairline, newer first, both chevrons to the left ⚑, label 19, position line above the pair, absent side removed, padding 64. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”); newerLabel and olderLabel (text, optional, 24 chars, defaulting from Labels — this design spends them harder than any other, since the two words are the whole composition). Read-only: pagination.page, .pages, .total, .limit, .next, .prev. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. 6 · Controls. Link size Small 15 body · Medium 20 heading · Large 28 heading. Sublabel Off · Page number. Chevrons Off · Beside the label · At the outer edge. Rule None · Above · Above and below. Four, then the block's four fields and its two doors — with Position line locked to Page count — Off stays drawn and disabled, and Post range left the block in the patch pass. Cut: a page-numbers toggle, which would have made this design 1 Numbers.
7 · Data. pagination.prev and .next for the destinations, .page and .pages for the sublabels and the centre. 0 → no route, no section. 1 page → does not render. 2 pages → the design's best case, one live link and one reserved slot on each page. Many → unchanged, and the reader can still only move one page at a time, which the panel names with 1 Numbers as the alternative. The sublabel is not arithmetic at all — the route hands over the previous and next page numbers, and the design draws the one it is pointing at, which is why it survives a pass that deleted every line a template had to work out. 8 · Empty state. One page → nothing on the site, the greyed outline and the one-line cause in the editor. First page, Hidden: the slot keeps its width above 767 and is removed below it; Dimmed: the label and chevron go to the muted-border step and the sublabel becomes “No older posts” / “No newer posts” — the only dimmed slot in A34 that says why, and a <span> throughout. Sublabel Off simply drops the second line and the row shortens by 19 px. No label authored → the Labels value supplies it. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. Two <a>s and two <span>s; there is nothing in the design a script could add. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination">, no heading, no list — two links do not need an <ol> and the DOM order is newer then older at every width, matching page order ⚑. rel="prev" and rel="next". The accessible name is the label plus the sublabel — “Newer posts, page 1” — so the two links are never announced identically. The position line is a plain <p>, not a live region: nothing changes without a page load. Chevrons are aria-hidden. Target is the whole link block, 60 px tall stacked and 44 minimum inline. Focus is A6's ring on the block including its chevron; hover underlines the label only ⚑, not the sublabel and not the arrow. Label 13.4:1, sublabel 5.7:1. Print: links absent, position line kept. Flagged ⚑ — the heading font at Medium and Large and the body font at Small, the one control in A34 that changes a typeface; the sublabel read straight from the route rather than counted, with a date range named as a finding; the reserved slot above 767 and removed below it; both chevrons to the left when stacked; Position line Off disabled, and the rule that a design may narrow a block field but never extend it; hover on the label alone.
