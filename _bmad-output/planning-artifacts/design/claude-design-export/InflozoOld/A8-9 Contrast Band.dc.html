Contrast Band

The section inverted onto the contrast token, with the quotations drawn as panels on the band. The band, its 44 · 64 · 88 padding scale and its derived mixes are A4·9's, carried; A8-0 names this design and 14 Slim Line as the two in the category with their own vertical scale.

What this design settles is a contrast failure. In dark mode the band is light — contrast is #EDE7DA carrying #171511 — and the pack's dark accent on that band is 2.3:1, which fails. So on a band the link's underline takes the carried colour rather than the accent, at both modes for consistency, and the sidebar shows the ratio that caused it. It is the only place in A8 where the accent is substituted rather than simply absent.

The dark accent on the light band is 2.3:1 against it, and a 2 px underline is a non-text element needing 3:1. So the value is not silently allowed: the sidebar shows the ratio and the underline takes the carried colour instead. The light-mode accent would pass at 4.8:1, and is still not used, because a link that is accent-underlined in light and carried-underlined in dark is two different links in one theme. One rule: on a band, the underline is the carried colour. The focus ring has the same problem and the same answer — on a band it is 2 px of the carried colour with the 2 px gap taking the band, which is A6's own band rule and the reason this design needed no new ring. So this section has no accent in it at all, in either mode, which makes it the quietest design in a category A8-0 already describes as mostly accent-free. ⚑ The substitution, its consistency across modes and the disclosure are mine.
