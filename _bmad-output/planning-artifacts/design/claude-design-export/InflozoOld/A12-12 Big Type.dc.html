EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. The statement edits inline at Display 48 on its 1,040 measure — the largest inline-edited type in A12, and the toolbar flips below the selection when the statement sits in the canvas’s top 48 px, per P0·1. Per person, name and role edit inline in the card; a held person is edited in the repeater row, since there is nothing on canvas to select. At Source Ghost authors the name and the bio are Ghost-owned: the toolbar is replaced by the plain-text lock pill and clicking says “Edit in Ghost”. The Role override is ours and stays inline-editable.
