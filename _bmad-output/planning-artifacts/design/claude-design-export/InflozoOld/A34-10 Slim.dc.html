Slim

One line under a hairline: where you are on the left, the two directions on the right. 44 px of section height at Padding Compact, which is less than a single row of A18's list, and the reason it exists is that some feeds should not end with a monument.

Every category in this library has a Slim and they all do the same job: they are the value a designer reaches for when the section above is the point and the furniture below it should be almost invisible. A18·3 Slim's row, A11·10's logo line and A14's caption strip are the same instinct; this is its pagination form.

PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED IN THIS DESIGN Post range leaves the Pager block, so Position line reads Off · Page count. A range of posts needs a page number multiplied by a page size, and a theme's templates cannot multiply — only the two designs that carry a script can count. Every frame that drew “Posts 13–24 of 132” now draws “Page 2 of 11”. This design gains the cleanest thing in the pass: it now has no responsive departure at all. Its single exception was the post range stacking into two rows below 767, and the value that triggered it is gone. One 44 px row, from 1440 to 390, on every combination of its four controls. The second phone frame is redrawn at Contents Direction only. Posts per page is INFLOZO's setting, not Ghost's. Ghost's admin has no posts-per-page control, so the read-only row points at the theme's own settings.

The row is 44 px tall and the type inside it is 13 and 15. The height is the target, not the type: a 15 px link in a 44 px row has 14 px of clear space above and below it, which is what makes a line this quiet still pressable. The two links keep 44 px targets by taking the row's full height, and their visible extent is the words alone.
The separator between the two links is a 14 px vertical hairline, not a middle dot ⚑ — a dot between two directional links reads as a list, and a rule reads as a divider between two choices. It is aria-hidden and it is the only vertical hairline in A34.
Labels shorten to one word here and only here. “Newer” and “Older” rather than “Newer posts” and “Older posts” — the block's Labels value supplies the noun and this design drops it at every width ⚑, because the point of the design is the line's height and the noun costs 76 px without adding a fact. Alignment Split is the default and Right is the interesting value. Split reads as a bar and needs the width to justify itself; Right reads as a caption and is the one setting in A34 that makes the pager subordinate to the feed rather than parallel to it. At Right the position line joins the cluster rather than staying at the far edge, because two right-aligned groups with 900 px between them are not a cluster. Direction Chevrons only is the only setting in A34 where a 15 px word is replaced by a glyph in a box at this scale, and it exists for a sidebar feed at 320 px where “Newer · Older” does not fit beside a page count. It is also the value that makes this design nearly invisible, which is either the point or a mistake depending on the page. There is no Contents Position only. A row that says where you are and gives no way to leave is a caption, and the control would let a user build one — the same hole 5 Counter's Arrows control deliberately has.

WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLES A design may set a different default for its own Padding, and this is the only one that does. ⚑ Nine designs default to Comfortable 96 because that is the library's section rhythm; a 44 px row with 96 px under it is 30% content, and a design whose purpose is to take no room should not open by taking room. Compact 64 is still the library's own value — the default moved, the ladder did not. A block field may be interpreted by a design as well as narrowed, defaulted, locked or ignored. Labels sets the words in nine designs and here it sets only the first word of each, with the noun kept for the accessible name. It is the fifth and last thing the block permits, and the reason it is permitted is that the alternative — a separate “short labels” control — would be a sixth control to save 76 px. Rule None is the value that makes this design disappear entirely, and it is offered because a feed that already ends in a hairline — A18·1's row separator, A17·17's panel edge — does not need a second one 48 px later. The panel's line names both of those designs, which is the first time an A34 panel refers to a specific design above it rather than to a feed in general. Four controls, and this is the design where a sixth was hardest to refuse. A Numbers pair was drawn — “Windowed page links on one slim line” — and cut: five numerals in a 44 px row is 1 Numbers at Separator None with worse spacing, and two designs that can become each other are one design with a control.

Both hairlines take border and both hold — A17's symmetric-step finding covers the horizontal one, and the 14 px vertical rule is the same value turned on its side, which needs no separate check because it is the same 1 px against the same ground. The vertical rule is the more fragile of the two at 14 px of length, and at #332E27 on #171511 it reads as a divider rather than as dust.
This design spends no accent at rest in either mode, like 2 Prev and Next and for the same reason: there is no current page to mark. THE WRITTEN SPEC · ALL TEN FIELDS
1 · Descriptor. A single 44 px row under a hairline: the position line at 13 px on the left, “Newer” and “Older” at 15/600 with their chevrons on the right, divided by a 14 px vertical hairline. The smallest pager in the library and the only A34 design whose arrangement is identical at every width. 2 · Structural descriptor. bar · none · page · few · none · one right-aligned line
A bar with no ground, which is what proves the archetype is a shape rather than a surface: it collapses like a bar and rests on the page. Separates from 3 Bar and 8 Contrast Band on ground, and from 2 Prev and Next on archetype — a row with three things in it against two links at the margins. 3 · Archetype. bar. No departure, at any width or on any combination of its controls ⚑ — the one exception was Position line Post range below 767, which stacked into two rows, and that value left the category in the patch pass. The row is identical from 1440 to 390. 4 · Responsive rule. 1440 row 44 tall, hairline above with 14 beneath it, position line 13 left, links 15/600 right with a 20 gap and an 8 px word-to-glyph gap, 64 below. 1080 and 834 identical; page margin 40 at 834. ≤ 767 identical but for the gaps — 20 → 14 and 8 → 6; type, height, targets and order unchanged; page margin 20; padding 64, which is already the default. There is no longer any exception ⚑ — the combination that stacked was Position line Post range, deleted in the patch pass. 5 · Content fields. navLabel (text, optional, default “Pagination”); newerLabel and olderLabel (text, optional, 24 chars) — drawn as their first word only, with the full value kept as the link's accessible name ⚑. A label whose first word is longer than about 12 characters widens the row and the panel says so. Read-only: the six pagination values. In the union, unread: moreLabel, endLabel. 6 · Controls. Contents Position and direction · Direction only — no position-only value ⚑. Alignment Split · Right · Left. Direction Words · Words and chevrons · Chevrons only. Rule Hairline above · None. Four, then the block's four fields and its two doors. Cut: a Numbers pair, which would have made this 1 Numbers at Separator None.
7 · Data. pagination.prev and .next for the links, .page, .pages, .limit and .total for the position line. 0 → no section. 1 page → does not render. 2 pages → the design's best case in the category: at two pages every other A34 design is overbuilt and this one is the right size. Many → unchanged, and unchanged is the point — a hundred-page archive draws the identical row, which is the one thing no other design here can say. 8 · Empty state. One page → nothing renders; greyed row and cause in the editor. At the ends is Dimmed by default — hiding one of two words either side of a vertical rule leaves a rule dividing one thing from nothing — and the spent link goes to the muted-border step as a <span>, keeping its glyph. At Hidden the rule goes with the link and the survivor sits alone. Contents Direction only removes the position line and the row keeps its height; the row is never shorter than 44 px whatever is in it, because the height is the target. 9 · Behaviour module. None; core assumed. No-JS: pixel-identical. Edit-safe. Two anchors, one paragraph, two hairlines — the least machinery of any design in the library, and the reason it is the frame a new Style Pack should be checked against first. 10 · Accessibility. <nav aria-label="Pagination">, no heading, two links, DOM order position line → newer → older at every width, including the stacked exception, where the visual order changes and the DOM order does not ⚑ — the position line is read first even when it is drawn second, because it is a caption for the section rather than a step in it. Each link's accessible name is the full label — “Newer posts” — not the drawn word. rel="prev"/"next"; both hairlines and both glyphs aria-hidden. Targets are the row's full 44 px height and, at Direction Chevrons only, 38 px boxes inside it. Focus is A6's ring at 4 px on the word-and-glyph pair; hover underlines the pair including the glyph ⚑, unlike 2 Prev and Next. Links 13.1:1, position line 5.4:1. Print: links absent, position line kept — and this is the design whose printed form is closest to its screen form, being a line of 13 px meta either way. Flagged ⚑ — Padding defaulting to Compact, the only one in A34; the vertical hairline instead of a middle dot; labels drawn as their first word with the noun kept for the accessible name; no position-only value; the Post-range stacking exception triggered by a block value rather than a width; the DOM order holding when the visual order stacks; hover covering the glyph; the row never shorter than 44 px whatever it holds.
