The name never shrinks to fit ⚑. A long name wraps to two lines at text-wrap: balance and the section grows — A24·10's rule, because a display size that changes per post is not a scale.
Two authors put both names in the one line and drop the bio ⚑: two bios under a 56 px line is a page, not a footer. Three or more reads "More from the Orbit Weekly desk" ⚑ — invented, and the alternative, three names at 56 px, is worse.

At 56 px the heading font's dark weight reads heavier than its light one ⚑ — the same finding A25 recorded at reading scale, here at display scale where it is more visible. No weight compensation is applied: the pack owns the face, and a section that thins its type in dark has stopped being token-driven. Measured dark: name 15.1:1, bio 6.8:1, meta 6.8:1.

POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Bylines carry no number“Rosa Menendez and three others” is now “Rosa Menendez and others”. A template cannot subtract the drawn names from the author count, so the number could only ever have been hard-coded or wrong. The row, the avatars and the type are unchanged.
Avatars with no photographThe avatar fallback draws one letter — “Rosa Menendez” shows R. Every person here comes from Ghost, which cannot produce two initials from a display name, so two initials were never available. The circle, its sizes and its colours are unchanged.
Item counts are a number pickerShare links is a stepper, 2 to 4, default 4 — new in this pass, where the row drew Two · Three · Four — drawing the first N of the site’s enabled destinations in the site’s own order. The + is greyed at four with the reason visible: a fifth link takes the row onto a second line.
“More from” uses the full nameThe display line already read “More from Rosa Menendez” and is unchanged. What changed is the prefix field it shares with the rest of the category: authorLinkLabel’s default is now the full name everywhere, so switching from this design to another does not silently shorten the name.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject in this design — it contains no subscribe and no sign-in form, so there is nothing for a designed notice to replace. The share links are real <a href> URLs and work unscripted; only the copy-link button is hidden.
Member buttons are conditionalNo subject at rest — this design draws no member action. At Blocks Everything the inherited subscribe bar carries the note, and the note is 12 Subscribe’s.
