The featured post's picture is the section: edge to edge, no page margin, no radius, with the eyebrow, title, meta and call to action sitting on it at the lower left over a scrim. This is the loudest thing A19 can do and the design a site reaches for when the featured post is the reason someone came.

Two things follow from putting the picture at the section's edges. There is no Padding control — the section's height is set directly, and it is the only design in the category without one. And there is no arrangement to fall back to when the post has no picture: the design draws Ground rather than reflowing — the picture's box becomes a flat field of the contrast colour and the text does not move — which A19-0 settlement 3 names as one of its three answers.
