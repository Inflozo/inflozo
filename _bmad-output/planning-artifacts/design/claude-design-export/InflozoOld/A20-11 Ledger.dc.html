WHAT THIS PANEL SETTLESThe head sits above the plane and not inside it ⚑, unlike 3 Cards. A plane carrying a section heading, a column head and eight rows has three levels of hierarchy in one box; two is the most this design can hold.Descriptions Off is a real arrangement change: the middle column is removed and the count column moves left to sit 24 px after the longest name, so the table does not become a name at one edge and a number at the other ⚑.No sorting, and no filtering. Both are the Data group's and both apply to every design at once. A per-design sort control is a second answer to a settled question.

DESIGN PATCH PASS · 29 AUGUST 2026 · EVERY CHANGE WITH THE NAME OF THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
Item counts are a number pickerThe Tags block’s Show is a number picker, 1–40, where it drew Four · Six · Eight · Twelve · All. Ghost returns at most 100 tags: “All” tripped Ghost’s own warning and silently returned 100 anyway, so the cap and the truncation are stated in the panel instead of promised away. This design’s own maximum is 40 — beyond forty rows the table wants pagination, which A20 does not have — drawn greyed with the reason visible.
The Remove button never greys outThe hand-picked list’s ✕ stays visible and clickable at one reference and explains why it cannot go lower — “a hand-picked list needs at least one topic”. Never dimmed, never hidden.
No design ever turns into another designNo subject in this design. It draws itself at every count — one topic is drawn as one topic — and nothing in it switches to a different design. The panel may advise at a count; advice is never a switch.
Member buttons · the no-JavaScript noticeNo subject. This design carries no subscribe form, no sign-in form and no paid-tier button — the all-topics link is navigation, not a call to action — so there is nothing to make conditional and nothing for a notice to replace.
NumberingUnchanged. This category is 1–15 and this design is still 11 Ledger. No gap was created or closed, no number reused.
