A3 FOOTERS · DESIGN 16 OF 16 · PAPER PACK

Mini Bar

A slim bar pinned to the bottom of the window while the reader is in the page, which lets go when the page ends and hands over to the real footer beneath it. The only design in A3 with a scroll behaviour, and the only one that is two things: furniture over the content, then a footer under it. It exists for long archives and long reads, where the way out is otherwise a thousand pixels away.

PRIMARY · desktop 1440 · light · pinned, mid-article

For fifteen years the answer to “where do you write?” was the name of a company. The personal page never disappeared; it went quiet, and quiet is not the same as gone.

What changed first was the tooling. Publishing to a domain you own stopped being a weekend project and became an afternoon's work, and the people who had been renting an audience noticed.

Then the feeds turned. Not all at once, and not everywhere, but enough that writers began keeping a copy of their own work somewhere they could still find it in ten years.

The result is not a revival so much as a return to the ordinary: a page, a name, an archive that does not reorder itself overnight.

Back to top is a button, not a link: it scrolls the window and moves focus to the skip-link target, so a keyboard user ends up at the top of the document rather than at the top of the view with focus still in the footer. Under reduced-motion the scroll is instant instead of smooth. Everything else in the bar is a plain link.

1 · The bar is the page's one <footer role="contentinfo"> — pinned and released are the same element, so there is never a second landmark and the DOM order never changes. It is last in the document and last in focus order at both states. 2 · Lockup as A3·1, at 22/16 px. Nothing in the bar is announced when it pins or releases: the bar is present at load, so it is not a live region and a scroll position is not an event worth speaking. 3 · Links are the first one, three or five of the authored list, in order. Which ones is not a control — flagged as mine: the alternative, a separate “bar links” field, would be the seventeenth field in the category for the sake of three links. 4 · Back to top is <button>, name “Back to top”, which scrolls and then moves focus to the document's skip target. Instant under reduced-motion. It is the one control in A3 that does something rather than going somewhere. Covering content · the page reserves the bar's measured height as bottom padding, recalculated on release and on resize, so nothing is ever hidden under the bar — including the last line of a post, a cookie notice from A2·13, and a focused element scrolled into view by the browser. At 200% text zoom the bar's height grows with its type and the reservation follows; if the bar would exceed a quarter of the viewport's height it unpins and stays in flow. Flagged as mine: the quarter-viewport rule. Contrast · text on the 92% translucent ground is measured against the worst case — the page's own background behind it — giving 5.4:1 for muted and 12.1:1 for the wordmark; where backdrop-filter is unsupported the ground is opaque, which can only improve it.

SPEC · A3·16 MINI BAR · full text in “A3 Footers — Spec.md”
Content fields wordmark · mark · linkColumns[] (the bar takes the first one, three or five links in authored order; the released footer uses the field as its own design specifies) · copyright · legalLinks[] · showAttribution · social[] (released footer only) · footerBelow (enum — which A3 design the bar releases into, default A3·1). No field is unique to this design: the bar is a projection of the footer's own content.
Controls · behaviour Reconciled this pass: Padding and Ground rows are retired into the universal Vertical spacing and Background role outside the design’s list; the attribution is “Built with Inflozo” → inflozo.com, disabled-on for Free (Pro may hide); new controls per the updated spec. Bar height: Compact 44 · Comfortable 52 · Spacious 60 (56 + safe area at ≤ 767). Ground: Background · Surface · Contrast, each at 92% with a blur where available and opaque where not. Links in the bar: One · Three · Five · None. Releases: At the page end · Never pins. Back to top: On · Off. Footer below: any A3 design, default Minimal Line.
Behaviour: pinned by default; releases in place when the footer below enters the viewport, losing translucency and shadow; the page reserves the bar's measured height as bottom padding and recalculates it on release, resize and zoom. Unpins entirely if the bar would exceed a quarter of the viewport height. With no JavaScript the bar is simply in flow.
