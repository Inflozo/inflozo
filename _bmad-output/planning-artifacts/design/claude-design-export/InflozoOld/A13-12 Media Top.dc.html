How a pitch becomes a Thursday letter

Three steps, about three weeks from the first email to the send.

  1. 1 Send the pitchTwo paragraphs on what happened, why it matters now, and who you would talk to. No attachments.
  2. 2 Two editors read itEvery pitch is read twice in the Monday meeting. You hear back either way within eight days.
  3. 3 Agree the shape, the length, the deadline and the fee before any reporting startsA commissioning editor writes it down and sends it to you. Nothing is agreed on a call alone.

The picture is object-fit: cover at one named ratio for the whole section, centred, at the pack radius, with no focal point, no per-step crop, no filter and no caption. A crop that cuts a face out is answered with a different upload. Cells are top-aligned and never stretched, so a step with a two-line title sits above a step with a one-line one and the pictures still line up — the pictures are what make the row read as a row, which is why the crop is a section value and not an item one.

This and 9 Big Numbers are the two designs with more than two numeral values, and here the third exists because the picture gives the figure a place it has nowhere else in A13. Crop: Landscape 3:2 · Square 1:1 · Portrait 4:5. EDITING · the P0 primitivesEyebrow, title, sub, note and the link label edit inline on canvas with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the link popover carrying Open in new tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. linkUrl opens the Ghost-aware Link Picker, and the section link takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label, off by default. Per step, title and body edit inline under the picture. image carries Image focus (Centre · Top · Bottom) in the Image Picker popover — the answer to a badly framed upload, where the answer used to be a different upload; the crop stays a section value and there is still no per-step crop. imageAlt ≤ 60 sits beside it. At Source From posts the step title and body are Ghost-owned: the toolbar is replaced by the plain-text lock pill and clicking says “Edit in Ghost”. The numeral is never editable — it is a rendering of position, so there is nothing to click and nothing to type.

Count per row counts planes across the page rather than items and stays a row of named values, the same reading the testimonials category was ruled on. Two to four drawn, five and above wrapped. The Remove button never greys outRemove is visible and clickable at every count and is never dimmed and never hidden. At two steps clicking it says a process needs at least two steps and the row stays, so the one-step state is not reachable from the button. Removing re-numbers and the pictures move with their steps.
Member buttons are conditionalA13 draws no button anywhere. Its one destination is the section link, and where that link points at a member action it now carries the ask: it does not render on a connected site with self-signup switched off or no payment provider connected, and where it opens Ghost own sign-up pop-up, with JavaScript off nothing happens. Scope-tagged apart from Member visibility, which hides the section server-side.
The no-JavaScript noticeNo subject for the notice — this design holds no subscribe field and no sign-in field, so there is nothing for a notice to replace, and no line on this frame ever promised a subscription without script. JavaScript off: identical. The crops are aspect boxes, the marker is absolute positioning inside one, and the plate is a server-side condition.
Ghost templates cannot count, add, or rememberThe numeral is a stylesheet counter, not arithmetic: the ordered list carries a CSS counter and each item renders counter(step, decimal-leading-zero), which is where 01 to 08 comes from — no padding helper, no authored field, no template maths.
