THE SAME MECHANISM AS THE SHARE ROW · ONE OF A KIND, PER ROUTE

The three states are the member-form module’s, not this design’s ⚑: resting, sending, sent. Sent replaces the form in place and keeps the bar’s height where it can, and Ghost’s own confirmation email does the rest — the design never claims the reader is subscribed, only that a link is on its way ⚑.
An error — a malformed address, a network failure — draws under the field in text, never in red ⚑: the packs supply no error token, and inventing one here would put a colour in a section that has one accent and no palette of its own. That is a finding for the architect: a pack needs a negative token before any A-series form can draw a real error state.

POST FOOTERS PATCH · 30 AUGUST 2026 · WHAT CHANGED ON THIS FRAME, AND THE RULE THAT REQUIRED IT
The no-JavaScript noticeThe claim that the form “posts natively to Ghost’s members endpoint” is withdrawn. Tested against both live servers, Ghost’s signup endpoint cannot accept a plain form submission, so the honest unscripted state is not a form at all. A designed notice now replaces it — one catalog sentence in text-muted at 15, in the space the field and the button occupied, the bar keeping its ground, its padding and its words — drawn on this frame at 1440 and at 390. The sent, error and loading states are unchanged and still apply: they are Ghost’s own script, and only the promise was wrong.
Member buttons are conditionalSubscribe and Upgrade do not render when the connected site cannot support them — self-signup switched off, or no payment provider connected for the paid ask — and the editor says which. The Upgrade button opens Ghost’s own pop-up: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. Both lines are on the Member Visibility row in the panel. Member Visibility, the three asks and every drawn state are unchanged.
A design may offer fewer choices, and must say whyBackground role stays locked to Surface with its reason shown — the bar is the design. Bar padding keeps its own name against the universal Vertical spacing. No value was added or renamed, and there is no “Inherit” choice here.
Item counts are a number pickerNo subject — this design counts nothing. It has no repeating unit of any kind, which is what made it the one A26 panel where the shared item controls land nowhere.
