The cards that carry copy sit on the inverted contrast colour at the wide column, carrying the pack’s carried text; the cards that carry photographs stay on the page ground, untouched. WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE · THE FOUR STATES EVERY A33 DESIGN OWES
In the editor every card draws at its resting state ⚑ — the toggle closed, no hover, no ring. The treatment is CSS, so the edit-time picture and the published picture are the same. With JavaScript off fourteen of the twenty cards are identical ⚑. Six depend on scripts that are Ghost’s or the provider’s, not A33’s ⚑: the toggle’s open and close, the gallery’s row ratios, the audio and video players, the signup card’s post, and the embed’s provider markup. The registry has no module for any of them — a finding, not a design, and A33 declares core rather than inventing one. The treatment never restyles what is inside an iframe ⚑. The embed card’s frame, radius and caption are A33’s; the provider’s markup is the provider’s. No control in A33 styles one card of one post ⚑ — a value is written once, site-wide, and every card of that type reads it.

Every state re-derives on the band and none of them uses the accent ⚑. The bookmark’s hover is the carried colour at 8 % over the band, not the hover token; the focus ring is 2 px of the carried colour, not the accent ⚑ — A17·7’s rule, and the reason the ring is drawn twice in this frame. The toggle’s divider is the carried colour at 20 %.

THIS DESIGN’S OWN ACCESSIBILITY NOTES
The band is where contrast is re-checked rather than assumed ⚑. Paper light: carried #FBF9F5 on #232019 is 14.8:1 for body, and the muted derivation at 72 % is 8.2:1. Paper dark: #171511 on #EDE7DA is 14.1:1, muted 7.9:1.The accent is not used on the band at all ⚑ — #D96C3F on #232019 is 3.4:1 and fails for a button fill, which is why the action becomes the carried colour. The control that offers the accent shows the failing ratio and is disabled, never silently allowed.The band is a <div> with no role, like every other plane in A33.A photograph never sits on the band ⚑, so there is no scrim, no filter and no contrast question for image, gallery or embed.

Every colour on the band is derived from two tokens — contrast and carried — and every derivation is stated with its ratio ⚑. That is what makes this treatment survive a pack change, which is the only test that matters here.

Which cards appear
Whatever the author inserted🔒
Read-only. The author writes the post ⚑ — a treatment styles every card Ghost renders and cannot add, remove or reorder one. A33 has no item list.
Card widths
The author’s, per card🔒
Read-only. Regular, wide and full are authored in Ghost, one card at a time ⚑ — the treatment decides what each of the three resolves to, which is the whole of designs 4 and 5.
Bookmark metadata
Scraped by Ghost🔒
Read-only. Title, description, icon, author, publisher and thumbnail are all scraped from the URL ⚑ — C.1’s matrix. A treatment chooses which rows show, never what they say.
Gallery rows
Ghost’s own script🔒
Read-only. Ghost’s card script computes each row’s ratios ⚑ and no registry module covers it. With JavaScript off the images render at equal widths — a finding for the architect, not a degradation A33 can quote.
Corners
From the pack’s radius token🔒
Read-only in every treatment ⚑. C.1’s matrix lists corners as a per-card setting; A33 answers that the pack’s radius token is that value and offers no other — a card that mixed radii would fail §3·1 in eleven of twelve packs.

A post whose only cards are images renders with no band at all ⚑ — the treatment is invisible, and that is correct rather than broken. No cards → nothing renders.9 · Behaviour module. Modules: accordion for the toggle card, core for everything else — the same two in all six treatments, because a treatment is a stylesheet ⚑. Edit-safe: yes; the resting state is the only state and nothing animates, posts or pins while the post is edited. No-JS, quoted: accordion — the registry’s quoted degradation does not hold here ⚑ — Ghost’s toggle is a container with a heading and a button, not native disclosure markup, so with JavaScript off it cannot open. A33 declares accordion because it is the nearest module and records the mismatch as a finding. core — “Never runs; the .js-enabled class is never set, so all JS-conditional CSS stays in its no-JS branch.” The one gap is Ghost’s own gallery row script ⚑ — it computes each row’s ratios, the registry has no module for it, and with JS off the images render at equal widths. That is a finding for the architect, not a degradation A33 may quote.10 · Accessibility.
