WHAT THIS STATE IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT It is drawn, on the site as well as on canvas. A publication whose tag matches nothing shows this, not an absent block — a reader can tell an empty archive from a broken page, and an editor can see the section they placed. It is never back-filled. The Data group's When nothing matches greys its back-fill value here ⚑: a "recent issues" list quietly showing the three newest posts is a lie about what went out. The label and the rule hold; the archive link does not. The frame stays so the block keeps its place in the page's rhythm, and the link greys with its reason at the control. Both lines are fixed strings in the translation catalogue ⚑ — no new content field and no new control. Whether a publication may author them is an open question in the specification, not a guess made here.

This reverses two written rulings and says so: the rows, the label and the rule used to go absent together, and the explanation used to sit in the editor's Data group rather than on the page. The height is one row's — 96 px at 1440, the block a left-aligned pair of lines inside the content box — so a page that had three issues and now has none does not jump. At 834 and 390 the same two lines hold at the listed row's own height.
