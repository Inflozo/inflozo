A26 POST FOOTERS · DESIGN 6 OF 15 · PAPER PACK · FIVE CONTROLS + THE UNIVERSAL TRIO

Slim

One line under the article: the tags as a dotted run on the left, the share links on the right, on a hairline, at a 44 px minimum height — and a second line for the two destinations when the design is asked for them.

It is the footer for a site that publishes every day, where a bio under every post is a bio the reader has already read. There is no author block at any setting ⚑, which is what makes it a bar rather than a short stack — the byline is A24's and this design does not repeat it.

Two lines and three hairlines is the whole design ⚑. Each line is 44 px minimum — the target floor, used here as a layout unit — and the tags are one text run joined by middle dots, not a row of pills ⚑: a pill is a shape, and this design has none.
The destinations truncate rather than wrap ⚑: previous clamps to one line on the left, next ellipses on the right at 44% of the measure. A 90-character title becomes "The night shift at the Port of…" and the line stays a line, which is the point of the design.
No author, no avatar, no picture, no button. The accent appears once, in A6's focus ring ⚑ — there is no action here to spend it on.

NOT AN EMPTY LEFT HALF AND A RIGHT-ALIGNED ROW ⚑

With no tags the share links move to the left edge ⚑ rather than staying right against an empty half — the line is a line of content, not a two-column grid. With no share links the tags keep the left and the line is one run of text. With neither, nothing renders — no bar, no rule and no reserved height.
Rules None is offered for a site whose article already ends on a rule — 11 Ruled's box, or a divider card as the author's last block ⚑.
