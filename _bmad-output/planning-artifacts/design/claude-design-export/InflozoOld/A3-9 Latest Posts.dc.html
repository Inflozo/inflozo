1 · “Latest” is a real <h2> and the posts are a <ul> inside a section labelled by it. Unlike the link columns this is not a <nav> — it is content, not navigation, and marking a feed as navigation makes a screen reader's landmark list useless. 2 · The image is alt="", always, because the title beside it is the link's name; Ghost's own alt text is used when the same image appears in the post body, not here. loading="lazy" and explicit dimensions, so a footer of images never shifts the page. 3 · One link per post wrapping image, title and meta; its accessible name is the title alone. Titles are clipped at two lines visually with the full title in the DOM, so nothing is lost to a screen reader. 4 · The date is a <time datetime>; “18 Aug” is the visible form and the full date is in the attribute. The tag is plain text here, not a second link — nested links inside a card link are a trap, and the tag has its own place in the link columns. Motion · the 1.02 hover scale and the 4% darkening are both suppressed under reduced-motion; the underline stays, so the affordance does not depend on motion. Contrast · titles 12.1:1 light, 11.8:1 dark; meta 5.4:1 / 6.4:1; the no-image panel's title on hover surface 11.4:1. Focus ring wraps the whole card at 2 px accent, 4.85:1 against background — above the 3:1 floor for a non-text indicator.

Content fields · data postsHeading (text, opt, ≤ 20, default “Latest”) · postsSource (enum) · postsCount (2–4) · plus wordmark, mark, tagline, linkColumns[] (one group drawn; further groups are kept and not drawn), social[], copyright, legalLinks[], showAttribution.
Data: title, feature image, primary tag and published date come from Ghost. Titles are clipped at two lines, never edited. Drafts, scheduled posts and pages are excluded. Members-only posts appear with no lock or badge — the footer is not the place to sell, and the post's own page handles the paywall.
Controls · empty states Reconciled this pass: Padding and Ground rows are retired into the universal Vertical spacing and Background role outside the design’s list; the attribution is “Built with Inflozo” → inflozo.com, disabled-on for Free (Pro may hide); new controls per the updated spec. Padding: Compact 36 · Comfortable 52 · Spacious 72. Ground: Background · Surface · Contrast. How many posts: Two · Three · Four. Post style: With image · Title and date · Title only. Which posts: Latest published · Latest in a tag · Featured only · A collection. Links: One column · Row under · Off.
No feature image on a post → a hover-surface panel of the same height carrying the title at 15 px, clipped at three lines. Fewer than two published posts → the block is removed and the design renders as A3·2 Columns with the tagline in the posts' place; the sidebar states it. A tag or collection with fewer posts than the count → it renders what exists, down to two, then falls back as above.
Responsive · a11y · flagged 1440–1081: posts on the measure, one link column at a fixed 260 px right. 1080–768: links leave the column and become a wrapping row under the posts with the lockup beside them; posts hold three across, title 17 → 16 px. ≤ 767: posts become a divided list with a 72 × 54 thumbnail at the left, rows 78 px, whole row the link; links go two-across.
a11y: the block is a section labelled by a real <h2>, not a <nav>; images alt="" with explicit dimensions and lazy loading; one link per post named by its title; date in <time>; tag is plain text, never a nested link; hover motion suppressed under reduced-motion.
Flagged as mine: the two-post floor and its fall-back, the no-image panel and its duplicated title, the 3:2 crop, the 72 × 54 mobile thumbnail, the 1.02 hover scale, the exclusion of a members-only badge, and moving attribution into the shared group to keep six controls.
