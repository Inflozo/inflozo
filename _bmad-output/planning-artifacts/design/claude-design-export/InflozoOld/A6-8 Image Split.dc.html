The picture's crop is derived from the text half's height rather than fixed. A5·7 fixes its rows at 3:2 because it draws two to four of them and they have to agree with each other; this design draws one, so the picture is as tall as the words beside it and its aspect is whatever that makes — about 3:2 at Comfortable with a two-line title, wider at Compact, squarer at Spacious. The floor is 320 px, below which the picture is dropped and the text half stands alone at its 720 px measure, with the reason named, because a 220 px strip of photograph beside a paragraph is a texture rather than a picture.
The text half is 720 and the picture takes the rest — 720 px is 2 Flush Left's Wide measure plus its padding, so the words occupy the same space they would in the design next door. The gutter is the text's own 76 px right padding rather than a gap: at Flush to the edge the picture runs to the window, and a gap between text and picture would leave the section with two different right margins.
This is the design where imageAlt earns its keep. 7 Full Bleed Image draws its picture as a background and keeps the field unused; here the picture is an <img> with an alt a reader may actually need, and the editor asks what the picture adds rather than defaulting it empty.

Both values are already in the library and neither is re-decided here. Framed inset is A4·3's mount, which A5·7 also carried: 14 px of surface, one border hairline, the picture at half the pack radius. At this scale the mount stops at the page margin rather than the window, so Framed inset gives the section a symmetrical margin and Flush to the edge does not — which is the whole choice: a picture that is part of the page, or a picture that is part of the window. The mount costs 28 px of picture width and the section keeps its height.

MEMBER ASK AND NO JAVASCRIPT · PATCH PASS · 29 AUG 2026 The ask is conditional. Where the primary is a sign-up ask or a paid-tier ask it does not render when the connected site cannot support it — self-signup switched off in Ghost, or no payment provider connected — and the sidebar names the setting rather than shipping a button that fails. An ordinary link is unaffected. Ruled by the owner, 29 August 2026: the button keeps its place and its scale, and the site chooses what it says and where it goes — a fallback label and link (fallbackLabel ≤ 24 · fallbackUrl, in the panel beside the ask), so a reader meets a working button rather than a gap. Left empty, this design’s own rule applies: no primary label or URL, no section. Where the ask opens Ghost's own sign-up pop-up: with JavaScript off, nothing happens. The link is a real href to a Portal address, so the page stays where it is and no window opens. No JavaScript: this design draws no form, so there is nothing to replace — every word and both actions are rendered by the server, and the banner is the banner.
