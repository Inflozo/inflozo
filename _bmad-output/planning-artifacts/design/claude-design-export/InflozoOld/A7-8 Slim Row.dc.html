Slim Row

The tier names and prices on one line, no benefits, and one action for the whole section rather than one per tier. A7 without its list — for a page that has already made the argument somewhere above and needs the numbers stated once. It is the only design in the category whose action is not per tier, and the only one that draws no benefits at any value.

One line, three prices, one action. The names are 20 px and the prices 34 — Medium on the price ladder, one step below 1 Cards' 48, because three prices on a shared baseline are read against each other rather than one at a time. Tiers are 48 px apart, the name and its price 10 px apart on a shared baseline, and the block sits against the page's left margin with the action at the right.
The action is the section's, not a tier's — “See all plans”, A1·1's button at 14 px/600 and 9×17, the same step down A6·10 Slim made and for the same reason: a 44 px button in 44 px of padding is a button with a strip around it. It links to the site's pricing page rather than to Portal, because a single action cannot choose a tier and a section that guessed would be choosing for the reader. That is this design's whole trade: it states the prices and sends a reader somewhere that can take payment. Where a site has one paid tier the action is that tier's Portal link and its label falls back to payLabel, since there is nothing to choose between.
The rules are what make it a section rather than a stray row — A6·10's argument, carried — and the padding scale is that design's too: Compact 32 · Comfortable 44 · Spacious 56, measured inside the rules.

The section has no <h2>: with no title there is nothing to label it with, so it is a plain <section> without aria-labelledby rather than one labelled by invented text. EDITING · the P0 primitivesNote and the section action's label edit inline with the P0·1 toolbar — bold · italic · underline · link, the popover carrying open-in-new-tab and rel nofollow · noreferrer · sponsored. Tier names and prices are Ghost’s: clicking one says “Edit in Ghost”. The action picks its target in the Link Picker — your pricing page, or the picker's Portal · Sign up at one paid tier — and takes an optional P0·2 icon before or after its label. The cadence words are catalog strings.
DATA · from Ghost · outside the design's listTiers come from {{#get "tiers"}} filtered type:paid+visibility:public in Ghost's own price order; the free tier is its own type:free object. A Ghost list: no Add, no Remove, no reorder. Every paid action and the cadence switch gate on @site.paid_members_enabled — Ghost seeds an active $5 paid tier at install, so a tier existing never means it can be bought. Members off in Ghost → the section does not render. Every paid action renders only where a payment provider is connected — no Stripe, or self-signup switched off, and the action is absent rather than dead. Portal is JavaScript: with JavaScript off these links do nothing, so the section reads but cannot be subscribed from. No JavaScript, elsewhere in the section: both of a tier's prices render side by side, each labelled in words, with no switch — and no saving line, which is the switch's own arithmetic. This design reads no benefits and no descriptions.
