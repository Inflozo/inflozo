Share Row

The header with the sharing links in it, as words rather than as coloured glyphs: a hairline under the byline, a label, and two to four destinations at 15/600. It declares share, and it is the only design in A24 that draws a control a reader can press.

Its case for existing is one decision: a share row above the article is a different thing from one below it. Below, in A26 Post Footers, it asks a reader who has finished. Above, it tells a reader who has not started that the piece is worth sending on — which is a claim about the piece, and belongs to the header that makes it. Everything else here is 2 Flush Left.
