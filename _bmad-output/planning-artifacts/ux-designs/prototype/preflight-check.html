Two rows the frame does not have, and both are required

FR-Q6 requires the RTL acknowledgement repeated here as a pre-deploy warning — it is where it bites. And FR-J16’s drift row sits among these checks on every deploy after the first: a passing row when nothing changed, and a stop when something did. Neither is drawn (prompt A3).

“Required templates present (index, post, page)” — two of those are Ghost’s

gscan requires index.hbs and post.hbs (GS020-INDEX-REQ, GS020-POST-REQ) and recommends default.hbs (GS020-DEF-REC); no rule requires page.hbs — read in gscan 4.49.7 and 6.4.2 lib/specs, the versions Ghost 5.130.6 and 6.58.0 bundle (VERIFY-AT-BUILD item 31). page is Inflozo’s own standard-set check (FR-I1), not Ghost’s. If the row is meant to read as Ghost’s check, prompt A7 drops “page”.
