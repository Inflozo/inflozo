Backup Gate

Step 3, and it blocks the deploy button until confirmed. Reading order is the design: first the modest truth — Inflozo writes exactly two things to your Ghost site: your theme, and routes.yaml — then the recommendation with its reason, then the ghost backup shortcut whose tick checks every row below and the rows stay visible, then seven individual rows (Theme · routes.yaml · Content, which does NOT include images · Members · Redirects · Images, the gap most people miss · Database), and last the master confirm, disabled until every row is covered. No frame anywhere in the export mentions a backup, ghost backup, or a consent checklist.

Nothing is drawn here on purpose. A picture invented in this file would be a second interface vocabulary beside the export’s, which is the one thing R-74 forbids. What the prompt must produce is described below and in EXPERIENCE.md Appendix A.

On Ghost(Pro), the shortcut does not exist and the gate says so

This claim is cited, not executed — there is no Ghost(Pro) test server yet — so the copy says what Ghost Admin does and does not offer and points the customer at Ghost, rather than asserting a platform-wide impossibility in Inflozo’s own voice. It closes: “This is not something Inflozo can fix, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.”
