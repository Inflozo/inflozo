Three things the drawer carries that S8e did not — drawn as D2a

1 · Pinning. A pin control on every row; a pinned version survives pruning; at most N−1 pinned — 9 of 10 on Pro, 2 of 3 on Free — and pinning the last unpinned version refuses, visibly, with the reason (D2b, below). Unpinning is always allowed, so nobody can trap themselves. 2 · The original-theme row, above the version list, marked as the site’s original rather than an Inflozo build and outside the 3-or-10 count entirely — Restore original is where flow F1 ends. 3 · A partial-success row — status uploaded, no active badge, and Re-activate in place of Roll back — drawn as D2d on Partial Success.
