The retention limit is therefore the true bound on how far back a customer can go, and the surface says so.

The pin refusal

At least one version must stay unpinned, enforced in the database, so deploying can never be blocked by pinning. The cap is N−1: 9 of 10 on Pro, 2 of 3 on Free. Unpinning is always allowed, by design, so nobody can trap themselves.
