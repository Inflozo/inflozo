Three things the drawer has to carry that S8e does not

1 · Pinning. A pin control on every row; a pinned version survives pruning; at most N−1 pinned — 9 of 10 on Pro, 2 of 3 on Free — and pinning the last unpinned version refuses, visibly, with the reason. Unpinning is always allowed, so nobody can trap themselves. 2 · The pre-Inflozo snapshot row, above the version list, marked as the site’s original rather than an Inflozo build, and outside the 3-or-10 count entirely. 3 · A partial-success row — status uploaded, no active badge, and Re-activate in place of Roll back. All three are prompt A2.

And one sentence S8e already gets right, which stays

“Pro keeps the last 10 versions. Free keeps the last 3.” A list that silently drops its oldest entry reads as complete when it is not, and the line stays on Free too rather than being hidden as an upsell. It gains one sentence: these artifacts are not regenerable, so the retention limit is the true bound on how far back a customer can go.
