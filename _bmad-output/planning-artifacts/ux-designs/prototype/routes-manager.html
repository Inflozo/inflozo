    template: post-grid

    permalink: /notes/{slug}/
    template: minimal-list
    filter: tag:notes

    filter: tag:reading

  tag: /topic/{slug}/

Filename collisions and renames are refused at the naming step

A custom-{name}.hbs filename is permanent from its first deploy: Ghost stores the exact string on every page that selects it and never revalidates it. So the rename control is absent rather than present-and-refusing, and the permanence is said once, plainly, at the moment of choosing.
