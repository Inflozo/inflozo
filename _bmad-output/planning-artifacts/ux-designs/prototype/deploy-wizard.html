The rail grows, and that is the whole shape of this flow

An ordinary deploy is four steps, which is what S8 draws. The first deploy to a given site is six, because two gates fire once per site and never again — the Staff Token Offer and the Backup Gate — and neither is drawn anywhere in the export (prompt A1).

What fires before step 1 even opens

Two sheets sit in front of the wizard rather than inside it. The Pro Exit Sheet fires on a Free plan with Pro designs in the site — the only moment money is mentioned. The Library Update Confirm fires whenever the library has advanced since this project’s last deploy, because a redeploy carries those changes whether or not anyone asked.
