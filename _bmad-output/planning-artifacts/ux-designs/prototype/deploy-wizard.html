What fires before step 1 even opens

Two sheets sit in front of the wizard rather than inside it, and both are once-only in their own way. The Pro Exit Sheet fires on a Free plan with Pro designs in the site — the only moment money is mentioned. The Library Update Confirm fires whenever the library has advanced since this project’s last deploy, because a redeploy carries those changes whether or not anyone asked.
