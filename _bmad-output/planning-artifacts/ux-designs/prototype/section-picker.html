What the picker never offers, and what it refuses

A design whose bindingContext does not match this template is never shown — not greyed, not shown-and-refused (FR-D12). A second Post Content section is refused at placement, with the reason (ruling R-37). And a search with no matches keeps the category rail and says what was searched for.

Not drawnThis surface has no frame in the export. No prompt owns them; they are states of a drawn surface will draw it; it has not been run.

Nothing is drawn here on purpose. A picture invented in this file would be a second interface vocabulary beside the export’s, which is the one thing R-74 forbids. What the prompt must produce is described below and in EXPERIENCE.md Appendix A.
