Style-Guide Fixture

The canvas never shows a real post by default — it shows this. One of every card type, a heading profile that descends three levels, lists, code, a table and a pull-quote, arranged as a piece anyone would read on purpose. It is the most-seen page in the product, so it is written rather than assembled. Swap to a real post from the content-source pill at any time. The paywall is deliberately not in the fixture — a cut mid-article would stop it being readable end to end, and it has its own canvas.
