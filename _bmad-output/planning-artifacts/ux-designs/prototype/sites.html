Sites with none, and disconnecting

Sites with no connection shows an empty screen in S3b’s shape — a drawing, “One handshake and you’re in.” and a centred Connect site that opens S11b. It read “the connect card as the whole page” until the owner’s test of Story 3.2 amended it (finding 7, 2026-09-08; EXPERIENCE.md § State Patterns → Sites). The full-page handshake did not go — it is where Connect site leads with JavaScript off. Disconnecting keeps the site’s pre-Inflozo snapshot — it is bound to the site record, not the URL, so it survives disconnect, reconnect and project deletion, and the backup gate re-fires on reconnect because consent is per site.
