One word on it is wrong

B15 says upgrade to Ghost(Pro) Creator. Ghost’s 2026 lineup is Starter / Publisher / Business, so it is Publisher or higher. Everything else on this frame is right, and S8a′ and S11a move to match it rather than the other way round.

Probed, never asked

At connect, hostSettings.limits.customThemes reports whether the site permits custom theme upload; its absence means self-hosted and unlimited. There is no user-declared-plan step in the happy path and no plan field in Manage Keys. FR-C5’s daily health check re-runs the probe, so the flag sets and clears without any user action — clearing is not the user’s job — and a successful deploy clears it too. The one path with a question is the unreadable-hostSettings case.
