Mobile — a phone-shaped viewport, not a narrow column

The canvas is a viewport, and switching device resizes it in both axes to a real device size — 390 × 844, not a 390-wide column of infinite height. That is why the sticky header sticks, the full-screen hero fills, and the fold is where the fold actually is.

The control the frame drew and this product does not have

B11 draws a user-operated Fit / 55% zoom picker. FR-D14 says there is no zoom in v1, and AD-21 says the only scale is fit-to-screen and it is never user-driven. Keep the chip, remove the control.

kept VIEWPORT 390 × 844 · SHOWN AT 55% It reports the true size and the automatic scale. A scaled canvas with no chip is a lie about size.
deleted Fit55%100% Browser zoom is the reader’s own accessibility setting and Inflozo neither owns it nor may defeat it. That is a different thing from canvas scale, and confusing the two is how a product ends up fighting WCAG 1.4.4.
