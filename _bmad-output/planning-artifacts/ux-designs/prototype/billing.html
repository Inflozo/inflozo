The limits, plainly — and where the frames disagree with them

Appendix F.1 is the sole definition of Free/Pro gating, and four frames disagree with it: S11c says Pro connects up to 3; S12a says unlimited projects and full history; S12b and M5 say 1 / 3 sites and Last 2 / Full history; S10b says uploads may be up to 30 MB. F.1 governs every one of them. These are product limits, so they are shown rather than implied.

Cancelling

No refunds and no mid-cycle cancellation (Appendix F.2). Auto-renew stops, access continues to the end of the paid period, then the account becomes Free — which is where the Over-Limit Sheet takes over. Cancellation always completes in three clicks or fewer and there are no retention dark patterns.
