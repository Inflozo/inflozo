Notes. Read the three panels top to bottom and only two rows repeat: the section name with its counter, and the design strip. Everything below the hairline is the design's own. Inline Toolbar

Exactly the four marks plus Remove link, which renders always and disables when the selection carries no link. A mark a field does not permit is absent, not greyed. ⌥F10 moves focus into it and Esc restores the exact selection.

TOOLBAR OVER BODY TEXT · CANVAS CROP 960 · SELECTION IN THE PACK ACCENT AT 25% · TOOLBAR CENTRED OVER THE SELECTION, 8 PX ABOVE
Why writers are leaving the feed

The web used to point outward. A blogroll was a list of other people's homes, kept in public, updated by hand — and reading one felt like being handed a map by someone who had walked the ground.

B I U
FIVE ACTIONS, NOTHING ELSE ⚑ · REMOVE LINK IS DISABLED AT 35% WHEN THE SELECTION CARRIES NO LINK · ⌘B ⌘I ⌘U ⌘K WORK WITHOUT THE TOOLBAR

The toolbar is editor chrome, not canvas content — white surface, editor border, 10 px radius, md shadow, five 30 px targets. It centres over the selection, 8 px above; when the selection starts in the top 48 px of the canvas it flips below. B, I and U are letterform glyphs in the canvas serif so they read as text marks; Link and Remove link are stroke icons behind a hairline divider. Remove link renders always and disables when the selection has no link — a five-slot toolbar that never changes width is the whole point of a fixed vocabulary.
