Small Screen Notice

A calm centred card at 390 — not an error page. “The editor needs a bigger screen.” Then what does work from a phone: the project’s deploy history with a one-tap roll back, the sites list, and billing. It fires on a coarse pointer at a small viewport, never on width alone — a 1440px display at 200% browser zoom presents roughly a 720px CSS viewport, and throwing a low-vision user out of the editor for zooming is a straight WCAG 1.4.4 failure. That case gets the editor, reflowed (prompt A8, frame D8b).
