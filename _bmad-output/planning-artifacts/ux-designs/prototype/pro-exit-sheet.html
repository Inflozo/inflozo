B.2 · #13 · PRO-CONTENT BLOCKING SHEET

The bill arrives at deploy, not at the canvas

A free user builds with the whole library and nothing stops them. The reckoning happens once, here, when they try to ship — and because it is the only moment money is mentioned, it has to be itemised, calm and genuinely two-way. Every Pro design in use is listed with the section it sits in, a Free design that would replace it, and the option to drop it instead.

Notes. The itemisation is the design. Every row names the Pro design, where it sits, and what would replace it — never a count and a paywall. Placed sections offer three remedies, since a section with no Free equivalent still has to be shippable: swap, remove, or go Pro. Treatments that are chosen rather than placed sit in their own group and revert to the Free one instead. The third row shows a completed swap in place with an Undo, so the sheet visibly does what it promises before the user commits to anything. Both footer actions are real verbs and both ship: Swap and ship free is a secondary button rather than a link, because a genuine second path presented as a link reads as a refusal. Marigold appears only as the Pro mark, and coral appears nowhere on this sheet — this is a money conversation, not a moment of delight.

Four remedies, because two was not enough

The frame drew swap and upgrade. FR-L3 has four, and the missing two are not decorative: “Remove it”, because some binding contexts have no Free design to swap to; and “Revert to the free one” for the non-placeable treatments — a paywall design, a card treatment, a pagination style — which are selected rather than placed, so each reverts on its own surface. Corrected in the 2026-09-04 export (A7 item 4): every row now carries Remove it, and a “Chosen, not placed” row type carries Revert to the free one.

And a data dependency the frame invented

B13a noted that “the swap-for suggestions are per-design pairings someone has to author — every Pro design needs a named Free fallback”. No pairing table exists or is needed: swapping goes through Shuffle, which offers any free design in that category’s ring. The note is deleted from the frame in the 2026-09-04 export (A7 item 4); the canvas’s own header still lists it among the things it flagged, which is a record and stays.
