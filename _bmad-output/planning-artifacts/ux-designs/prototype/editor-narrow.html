The same four parts, rearranged rather than redesigned

The export drew the editor at 1440 and at no other width until prompt A8 ran (2026-09-04). Two things need it narrower and neither is a phone: tablet (834, touch) is in scope by the owner’s ruling, and a desktop at 200% browser zoom sees roughly a 720 CSS px viewport — throwing that user out would fail WCAG 1.4.4. Layers collapses to an icon rail, the Controls sidebar becomes an overlay over the canvas with a scrim, and the top bar keeps the switcher, View as and Ship it and sends the rest to one overflow menu. Below that the product shows the Small Screen Notice instead — on a coarse pointer, never on width.

The skip link

The first focusable thing in the shell, over the top bar’s left edge: hidden at rest, visible on keyboard focus, reading “Skip the canvas” — because the canvas renders the user’s own site, and tabbing through it means every link that site emits. The ring is the Calibration Set’s corrected token, 2px solid #C2381F (A7 item 7).

Where focus opens on a destructive confirm — the rule

As any irreversible confirm opens, focus is on the cancelling action, never on the danger fill. The subject is B5c’s “Take over from Rosa?”; the same rule governs Delete account, Roll back, project delete, delete-in-use assets and “Overwrite and ship anyway”. D8f inherits B5c’s wording — “7 unsynced changes” — where §AD2 makes edits canonical (the one deviation on Edit Lock); D8g already says edits.
