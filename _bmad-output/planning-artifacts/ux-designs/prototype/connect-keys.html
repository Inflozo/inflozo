What validation decides, without asking

J1 step 5. A server-side GET /admin/config/ runs the moment Connect is pressed: Ghost 5.x and 6.x accepted and 4.x refused with “please update Ghost”; a missing hostSettings key marks the site self-hosted (executed on 5.130.6 and 6.58.0, MEASUREMENTS §15h) and hostSettings.limits.customThemes — a theme-name allowlist, whose Ghost(Pro) shape is still ⛔ VERIFY-AT-BUILD item 2 — decides Preview-only without asking; http:// is warned; code injection raises a one-time notice that the live page can legitimately differ from the canvas; Portal’s floating-button state is read, or asked once, defaulting to on.
