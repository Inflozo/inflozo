## Overall verdict

The v2.3 failure mode was **false external facts** — claims about Ghost that source did not support. v3.0 fixed those: the membership-template fossil is correctly demolished, the `tokenPermissionCheck` allowlist is quoted verbatim, the seeded `$5/mo` Default Product is exact, the `page-{slug}.hbs` precedence trap is caught, and the empirical 0-error/0-warning gscan gate reproduces byte-for-byte on a *newer* gscan (6.4.2) than the one it was written against. Forty-seven distinct Ghost claims were independently verified against `TryGhost/Ghost` @ 6.58.0-rc.0 and hold. The arithmetic reconciles on 53 of 58 recomputed rows, including every count that governs scope. This is a genuinely different document from v2.3.

The new failure concentrates in three places, and they are structural rather than factual.

**(a) Claims with no gate behind them.** FR-J1 hangs the product on "hand-editable and production grade, at Casper/Source quality," and the entire enforcement stack behind that sentence is gscan's 0/0. A reviewer built a theme with no `lang` attribute, no viewport meta, no `alt` text, an inline `onclick`, `<h4>`→`<h1>`→`<h6>` heading order, 1.5:1 body contrast, no pagination, no `error.hbs`, no `tag.hbs`, no `author.hbs` and no locales — and after adding two CSS lines and one `{{#if @page.show_title_and_feature_image}}` it scored **0 errors / 0 warnings on both the v5 and v6 specs**, out-scoring Casper and Source on the v5 rule count. The same pattern repeats across the document: §1.2's "extremely easy, genuinely fun" is measured by G1/G2, which no epic owns and §4's DoD omits; §7.6's fourteen verify-at-build items carry the instruction "re-confirm each before the dependent epic starts" and no epic in §8 owns doing it; FR-G5's uniqueness bar is a "CI assertion over the inventory" against free English prose; NFR-6(c3) is the only gate that ever compares the canvas to a real Ghost page, and it has one sentence of specification and — per the facilitator's independent check — **cannot pass on any post template**, because NFR-6(c) inherits (a)'s 1%-pixel threshold while NFR-3 mandates dummy placeholder content for every post body. The verification budget was spent on the axis the architecture had already closed.

**(b) The same root cause recurring: a fix propagating only to its home FR.** This produced ~25 of the 135 v2.3 findings and it recurred in v3.0 in at least four distinct places. The `.memlog.md` final-verification entry is the tell — it certifies "Zero stale counts (487/104/132/8766/459) **anywhere in prd.md**," and that scoping *is* the defect. Two normative companions still say 487 (`appendix-h1-string-catalog.md:287`, `appendix-b1-template-contexts.md:267` — CONFIRMED; the occurrences in `reconcile-*.md` are historical artifacts, not normative, and are excluded). The `data-portal="upgrade"` correction landed in FR-F6, Appendix B and A30 and never reached `sections-inventory.md:421` (A22) or `:588` (A32), so `sections-inventory.md` — the file the E9–E11 category stories are authored from — now contradicts itself: `upgrade` is offered as a control value in two places while `:557` states "`upgrade` is not a Portal action" (CONFIRMED). The C10 canvas-reads-no-body-HTML decision reached NFR-3, §7.3 and FR-H4 and left FR-D16 in its pre-C10 form. The tier-purchasability rule reached A30 and A32 and not A7.

**(c) A new pattern, and the most dangerous one: prior reviewers' unverified assertions entering the PRD with the authority of research.** FR-I2 states as settled platform fact that "NQL has no relative-date syntax, so 'within the last 30 days' cannot compile into a static `routes.yaml` at all." Run directly against `@tryghost/nql` 0.13.4, `published_at:>now-30d` parses to `{"published_at":{"$gt":"2026-07-19 10:02:12"}}`, and the routes.yaml `filter` string is re-parsed per request, so it does not freeze. The claim never came from Ghost: it originated as an unverified assertion in `review-st-buildability.md:222`, was escalated to that review's finding H1 at `:370`, carried into `validation-report.md:194-195`, and was then adopted into normative PRD text — where its confident *reason* ("cannot compile … at all") will stop anyone downstream from re-examining it. A working, frequently-wanted feature was deleted on a review artifact's say-so. The same shape appears in FR-I4's "there is no automated upload path, on any Ghost version or host," which is false in a way the PRD itself disproves two requirements away.

**The citation-backed research companions were consistently more accurate than the PRD body.** Spot-checks of `research-ghost-membership-pages.md` and `research-ghost-binding-contexts.md` match current source exactly. In at least one case — the per-collection `limit:` key — the PRD body asserts the *exact opposite* of what its own normative companion (`appendix-b1-template-contexts.md:66`, `:269`) and its own research file (`research-ghost-binding-contexts.md:201`, with the correct source citation) both say. **The regression risk is in the distillation step, not in the research.** Any remediation pass that re-reads only `prd.md` will miss this class entirely.

Three further things the owner should hold alongside the finding list. First, the section library is **not yet buildable as a whole** — roughly 300 of 485 variants are authorable today; the rest sit behind six gaps, and the root cause is not that one-line variant descriptors are too thin (they are mostly exactly right) but that the **34 category headers were written as the category's centre of gravity rather than as the union of what its variants need**. That is an afternoon of editing 34 lines, not 485 specs. Second, the WYSIWYG promise is defensible as *"the canvas renders exactly the markup Ghost will render, styled by exactly the CSS Inflozo ships"* — a real, differentiating claim — and is not defensible as *"the canvas is the page"*: four of the four things that actually make a Ghost page look different from Inflozo's canvas are downstream of markup, and three of the four are not mentioned anywhere in the document. Third, the business model in Appendix F prices infrastructure and omits authorship: 485 hand-authored variants, a *perpetual* monthly-drop retention commitment, 8,730 screenshot baselines needing a first human approval pass, and ten designed starter projects appear in no cost line. "Break-even at 9–10 Pro subscribers" is a statement about hosting presented in G7 as a statement about the business.

## Findings by severity

### Critical (23)

**[Ghost truth · WYSIWYG]** — FR-Q6's "exactly one locale file" ships a site rendering raw translation keys (FR-Q6, Appendix B, `§7.4` tree)
*Found independently by 2 reviewers.* Ghost's `{{t}}` resolution chain is `locales/{SITE_LOCALE}.json` → `locales/en.json` → `{}`, with no theme-declared locale and the key echoed verbatim as the fallback (`ThemeI18n` forces `_stringMode = 'fulltext'`). A French project ships only `locales/fr.json`; the connected site's `locale` is Ghost's default `en`; the loader ENOENTs, `locale === 'en'` so no fallback branch runs, and every `{{t}}` falls back to its key. Because FR-Q6 mandates dotted keys, the visitor sees `pagination.older_posts`, `error.page_not_found`, `card.read_more` across the entire site. gscan's translation check is 38 lines of `JSON.parse` — **a theme with zero locale files passes** — so FR-J6's 0/0 gate is green on it. The stated rationale is inverted: `en.json` is not a competing file, it is the fallback Ghost is hard-coded to look for. Ghost's own docs advise readable-English keys for exactly this reason.
Fix: always emit `locales/en.json` with the full English-default catalog; emit `locales/{lang}.json` *in addition* when the project language is not English; reword FR-Q6 to state the real resolution chain; add a pre-deploy warning when project language ≠ the connected site's `locale` setting (a `GET /settings/` read the product already makes), and add `@site.locale` drift to FR-C5's daily health check.

**[Ghost truth]** — FR-I2: "NQL has no relative-date syntax" is false; a real capability was deleted on a false premise (FR-I2)
`@tryghost/nql` 0.13.4 run directly: `published_at:>now-30d` → `{"published_at":{"$gt":"2026-07-19 10:02:12"}}`; `created_at:<now-1y` parses; they compose with `+`/`,`/parens. And it does not freeze — `collection.js` passes `res.routerOptions` (carrying `filter`) into `dataService.fetchData` on **every request**, so `now-30d` resolves relative to the request. Provenance: the claim originated at `review-st-buildability.md:222`, escalated to that review's H1 at `:370`, carried into `validation-report.md:194-195`, and was adopted into FR-I2 as settled platform fact. It was never checked against NQL.
Fix: restore Published date to the filter builder — `within the last N days/weeks/months` → `published_at:>now-{N}d|w|m`, plus `before/after {absolute date}`. Keep the "a fixed date silently freezes" caution attached to the *absolute* option only, where it is true. Add both forms to FR-I2's normative field→NQL mapping table.

**[Ghost truth]** — FR-I4: "no automated routes.yaml upload path, on any Ghost version or host" is false (FR-I4, FR-I5, §7.6 item 9)
Two errors, one load-bearing. (a) The route is `POST /settings/routes/yaml` (multipart, field `routes`), not `PUT` — there is no `PUT` on that path in Ghost 6. (b) `tokenPermissionCheck` (`admin/middleware.js:17-42`) applies the integration allowlist only to tokens **without** a `user_id`; a staff token hits a three-entry blocklist (delete-all-content, transfer-ownership, reset-authentication) and otherwise falls straight through to the permission system, where fixtures grant `Administrator: setting = all`. FR-C1 already mandates the Owner's Staff Access Token as required, not optional, and FR-J13/FR-J16 rely on the identical bypass to reach `GET /themes/*`. **The PRD proves the mechanism in one requirement and denies it in another.** This also settles §7.6 item 9 definitively, for both `themes` and `settings`.
Fix: correct the method; rewrite FR-I4 so automated upload via the Owner's Staff Access Token is the primary path and the guided Labs card is the designed fallback for token-absent/revoked/non-Owner cases; keep FR-I4's byte-for-byte `GET /settings/routes/yaml` verification verbatim as the confirmation step for the automated path; close §7.6 item 9 with the source citation; re-examine FR-I5's product claim, which currently trades on the manual step existing.

**[Theme quality · Ghost truth]** — FR-Q5's "always compile three built-ins" collides with GS100 and blocks the deploy (FR-Q5, FR-Q2, FR-J6)
*Found independently by 2 reviewers, both reproducing it empirically as an **error**.* `GS100-NO-UNUSED-CUSTOM-THEME-SETTING` fires per declared-but-unreferenced key at `level: 'error'`; FR-J6 blocks deploy on errors. Three ordinary failure modes: a Light+Dark project whose header uses a text wordmark never emits `{{@custom.dark_logo}}`; `color_scheme` under FR-E4's "Auto follows the visitor's system preference in pure CSS" need never appear in `.hbs` at all (Casper avoids this only via `{{#match @custom.color_scheme "Dark"}}` in `default.hbs`, which FR-Q5 does not say Inflozo copies); dark accent is "probably" fine, and probably is doing the work of a requirement. The user did nothing wrong and cannot deploy.
Fix: decouple **reservation** from **emission** — keep FR-Q2's 3-of-20 slot reservation unconditional (it costs nothing and preserves the uniform cap), and declare a built-in in `package.json` only when the assembled templates contain its `{{@custom.*}}` read. Add a compiler post-pass asserting `declared_keys ⊆ referenced_keys` that fails with an Inflozo-side message *before* gscan, whose own message ("declared but never referenced from a template") is unactionable for a user who never saw a `package.json`. Or specify exactly where `default.hbs` unconditionally reads all three.

**[Theme quality]** — `wrapGuard` emits a garbage guard identifier — silent, permanent content loss (`spike-compiler/compile.js:154`, §7.3, NFR-2 assertion 5)
CONFIRMED by the facilitator. `wrapGuard` derives the guard field via `expr.replace(/^\{\{|\}\}$/g,'').split(' ').pop().replace(/"/g,'')`. For `data-bind="published_at|date:D MMM YYYY"` with `data-empty="hide"`, `expr` is `{{date published_at format="D MMM YYYY"}}` and the last space-delimited token yields `YYYY"` → `YYYY`. The compiler emits **`{{#if YYYY}}`** — not in any Ghost context, always false, element never renders on the live site, on every post, forever. No error at compile, gscan, upload or render. The canvas takes a different code path (`applyBindings`, which evaluates the value) and shows the element correctly, so **§7.3's "canvas and shipped markup agree by construction" is violated by this bug specifically, invisibly, inside the editor.** The attribute path (`compile.js:148`) is only accidentally correct: it passes the raw field to the same mangler, which then runs its brace-stripping over a string with no braces — CONFIRMED, and it is why the tests stay green. `test.js` covers only the attribute path (assertion 10). NFR-2's assertion (5) — "no unguarded `src`/`srcset` survives compilation" — passes, because the guard is present; it is just wrong.
Fix: fix the derivation before E4 exits, and add the helper-bound-guard case to the spike's assertions (a date, a reading time, any `img_url`-bound text with `data-empty="hide"`). Every mechanic §7.3 elevates to "binding on the implementation" is a token/ordering mechanic; this entire class — guard-field derivation — is unmentioned, untested and wrong in the reference implementation.

**[Theme quality · WYSIWYG · Ghost truth]** — No `config.image_sizes`, but every image uses `img_url size=` — all sizing is a silent no-op (FR-J2, §7.4, NFR-2 assertion 1)
*Found independently by 3 reviewers.* CONFIRMED: the spike's emitted `package.json` `config` block contains only `posts_per_page` and `card_assets` — **no `image_sizes`** — while `post.hbs:7` and `page.hbs:8` call `size="l"` and `partials/post-card.hbs:4` calls `size="m"`. With no map there are no generated renditions and no `/size/wN/` segment: every image on the site serves at original upload resolution (a 4000px hero behind a 300px card). `grep -rl image_sizes node_modules/gscan/lib/` returns nothing — **gscan never validates `image_sizes`**, and an unknown `size=` key silently returns the unsized URL (`frontend/utils/images.js`). Compounding it, `size="m"` has no fixed meaning independent of `package.json` (Casper's `l` is 1000; Source's `l` is 960), and NFR-6(c2) asserts shim output against "recorded real-Ghost output" without stating whose `package.json` was installed when the recording was made — if it was Casper's or Source's, the test enshrines the wrong scale and passes forever.
Fix: pin the `image_sizes` map as a normative table in FR-J2 with actual widths; add a compile assertion that every `size=` argument in emitted `.hbs` exists as a key in the emitted `config.image_sizes`; state in NFR-6(c2) that recordings are made against an **Inflozo-emitted** `package.json`, and add the external-image and Unsplash pass-through cases to the recorded input set (`img_url` returns non-internal images unchanged, except Unsplash).

**[Theme quality]** — `engines.ghost: ">=6.0.0"` contradicts NFR-7's 5.x support, and no gate sees it (`spike-compiler/theme-output/package.json`, FR-J2, NFR-7, §4, FR-C8)

**[Buildability · Theme quality · WYSIWYG]** — No JS behavior-module contract exists anywhere, and FR-J4's module list is a 3× undercount (§7.3, FR-J4, FR-G3, FR-G4, NFR-2)
