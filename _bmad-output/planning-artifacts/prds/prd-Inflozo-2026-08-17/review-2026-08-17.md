# BMad Review Findings — Inflozo PRD v2.0 (2026-08-17)

Full multi-lens stress test of `prd.md` (same folder). Lenses: adversarial (18), edge-case-hunter (25), editorial structure (10), editorial prose (10 + 7 minor). Verification-gap skipped (code-only).

**Verdict:** Strong, precise PRD; editorial lenses found no shape problems, only reference-integrity fixes. The dominant theme in the behavioral findings is **undefined lifecycle transitions** (downgrade, disconnect, rename, delete, library update, mode toggle). Two outright spec contradictions (§7.3/NFR-3 sandbox vs. rect-measurement; FR-E4/FR-G4/FR-Q5 no-JS dark mode) and one likely compliance defect (FR-A5 deletion cascade omits suggestions/votes/notifications).

**Highest-confidence findings (flagged independently by two lenses):**
1. GDPR deletion cascade gap (FR-A5 omits suggestions, votes, notification rows)
2. Library-update migration policy missing (FR-J14 — no per-project pinning, no doc migration/deprecation rules)
3. 20-setting cap unvalidated at the Light-only↔Light+Dark toggle (FR-Q2/FR-Q5/FR-D7)
4. Zero-feed collection templates undefined (FR-H2/FR-I1 — duplicate /page/N/ SEO defect)
5. Theme-name/slug immutability undefined (FR-J10 — renames orphan themes; slug collisions overwrite)
