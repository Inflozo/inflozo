## In plain English

Until now the editing canvas has had nothing to show: a section that lists your latest posts had no
posts to list, and a design that wraps an article had no article to wrap. This story writes the sample
publication — **Orbit Weekly**, with its posts, tags, writers, tiers, menu and pictures, all made by us
so nothing is borrowed — and a **style-guide article** that carries one of every kind of block Ghost can
put on a page, generated by asking a real Ghost to print it rather than by writing it out by hand. After
this, every design in the library has something real to be judged against, and you can open one internal
page and see the article, the comments, the page version and a second sheet carrying every variation of
every block, exactly as a customer's own post will look.

## Intent

**Problem:** Every design in the library is authored against a sample dataset and a generated article
that do not exist. The consumption seam is already built and already refuses: `ShimContext.fixtures`
(`packages/ghost-shim/src/index.ts:569`) and `RenderInput.fixtures` / `getRows`
(`packages/section-runtime/src/core.ts:126,:132`) were written for this story, and `bareHelper('content')`
throws by name rather than inventing a body (`ghost-shim/src/index.ts:582-587`). `previewSeed` is a
dangling reference — one value, `"orbit-weekly"`, validated only as a non-empty string
(`packages/library/src/validate.ts:361-363`), resolved by nothing. Until the data arrives, no design can
be previewed, no pilot can be authored (4.10) and nothing can be diffed (4.11).

**Approach:** Land the dataset as versioned data under `packages/library/orbit-weekly/`
(`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:491`), sized by FR-H3's two normative rules rather than by a number; record the
three fixtures from a **real Ghost on both majors** through the Admin/Content APIs so the bytes are
Ghost's own renderers' output by construction, checked in with their capture date and command (AD-23,
`ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:250-254`); add the one mechanism that is genuinely missing — an offline evaluator
that turns a `DataBinding` into rows, because `expandRepeats` refuses a declared `{{#get}}` key with no
rows (`core.ts:869-875`) and nothing resolves one today; and give the owner one internal page to look at
it on, beside the existing component gallery.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- **The dataset's size is two rules, not two numbers** (`prd.md:315`): the feed must exceed
  `posts_per_page` by enough to render a **middle page** (the only state carrying both a previous and a
  next link) *and* a partial page, and the two must never be equal. Write the rule, assert the rule, and
  let the arithmetic derive — the values below satisfy it and the test proves it rather than restating it.
- **The composition is the owner's, ruled 2026-09-13 (Q2), and it is deliberately ahead of what any
  design needs today** so a section authored later does not have to grow the dataset again: **52 feed
  posts · 15 featured · 16 authors · 6 tiers · 5 newsletters · 6 topic tags plus the `newsletter` tag
  A22's default binds to · twelve press logos**. Each figure clears the family that was short of it —
  the Writers grid built for twelve, the Pricing designs' two-to-three paid cards, the Picks design that
  offers a count of ten, the Logo Wall's twelve publications — with room over.
- **Each of the 16 writers appears on at least six posts, and that works because a post may carry more
  than one author.** Ghost's own model, and both forms are already in the recorded surface — `{{authors}}`
  renders the list, `{{primary_author}}` one name (`packages/ghost-shim/fixtures/ghost6/post.json`,
  `values.AUTHORS`) — so a by-author Source (`authors:slug`) fills for every writer while a card still
  shows the single byline the frames draw. Sixteen writers each needing six posts is 96 author slots over
  52 posts; one author per post cannot do it and co-authorship does, with nothing invented.
- **Every Data-group Source returns a usable set** (`prd.md:317`): featured, each tag, each author, and a
  combined filter (featured AND by tag) all return rows. A Source that renders empty in the editor is
  indistinguishable from a broken binding, which is the whole reason the composition is specified.
- **All imagery internally produced and licence-clean** (`prd.md:313`). `tools/probe/seed-ghost.py:74-76`
  points every feature image at `static.ghost.org` and is **not** reusable for this.
- **A Ghost fact enters only as a recorded fixture** with its capture date and the command that produced
  it, read by tests, never paraphrased (AD-23). A recording missing for either major fails by name —
  follow `packages/ghost-shim/src/contract.test.ts:80-126`, which never skips.
- **A result whose control did not pass is not a result** (standing rule 2). The recorder's control: the
  four documented root classes (`kg-image-card`, `kg-bookmark-card`, `kg-gallery-card`,
  `kg-toggle-card`) come back on **both** majors, as `MEASUREMENTS.md:2656` uses them.
- **The fixtures are excluded from every feed.** They are not among the feed posts — otherwise the page
  arithmetic stops holding — they are returned by no Source, and they contribute to no tag or author
  count. They are reachable only as preview subjects (FR-D22, `prd.md:247`).
- **Purity.** `packages/library`'s code takes no `fetch`, no `node:fs`, no `Date.now`, no `Intl.*` and no
  locale-reading method (AD-1, `eslint.config.js:22`'s derived `CORE` glob already covers a new
  directory). The recorder is a `tools/` script that runs offline and writes files; the checked-in result
  is what the runtime reads.
- **The generated body is trusted by construction, and NFR-3 is untouched** (`prd.md:481`, `:325`). The
  one sanctioned `innerHTML` on a canvas render already exists and already says so
  (`core.ts:606-611`); this story supplies its input and loosens nothing about what the canvas may read
  from a connected site.

**Ask First:**
- **Both questions are ruled (owner, 2026-09-13) and nothing waits on them** — the article and the
  variation sheet are both built, and the dataset is grown to the sizes he set. Read the rulings before
  the tasks; they are what the composition bullet below and the corpus task carry.
- Any change to `packages/ghost-shim` or `packages/section-runtime`. The seam is finished and this story
  fills inputs; if an edit there looks necessary, it is a sign the data is the wrong shape — stop and ask.
- Widening the dataset beyond the sizes Q2's ruling sets, or adding any third-party dependency (no `@tryghost/*`
  package is installed and none needs to be — see Design Notes).

**Never:**
- Never hand-write the card markup. "Generated from Ghost's own renderers, never hand-written"
  (`prd.md:319`) is the point of the fixture: a target bump must surface as a visible fixture diff
  rather than as silent canvas drift (`prd.md:487`).
- Never snapshot Ghost 5's **mobiledoc** renderer. R-66 targets the current (Lexical) renderer only, and
  six card types emit different class sets through the old one (`MEASUREMENTS.md:2694-2718`).
- Never write the `card_assets` exclude list down. It is derived from which cards the user designed
  (`prd.md:373`) and the simulated bundle is its complement (`prd.md:319`) — no card is designed yet, so
  today the bundle carries every chunk, and it shrinks on its own as categories land.
- Never edit the design export (R-74). Never author a design, a pilot or a card treatment here — those
  belong to their owning category (4.10 and Epics 9–11).
- No editor, no canvas route, no Style Pack. The review page is a static internal surface, not E5's editor.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| Every Source returns rows | each `DataBinding` shape: latest, featured, by tag, by author, hand-picked `ids`, featured+tag combined | a non-empty row set for each; `ids` preserves the authored order | N/A |
| A Source matches nothing | `filter: 'tag:does-not-exist'` | `[]` — the design draws its own empty state | returns empty, never throws: an empty Source is a legitimate answer |
| A fixture leaks into a feed | any Source, any filter | the three fixture subjects appear in no row set, and in no tag or author count | test asserts by id over every Source |
| The page arithmetic | `posts_per_page` from the dataset | a first page, a middle page carrying both links, and a partial last page | test derives the page count and fails if either rule breaks |
| A deep pager | a design asking for more pages than the feed has | a pagination context carrying the depth **without inventing posts** — a pager reads only `pagination` | N/A |
| `{{content}}` with no fixture | `RenderInput` without `fixtures.content` | the existing refusal, by name (`ghost-shim/src/index.ts:582-587`) | unchanged — assert it still fires |
| A recording is missing | `orbit-weekly/fixtures/ghost5/` absent | the test fails naming the fixture **and the capture command** | `contract.test.ts:80-90`'s pattern; never skip |
| The recorder's control fails | a documented root class absent on either major | the run is void: exit non-zero, write nothing | standing rule 2 — a partial recording is worse than none |
| A card Ghost has no renderer for | the corpus asks for a card absent from the target | the recorder refuses by name rather than writing an empty snapshot | an empty snapshot reads as a passing test |
| The two majors disagree | a card's class set differs between 5.130.6 and 6.58.0 | both recordings are kept and the difference is asserted **positively**, never normalised away | normalising blinds the one test that underwrites WYSIWYG |

## Design Notes

**Why a real Ghost and not `render*Node` directly.** FR-H3 says the body is "generated from Ghost's own
renderers, never hand-written", and names snapshotting each `render*Node`'s output. Invoking those
renderers ourselves means installing or SSH-reading `@tryghost/kg-default-nodes` — which is installed
nowhere (the 70 `@tryghost/*` entries in the lockfile are all transitive under `gscan`), lives at a
different path on each major (`MEASUREMENTS.md:2656-2659`), and takes an options object nobody has ever
executed. Creating the corpus post on T1 and T3 and reading `html` back through the Content API produces
**the same renderers' output with none of that machinery** — `post.html` is what `{{content}}` emits, so
the recording becomes the stronger fact: not "what the renderer returns when we call it", but "what
Ghost actually prints". Both test servers already exist for exactly this (standing rule 1), and
`record-shim.py` already does every step. The intent — never hand-written, snapshotted per card, diffing
on a target bump — is met in full; only the means is lazier. Record this in the spec's `## Verification`
and carry it to FR-H3 if the review agrees.

**The exclude list is a complement, and that is why it never goes stale.** `card_assets: { exclude: [...] }`
names every card the user designed (`prd.md:373`); the simulated bundle is what is left. No card is
designed today, so the bundle is the whole chunk set and the fixture shows Ghost's defaults everywhere —
which is the honest state. As categories land, the exclude list grows and the bundle shrinks with no
edit here. Derive it from the chunk universe (`research-ghost-koenig-cards.md:179-199`) minus whatever
declares itself designed; never write either list down. Two traps recorded in the research: `header` and
`header_v2` are **separate** names and both must be listed, and an unknown name is **silently ignored**,
so a stale list degrades quietly rather than erroring.

**Two contradictions resolved here rather than asked, because the rule already decides them.**
(1) The comments fixture's count is FR-H3's, not the 24 the A28 frames draw: R-74's scoping is that the
PRD decides what a surface *does* and the export decides what it is *built from*, and a count is data.
So the fixture carries FR-H3's threads, drawn exactly as `a28-kit.js:91-139` draws them. (2) C4's toggle:
its Notes say closed while the frame draws it open (`:1802-1808`). FR-H3 wants both states, the corpus
carries both, and the readable article uses the state the frame **draws**, since the drawing is the
authority and the note is prose about it.

**Why sixteen writers and fifty-two posts are not in conflict.** The Source usability rule wants every
author's archive to fill, which for sixteen writers at six posts each is 96 author slots — more than
there are posts. Co-authorship closes it at about 1.9 authors per post, which is what a real publication
looks like anyway: NQL's `authors:slug` matches any author on the post, so every writer's page fills,
while `primary_author` stays single so every card keeps the one byline A17's and A27's frames draw. No
design filters on *primary* author — the Source vocabulary is `posts · tags · authors · tiers`
(`vocabulary.ts:167`) and the filter is NQL text — so nothing is left thin by the split.

**One thing the dataset cannot exercise, by construction.** Its images are local URLs, so `img_url`
passes them through unchanged and no sized rendition or `srcset` candidate is produced. That is correct
— there is no Ghost to resize them — and it is precisely why Story 4.3's recorder uploaded a real image
to each server. Nothing here should try to fake a rendition.

## Questions for the owner

### Q1 — the style-guide article: one readable post, or every variation, or both?

Two approved documents describe this fixture differently, and no single artefact can satisfy both.

The **product requirement** (FR-H3) asks for every variation of every block that changes how it looks:
an image at three widths with and without a caption, a callout in nine colours with and without an
emoji, a call-to-action in nine backgrounds in two layouts, and so on. That is hundreds of blocks.

The **drawing** (frame C4, which is the design authority) draws one readable article with **one of each**
block, and says in its own notes that two of the same card in one article "would look like a bug".

**An example of the conflict.** The callout box — the coloured box with the emoji. FR-H3 wants nine of
them, one per colour, so that every colour can be checked. C4 draws exactly one, in orange, because an
article with nine coloured boxes in a row is not an article anybody would read.

**The options:**

1. **Both, and each has one job (RECOMMENDED).** The readable article is C4's, one of each block — that
   is what the canvas shows on every post, and what every post design is judged against. Separately, a
   **variation sheet** carries every version of every block; nobody reads it, it is what the card
   settings panels preview against and what the picture-comparison test compares. Cost: one more
   generated file, produced by the same recorder in the same run. The article stays readable and nothing
   goes unchecked.
2. **The readable article only.** Cheapest, and the canvas looks right — but a colour, a width or a
   layout nobody drew can ship broken and no test would catch it. Later epics would have to add the
   variation sheet anyway, and re-record everything to get it.
3. **The variation sheet only.** Every case covered, but the canvas would show a wall of examples
   instead of an article, and every post design in the library would be judged against something no
   reader would ever see.

**Ruled: option 1 (owner, 2026-09-13).** Both, and each has one job. One corpus, recorded in one run,
rendered twice: `fixtures/{ghost5,ghost6}/…` carry C4's readable article as the `{{content}}` value every
post design is judged against, and `fixtures/variations.html` carries every class-affecting variant,
labelled, for FR-Q7's card panels and 4.11's comparison. The header card lives in the sheet and stays out
of the article, which settles C4's ABSENT row against FR-H3's enumeration without either giving way.

### Q2 — does the sample publication grow to cover the designs that need more than it carries?

Orbit Weekly as specified carries 32 posts, 6 tags, **3 writers** and **2 tiers** (one free, one paid).
Six places in the design library need more than that, and each was flagged during design review with no
decision taken. Where the sample runs short, the design previews empty or half-full in the editor — and
an empty preview looks exactly like a bug.

**An example.** The "Writers" designs draw **twelve** author photographs on every frame, and one of them
switches itself off below six writers. With three writers in the sample, a customer opening that design
sees three faces in a grid built for twelve, and the four-column version cannot be shown at all.

The six shortfalls: twelve writers rather than three (the Writers designs); a second paid tier (the
Pricing designs draw two or three paid cards); at least three newsletters, one of them paid (the
Newsletter designs, and one of them defaults to a `newsletter` tag that is currently empty); ten featured
posts rather than eight (one Picks design offers a count of ten); a set of press logos (the Logo Wall
designs preview against twelve publications); and a deeper page count for the pagination designs — that
last one is free and I have included it either way, because a pager only reads the page numbers and needs
no extra posts.

**The options:**

1. **Grow it now, in this story (RECOMMENDED).** Add the writers, the second paid tier, the newsletters,
   the two extra featured posts and the press logos while the dataset is being written. It is more
   authored content and more drawn pictures — perhaps half a day — and it is much cheaper here than
   after four hundred designs have been authored against a sample that was too thin.
2. **Leave it as specified and record that those designs preview short.** Fastest now. Every affected
   design is authored against a preview that does not fill, and whoever authors them has to decide, one
   at a time, whether what they see is the design or the data.
3. **Grow only the writers and the second paid tier.** The two that break a layout outright, deferring
   the newsletters and the logos. A middle cost, and the newsletter designs stay unpreviewable.

**Ruled: option 1, at the owner's own sizes and deliberately above every stated need (owner,
2026-09-13):** **52 feed posts · 15 featured · 16 authors · 6 tiers · 5 newsletters**, plus the twelve
press logos option 1 carried and the `newsletter` tag A22's default binds to. His reason, recorded
because it governs the next person who reads this: *"helpful if we add more new sections in future which
may need these increased counts"* — so the dataset is built to be grown into, not to be grown again.

Two consequences of those figures, both handled and neither a change to them. **The page arithmetic still
holds:** 52 against a `posts_per_page` of 12 gives four full pages and a partial fifth, so a first page,
three middle pages carrying both links, and a partial last page all exist, and the feed never equals
`posts_per_page`. **And each of the sixteen writers still gets a full archive**, because a post carries
more than one author — Ghost's own model, `{{authors}}` and `{{primary_author}}` both already in the
recorded surface — which is what makes six posts each possible from 52; see Design Notes. Cards keep the
single byline the frames draw. Read "6 tiers" as six including Free, so five are paid, and at least one
of the five newsletters is paid; both clear every design that was short, and either is a one-line data
change if he meant otherwise.
